This collection represents the efforts of Connie G Hicks to document the response to the attacks of September 11, 2001, both in the rural community on the Upper Peninsula of Michigan as well as the broader national and global communities.  The collection consists of more than 100 pages of hand-written notes recording the reactions of neighbors, students, as well as world leaders and newspaper headlines.
