Below is the real-time email record of a group co-workers evacuating lower Manhattan on September 11, 2001. The emails were sent via the handheld wireless email system Blackberry, while the co-workers escaped their offices near Ground Zero. The first email was sent just minutes after the first impact. The last was sent when all were counted and safely evacuated.
