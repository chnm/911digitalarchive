A four CD collection of images created by various photographers. Donated by Michael Garcia, via Mills Kelley, this collection comprises 2 GB of images. This imagery was photographed by Special Agent Michael Garcia OSI, DPS, office of the Secretary of Defense following the terrorist attack on the Pentagon 11 Sept 2001. No captions were taken and no names are known.
