Be a part of how the history of 9-11 gets told by recording your personal story for the September 11 Digital Archive. Your story need not be heroic or harrowing. Just tell us where you were and how you were affected by the attacks that occurred on September 11. Including your name, age, and zip code is optional, but will help us and will increase the historical value of your recollections. Unless you instruct us otherwise we will assume that by leaving this message you authorize us to use your voice in our online and other projects. You have up to three minutes to respond.
