As a way of dealing with the hurt and frustration of 9-11, I wrote a Simpsons   script to show how the events of that day affected the residents of Springfield.  I'm not sure if there is a place on your site for such a work, but if you would  like, I'll attach it here for you to read. It's not that long, and though it starts out like any other Simpsons script, with laughs, it eventually takes on the tone of a serious episode and hopefully, a message of hope.
After writing this as a script, I've toyed with the idea of making it into a comic book like the NYFD. I'd never take a single penny for this and if this could be used to help out unfortunate people and organizations that help those in need,  I'd certainly sign it away for them to use for good.

Sincerely,
Dana Goudreault
