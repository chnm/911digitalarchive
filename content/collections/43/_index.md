A collection of personal photographs I have taken of the WTC disaster.  I work for a cellular carrier in NYC and was on the roof of a building in Chelsea on the morning of the 11th.  The next day I was called down to help search for people who were apparently calling for help on their cell phones from the pile.  While this was fruitless (I think they were hoaxes) I spent the next few months working in the Zone taking pictures the whole time.  I have sent these photos around to coworkers and friends but they are unpublished and frankly "better" than anything I have seen out there.
