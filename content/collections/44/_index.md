William Wade details the experiences he had while on a business trip in New York City, during September 10 through September 15, 2001.  In journal like format, Wade describes the horror and confusion associated with experiencing this traumatic experience.  Wade illustrates his experience with the thoughts he had at the time using vibrant descriptions of the weather, buildings, people and atmosphere.  Interspersed with Wades commentary, are images that depict his explanations and add meaningful insight to the drama prose.
