I am Carl Dorsey.  I live in Easton, Pa.  I was so moved by 911 that I wrote 13 songs to commemorate it.  The songs reflect on the lives of all of those lost, their loved one and the America that remained.  I do not sell the music.  It is available to listen to and download free at www.sept11music.com or www.cdomusic.com.  The site also includes music videos to some of the songs as well.  Please share this link with anyone who might like to hear a complete tribute to the people and events of 911.  It was written as a historical memorial so I thought you might be able to use it.  My very best regards!

Sincerely,
Carl S. Dorsey
