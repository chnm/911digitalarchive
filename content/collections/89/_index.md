“On September 11, 2001 the citizens of planet Earth were united in human tragedy.  Silent irony stood mute witness to the events unfolding at her feet and across the globe.  This is her story.”  The following movie contains a slide show of images from the destruction of the World Trade Center Towers on September 11, 2001, and a collection of images depicting people from many different countries mourning those killed during the events.  A picture of the Statue of Liberty is superimposed over all of the images on the left side.  The location of each image is given briefly in the lower right hand corner as each picture is shown.
