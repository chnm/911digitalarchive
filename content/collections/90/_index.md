Shortly before September 11th, 2002, I realized that I needed to tell this story.  I had taken 125 plus photographs and put them away.  Frankly, no one really wanted to see them.  However, that Day, that week and the weeks to follow were very important to my nieces and I and are a part of history.  So, I compiled 125 photographs into a 38 page PowerPoint presentation and wrote our story.

This collection contains a Word document and PDF file of a condensed version of the PowerPoint presentation.
