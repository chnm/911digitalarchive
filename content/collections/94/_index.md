I found this clip to be very moving. I downloaded it from a computer animation bulletin board on 9/11 or 9/12. Everyone at our studio (located in Buffalo, NY) remembers 9/11 intensely. After we all gathered for work and huddled around the radio and cnn.com we wondered how much further the attacks would go. The images of the twin towers in flames will never be erased from our minds. We all left the office almost as soon as we arrived, unable to even begin to work. Our thoughts went out to friends and clients in Manhattan, hoping they were safe from harm, wishing that we could somehow lend a hand.

I wish you all the best with your 9/11 archive. I am sure it will be a valuable resource for those looking back to that incredible day.

Ben Porcari
