        I was sitting in class, working on Math along with everyone
        else in the room.  It was just another day of school.  The
        principal's voice came over the loudspeaker, but we all just
        figured it was a routine announcement.  Were we ever wrong!

        Pentagon and two other planes had flown directly into the
        twin towers sending them crashing to the ground. It all
        seemed unreal.  He asked anyone who had parents working at
        the Pentagon to report to the office.  Everyone was in a
        sense of terror, panic, and disbelief.  I know for me, it
        was more disbelief.  How could this be happening on just a
        typical September school day?  Why today?  Everyone was on
        their cell phones or in line at the office phone attempting
        to contact their parents, friends, or relatives.  There was a
        sense of urgency to find out if they had been directly
        affected. Luckily no one in my family was a victim of this
        terrorism, but several people I know lost loved ones.  It
        was a day no one will ever forget, a day to go down in
        history, September 11, 2001.
