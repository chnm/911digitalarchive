        911 woke me up


        'Headline news', broke through the office
        the day was shot as the TV was the focus.

        I quickly received emails
        phone calls, discussions
        of shock, of horror

        I received emails of gore
        and emails of beauty

        We prayed, cried and hugged
        in the following days

        More emails, more sentiment
        the hawks and doves divided

        Some old doves wore new talons
        some old hawks had softer beaks

        911 an emergency, a wakeup call
        woke me up

        The incident is not important
        the reaction is

        What happenned is physical, material, earthbound
        the reaction could be, but shouldn't be

        911 woke me up
        while hate and vengeance were preached

        I wondered why, what will it solve
        won't war always begets war

        Wasn't WWI the war to end all wars

        Weren't Hiroshima and Nagosaki horrific enough to remember

        Didn't enough sons and dads die in Korea and Vietnam

        Isn't 25% of Iraqi's enough people to eliminate

        Isn't it time to stop

        Buddha, Krishna, Moses, Jesus, Mohammed, and others
        preached peace, unconditional love, forgiveness,
        acceptance, gratitude..

        Who is your leader, guide, mentor

        Has 911 woke you up

        The time to end hypocricy and war is now

        If not now, when

        If not us, who



        wil allyn
        9-9-2002
