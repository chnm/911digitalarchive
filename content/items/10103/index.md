        I was at home with my two small children on this horrific Tuesday morning.  Watching with disbelieve, I prayed that this was only a terrible dream.  I was completely in awe. And I remind myself that there are parents that have lost thier chldren on this day.  I cannot image the feeling of losing children, parents, siblings or friends to such sensless death.
