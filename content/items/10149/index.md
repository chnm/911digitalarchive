      text: I was getting ready for work when my mom rushed in and told me to go to the nearest television.  I watched as they showed over and over the planes hitting the towers.  As that was happening a local station out here happened to be at the Pentagon and we watched live as the the reporter heard an explosion and saw construction workers running away. It was horrible, but it also brought out the best in our country.  It gave me a new faith in America.  God bless America and God bless all our soldiers fighting to defend it.
