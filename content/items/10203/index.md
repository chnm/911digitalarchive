      text: I'm a civilian employee at Luke Air Force Base.I was entering the base about 0600 Arizona time, 0900 New York time. I usually listen to one of funny wake you up morning radio stations as was the case that morning. Instead of hearing laughter and music, I was surprised to hear the urgency and the fear in the broadcaster's voice. As soon as I was at my desk my wife called me at work and told me to get to a TV.
