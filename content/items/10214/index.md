        I run a small daycare so my husband new that I probably had the Disney channel on and didn't know what was happening.  He called and told me to turn it over to a regular channel.  All I could do when I saw what was happening was sit on my living room floor, hold my 9 month old daughter and cry.  My God, this is America - these things don't happen here!  Even though I was safe in my small Iowa town, in the confines of my own home I felt so vulnerable.

        The mother's of the other two children I watch called, knowing that their kids were fine, but just needed to be reconfirmed of that fact and to hear their small voices.

        Today, September 11, 2002 - I feel a strange emptiness that can't be explained.  I didn't know any of the victims, but I have shed many tears today.

        One more thought - PLEASE hug your children every day and let them know how special they are to you.
