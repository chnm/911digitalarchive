Then as we turned up the volume we had heard that a plane had been hijacked and had crashed into the north tower at 8:48 a.m.  We had to go to our next class and my next class was Global Studies.  When we got there we had heard that a second plane crashed into the south tower eighteen minutes later.  Then a third plane was seen over the Pentagon and crashed down into the western part of the Pentagon.  A fourth plane was forced down by the passengers in Pittsburgh Pennsylvania.  In all 266 people perished in the four planes and several others died elsewhere.  When I got home my mother had heard something on this radio station and thought that they were just kidding around.
