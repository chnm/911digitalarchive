        I was working at my desk in the Adult Education Dean's office when my daughter called from home to say that a plane had flown into one of the WTC buildings.  We all thought it was an accident until the second plane hit.

        I can hardly explain my feelings that morning...profound sorrow, astonishment, fear but, above all...a feeling of being cut off from reality.  Could this really be happening?  As the events unfolded, I cried for the victims, their families, their friends, and for our country.  My co-workers hugged each other and gathered in silence.  There was not much to say; we were in shock.

        I thought about New York City (I was born and raised there) and imagined the devastation created by this violence.  I have nothing but admiration for all the people who were in New York helping during that trying time.  I admire the passengers on Flight 93 and their resolve to fight back.  It makes me so proud to be an american.

        Above all, I think about the families affected by this event.  I can not imagine living with such anguish.
