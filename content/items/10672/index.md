        I am an over the road truck driver. The morning of Sept. 11,
        2001, I was sleeping in my unit at our terminal when I was
        awakened by a message on my computer. It was my daughter wanting
        to know where I was at.
          I was in the New York area a few days before and she wanted
        to make sure I was ok.
          I turned on my T.V. and saw what was happening. The second plane
        was just hitting the tower. I just sat there stunned,trying to comprehend
        what was happening.
          I didn't have to leave any time soon so,I just watched as
        it all unfolded.
          I sent my daughter a message that I was alright.
          My wife called me with the same questions. I assured her I was
        fine.
          One of my brothers,who is also a truck driver,called asking where
        I was and if I was ok.
          I just spent most of the day watching the news,wondering
        who,what and why,like most of the country was I suppose.
          I was on Long Island the night they turned on the search
        lights. As I went back to New Jersey around 20:30 hours,it
        was quite a sight to see them!
          I cannot imagine what was going through the minds of all
        the people. All I could do was pray for them.
