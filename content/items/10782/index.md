        What a beautiful day it was. I took my 1 yr old daughter to have her shots done. At 9:00 am I noticed several people crying, I assumed these people had just lost a loved one, little did I know we all had lost a loved one. I stopped at a family store where they was watching tv. They were crying too. I looked up and saw the most hiddious site. I attempted to retrieve my other two children from school. At this time I was sick and took myself to the doctor's office to have my unborn child checked due to the stress I was in. I was told 'OH , It will be another week'. My children and I went to visit several family members. I began to experience pains again. At 10:29 pm that evening I delivered a beautiful baby boy. I will look at this child and remember that although these terrible people took our families and friends we have the next generation to protect and teach the importance of freedom and love. For each live taken there was two lives born. With love and prayers to all remember to stand together.
                                         Bambi adams & Family
