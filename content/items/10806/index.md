        They say we'll never forget where we were on the morning of September 11, 2001.

        I was making the short drive to work from Oak Cliff when I first heard the news on a rock station...I thought, "My Lord, these shock jocks have no conscience left."  I switched stations as I approached the Commerce Street bridge towards downtown Dallas.  I heard the same news.

        As I crossed the bridge, I looked up at the panoramic view of downtown Dallas directly in front of me.

        There was not an aircraft in site...anywhere.  Never in my 40+ years had I seen the empty skies above Dallas.

        My heart sank and then reality sank in as well.  I will never forget the empty sky and will always remember that moment of dispair and wondering what would happen next.

        I hope peace will someday be the rule for all.
