        We saw the horror on television, after hearing 'rumors'...my friend and I walked down to our lunch room, and as we walked in, we saw the 1st tower smoking.  Then, the room went silent as we saw a plane come from seemingly nowhere and slam into the 2nd tower.  We were all stunned.  Our thoughts and prayers were with our business associates at Empire Blue Cross.  They lost fewer employees than others on higher floors, thank God.

        My reaction, as always is to write poetry.  I have written this poem "Anniversary", in remembrance:

        Anniversary

        Only a year has passed
        Yet it seems we have always lived it.
        365 days?
        so quickly gone by
        yet so achingly empty at times.

        We compare it to Pearl Harbor,
        Kennedy's assassination
        King, another Kennedy.
        Yet, the horror is multiplied
        By 3000.

        Loved ones never to be found
        Literally dust to dust.
        Bits and pieces of others.
        A watch, a camera,
        Only these remain
        Sad souvenirs.

        What can make us whole again?
        A war?
        More violence?
        Other shattered lives?
        Thousands of other
        Innocent victims?

        I can't remember life
        Before 9/11/01.
        How peaceful and
        Na?ve we must have been.
        Changed in minutes
        Altered forever.

        Mlp
        9/10/02
