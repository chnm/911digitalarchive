      text: The attacks occured on a Tuesday which is one of my days off from school (University of Baltimore)so I was sleeping in.  As I was sleeping my mother came down and woke me and said "I think I sould tell you what has happened this morning".  At that time the first plane had already hit one of the towers.  I went upstairs to the kitchen to watch the TV and saw smoke pouring out of the tower.  I counld not believe my eyes.  My first reaction was that this must have been an accident.  It seemed as though it was something right out of Hollywood.  However, as a pilot of small planes myself, I realized that there is no way that this could be an accident.  It was a clear day,  and it was not a prescribed air traffic pattern.  In other words, no plane would have been anywhere near those towers.  It was at that moment that the second plane hit the other tower.  I could not believe my eyes.  This had to be a nightmare.  I was glued to the TV for the remainder of the day and a short while later I heard that another plane had crashed into the Pentagon.  I, like the rest of America, had no idea what was going on.  I felt a tremendous amount of anger as the day wore on and suspicion turned to Osama Bin Ladan.  I wanted revenge.  For the remainder of the day I thought of nothing else and founds it hard to do anything else.  This was the case for roughly two weeks.  It was such a shock to my system.  I could not believe that such an attack had occured on our own soil.
