        AT ABOUT 9:15 am the morning bus driver told me about it.
        I was heading home on the bus at 2:30pm and I was stuck on the school bus and couldn't come home until 8:30pm!!! We tried I-80 it was bumper to bumper we tried Route 46 it was bumper to bumper! we tried Teaneck rd. IT WAS BUMPER TO BUMPER!!!!! It was a very long and sad day for me that I shall never forget.
