        My name is Donna. On 9-11-01 I was in my bed thinking that I was recovering from a gallbladder  surgury. I lay in my bed and watched as the news broke for a major news story.
        I wasn't as well as I thought I was, so I just laid there and remember saying to myself that alot of people just lost their lives and for what and why is this happening.
          The next day I was put on life support.  I wasn't recovering at all.  It took me a few days after being in the hospital for people to tell me that we were in a WAR.
        My heart just broke in two.
        I felt helpless, that I was in this stupid hospital and I couldn't help anybody. I didn't leave for 46 days.
        But as my family let me (trying to help me) watch the news I was amazed how Americans were all pulling together to help one another. It was a time for love and lots of it.  And we as a nation did that and are still doing it.
