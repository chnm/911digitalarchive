      text: This is not much but it is my story.  On the morning of September 11th 2001, I was watching Good Morning America in a car dealership waiting room (waiting for my wife's car to be serviced).  Suddenly, the news broke that something had happened at the World Trade Center.  They showed Tower 1 on fire and I watched in disbelief.  At the time, they didn't know what had happened.  They were speculating that either a plane had struck the tower or that possibly a missile had been fired at the building.  Again, it was all speculation because nobody had spoken to an eye-witness yet.  Just then, I was told that my wife's car was ready so I headed for work (about a 3 minute drive).  While in the car, I left my wife and mom phone messages describing what I had seen on television. After that, I turned on the radio.  At that moment, a radio reporter was interviewing an eye witness when suddenly he started screeming that the second tower had been hit.  When I heard that, the hair on the back of my neck literally stood up.  I was shocked.  After that news, I rushed into a work parking spot, ran into the building, and watched the horror of September 11th unfold.  I will never forget how I felt when I heard the second tower get hit.  The sound of that man screaming will always be with me.
