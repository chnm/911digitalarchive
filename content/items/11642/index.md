        In a little room in the skyscraper district of Tokyo, it was still 9/11 in Japan, but during the nightly news a box was suddenly inserted in the corner of the TV screen with live footage from CNN. It was mostly shaking heads and embarrassed smiles to me, a Bostonian in their midst. It seemed that the media gradually gave people a discourse structure to discuss the events, but it was surprising how different the view from Japan came to be.
