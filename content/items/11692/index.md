        On the morning of September the 11th I was at home with my  daughters. I was woken up from a phone call from my husband, who told me to turn the news on. I called my husband and told him to come home.We were having problems in our marriage and I had to make it right.The way things were going that day I didnt know if I would see tomorrow,and I had to let him know how I really felt that I love him and I really care about him. I told him how I would feel if something ever happened to him. From that day forward our relationship has een on the up side. So for all of you who are reading this when you go home tonight make sure that you tell your family, friends, and anyone dear to you how much you love them, because you dont know what tomorrow brings.
