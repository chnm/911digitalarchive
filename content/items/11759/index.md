        On June 7, 2002 at the introduction of the "Heroes2001" United States Postal Stamp, I saw the a chance to Honor
        the 343 Fallen Firefighters of the World Trade Center.

        2002 during The National Fallen Firefighters Memorial Weekend. On May 10, 2003 it was installed and completed
        in Cecil County Maryland. The entire project took less than
        one year from conception to completion, not 60 years like the WWII Memorial.
        I believed in "BUILD IT NOW, SEE IT NOW".
