      text: I was 9 months pregnant and had just dropped my 3 year old off at preschool and heard on the radio that the first plane had hit the WTC.  Of course, I thought it was a terribly tragic but solitary accident.  When I got home my husband called from work to ask if I knew what was going on.  That was when I turned the TV on and saw images of the second plane crashing.  I sat there on the couch not able to comprehend what was happening.  I was relaying everything I saw to my husband and him to his coworkers, I was their link to information.  I remember vividly the sick feeling in my stomach and praying that I wouldn't go into labor.  I didn't want to bring my baby into the world on such a grim day.  Fortunately, little Jacob Christopher waited 6 more days to make an appearance and he is such a blessing.  I think that we all knew of someone who died that day, I was lucky enough to not have any close friends or family perish but I feel for all those who did.  I think about 9-11 everyday, we went to NY for the first time since the attacks over Labor Day Weekend and I can really say that it is a wonderful city and that I love New York.  God Bless NY, DC, PA and the USA.  I will remember always.
