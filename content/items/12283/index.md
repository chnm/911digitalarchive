        Its been a long 5 years, and I've come to accept that I'll always tear up when there is a clip of that day on the news.
        There is a hole in my sky-the view from my balcony faces directly on lower Manhattan and the Towers were the highlight of that view, in rain, sun, storm and brilliant blue skies, like that day.
