      text: "I was in my freshman geometry class and school had just begun.  I was tired and didn't want to be bothered by math.  As the class was going by, a teacher rushed into our classroom and told us to turn the television on.  My teacher did and we heard reports of the World Trade Centers being hit by planes.  I had been at ground zero a few years before and thought as a place that would always be there.  The base of a the tallest buildings in NYC was something that I took for granted to be like an eternal thing.  With this in mind, I could not believe what I was seeing and what I was being told.  Other people around me were freaking out and getting scared, but I just remained in my seat wondering how this could have happened.  America, with the best military in the world, the most freedom, the most accepting of other cultures; how could this happen to us.  I still considered it to be somewhat far away in New York, but then I heard that the Pentagon was hit.  That was when I realized that this was a real thing happening because that is where I live.  My father worked in the pentagon just a couple of years earlier and I just wanted to know that my parents were okay.  It was a scary time and I became alert immediately.  People all over the school were reacting in different ways: crying, silent, wondering what would happen next.  My life has changed.  I am much more concerned about world issues now and what is going on with homeland security.  I just hope for a safer America in the future, and pray that nothing like this will ever happen again."
