        On September 11th I was sitting in Mrs. Bravard's fourth period Science Class. We were finishing up class and getting ready to leave. We had just gotten our homwork. Mrs. Bravard then turned on the T.V. We were fine and content until what happend next. It would change History forever.

           We saw a replay of the plane hitting the first Trade Tower. We couldn't figure out what had just happend. We thought it was an accident.Until a second plane hit the other tower. We then knew that it wasn't an accident.We just sat in shock for lack of words. We all went to lunch silently and also ate silently. In fifth period, we dropped all of our work and watched T.V. I didn't know what else to do, so I jsut cried. It seemed like the only thing to do. For the rest of the day we watched in disbelief. That night there was a memorial for all who died. I didn't sleep at all that night. My life was changed forever. I will never forget what happend or where I was on that fateful day. Peace and God Bless!!
