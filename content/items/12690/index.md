        GOING HOME

        Silent screams fill the night
        Begging to be heard
        Reaching out toward hopeful hands
        To anyone who might be there
        Struggling under the rubble of hate
        Tears break the sudden silence
        Pooling at their feet
        A million thoughts circle their heads
        Home
        Family
        Children

        Questions come up ready
        Answers begging to be found
        Hope is all they ask for
        To see the sky again
        To smell their partner's hair
        To hold their children in their arms
        To be found
        To be safe
        In loving arms
        Again

        Home is where they belong
        Not under some hateful crime
        Crashing down upon them
        One wish is all they beg for
        Home, home again
        Where someone will be waiting
        With open arms
        Open hearts
        Loving hands

        They will be home soon
        They will be safe
        In a place where there is no hate
        No pain
        No fear
        It isn't hate that sets them free
        It is something greater
        It is love.
