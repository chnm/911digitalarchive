        My name is Lena joyner. I am a 26 year old mother of three boys. On the morning of September 11, 2001 I was on my way to bring my boys to school. I turned on the radio in the car and heard nothing but tragidy. I remember at that moment being in pure shock. I could never believe something like this could happen to so many lives. I could have never emagined it happening to us as Amaricans. that day my five along with millions of others changed. I now more then ever realize really how valuable life is. Although there are no pictures of the tragidy actually happening and people have made the best of thair lives. When i hear the stories of all the widdowed and the children left without mothers or fathers. My heart aches just as it did that morning. To hear the wives of the men that lost their lifes tell thair stories, It hurts me. I cry for those out there trying still today to make it another day without thair loved ones. i hope this day rings in the hearts of every Amarican. And that no one , even generations from now know and remember the ones that lost thair lifes and honer this day. And for the people that lost thair loved ones, I salute you.
