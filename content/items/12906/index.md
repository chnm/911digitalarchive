      text: It was a typical day I began early going into work.  While I was typing away and listening to the radio and all of the sudden the DJ started talking about a plane flying into one of the world trade center buildings.  I remember feeling something that I never felt before (and hope to never feel again) which was fear and helplessness.  I tried logging into newspaper websites and couldn't get into any of them.  I called my boyfriend because he was still at home and wasn't coming into work until after lunch.  I told him what I had heard and he turned on the TV and we talked for a while and he recounted what he was hearing.  I told him to keep calling me every once and a while to keep me updated.  I remember hanging up with him and talking with some of my family and just praying.  I remember praying for our country and those that were lost and there families and for our president.  I knew President Bush was going to have alot to deal with and alot of tough decisions to make.  I remember praying for my family also and thanking God that they were all safe.  I remember not getting any work done all day at work.  All my co-workers were by my desk and we were all talking about what was going on.  I remember listening to the radio and hearing about the other tower being hit and then the Pentagon.  I couldn't believe this was happening to America.  I never use to pray for our country but know I realize how important it is to pray for it.
