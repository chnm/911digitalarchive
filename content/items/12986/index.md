      text: |-
        I woke up at 6:30 in the morning on September 11th and I
        went out into my living area and my mom had the news on. It
        showed the twin towers falling. Right at that moment in time
        my heart sunk to the floor. It did not feel real. It felt
        almost like a horrible nightmare, that u could never wake up
        from. And then I saw people jumping from the buildings and
        I was like oh my gosh. I was very thankful that none of my relatives
        live in NY. I would be heartbroken if I lost any of them.
        I would be in great sadness. Which I still am for those who
        had their lives taken away. I wrote this poem about 911.

        PEOPLE DROVE TO WORK,CHILDREN WALKED TO SCHOOL
        THE WEATHER WAS NICE AND COOL
        NOBODY EXPECTED IT
        THEN SUDDENLY IT HIT
        THE TOWERS STOOD TALL
        NOW THEY BEGAN TO FALL

        PIECE BY PIECE THE FIRST ONE CAME DOWN
        EVERYBODYS FACE CARRIED A FROWN
        THE SECOND PLANE CAME BY
        AND THE SECOND TOWER BEGAN TO DIE
        AS MILLIONS OF PEOPLE STARTED TO CRY
        THEY ASKED GOD WHY

        FIREFIGHTERS WENT IN
        AGAIN AND AGAIN
        PULLING OUT THE INJURED AND CARRYING THE DEAD
        AND LYING THEM IN THEIR FINAL RESTING BED
        SOME PEOPLE MADE IT, MANY DID NOT
        SO MANY LOVED ONES SO GREATLY DISTRAUGHT

        FOR EACH LIFE THAT WAS TAKEN
        A NEW ONE WILL BE BORN
        A COUNTRY WILL REMEMBER AND CONTINUE TO MOURN
        OUR FLAG STANDS FOR FREEDOM AND PRIDE
        AND THAT I CAN SAY WILL NEVER BE DEPRIVED
        THEIR ALL FILLED WITH FEAR HOPING A DAY LIKE THAT WILL NEVER
            AGAIN NEAR

        WITH THE LAST BEAM REMOVED, THE VOLUNTEERS WALK AWAY
        THE UNRECOVERED WILL FOREVER LAY
        IN THE SILENT PILE OF DIRT THAT WAS ONCE AT GREAT HEIGHTS
        ALL THE SOULS OF THE SLAIN GLOW IN THE SKY OF LIGHTS
        NOW SOME ARE AT EASE WITH OUT A FRIGHT
        BUT OTHERS STILL THINK ABOUT THAT TERRIBLE FLIGHT


        Thanks for reading my poem and 911 experience. I thank
        u again.
