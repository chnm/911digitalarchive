      text: I LOST MY GRANDMOTHER ON SEPTEMBER 11, 2001.  HERE IS MY STORY.  I woke up Tuesday September 11, 2001 and logged onto AOL to check my email. That is when I saw that the World Trade Center had been hit by a plane.  I figured it was a twin-engine plane or something of that nature.  An accident.  However, I was wrong.  It was TERRORISM.  I was very shaken up.  I was in tears  and couldn't tear myself from the news.  I tried to contact my loved ones, however cell phones were not working that day.  I couldn't get through to my family.  It was an emotional day.  I was drained.  By about 1:30 pm  I settled down in my living room.  I called out of work and closed my eyes, and finally went to sleep.  My home phone rang around 2:00pm.  It was my Aunt Cindy in Colorado.  She told me my Grandmother had died of a heart attack!  I was devastated.  I was very close to my grandmother.  The onsite nurse had found her in her apartment around 9:20 am.  She was lying on the floor with her television remote control in her hand.  CNN was on.  My Grandmother was an avid CNN watcher.  I know she had a heart attack as a result of what she watched on tv that morning.  I am sure others have loved ones who passed in a similar way.  My condolences go out to ALL families who lost a loved one on 9/11.  I will never forget that day as long as I live.  My gradmother is missed and I think of her EVERY day!!!!!!!
