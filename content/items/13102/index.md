        As a class we are learning about September 11, 2001.  It is now 4 years later and we are recalling what our class was doing on that day.

        We were in Mrs. Resitvo's, Mrs. Leapglow's, of Mrs. Seighbor's 4th grade classrooms.  Some of the classes continued with their regular work, but others watched the news on Tv that day.    Some of us watched the president that night on T.V.
