        Due to the Disaster at the World Trade Center


        Glorious old N train
        ambling into the station
        like a happy hound dog
        stopping to slurp up passengers
        like treats.

        Waiting on the platform,
        three out of four men
        are achingly handsome,
        and three out of four again.

        Today without the airports open and
        with the towers gone
        I am awakened and as omnivorous
        as a child, new in the world,
        taking everything in
        instead of tuning most everything out.

        To keep from crying I
        want everyone to say,
        beautiful city!
        Mine, mine, mine!

        --DP, 9/14/01
