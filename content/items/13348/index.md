        On the morning of 9-11, my husband, Cornell Roberts, and I were
        scheduled for a 10:00 a.m. business meeting at the World
        Trade Center. We had planned to travel from Virginia to New
        York CITY on the previous afternoon and had scheduled to stay
        in a NYC hotel. Since neither of us had ever visted inside
        the WTC, we made plans to enjoy a light breakfast on the
        top floor of the WTC at about 8:30 a.m. We told no one of
        of our plans- not family nor friends. ( not an usual thing
        for us, as we often traveled without telling anyone)

        THE ONLY REASON WE ARE HERE TODAY IS DUE TO A TWIST OF FATE!

        I became violently ill due to something I ate on Monday morning,
        so we canceled our plans for September 11th. Had we carried out
        our plans we surely would have been killed. I shutter to think
        about how our family and friends would have anguished over our
        being missing and the not knowing where we could possibly be.
        (Cornell says he thinks he casually mentioned to a friend some
        weeks earlier that we had planned to visit NYC but wasn't sure.)
        We will never again, make travel plans without letting someone
        know of our whereabouts.

        We are grateful to God almighty, that we are here to tell our story
        but, first and foremost, to express our deepest sympathies to all
        those families touched by the tragedy. September 11th has profoundly
        touched anyone around the globe with a heart and a sense of humanity.

        In the end there is only LOVE. Perhaps someday humankind will learn
        and apply the proper balance of LOVE, PEACE, POWER and JUSTICE. May
        they triumph over hate, discord, dominence and injustice.
