I thought about the time just a month before when I went to the top of the World Trade Center. For some reason that I can't comprehend, they made us turn off all tv's and radios so that we had no idea what was going on.  As a result, false rumors started spreading around the school. I only found out the true stories of the terror of 9/11 when I returned home from school and I watched the news non-stop.  For my generation, this was the event in history that we will never forget where we were such as JFK's assasination and the Challenger exploding for previous generations.
