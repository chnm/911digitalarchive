      text: I work at home and was busy working that morning.  I called my doctor's office around 9 AM to make an appointment and heard the women in the office talking about somebody's son who worked "there" and they were wondering about how he was.  At this point, of course, I had no idea what was going on and decided to turn on the television, just in time to see the second plane hit.  From that point on I could not turn it off.  I called my sister to let her know what was going on.  I tried to call my mother, who is a civilian employee for the Navy downtown, but was unable to reach her.  I could only pray for her as I watched the proceedings on the television.  I saw the first tower start to sway and then collapse and could only wonder why I saw this on the screen, but the news anchors were not talking about it until after it happened.  At this time I started to pray for everyone.  Needless to say, I accomplished very little work after that point.  While I lost no one that day, sitting here and writing about this still makes me cry.
