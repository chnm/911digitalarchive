        My husband and I and our 5 and 6 year old were on a US Air flight out of Logan Airport headed to Orlando for a family vacation.  Upon arriving at Orlando the pilot of our plane came to the front and announced that there had been a major tradgedy and to please depart the plane as quickly as possible and get to where you are going as quickly as possible.  With this, my husband and I and the rest of the people aboard looked in horror at each other wondering what had happened. We tried to make our family trip a good one for the sake of the children, but it was difficult because we were always thinking of how many peoples lives were affected by this tradgedy.
