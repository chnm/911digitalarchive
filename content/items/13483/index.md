        I am a college student who in 2001, only had classes on Monday, Wednesday's and Friday's.  So on Tuesday, the 11th, I was sleeping in.  I woke up to the sound of the phone ringing.  When I answered, I remember looking at the clock and noting that it was 9:00am central time.  I picked up the phone and it was a representative from the Army trying to get me to join.  After I politely declined, I decided to turn on the TV to watch Regis and Kelly.  What I saw instead when I turned on that TV was the terrifying imnages of the terrorist attacks.  I had no clue what was going on until I read the captions under the pictures.  They said that it was an attack on NY and DC.  I immediatly picked up the phone to call my mom who was at work in St. Louis.  The phone never rang on either end.  She was on the line as I picked it up.  She was trying to call me exactly when I called her.  We talked about what had happened before hanging up.  I settled in front of the TV in the living room to watch the coverage.  I saw the people waving from the remaining tower (the first tower had already collapsed).  I remember praying that they'd make it out as I went to get a drink of water.  By the time I came back to the TV, the second tower had collapsed.  The reporter then mentioned that there were as many as 11 planes still up in the air and unaccounted for.  I was terrified as I lived very close to a large city.  I was scared that St. Louis would be next.  My mom came home soon there after and we watched more of the coverage.  Finally, needing to get out for awhile, I went for a drive.  As I drove down a busy street, I noticed that someone had parked their truck on the side of the road and had a large American Flag hanging out the back.  That's when it first struck me that this day would be one that I would never forget.

        I haven't forgotten it.  I pray for the victims and the country all the time.  I have a flag hanging on the wall of my room.  I wear shirts with American Flag's on it at least once a week.  I am so proud and so thankful to be an American.

        God Bless this wonderful country.
