      text: |-
        On the morning of Sept 11th 2001, a co-worker in my office
        heard that an airplane hit one of the towers at the World
        Trade Center.

        We all went to the room where the TV showed us one tower on
        fire and smoking. I know going through my mind was: How
        could an airplane NOT miss that tower! Of course, not long after
        the second plane hit the other tower and there was no denying
        we were under attack.

        The whole day was surreal ... the only times the phone rang
        at our office was when hospitals were calling to rent critical
        care equipment from our company. Allegiance Respiratory Care.
        They thought there would be a lot of injured people inundating
        their hospitals. Unfortunately, they were wrong, too few people
        came out of the towers alive.

        I think when the plane hit the Pentagon is when I really
        began to fear what was happening, and then there were
        unconfirmed reports of a plane down in Western PA.  You
        had to wonder "what's next"?

        Our country has become stronger through this, with a renewed
        sense of pride and love for America.  God Bless Us and Protect
        us from the intense hatred of the middle eastern people.
