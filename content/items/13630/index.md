      text: It's a day I think I'll never forget.  How could anyone?  I am the Office Manager in a pediatric dentist office. A parent came in saying something had hit the World Trade Center in New York.  We went to the back and turned on the television.  I couldn't believe what I was seeing...maybe a movie?  Was it real?
