        I would like to dedicate the following poem to the two friends I lost on September 11, 2001.

        Richard Caproni of Lynbrook NY
        Mike Hannon of Lynbrook NY

        You Can't take my America Away



        There was a jealous coward that showed up at
        our door. We fed him,We taught him
        But still he wanted more
        He saw our freedom, our children, our future
        and life
        We let him see what he could be
        Without war, stress and strife
        Like a soul in love starved poverty
        In a jealous rage he stole from us
        Our loved ones, our planes and property
        We want justice for his evil
        This wicked invisible flea
        We mourn, we love, we're better
        Than the jealous coward will ever be
        Let this be a lesson-About the sin of
        jealousy. You seek to demolish-That which
        you know, you could never be
        We've shown you a better place
        Where love and freedom rule the day; Make no
        mistake- You can't take my America away!

        Daniel James Egger
