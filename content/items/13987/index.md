        I awoke to the sound of my phone ringing and heard my friend tell me to turn on my tv. The channel didnt matter. The first thing I saw was the replay of one of the impacts. I glanced at my computer screen and saw an instant message from my friend who lives in New York city that said "oh my god i just saw a plane crash into the world trade center". I will never forget that moment for as long as I live.

        I was unforunate enough to have an economics final exam scheduled for that morning. I took the exam but all I could think about were my friends in New York and both my parents who were on airplanes at the time. I found out as soon as I returned from my exam that my parents were safe.

        I was lucky enough to quickly get through on the phone to both of my friends who live in New York, and they were safe too.
