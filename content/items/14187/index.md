        I had just got out of the shower.I sat down to watch some TV when the news broke into the show and took us to New York city.I can remember i was just about to turn the channel when i saw the World Trade Center on fire.At first i thought it was an accident(as im sure everyone did watching),then while Im watching this live broadcast another plane flys right into the other tower.I
