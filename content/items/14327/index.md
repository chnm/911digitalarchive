        I just sent my son off to first grade and about 1 hour later my cousin called and told me to turn on the news that a jet hit the WTC.  While watching in disbelief another hit the second one.  Then the announcement of the Pentagon and the other jet crashing.  I was stunned to say the least and thankful that all I love were okay and near.         I am so glad we have a President like Mr. Bush and I have every confidence in him.
