        On September 11, 2001 I was in 2nd period gym class when
         I heard about the first world trade center collapsed
        from a plane crashing into it.  I didn't really believe
         it at first because I thought people were just making it
         up to scare us.  When I entered 4th period Studio Art I
         found out that it was true and the second world trade
        center had collapsed.  During 4th period we watched the
        news the whole class period and found out some more
        information.  I found out that another plane crashed
        into the Pentagon in Washington D.C.  I was in total
        shock of that because I didn't expect the terrorists to
        do such a horrible thing.  When I found out that this
        was a terrorist attack I didn't really know what that
        was because I never heard of terrorists before.  When
        I heard that the Pentagon was partly destroyed I was
        kind of glad that it didn't get all the way knocked
        down.  Next period was 5th and I thought things were
        going to get better.  Well they didn't get better
        because there was a bunch of suicide bombers all over
         the United States waiting to blow up everybody and
        everything around them.  That I think was the worst
        that a person could ever do; give up their life for
        something they believe in.   In my eyes that is very
        pathetic.  The next period was lunch and I was already
         in a bad mood about all that happened, so I wasn't
        really in the mood to watch the news in lunch because
        I was afraid I might get sick to my stomach from seeing
        all the dead and blooding bodies.  There was a bunch of
        people that were being carried away that were dead or
        wounded from jumping or just walking along the building.
         There was one issue that really made me think.
        There was this little boy that just seen his mother and
         then five minutes later, she died in the World Trade
         Center, the second building.   He was crying because
        he didn't know where his mother was and couldn't find
        his dad.  He found out a little later that his dad also
        died while walking along the sides of the World Trade
         Centers.  He was later sent to live with his
        grandparents.  That issue took all my lunch period and
        I moved on to 7th period, which is Earth Science.
        We watched CNN the whole class period.  I learned that
        the person who did this was Osama Bin Laden.  He is the
        worst terrorist that could ever live.  I heard him
        make a speech about what happened two hours ago, which
         he denied doing anything.  When I looked at his face
        I knew he had a great part in this horrific event.
         I was trying to hold tears in when they showed all the
         victims.  The end of 7th period was nearly approaching
        the end and I didn't want it to end because I was just
         getting into it.  But, I was glad because as soon as
        I got in 8th period that it was we watched.  In the
        middle of 8th period we had a moment of silence to
        pray for the family of the victims.  We then continued
        to watch the news, nothing really new happened during
        that period and onto 9th period.  They kept on showing
         the collapsing of the WTC's and the crashing of the
        pentagon.  Later that evening I went to my friends
        house and watched President Bush make a public speech
        to issue the public that we might be entering a war.
        People were really scared of what was going to happen
        after that.  At about ten o'clock the U.S made is first
         attack on Afghanistan, which blew up the entire country.
          On September 11, 2001 was the worst day of my entire
        life.
