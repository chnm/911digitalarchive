        The day when we were attacked shocked the entire USA. People now care for each other even more than before. People now think of the people that were horribly killed during the incident. Almost everyone was changed after the Twin Towers collapsed, including me.
        I now think of the people who lost their lives, and feel hatred for the people who did this. I take more time to watch what I say. I also appreciate my friends and family because they might not have been here the next day. I am so thankful that they are still here.
        My friends were just as affected as me. They are thankful for their lives and fell sorrow for the dead. But some of my friends are still felling the pain over this incident, and others put it in the past but still think about it once every so often.
        I think my generation will be changed forever. We will have to live with the grief of this event forever. People that did not see it will not have the same feelings about it but will still learn about it. I do not think that we the people will ever forget this.
        So in the end I think everyone was affected, one way or another. Now people are starting to move on, but will never forget about this tragic event. I know I never will forget it because of all the trauma, on 9/11.
