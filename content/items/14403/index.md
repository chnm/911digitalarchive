        I live in Northern California.  On September 11, 2001, I woke up before the alarm.  I usually get up at 6 a.m. to get ready for work.  Instead, I awoke about 5:30 and couldn't get rid of the nagging feeling that I had to get up-NOW!  So, I did.  I stumbled to the kitchen, nuked a cup of coffee and sat down to read my email.  Minutes into it, my sister IM'd me (she doesn't get up til 7!  Noon on weekends!), "OMG, I can't believe what is happening.  A horrible accident!  Are you watching TV?"  I turned the tv on just in time to see the second plane hit the second tower.

        Horrified, I sat on my sofa for the next two days.  I believe I ate during that time, not sure.  But I was transfixed and unable to move.  I did have an overwhelming urge to gather my kids and grands around me, though.  I am blessed to work for a company that cares.

        And what I've learned over the last twelve months is how very blessed I am in every way.  The struggles of the last few years, my normal life, is suddenly a very contented place.  September 11, 2001 adjusted my attitude and, in an instant, rearranged my priorities forevermore.
