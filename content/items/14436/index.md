        My dad was taking me to work, I don't remember why.  He arrived early, I was still in bed.  He can't work my TV so he got me up, he was nearly hysterical.  As I got the TV running, the second plane hit the second tower behind Katie Couric and it went down.  Couric did not even know it had happened.

        I went to work, I am the secretary at an independent insurance claims adjustment firm and other people's problems continue.  The owner showed up.  None of the adjuster's came to work. One didn't show for 6 working days.


        No claimants called until late that afternoon.  Those calls were rational and to the point, not a normal type of call.  Calls often run on forever, are illogical, or rush over minor issues or issues that can't be rushed such as drying the water. It was 3 days before I received a rush call, that lady needed her car to get to work.  Calls picked up and became more rambling, rushed or pointless over time.  I did not receive an angry call for no reason at all until July, 2002.  This includes a team working the Rodeo-Cibeque fire in Northern Arizona.
