        I am a Quality Assurance Specialist at Letterkenny Army Depot, a small Army maintenance facility in south-central Pennsylvania.  On the morning of 9/11, I was sitting at my desk when I overheard someone saying that a plane had crashed into one of the World Trade Center towers.  I immediately thought that it was some sort of terrible accident.  My thoughts were of a private, single-engine aircraft bouncing off the building and falling to the street below.  A few minutes later I had to go to another office and the route took me by one of the employee break areas.  A TV in the break area was tuned to CNN and a number of employees were in the break area watching the events as they unfolded.  I stopped to investigate just what had happened and while we were watching, a plane hit the second tower.  We stood there and watched as both towers eventually collapsed.  I can still remember the feeling as the newscaster became silent when the first tower fell.  I really don't think he knew what was happening at first.  I eventually returned to my desk to find a message from my wife on my voice mail.  She was, of course, concerned for my welfare since I work at a Govenment installation.
        The events of that day are still fresh in my mind as if they happened a week ago instead of a year.
