        On my drive into work, I was noticing what a beautiful morning
        it was and how lucky we were to have such good weather in
        September. I was half listening to the radio as I parked the car
        and I heard a late breaking report about some plane hitting
        a Tower in the Trade Center. I hurried into the office and
        went to a break area with a TV to see if there was more
        information. I looked in disbelief at the destruction and
        immediately began praying for all those people in the building.
        I stood rooted to the spot, unable to really believe that
        someone could accidentally hit a huge building. The second
        plane came onto the screen as the broadcasters continued their
        emotional reports and in seconds the second tower was a blazing
        inferno. I dropped to the floor in horror. I couldn't believe
        what I saw and I immediately knew...this was NO ACCIDENT. The
        only thing I wanted to do right then was get in touch with my
        children to verify their safety. We spent a great deal of the
        rest of the day discussing who would do this and why amid the
        tears of anger, hurt, unbelievable pain for the families and
        the country that would never be the same. And we continued to
        pray.
