        Written after my brother received alert orders for deployment.  My heart said, No...not again...

        THOSE OF A SOLDIER

        They are not here, they are away,
        "God, keep them safe."
        Each day we pray.

        Mothers and fathers, daughters and sons,
        Have turned and kissed their beloved ones.
        "Good-bye!" they wave
        "So-long!" they say
        And we ask to keep them safe that day.

        These losses overwhelming
        Our chests heavy with pain
        We must be strong
              or so they say
        So as not to let Hate get it's way!

        No face
        No uniform
        No parallel to cross,
        Fight whom? and how?
        We are at a loss.

        Trained to fight for Freedom's sake
        We fight Hate, which has no face.
        It cowers in the soul and preempts Grace.

        United we stand,
        Our colors now blend...
        Into ONE of LIBERTY to defend our land.

        They are now gone
        They are not here
        One sits and stares
        With loneliness to bear.

        They left us this morning with a kiss and hug good-bye
        No hand now to hold, so we sit and cry.
        Good Lord, bring them home, please don't let them die!

        They are not home, they are away
        So we keep our proud vigil...and pray they return.
        -----------
        Daughter of a WWII veteran
        Bride of a Vietnam veteran
        Sister of a Gulf War veteran
             and patriot
                   Christine Dybas Polodna
