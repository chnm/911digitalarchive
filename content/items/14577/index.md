        I was in my french class when i first heard about it. A boy in my class had told us at the being of the hour that planes crashed into the World Trade Centers. We all laughed at him because he was known for making trouble.

I looked at the TV to see what was going on and i saw the  first towers burning. I watched stunned for a little while and then the second plane came. I sat there wondering what is this, did it happen years ago. I then saw it was dated September 11, 2001 and I knew it didn't happen years ago. I then thought it's a movie but so life like. I sat there and my best friend turned around and saw, she was scared, I looked around and saw people praying and then I knew it was real.

          It brought us back to the towers and thats when we firts saw people jumping. They closed up on one person and my friend sitting next started screaming. She said it looked like her mom. Her mom worked int he towers at one of the very top floors. My friend later called her dad and he told her that her mother had died. Her mother had been one of the jumpers but we didn't know for sure if it was the person we saw.
