I must have been zoned out or something because I didn?t hear the sonic boom that my sister heard. Since we live near Wright Patterson Air Force Base airplanes were taking off, two of witch made sonic booms. My sister came running saying someone was bombing us and thing of the sort. Our neighbor made her even more scared by telling her about other things like a fire that was caused by an airplane crash near our house. It was just a fire but my sister believed her.
