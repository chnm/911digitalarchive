        The day that AMERICA stood still will always be imbraced in my heart,mind,& soul. On that fateful day I was hanging out with a friend of mine before going to work. We were listening to a radio station~93.9 WKYS~ when they interupted the song they were playing to announce that a plane had just hit one of the Twin Towers. At the time I just that the plane crash was an accident. Then they annouced that another plane had struck the 2nd Tower. I realized then that the UNITED STATES was being attacked. It really hit close to home when it was announced that a plane was hijacked after just leaving Dulles Airport and that it hit the PENTAGON. The last plane to be announced was the one that crashed in Pennsylvania after the members on board decided to take on the hijackers. AMERICA and its people will never be the same again. May GOD bless all the familys and friends that were involved in this tragic affair and also everyone else who was touched by this.

                            ~GOD BLESS AMERICA~



                               ~BRIANNE DEANA ARD~
