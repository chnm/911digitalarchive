Spanish media were filled with 9/11 materials. Spaniards extended condolences and support. We waited at Madrid's Barajas airportfor two days beyond our scheduled departure home to Philadelphia. On our airport hotel TV we saw the opening of the Spanish Supreme Court session, attended by King Juan Carlos. All were in formal dress. We heard the Chief Justice express his sorrow and support and emphasize the of importance of upholding the rule of law. When we did take off all the passengers cheered.
