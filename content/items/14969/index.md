It was about 10 minutes after the inital plane struck the first tower.  I received a call from one of our customers, he asked me if I was seeing this. My family was all safe, including my new born that was home with the beautiful love of my life.  This was a very sad day for me personally, as well as our great land.  I can't imagine a life that would breed such anger to perform a deed like this.  The most disturbing memory I have of that day was the footage that I saw on the television.
