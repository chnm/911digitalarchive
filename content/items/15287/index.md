        I will never forget the fear, the hurt, the anger, the shock
        I felt during the entire day.  I watched those news reels in
        horror and disbelief.  I didn't personally know any of
        those whose lives were taken that day but they were Americans
        and therefore my brothers and sisters.  I can only imagine how
        their friends, families and co-workers felt and must still feel
        to this day.  My heart went out to them then and still aches
        for them now.  I am proud of the resolve of our nation and it's
        president that we would not and did not let this attack go
        unanswered.  For all the hurt, I was so proud to be an American
        that day and in the days following.  If anything good can be
        said to have come from the ordeal it is that I have been better
        able to put into perspective what truly matters and what is
        important.  My most treasured gift, my little girl, became even
        more precious to me that day because I was reminded of how quickly
        things change.  I never did, but especially now never will, let an
        opportunity to hug or kiss her or tell her I love her pass me
        by.
           We ARE a nation under God, indivisible, with liberty for all
        and for that I am so grateful and so proud.  We proved that day
        that united we stand!

        Susan Mullen
        Baylee Mullen
        Massachusetts
        9/11/02
