        I was at work.  It was a regular day or so I thought.  I work for the Post Office. I was trying to get the mail out when it came over the radio about the first plane.  I thought how could a pilot hit the WTC on such a clear day.  We all went to the swing room to check out the TV.  There it was the second plane striking.  For the first time I did not feel safe in my own country.  After the devastation, I was happy I did not know one person who was killed.  I began to feel bad because like it or not those were my fellow countrymen who rode on the bus or train that I do every morning.  I felt empty all of these emotions.  So in days to come I read the poem Footprints.  It made me feel better.  When I see it on the news the stories, the devastation I change the channel.  In order to heal I must move on but I WILL NEVER FORGET THAT FOR ONE MOMENT I WAS NOT SAFE.  We are strong we will move on.  One day at a time.  It may feel as if we can't, but we must for our children.  They are our future.

        TC EVANS
