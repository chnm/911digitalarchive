        September 11th

        	On September 11th, 2001 a terrorists group high jacked two commercial jet planes. Then a second plane crashed into the second World Trade tower and it caught on fire.  Firemen, policemen and average people were helping people to get out of the towers.  People on the floors above the crash were trapped and many died from jumping out the windows instead of being burned alive.
