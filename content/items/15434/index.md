      text: |-
        While sitting in a Hotel Room on a business trip, conversing with my co-workers I was directed to turn on the TV.  To my Horror and surprise I was watching the World Trade Center being destroyed.

        There I was on the other side of the world at 11:00pm in Penang, Malaysia.  Not believeing what I was seeing, I said goodnight to my co-workers, shut down my laptop and watched as if I were watching some movie the destruction that was happening in my Country.

        I was partially afraid for being in another country (as an American) wondering if we were all targets, worried that my family and friends would be in danger. I was mostly sadden and heartbroken with the realization of the people whose lives were suddenly ended.

        October 12 was quite a different day for me.  I experienced first hand the human quality of compassion from the people of Penang.  All were quite sad and angry at what happened.  I was surprised by the reactions from people on the street who would come up to me and express their sorrow for the loss of my countrymen.

        Coming from a Military Family I already understood how closely knit we are as a Human Family. The people of Penang and others reinforced in me the Love and Compassion we have for all fellow Humans.
