I went to work in San Francisco, while on Bart there was talk of the towers and PA. Everyone was talking to each other when normally everyone would either be asleep or buried in their books or newspaper. WHen I got to work I was told to go back home. When I headed outside, it was like a ghost town(in San Francisoc!). I head back home to Oakland and was glue to the tv for the rest of the day and most of the night. I had always felt that America was safe from these kind of violence, violence that happen only in other countries. I still fell safe, just more cautious.
