        I awaken by the sounds of my alarm radio, at first I thought that there was a play on the radio station.  I can remember hearing the frightning voices of people calling into the radio stations claiming there was an attack at the WTC in NY.  I could'nt believe what I was hearing, so I turned on the TV, and sure enough the damage was done. I was unable to comprehend what was going on and why this was happening.  Soon after, turning on the TV I walked through the house with such an eerie and an array of feelings.  I asked my sister-inlaw if she knew what was going on?  She replied and looked at me in a daze.  Later, that morning I wasn't sure how to go about my day; do I get ready for school? Do I wait for more news?  I just didn't know. Finally, I decided to get dress and carry on like normal, however; it was not a normal day, not for me nor anyone else in the world.  Suddenly, it occurred to me that whatever problems I had been having didn't seem to matter to me at that moment and some time afterwards.  Words are not enough to know just how I felt along with the rest of the world; sadness,disbelief,why, insecured, etc.  Unfortunately, this has happen to us all, yet we are strong people, and we will stand together?

        From: Diane Kaluza-Luna
