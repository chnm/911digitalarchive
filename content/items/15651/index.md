      text: I was at work and I remember the receptionist calling on the phone to tell everyone that a plane has just flew into the World Trade Tower.  A few minutes later the second plane flew into the other building.  I remember how we all stood there wondering what was going on.  Then it hit how everyone thought we were under attach.  All I could think about was was daughter, who was at school and my husband was away in Maine on a hunting trip.  I just wanted to be surronded by my family but I couldn't get in touch with my husband anyhow, but I was able to get to my daughter.  That evening all I did was cry and hold my daughter for dear life.  How empty I felt that nothing could be done for all those people.  I never did get a hold of my husband until a couple days later and I think he was still in shock by the whole incident.  Coming home from his trip in Maine he drove through New York and still couldn't believe what he saw.  I have lost my sister a year prior who was also very young and I thought I would see life in a different way, but when I saw what happen on Sept. 11, 2001 I will never take life for granted.  Life can come and Life can go.  Appreciate what you have and hold on till there is no tomorrow.
