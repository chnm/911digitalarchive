      text: |-
        Proud to be American!

        I am a new Citizen, who so proudly
        rose the flag to fly it in the sky
        just a short year ago.
        The words of the Oath of Allegiance
        still so fresh in my mind and edged
        in my
        heart for ever there to stay.
        I am going to treasure
        these words of  honor and pledge
        to the Nation, I Love so much
        and will admire as long as
        I shall  live.

        I was born in a country, enslaved
        by the enemy at that time, and never will
        forget the picture my father secretly
        showed me when I was just a child.
        It was a beautiful statue with a torch,
        held so high
        that it brought light and hope for
        all of us in the darkest of times.
        That is still the light, so bright
        that brings together
        all Nations yearning for peace,
        and gives us mankind`s most valued
        asset - the Freedom to share.

        When so horrible a tragedy struck
        my so beloved new home, the United
        States, I took out the flag to
        fly it again in the sky on the wings of
        gentle winds,  just as proudly as
        I did  last year.
        The torch, now in every American`s heart
        has became a guide to overcome evil and
        horror, and gives us the light to carry on
        and fight the fears, that were planted so
        crudely into our peaceful days.
        The American people are so strong,
        like never before,
        and with tolerance in their hearts
        they are calling out for justice
        in our  troubled world.
        On a sunny so beautiful
        September morning
        sudden suffering crashed down
        on a great Nation.
        In  the black cloud of vaporized concrete and
        death
        Heros in line of their duty gave
        their own precious life to save
        innocent human beings.
        This is the  spirit of the country
        I name so proudly my own.

        We, the people with many different
        heritages and faith,
        we stand all United in our
        deepest sorrow
        but we stand United in our wish
        too,
        to rise above
        this unimaginable tragedy
        with the glory of our
        History and the Flag, Red, White and Blue.
        We fight for a higher cause, with
        all our strength and soul,
        with the assurance in our mind that
        we are going to win not just this battle,
        but also the war
        against terror, all over the world.

        Jutka T. Emoke Barabas
        September 11 2001
