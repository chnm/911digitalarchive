        I was on a cruise with some friends from OHIO:
           I woke up at 8:00 am looking out at green mountains. I call my mom around 8:15 in Florida to tell her we had arrived at St. Thomas. Then, he screamed, "Oh S***! Another just hit!!!"  I was in total shock. I didn't know what to think.  At that time I go to turn on the T.V., but are signal was not working.
           Finally at 1:00p.m. I saw the footage of the "World Trade Center"  It was horrible.  I was so scared.  I didn't know if I should cry or not.
           I just hope something like that will never happen again!
