      text: I would have woken up about 7:20, and gotten to school about 8:30. Someone came up to me as I wondered the school halls before class and told me that the Whitehouse had been bombed. I thought for sure they were mistaken, and obviously they were. Still though, I hadn't heard the news. I noticed the students crowded around the TV the moment I stepped into my first period class. However, the administration made the decision to not allow the students to watch the news for the rest of the day.
