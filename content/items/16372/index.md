        I had just purchased a new set of headphone for my Walkman - it was about 6.00 am PST.  I decided to listen to the radio.  Something about a terrorist attack - was it real, or was I listening to a radio play?

        I changed the station - Federal ground stop.  I walked over to the Southwest Airlines gate and asked them if we were flying - they said yes.  I asked for a refund.  I didn't think I was going to make it to Portland, OR.

        My brother, I had to call my brother - he lives in New York - and works near the World Trade Center.  I call, and call - no answer.  I did not know it at the time, but he had been in the World trade center when the first plane hit his tower.  Finally he answered.  He was on a boat - heading to Staten island.

        I was glad to talk to him - and then deeply saddened.  I went back to the office.  We decided to work as normal.  My next meeting was at the offices of a defense contractor.
