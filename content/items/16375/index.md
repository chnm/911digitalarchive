      text: I was in my sociology class at 9:25 on Tuesday morning.  My professor adressed the class stating that she heard on the radio that morning of the first building being struck by an airplane but their wasn't much information about why. Our whole nation as a whole yearned for more information about what conflicts there were between us and the middle east.
