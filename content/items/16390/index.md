      text: I was sitting in the middle of lunch when all of a sudden I heard a few people talking. They were talking about plane crashes in New York and at the Pentagon. Well, as it turns out he was moved to another section that very day. He only had minor injuries and was going to be great. Unfortunatley alot of his friends weren't as lucky. I went to a big funeral for all the people at the Pentagon. I remember for the first and probably the last time seeing my strong grand-father break down into tears.
