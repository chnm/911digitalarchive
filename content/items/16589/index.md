        On September 11,2001, I got up around 6:00am thinking it was going to be a normal day.  Once I got to school, my friend came up to me and told me that there was some plane crash in New York.  I was concerned, but I didn't realize how big of a crash it was.  It wasn't until I got to my third period class that I heard that the plane had crashed into one of the World Trade Centers.  I couldn't believe it.

        Throughout the day, I didn't hear anything about it at school.  Once I got home, I turned on the tv to find that it was on almost every channel.  I thought it was terrible that someone would attack America like that, and I was sad for all the people who lost their lives.

        It was weird for me, because in 1998 I visited New York and went into the second Tower that was hit.  I remember being really frightened that something would happen, because it was such a tall building.  There was even a small fire in the restaurant there, and they shut down all the exits so, in the event that the fire would spread, it wouldn't go far.

        Once I saw the news footage of the people in the burning building, and the people falling out of the windows 110 stories up, I remembered how scared I was just when the exits were closed.  I can't even imagine how terrifying it was to be in those buildings.  I have a huge amount of respect for the firefighters who willingly went into the buildings to save people.

        September 11, 2001 was a very horrible, memorable day.  It's definitely a day that I will never forget.
