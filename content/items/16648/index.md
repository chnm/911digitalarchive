        It was ten oclock in the morning on Tuesday September 11, 2001 and it was
        such a beautiful day that I thought nothing could go wrong. I was in the
        weight room at Cohasset (Massachusetts) Middle High School and little did I
        know, that my life as an American was about to change forever. Then, out of
        the clear blue, the radio was turned off and in a solemn voice coach Sweeney
        declared  Any child whose parents flew out of Logan airport this morning
        please see me! When the children who had talked to coach Sweeney left the
        room crying I just thought that maybe a flight was delayed or something.
        Then about fifteen minutes later, principal Jones came on to the intercom and
        spoke to everyone: Students, this morning a very terrible act has taken place
        in our country. So far the only details we have is that terrorists have hijacked
        planes from Boston and Newark and have flown them into the World Trade
        Center towers in New York City. If we learn anymore details, we will inform
        you immediatly!At first I thought to myself  How bad could this possibly
        be? Were America, no one will ever attack us on our home soil! Then, after
        hours of uncertainty, I arrived home and witnessed the terrible truth: Both
        World Trade towers had disappeared from the New York Skyline! This sent
        me over the edge. I began to cry and was as scared as I have ever been. That
        special security blanket that most Americans had, had just been stolen from
        us! That night, I tried to release my feelings by attending a vigil at my parish,
        but I just could not get those gruesome photos that were on the TV out of my
        head. That night, I cried myself to sleep, and in the morning, I woke up ready
        to endure my first day of post 9/11 America.
