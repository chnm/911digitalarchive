      text: I was 3 months pregnant and had been terribly sick with all day morning sickness.  I had to go to the hospital the night before to get IV fluids so I was sleeping when the first attack occurred.  I awoke to the phone ringing and it was my mother, who seemed very upset.  She asked me if I could believe what was happening and I had no idea what she was talking about.  I jumped out of bed and went into our living room and turned on the television and saw an unbelievable horrific sight, the second World Trade Center had just been hit.  I sat and cried and was scared.  Nothing can describe the terror and saddness I felt when the buildings fell to the ground.  I couldn't stop crying throughout the entire day.  I had a doctor's appointment later that day and was happy to find out all was well with my baby.  I sat and thanked God for the wellness of my baby and prayed to God to help the families affected by this trajic event and also to help us as a nation deal with what had been done.  I also prayed for all of the babies who would never see one of their parents again.  This makes me sad to this day.
