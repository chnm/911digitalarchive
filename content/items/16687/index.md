      text: I was home on the morning of September 11,2001 reading the morning paper and watching TV when I saw what had happened on TV on NBC I was really upset at what had happened to our Country.  I have a Daughter that was a Flight Attendant for Continental Air Lines, stationed in New Jersey and I knew she was on duty that morning.  I was very worried for all of America.  I began to pray for all of the families that were effected by this attack and I prayed for my daughter to call and let me know she was ok and she finally did call and let me know that she was ok and not to worry.  I watched tv all day long I just could not remove myself from the tv.  I am very proud of America and all of the people that help in this time of need.  My daughter did fly that day and for several month's after the attack she said she was not afraid to fly and some flight attendants were afraid to fly again and they did not fly after that day.  She is no longer a flight attendant as she was laid off her job because of this attack on America but she enjoyed flying as this was her dream every since she was a teenager and she was living her dream.  She has not been called back to work for Continental Air Lines.  I am glad that I live in America and I am very proud of the way our President George W. Bush has handled things and still is handling America.  He will go down in history as being one of the Greatest Presidents. I still Pray for all of America and our leaders and all of the Families of the 9-11-01.  God Bless AMERICA is my prayer.  Mary Margaret Wilson (Daughter is Kristy Denise Wilson)
