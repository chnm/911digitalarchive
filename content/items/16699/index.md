        I was on a bus with some of my classmates and other classes. We had just got done with our first hour class in New Rockford. We got on the bus and the radio was on and it was talking about the Twin Towers and planes. We asked our bus driver, DJ what was going on, and he explained to us that the Twin Towers had just been hit by a plane.

Later on after the bell rang to go to our next class we heard people talking that the other Tower had got hit too. The whole day in every class we were talking about what happened and trying to get on the internet to find out more information.
          I will remember this day for as long as I live.
