It took me a couple of minutes to pull this all in and I went back out to tell my co-workers what I had just seen and they didn't believe me! Then the radio starting broadcasting the attack on the Pentagon and everyone at work just stopped what they were doing and we were all just standing there staring at the radio waiting to find out was happening. It was about an hour later that we found out about the plane in Somerset, PA. Which is about an hour away from where we were working! And all I could think about was getting home to my children!
