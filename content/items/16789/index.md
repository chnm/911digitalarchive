        September 11th 2001 began as a average day in school at
        Northwestern Lehigh HS, students talking, teachers yelling
        for them to get to class, students rushing around trying to
        see as many people as possible before class begins, but soon
        my life will drastically change for the worse. I was sitting
        in history class listening to the teacher lecture the lesson
        to the class when all of the sudden the t.v. was turned on
        at the bottom of the screen I appeared..."Breaking news,
        the Twin Towers have been hit!" In my mind I couldn't
        believe that this could be true. I watched in horror as
        I saw a large plane slam into the north tower around 8:45
        a.m. I was in disbelief of what was happening, how could
        anyone ever do such a horrible, life-altering thing?


            My class and I were in awe as we watched the second
        plane barrel into the south tower. As we watched in anger,
        and disbelief, we saw people waving shirts and cloths out
        windows trying to call for help, people jumping out of
        windows, and people running franticly down the street.
        Then the most heart-stopping thing happened, the south tower
        clasped right in front of our eyes, and then later the
        north. It was the most horrific sight I have ever seen in
        my life.

            Later on the news, it was announced that a plane had hit
        the Pentagon and one had crashed, after it had been retaken
        control by brave Americans, in the small town of
        Shanksville, Pennsylvania.

            It is now September 11th, 2002, a year after this
        tragedy happened. I am still shocked and somewhat confused
        about how anyone can cause such pain when i see photographs
        or tapes of Twin Towers and to think that they I will never
        again see or visit this wonderful landmark. Many memorial
        ceremonies have been held in remembrance of the over 3000
        lives lost. We held a service outside our school to pay
        tribute to the brave soles that died for there county.
        To me, they will be in my heart forever. I pray for the
        families that have lost love ones, and hope that they can
        get though this horrible event. I believe that our country
        is strong and determined and will get through this as one
        united nation.
        "God Bless America!"
