        September 11th


        	I woke up on September 11th and it was a sunny,
        semi warm day. It felt like any other day really. I got on
        the bus and went to school. I hung out with friends in my
        first and second class. In the middle of third period is
        when I found out about the terrorist attacks. I left class
        to get a drink and a teacher was walking down the hall and
         saying something. At first I thought he was talking to me,
         so I asked him what he said. He told me to go and tell my
        teacher to turn it on CNN. So I hurried back to my class
        and told Mrs. Lamb to turn on the TV. As soon as we turned
        it on they showed the first tower in flames. I was very
        confused. I had no idea what was going on, to be honest I
        was a little scared to know what was going on. I wasn't
        sure I wanted to know. Then a newscaster came across the
        screen and said that a plane had flown into the tower. After
         hearing this, the first thing that popped into my head was
         the bombing in the twin towers in 1993.  When class was
        over everyone in the halls were talking about it. I stepped
         into fourth period class and it was so quiet, which is very
         unusual for my art class.  We sat there the whole time with
         our noses stuck to the TV. It was so sad. By this time the
         other plane hit the second tower. When the towers started
        to collapse I started to get teary. I still couldn't believe
         this was happening. I thought it was just a really bad
        dream. Then later on I heard that a plane hit the Pentagon
        and then one crashed in Somerset, PA. The plane that crashed
         in Somerset, PA from what I heard was supposed to come to
        Somerset, NY. It was suppose to hit our power plant, AES.
        	During the next few days of school we watched over a
        nd over again the devastating disaster. Each time I watched
         it made me think to myself, wow what if that plane had hit
         our power plant?  What would have happened to us? It was so
         important to watch the news every night and see what was go
        ing on and how people had reacted to all this. It still
        amazes me that someone can be so cruel and hurtful. After
        the devastating disaster, everyone, including me was very
        sad and had hopes on pay back for Osama bid Laden. This
        tragedy influenced people and we know that America became
        united once again. We pulled through it and now are trying
        to move because everyone is "PROUD TO BE AN AMERICAN!"
