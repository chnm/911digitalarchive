        On the morning of September 11 I was in the pool for morning
        water polo practice. While we were passing I saw one of the
        coaches enter the pool deck and tell our coach something we
        didn't know what he was just told but we knew it was
        something bad. Our coach just shook his head and our other
        coach put her hands over her mouth. When we got out and
        where in the showers one of our teammates came over to us
        and said," Hey, did you guys hear about the plane that was
        hijacked and flown into the world trade center?" We all
        thought it was part of a joke so we said no and waited for
        the punch line. She just stared at us and said,"No, its
        true a plane really did crash!" Then we stared at each
        other in disbelief. I had not fully realized how bad it was
        until I went to my 4th period class and actually saw the
        plane hit the 2nd WTC and I began to cry. When I got home
        that day my uncle called. He had just recently become a
        firefighter. He told me some of the men from his station
        were leaving for NY but he wasn't because he was new. After
        a whileof silence my uncle said,"Being a firefighter myself,
        I know for a fact that if I was a NY firefighter I would
        not hesitate whatsoever,running into one of those buildings
        to fullfill my duties."I cried some more and said goodbye.
        The thing that will stay in my mind forever is what my
        uncle said that day. I know that those men and women who
        perished are truly heroes.
        I will never forget....
