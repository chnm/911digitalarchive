        When the whole sky turned gray
        Friends and family cried

        While the fearful witnesses
        Faced a harsh uncertainty

        Days passed so slowly
        With no sign of relief
        Joy had been replaced
        By horror, shock and grief

        Then just when it seemed
        That the mourners could not cope
        A friend brought the Good News
        That gave eternal hope.

        And?
