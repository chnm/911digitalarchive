        I am a teacher trainer---staff developer.  On that morning, another teacher entered my room and said, "Do you have a radio?  A plane just hit one of the Twin Towers?"  I thought it was a small plane with some dummy of a pilot.  But my sons both work in finance in Manhattan, and one had just interviewed at Merril Lynch on Liberty Street.  I was concerned about him, but not frantic.  It seemed an isolated incident.  Then I heard a second plane  hit the South Tower.  In NYC all our phones went dead.  I called around on my cell phone.  I was wild with worry about where my sons (and my son-in-law, who worked at the World Financial Center, behind the towers) were.
        God was good.  Everyone was OK.  We stayed in school till 6 PM that night, since so many of our students had parents who worked in Manhattan.  We couldn't go home till every child had a place to go.
