I told him no so he told me to turn it on, a plane had just hit the WTC, I thought "how terrible" I wonder if the pilot was on drugs, had a heart attack or what", I was thinking accident..........as the tv came on I could see the other one hit the WTC.  I was shocked, I thought............ARE THEY CRAZY!!!! DO THEY HAVE ANY IDEA WHAT THEY HAVE DONE!!!! I started crying and thanking God that I had found my children before this happened.
