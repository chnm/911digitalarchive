        On this day of mourning and history, I was starting my day like any other day as a freshman at Stony Point High School. I had to wake up real early for football practice and practice from early in the morning until about 30 min. right before school started. As I walked down the halls nobody had a clue what had happened and what was still to come. As I walked into my first period class, I saw my teacher was watching CNN for some reason. I paid little attention to the TV thinking either a school getting shoot up again or an accident with a plane crashing. But, little did I know it was no way an accident. My next to periods were crazy everybody saying what they thought had happened.
        That same day my mom picked me up early to go to the eye doctor. Not until then did I real have just about the truth about what had happened. After I got my eyes checked I watched the 9" TV in the back of the doctors office, that was when everything came clear and when I found out the time line of the events that just occurred.
