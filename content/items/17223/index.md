        about any accident that morning, not till I heard it from
        my friend, on my way to school.  My class was almost over
        when the second plane hit.  I felt ignorant later when the
        scene was being played on our tv screens at every class I
        went to in school.  I have no way of knowing how terrible
        it must have been for those who were affected directly and
        indirectly.  Justice will be met.
