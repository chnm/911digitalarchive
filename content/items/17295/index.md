        I was at work when the attacks happened and we turned on the radio to hear exactly what happened.  We then went to a store and bought a television so that we could watch what was going on.  It was so unbelieveable to see the destruction and to even try to imagine that we were under attack.  We were as stunned as the people in New York and our hearts went out immediately to everyone of them.  As we watched in dismay, there was not a dry eye in the office. This was so hard to comprehend at first and when the towers started to collapse, we just could not believe that this could be happening.
        It has been one year today and it seems like only yesterday that this all happened.  Our hearts and prayers have done out to all the people killed and the loved ones left behind throughout this year and will continue to go out to them because as they say, We Will Never Forget.  God Bless America.
