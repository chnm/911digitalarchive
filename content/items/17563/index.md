        September 11th has had a monumental impact on my life. Now Im going to give you my thoughts, September 11th
        2001, a day which I will never forget. At the time I was attending St. Peters Boys high school on Staten Island. 1st period, Religion class, a few minutes into the
        class our principal came into our classroom and told us that a plane had hit the World
        Trade Center, the class started praying and I didnt, I was in total shock and
        didnt know what to do. Second period someone came into U.S. History and told
        us that the Pentagon had been hit, I then knew something was up. I was never told about the second plane . At lunch I went
        down to the bookroom to see Mrs. Allen and I asked her what was going on, she told
        me the two towers had fallen and that nothing was left.I didnt even believe her, I
        couldnt. My mother came later and picked me up, hysterical, she couldnt
        stop crying. My father is a Battalion Chief at Battalion 22 for the FDNY, my uncle
        Robert is stationed at a firehouse in Brooklyn, my other uncle is stationed at a
        firehouse in Brooklyn, and finally my uncle Dave who died on September 11th was
        stationed at Engine 226, Brooklyn. That day my mother and I went home and she said
         that my father was alright, she also said my other two uncles called and said they
        were alright. The only one who hadnt called was my uncle Dave. I sat there
        watching the planes hit the tower time and time and time again on the television. They just kept showing them being hit and then falling. My
        father wouldnt come home for three days, he came home covered in soot, or
        should I say, ashes of the victims. Days went on, my uncle was still missing, we all
        had bursts of hope that he would survive, we would hear stories of small voids that
        people could have survived in. Days turned into weeks and we soon came to realize
        that my uncle wasnt coming home. My father was right there the day of
        September 11th, right across he street, he set up a command post, the first tower fell
        and he ran into an underground parking garage with other emergency rescue men, he later got out with some other
        firefighters and ran behind the glass building next to the World Financial Center, thank
        God he is still alive. He lost countless friends and his own brother. Things will never be
         the same for my family and I. Months later we had a memorial for my uncle, a mass.
        We went in limos and firefighters lined the strees, bagpipes played and people like you
         wouldnt believe were there, it was a lot to deal with. A year later September 11th
        2002 I would get up and run throughout the streets with an American Flag, people
        loved it and went nuts. That day was also a day which killed me, our family decided to
        bury a casket, an empty one, you know that really kills me deep down inside that not
        only did they take my uncles life but that these lowlifes left nothing, empty.....
        The New York City Fire Department has played a major impact on my life, I would ride
        with my father when  was younger and once we actually responded to the World Trade
         Center, its just strange to think that I was once there. But one thing is sure, I will
        never ever forget September 11th for the rest of my life and every Firefighter and Police
        Office that ran into those buildings that day are true HEROES!!!! God Bless the victims
         of those attacks and God Bless the true heroes who brought down that plane in
        Pennsylvannia and stopped it from hitting another building and taking more innocent lives. Dont ever tell me to forget
        September 11th!!!!!! I have started a patch collection in memoriam for the heroes of
        September 11th, right now I have close to 300 fire department patches from all over the
         world in my room. F.F. David P. DeRubbio  F.F. Faustino Apostol  Jose Angel
        Martinez  Brigette Ann Esposito Forever in our hearts!!!! RIP 9/11/01!!!!  Tunnel to Towers 04' Be there!!
