        My son and I were just getting into the car to go to work when I heard on the radio that the first Tower had been hit by an airplane.  We were shocked. We were in our driveway and I said lets go in to watch television and see how bad it was. My daughter had stayed home from school that day and I was glad she was sick for once.  One more of the ones I love close to me on a day like this.  Well while we were watching, the second plane hit Tower 1. I called my husband at work and told him the news and he could not believe it.  We watched and all I could think of was getting my family together because as a family we would be safe.
