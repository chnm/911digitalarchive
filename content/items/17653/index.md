        My birthday is on Sep.11 so it's a difficult day to celebrate.  In the morning, I was jumping around and dancing and celebrating my birthday. My mom and I started crying and calling loved ones.
              Later, I found out that my aunt who is a flight attendant for American Airlines, was scheduled for the flight out of Boston.  Instead, she traded with a friend who took the flight instead. Her friend will be remembered in our hearts forever.
