      text: On Labor Day weekend I left my 3 children with my ex-husband in Illinios to finish out the school year before following be to Texas.  As so many single parents do, I work very hard every day to take care of my children who were all under the age of 8 at the time of the attack.  I was staying with my mother in her 1 bedroom apt in San Antonio, and was looking for work.  On the morning of the attacks, I woke up at 7:45 or so.  I turned on the TV and went to the kitchen to fix a cup of tea, I heard the news on but wasnt paying any attention to the conversation until I turned the corner into the living room.  I looked at the TV and saw the plane hit the tower.  At first I thought I was seeing things.  How could this be?  That is New York, not some 3rd world country.  I made it to the sofa, realizing that this was really happening.  I called my father who is a long-haul driver for a major transportation company to see where he was in the USA.  He had no idea of the situation, and instantly turned on the radio in his rig.  I was happy that he was no where near the eastern part of the county, but felt guilty and sorrowful for all those we lost.  I cried for the next few hours, Cried for those gone, those unknown, and those left behind. I couldnt peel myself from the TV. I did not shower, missed an interview, and did not eat on Sept. 11th 2001
