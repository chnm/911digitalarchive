        I was attending a computer class when the instructor
        announced that a second plane had hit the twin towers
        and class was dismissed for the day. I rushed home to
        retrieve my cell phone to call my daughter and her husband
        who live and work in Georgetown. I immediately turned on
        the television and was horrified/mesmerized by the broadcast.

        announced  that another plane was (he thought) headed
        for Washington. The agony of trying to reach my family
        and watching the horror unfold is something I hope
        to never live through again. It was late afternoon
        before I heard from my daughter and she and her husband
        were physically ok.
        I can't imagine the frustrations and emotions that families
        have gone through who lost loved ones on 9/11.
        This event has left emotional scars on everyone's heart
        and soul.
