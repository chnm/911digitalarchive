      text: I had woken up early that morning, in order to take my grandmother to an appointment with her eye doctor.  I had a few extra minutes before we had to leave, so I was playing video games. As soon as I shut off the power on the system, the image of the first tower hit was displayed on the television screen, in all of its horrific destruction.
