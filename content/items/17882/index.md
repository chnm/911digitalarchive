      text: My name is Tina.  I have a sister that works in the pentagon.  She was a few hundred feet from the blaze.  Just the day before, she had not finished writing her transcripts for her secretary job.  She had decided that she would finish them in the morning. I want to say thank you to all those who helped save lives, and to all the brave people who risked their lives for others.  That is what our country is about, lending a hand when others are in need.  I could not imagine what those families are going through, I just thank god that I am not in their shoes.  May god bless all those families that have been hurt by this tragedy, and know that you are in our prayers.
