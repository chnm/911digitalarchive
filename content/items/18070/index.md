        I was on the phone with our supplier and she told me a plane had flown into the world trade tower. As we spoke of how tragic this was another plane hit tower 2 and she said she thought it was planned and a attack on our nation was happening.
        I went to the other offices to let my fellow workers know what was happening. I told the group in a meeting I was sorry to interrupt but that two planes had flown into the world trade towers and one of our co-workers burst into tears as she had a freind who worked there. (Thank God her freinds office had moved into another building and he was fine.) We sent for a tv and spent the better part of the day in shock watching an event that was too horrible to imagine.
        I went to prayer and have not stopped since. I wear my t-shirts that  tell of my support for my fellow Americans and I pray over the families of the deceased all the time.
        I had just lost my Mother and was so glad I did not have to tell her what had happened in New York.
        I also remember the firemen and police and rescue people and their families in my prayers. These people are heros and I shake the hand and hug any firemen I see. They did not give up even when they knew no one else was alive.
        This was such a wake up call for us in America. We are not invincible without working together. May God always be with us no matter where we have to be.
        One Nation Under God....We will always Remember 9-11-01. Another day in infamy. Pearl Harbor revisited
