      text: |-
        I had heard of the first plane to hit the WTC on my car radio
        on my way into work.  When I got to my office, I joined most
        of my co-workers in the conference room to watch the new coverage
        on TV.  We sat in shocked dismay as the second plane hit, and
        then later as the towers crumbled and fell.  It was unbelieveable.
        We felt and heard the impact of the plane that hit the Pentagon
        while we were in the conference room, but didn't know what it was.
        A co-worker whose wife works at the Pentagon called him on his
        cell phone to tell him what had happened and that they were
        evacuating.  We then went to the rear side of our building, and
        could see from the rear windows the smoke arising from the
        Pentagon.  It seemed that the world was coming to an end.  An
        hour later, we were all told to evacuate the Lab and go home.
        My commute is about 45 minutes long, and all the way home I prayed
        for myself and my family and all those that had to have been
        killed or horribly injured in all the attacks and their families.
        I still can't fathom the misery that the victims families have had
        to bear, and on the first anniversary of this terrible trauma to
        our people and our country, I pray again that nothing like this
        can ever happen here again.  God bless America!
