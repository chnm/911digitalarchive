        I was in science and the class was almost over.  My teacher was like, "oh did any of you hear about what happened in the states this morning?"  Every one didn't know what she was talking about.  Than she said, "I guess two planes hit each other in mid-air."

        That day I cried about seven times.  I was felt with sorrow, as I listened to people who were looking for their family and their friends.

        The next day I went back to school. Not knowing what was going to happen.  It was scary for me because that was the first time I ever seen something like that.
