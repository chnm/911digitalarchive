        I was a student at the University of South Florida in Tampa, FL.
        I woke up early on the 11th of September to finish some research for one
        of my classes. I signed online and noticed a hot link on my computer that said
        a small plane had crashed into the World Trade Center and a plane had
        crashed into the Pentagon. I thought it was one of those respond to the survery
        hypothetical story links. I then decided to click on it and the response never went
        through. I went to my television and turned on the Today show on NBC. As I sat in my
        living room, I was in disbelief as I watched Katie Couric talk about the crashing of
        the plane into the building. Being an ex-military Air Force man, I kept wondering
        how a plane could crash into such a huge building. As I watched in horror the second plane hit
        the second trade tower. I could not beieve my eyes and my heart was crying for the loss of
        life. As the events of September 11 unfolded, I was totally caught up in the entire news coverage.
        I left for class about 11:00am and turned on the radio expecting to hear some kind of good news, of course
        that never came about. I arrived to the Tampa campus around 12:00 and all of the students on
        campus were huddled around a small television set that was broadcasting the events of this horrific day.
        People were crying, hysterical, it was an unbelievable scene. One which I hope I never have to experience
        again.
