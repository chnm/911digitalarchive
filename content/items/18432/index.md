        On September 11, 2001, I was at school. Miss Loft
        (teacher's aide) was on the computer and we had just

        my Mom sat down with me and my brother and explained
        to us what had happened.

        I did not want our Country to go into war.

        Now when I see people who look like Bin Laden I am
        scared. I fly to California every summer to see my
        Grandma, now I am afraid to fly ever again. When I go
        to the Navy Base, Whiting Field for Starbase I am
        scared because soldiers with guns come on our bus and
        look everywhere even under our seats.

        Now our Country will never be the same. When I grow up
        and I have kids of my own I will have to tell them the
        way the world use to be, before September 11, 2001.
