        I remember the day of september 11, oh so well. I was sitting on my couch watching t.v when the breaking news came across N.B.C. All of a sudden i saw a plane crashing into one of the twin towers!. When that plane struck that tower, i thought" oh my god,those people,all those people are going to be dead!. No words can describe how the impact of that plane hitting that tower had on everybody up here in canada. We are a country who pulls together in times of need, whether it be for friends and family or just total stranger's. The new's stations had their phones ringing off the hook, with people asking about what they had just witnessed on our local news channels.The aid started pouring in at that very moment. Our local police and fire departments, the red cross,just everyone.
