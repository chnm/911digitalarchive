      text: My alarm is set to KNPR radio station.  I was awaken at approximately 6:30 a.m. to the news broadcast that the Trade Center had been hit by an airplane.  I jumped out of bed and turned on the television and much to my horror I caught the broadcast showing the first plane hitting then the second one hitting.  I woke my son up and had him sit with me.  We sat and held hands and cried.  I told him that we had to attempt some type of normalicy even in the tragedy.  As we were driving to school, we saw several children playing on the playground of a school.  It looked so incredibly surreal.  It reminded me just how safe we feel in America.  And now in these troubling times, I wonder just how safe we really are.  As a result of 9/11, I now have an idea of what it must be like to live where buildings and homes are being bombed.  It is earth shattering.  To this day whenever I see fotage of the Trade Towers being hit, I cry.  My family and I will never forget 9/11 or all those that perished leaving orphaned families.  We are forever grateful for all of the brave souls who made the ultimate sacrifice and perished trying to save others.
