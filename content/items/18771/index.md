      text: I had given birth to my beautiful baby girl at 6:36 am.  Around 9:00 am the nurse had finally taken my husband and I to our private room.  When we got there the tlevision was already on CNN. It was showing the actual broadcast after the first attack had taken place. I watched some t.v. but really didn't pay that much attention to it. I just wanted to believe that it was all a movie, make blieve. As the day progressed we heard of all the reports.
