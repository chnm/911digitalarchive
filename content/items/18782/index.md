      text: I remember watching television that morning as I do every morning, the today's show, when Katie made the announcement that a plane crashed into the World Trade Center.  I thought oh Lord.  Then they switched to the World Trade burning and I saw the second plane strike and I was horrified.  I though Jesus this is terrorism right off the bat.  I was in shock and when the World Trade center fell, I was on speechless.  I had called my neighbor who never watches television but on that day, she did after I told her.  I remember not sleeping because I felt so bad for the people who lost their lives there and for the families that will miss them.  I was sleepless for about ten nights.  I could not stay away from the television no matter how I tried.  It was so hard to comprehend.  I know the country was in shock as well.  I know we all had to actually pray to keep ourselves together and to pray for the families that were in distress.  This was a terrible blow to us all. I do believe we have become stronger as a country as a result of this and I believe we will stay strong.  Pray for the families that suffered a loss.  Pray for their recovery.  God Bless America.  The country that they are jealous of.
