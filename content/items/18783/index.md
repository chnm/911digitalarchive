        My friend who calls himself HATS, was working at ground Zero immediately after the terror attacks.  He wrote me that he was upset about all the movie stars and famous singers and generally curious public that was standing in the wings, watching him and the rest of his men, posing for pictures and giving out interviews.  I wrote this for him and for the men and women working tiredlessly, day after day, at GROUND ZERO.

        to hats


        to look at it
        a huge still smoldering tangle
        of twisted metal
        concrete
        and smoke
        one cannot yet see the beauty within it
        the resting place of nearly 5000 souls

        the resting place of dreams
        hopes and fears
        love lost
        fortunes destroyed
        tears shed
        bodies torn apart
        and still
        a place where hope is alive

        the hope that we
        as human beings
        can learn to love again
        learn to trust again
        learn to crawl out of the ashes
        and still the flames of hatred

        the hope that love never dies
        that memories will never take the backseat
        to apathy
        the hope that hope itself
        trusting in itself
        will somehow be enough
        for tomorrow

        and yes my friend
        as movie stars traipse
        and tourists gaggle
        and onlookers disturb you
        please remember
        that todays stars may actually make a difference
        tomorrow
        if their art
        their acting
        their writing
        their singing
        takes an upward turn
        after viewing
        Americas graveyard
        and they start saying no!!!
        to just making the buck
        and spend some time
        spreading the message
        of Love.

        with love to all of you

        M
