        IT STARTED OFF TO BE A BEAUTIFUL DAY. I WAS ON THE FRONT END WHERE THE REGISTERS ARE AND I WAS TEASING MY BOSS AND ASKING HIM IF HE WAS GOING TO BUY A TV HE SAID NO THAT SOMETHING HAD JUST CRASHED INTO ONE OF THE TOWERS AND I SAID "WHAT?"
