      text: I was working at Anthem Blue Cross & Blue shield on the morning of the attacks when a friend had told me that a plane had crashed into world trade tower 1. We thought nothing of it, we were just thinking that a plane had crashed. Then we heard about the second plane just minutes later. We really thought something of it then. People all over work had the t.v.s turned on as well as their radios. People were crying and showing their sympathy for the families and victims. It was the most horrible thing we had ever seen. All day long we were watching the tv at work and listening to the radios. We then heard that a man intown was running around with a gun. So we all got really scared. However, it didn't link to the attacks at all. It was just an upset man running around with a gun. Police had caught him. Our hearts go out to all of the families and all of America!
