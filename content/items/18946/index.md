        I had been at a birthday party of a friend and I was getting home at 10.30pm Australian time (8.30am NYC time).  I decided to turn on the TV for a while before going to bed.  A local sports show was interupted by a breaking news story.
        It took me some time before I realised that the burning building in the picture was one of the WTC Towers in NYC.  I could hardly belive what I was seeing.  I was trying to make myself belive that it was something that could be detained and put right until....out of the side of the TV screen came a plane.  As they say - the rest is history.
        NYC had a special meaning to me at that time.  My best friend had just excepted a 2 year contract to work and live in the city.  He was due to fly out of Australia on September 27th.  He took that flight and is still there.
        Even though I am on the other side of the world my heart breaks everytime I see the images of that day.  I have had the chance to visit NYC in May 2002 and I fell in love with it and the people.  I just wish that I had had the chance to see the Towers tall and strong.  I did visit the WTC site with a heavy heart.  In a way I felt like I was intruding.  All those people who's lives ended in such a senseless way - their families desperate for something to hold onto.  I felt like I was prying into their lives. If I was - I'm sorry.
        My love goes out to everyone involved in the rebirth of a great city.  Stay strong.
