        I WAS AWAKENED BY A PHONE CALL FROM MY GRANDMOTHER IN TEARS, SHE SAID TURN ON THE TELEVISION.  SHE IS DRAMATIC AT TIMES SO I THOUGHT WE MUST BE GETTING A SEVERE WEATHER REPORT OR SOMETHING SILLY... AS I TURNED ON THE TELEVISION ONE TOWER WAS ON FIRE AND THE OTHER WAS SECONDS AWAY FROM BEING STRUCK BY THE SECOND AIRPLANE.  I WAS SPEECHLESS.  I KEPT TELLING MYSELF THAT THE BUILDINGS MUST BE EMPTY, THEY HAD TO BE THAT MANY PEOPLE COULD NOT BE IN DANGER.. I WAS WRONG.  AFTER A FEW MINUTES THEY BEGAN TO COLLASPE, THE FEELING OF TERROR AND HELPLESSNESS WAS OVERWHELMING, YOUR LIFE FLASES BEFORE YOUR EYES.  THERE WAS SUCH HORROR.  NOW THAT A YEAR HAS PASSED I NOW LIVE IN WEYMOUTH, MASS AND FEEL CLOSER TO THE PEOPLE HERE, BUT STILL A ENOURMOUS LOSS OF LIFE.  I BELIEVE WITH ALL MY HEART THAT EVEN THOUGH THIS WAS TRAGIC THERE IS A BIGGER MESSEAGE AND MEANING TO THE LOSS OF SO MANY LIVES, I BELIEVE GOD HAS A MESSEAGE FOR US ALL.  IT TOOK SUCH A TRAGIC EVENT FOR US TO UNITE AND COME TOGETHER IN PRAYER AND UNITY, AS OTHER EVENTS HAPPENED OVER THE YEARS WE TURNED TO EACH OTHER FOR SUPPORT AND THE CHURCH THEN AFTER A WHILE WE REJOINED OUR OLD LIVES.. I THINK WE NEED NOT ANOTHER TRAGDEY TO STAY TOGETHER AS A COUNTRY AND IN THE EYES OF THE LORD.  WE SHOULD STAND STRONG AND IN GOOD FAITH BECAUSE OF THE GOOD THINGS IN LIFE WE HAVE AND WILL HAVE IN THE FUTURE.  THAT'S HOW WE WILL CONTINUE TO SURVIVE THE WORLD WE LIVE IN.  I PRAY FOR ALL THE FAMILIES FOR THEIR LOSSES, BUT KNOW THEIR IN  A BETTER PLACE AND THEIR WAS PURPOSE FOR THEIR LIVES.
        RIKKI KILLOUGH
        BRKILLOUGH@AOL.COM
