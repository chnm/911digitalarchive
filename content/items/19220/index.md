      text: It was 9:33 and I walked into my third hour Creative Cuisine class in Hickman High School. My teacher had the radio on which was very unlike her. Nobody really knew what was going on but they thought it was a terrist attack. It was all very shocking and I had never experienced anything like it before so I didn't really understand what was going on. My teacher could tell that none of us really understood what was going on and I realized how serious it was when my teacher said, "This is history guys," and said that it was worse than Pearl Harbor.
