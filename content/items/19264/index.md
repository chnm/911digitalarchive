        I was at work.  Someone menitioned that the WTC was hit by a plane.  I had no TV, just a radio.  So I got on line.  It took a long time to get on line.  I guess a lot of people were doing the same.  As the day's events unfolded, I still had a job to do.  Work was going on as if nothing has happened.  I mean, we still had to load and ship Mack Truck engines to our customers.  At one point, I said to a friend, "we act like nothing has happened.  Don't you understand that we have just witnessed the start of a war.  We are at war.  This won't end today.  This will go on for years."  As a Gulf War Vet., I know the taste of war. As an instructor at Gettysburg, I know that the bodies of that great battle were not all recovered until the following March.  I did not want to think how long it would take at the WTC.

        I was to leave the Marine Corps after 20 years.  I was lucky my continuance was approved until 2005.
