        I remember that i was walking my girlfiend to work and a woman drove by saying "turn on a radio, we're at war." and at the time, I didn't think anything of it until i got home and my landlord was standing on the front porch with a phone saying "you might want to call your family." and then i walked inside and flipped on the t.v.

        Like many others, I can't even begin to describe how I felt...a sense of utter shock, a thought that "It could never happen here."

        It took me over 45 minutes to call my family and find out that they were ok and i ount myself amongst one of the luckier ones as if my mother hadn't called in sick that day, both she and my daughter would've been ringside at Ground Zero.

        I remember her saying "things will never be the same here."and even to this day, it still holds true.

        It's been a long time since i thought of what happened and mythoughts drift to those who lost family,friends,loved ones ad my heart still goes out to them.

        We as a Nation and as a people should never forget what happened and strive to bring those responsible to justice.
