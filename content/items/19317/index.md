      text: I was watching Good Morning America while I was feeding my 4 week old baby, Laney.  They stopped talking about their topic and said they were going to a report about the World Trade Center.  I saw camera footage of one of the towers smoking due to the huge hole in it.  A witness was talking about it sounding like a bomb but others recalled seeing a plane crash into it.  Alot of people were confused.  Another bomb?  A plane crashed into the tower?  What was going on?  I didn't know what to think!  Then, I saw another plane flying too close and it turned almost sideways and crashed into the otherside of the second tower.  I went numb.  It felt like the world was coming to an end!  I called my mother at work to tell her what was going on and how scared I was.  Then, another plane hit the Pentagon!  It was unbeleivable.  I remember asking her "do you think we're about to go to war?"  She agreed that's exactly how it seemed. Later, they found another crashed but luckily it didn't hit a building.  It was still just as horrible!  It seemed like seconds later the beautiful Twin Towers collapsed, one by one.  Throughout the day, I most have called her a billion times!  I called my best friend, in Indiana and my grandmother.  I don't know who all I talked to that day but I know I was really in shock and scared to death of what was about to happen!  I almost had an anxiety attack!  For the longest time afterwards, the sound of a plane flying over my home, whether they were headed toward or away from Bush Field or Fort Gordon, I felt sick.  Still now, one year later, I get upset watching all the news stories and seeing the scenes and pictures.  What an awful tragedy!!!  I'll never forget it.
