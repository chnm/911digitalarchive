      text: The first thing that I remember about September 11 2001 was what a truly beautiful morning it had been. There was not a cloud in the sky as I drove to the highschool where I taught.  The day started out normally but around 9:30-10:00 A.M. while I was on my plannning period another techer came into the teacher's lounge where I was sitting and told me that an airplane flew into the WTC.  At first I didn't believe him and I wanted to check it out for myself.  I could'nt get to a radio or tv so I went to my car and called my mom and asked her what was going on.  She said two planes had hit the WTC and one just hit the Pentagon.  I quickly went back into the school and when I reached the office the principal was deciding if she should make an announcement about the terrorist attack.  After much thought she decided to make an announcement and asked for a moment of silence for those who had died.  From that moment on it seemed like that day would never end.
