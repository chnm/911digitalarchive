      text: A panicked teacher walked into our room and whispered something into my chemistry teacher's ear.  The teacher left and ours looked on the verge of tears as she announced that "a plane has just run into one of the World Trade Center buildings in New York." So here I am a year after the event, in class, trying to remember exactly how I felt when everything happened.  It all happened so quickly, nobody had time to prepare themselves for this.
