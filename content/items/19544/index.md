        I WAS NOT LISTENING TO MY NORMAL RADIO STATION (WLS TALK RADIO) THAT MORNING.  A CO-WORKER CAME INTO MY OFFICE AFTER HIS WIFE HAD CALLED TO TELL HIM TO TURN ON THE NEWS.  I CHANGED TO WLS THEN BEGAN LISTENING TO THE DESCRIPTION OF EVENTS THAT WERE UNFOLDING AT THAT MINUTE.

I COULD NOT BELIEVE THEY WERE BROUGHT DOWN AND THE PEOPLE JUMPING FROM THE TOWERS AND THE FEAR THEY MUST HAVE FELT.  I BEGAN TO CRY FOR ALL THOSE INNOCENT PEOPLE.  I CALLED MY SONS TO HEAR THEIR VOICES AND KNOW THEY WERE WHERE THEY WERE SUPPOSED TO BE.  A FRIEND CALLED ME AND WE JUST COULD NOT BELIEVE WHAT WAS UNFOLDING.

        TODAY I AM JUST AS SAD AS I WAS A YEAR AGO AND HOPE WE NEVER FORGET WHAT WAS DONE TO THOSE PEOPLE AND THEIR FAMILIES AND WE AS A NATION.  I HOPE THAT ALL OF THE PERPETRATORS ARE BROUGHT TO JUSTICE AND SWIFTLY.  I PRAY TO GOD THAT BREAK THE RING OF HATRED THAT GAVE BIRTH TO THESE MISGUIDED, EVIL PEOPLE.
