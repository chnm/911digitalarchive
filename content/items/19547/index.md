      text: |-
        I WAS AT WORK ON SEPTEMBER 11.  AS I SAT DOWN AT MY SEAT PEOPLE STARTED COMING INTO THE OFFICE ASKING IF WE HEARD THE NEWS.  THE PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY TURNED THE NEWS ON AND THAT IS WHEN I FIRST SAW THE IMAGES OF THAT TRAGIC DAY.  I REMEMBER THAT AS I SAW THE IMAGES ON THE TV MY FIRST THOUGHTS WERE "OH MY GOD ALL THOSE PEOPLE".   I REMEMBER THINKING THAT THIS WAS ALL HAPPENING RIGHT OVER THE BRIDGE.   IF I WERE TO DRIVE DOWN THE STREET I WOULD BE ABLE TO SEE EVERYTHING.   THE PRESIDENT DECIDED THAT IF WE ALL FELT SAFER WE COULD GO HOME.  ALL I COULD THINK ABOUT WAS BEING WITH MY FAMILY.   AS I DROVE HOME ALL I COULD HEAR ON THE RADIO WERE THE STORIES.  THE SECOND I GOT INTO MY HOUSE I TURNED ON THE TELEVISION.  ALL I COULD DO WAS THINK ABOUT WHAT WAS GOING ON AND HOW TERRIBLE IT MUST FEEL IF YOU HAD A FAMILY MEMBER THERE.
             AS THE HOURS PASSED ALL I FELT WAS SORROW FOR THE TRAGIC SOULS THAT WERE LOST THAT MORNING.  AS THE TOWERS FELL I JUST GOT SADDER.  AS THE TOWERS FELL MY MOOD GOT WORSE.   I NEVER FELT AS MUSCH ANGER TOWARDS A PERSON AS I FELT THAT DAY WHEN I HEARD THAT COWARD, OSAMA BIN LADEN, NAME ON THE BROADCAST.   ONE MONSTER COULD TURN OUR WORLD UPSIDE DOWN.   I COULDN'T EVEN COMPREHEND EVERYTHING THAT WAS GOING ON. AS I SAT WATCHING TV I COULD HEAR THE JETS FLY OVER MY HOUSE.   IF I WENT ONTO THE FRONT LAWN AND LOOKED UP I COULD SEE THEM FLYING BY.
             AS I CROSS THE GEORGE WASHINGTON BRIDGE NOW I DON'T SEE IN THE DISTANT THE TOWERS.  I NEVER NOTICED HOW SYMBOLICLY IMPORTANT THEY WERE UNTIL THEY WERE GONE.   THAT DAY CHANGED THE LIVES OF ALL AMERICANS, OF ALL HUMAN BEINGS.   SINCE 9-11 I CHERISH EVERY DAY THAT I COULD WALK ON THIS EARTH.  SO MANY INNOCENT PEOPLE WERE LOST ON THAT DAY.  I KNOW THAT FOR ME I WILL NEVER BE ABLE TO FORGET.   THE IMAGES WILL FOREVER ME IMPRINTED IN MY MIND.   MY HEART GOES OUT TO ALL THE PEOPLE THAT LOST A FRIEND, FAMILY MEMBER, CO-WORKER ETC.   MY WORLD WAS FOREVER CHANGED.  I MIGHT NOT KNOW THE NAMES OF ALL THE PEOPLE LOST BUT I WILL REMEMBER WHAT HAPPENED AND IN MY ON WAY I WILL REMEMBER THEM.
