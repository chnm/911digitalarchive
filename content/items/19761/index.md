      text: I put on the morning news, as I did everyday since I had our first child.  Aiden was only 4 months old.  When I turned on Channel 10 I saw the first Tower on fire, then picked up the phone and called my husband at work.  He told me they were trying to get a television in the shop so they could see what was happening in New York City that day.  Just before I was about to hang up the phone the second Tower was hit.  At first I thought it was a replay of Tower 1, then I heard the newscaster yelling.  I told Conor, my husband, that the second Tower had been hit.  He told me he'd call back, someone needed the phone, and that he loved me.  I sat on my couch and looked down at our baby boy.  Aiden was lying on a blanket, playing with his toys.  He was just smiling away, not even knowing at the time what horrifying things were going on.  Then I cried.  I didn't want my boy to grow up in a world like this.  I cried until Conor came home that evening around 5pm.  He sat down and watched everything I had seen during the day and hugged Aiden.  I knew what he was thinking.  But, we all knew America would bounce back after that day.  This tragedy  would make us stronger.
