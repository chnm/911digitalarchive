        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 9:24 PM
        Just compensation

        To whom it may concern,
             I want to address the issue of compensation for
        the families of gay and lesbian persons who were
        killed in the attack on September 11.
             It seems to me that any effort to exclude
        partners or children of gay and lesbian persons who
        were killed or injured in the attack on the WTC would
        be unjust. Even though there is no law that allows gay
        and lesbian persons to think of their partners as a
        family, I believe with all my heart that there is a
        moral justice issue involved here. All persons and
        their families should receive the same consideration.
             The terrorists who attacked the WTC made no
        differenciation as to race, gender, age or sexual
        orientation and neither should the regulations
        governing the compensation of victims and their
        families. As a citizen, I urge the committee to extend
        their compasion and that of the whole country to the
        partners and children of gay and lesbian persons who
        were victims on September 11.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Eugene, Oregon
