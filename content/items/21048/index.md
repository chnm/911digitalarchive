        Sunday, March 17, 2002 2:54 PM


        If our government believes that it is their duty to compensate the families
        of 9-11, they need to put aside personal prejudices and be fair.  I, as a
        taxpayer, have had no say as to whether I want to compensate "victims", and
        I can accept that.  However I cannot and will not accept without comment the
        gross unfairness with which the funds are being distributed.  If illegal
        aliens can be rewarded for being in our country by being given hundreds of
        thousands of dollars, if unborn fetuses are being used in figuring the
        amount a family gets, then I find it unbelievable that gays and lesbians are
        being discriminated against by being denied compensation.  This
        administration has gone way overboard to foist their beliefs on Americans by
        using and abusing their powers and by declaring any dissenters unpatriotic.
        Please reconsider your rigid stance in this matter, and do what is right for
        all Americans.

        Individual Comment
