        Please take into account that the families will most likely be suffering from Post Traumatic Stress Disorder for some time to come.

We also can't compensate all those who witnessed in person, as the numbers would be overwhelming and limited control on proof.
        But the ramifications to all of us.....they are immense, and the
        aftereffects are happening.  We are concerned.
