        The following are my objections to the Victims Compensation Fund:

        My husband's life insurance policy, which he paid for, which would pay
        off my mortgage, should not be deducted from any benefits I may receive.
        This was purchased privately and has nothing to do with any government,
        state or city benefits that I would/could receive because of this
        tragedy.  (Collateral source deductions are not required in court
        cases.)

        The proposed awards for non-economic damages ($250,000) per victim are
        only one-tenth the level paid in comparable cases.  For example, a
        spouse was awarded $5 million in non-economic damages in a lawsuit
        arising out of Lockerbie.  Congress explicitly enumerated a broad range
        of non-economic damages for which victims and their families shall be
        compensated and DOJ ignored this.

        Individual Comment
        Hillsdale, NJ
