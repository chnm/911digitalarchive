        To Kenneth Feinberg Special Master,

        I received this e mail from a dear friend of mine whose husband was killed at
        the WTC.  I feel that there is an injustice being perpetrated.  Thank you for

                     Individual Comment

                                                                                                  Sincerely yours,


        Victims' Compensation Fund

        Dear Friends,
        There are several unfair issues as established under the rules set forth by
        the Special Master. People like myself would actually receive nothing under
        the rules set forth.  I don't think that is fair.  We are being penalized for
        living according to the rules, paying into pensions, life insurance etc. All
        of that will be deducted from the compensation.
        They include:
               1. use of out of date statistics to compute the tables set forth for
        compensation
               2. $250,000 as the maximum award for non-economic loss (with $50,000
        for each dependent.)
               3. families should be fully informed of their compensation prior to
        surrendering their right to litigate
               4. elimination of the 24 hour rule which limits the ability to seek
        compensation if you were injured helping at the WTC and did not seek medical
        assistance within 24 hours.
               5. decrease in compensation through subtraction of pension, social
        security, workers'compensation and life insurance from the compensation
               6. no mention of disabled children in the compensation rules

               president@whitehouse.gov
               senator@clinton.senate.gov
               senator@schumer.senate.gov
               victimcomp.comments@usdoj.gov (this is Kenneth Feinberg Special Master)

        Any senator or congressman you can think of would be appreciated.

        Thanks,
        Individual Comment
