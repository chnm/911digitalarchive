        9/11 Victim Compensation Fund

         In response to your request for public input on how the 9/11 Victim
         Compensation Fund should be administered and funds distributed, justice  will only be served when all families are treated equally including those  who lose their life partners, de facto parents or children without regard to sexual orientation or marital status.

         The State of New York set the correct tone when Gov. Pataki granted
         compensation benefits "on a showing of mutual interdependence with the  victim, in recognition that anyone who shared with the victim living  expenses, day-to-day activities and the emotions bonds of family deserves  help . . . "

         I expect no less from the Federal government in administering the 9/11  Victim Compensation Fund.  Thank you for your kind consideration in this regard.

        Individual Comment
        Irvine, CA
