        Victims compensation fund

        To Whom It May concern

                       I am writing to you because I would like to know what the
        difference is between a grieving spouse and a grieving domestic partner who
        are interdependent on one another? Is my loss any different because I don't
        have a marriage certificate? Is my pain and suffering and economic loss less
        or insignificant because I wasn't married? Did I love my fiancee less than
        the next person loved their spouse? Did her paycheck pay bills or go for
        entertainment? Who decides who has a valid claim? Who decides what the value
        of that claim is? I understand there needs to be checks and balances but
        documented pain and suffering and economic loss of interdependent victims and
        domestic partners should be included in the claim process because we are no
        different than anyone else who lost a loved one.

        Individual Comment

        Brooklyn, NY
