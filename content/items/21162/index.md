        Tuesday, March 12, 2002 1:29 PM
        Distribution of 9/11 funds


        I cannot sit quietly. I am seething about the inequity of the management
        and distribution of 9/11 funds as described by Kenneth Feinberg on the
        Sunday, March 10 broadcast of NBC's "Meet the Press,"

        Specifically, I am angered that the loved ones of Gay or Lesbians who
        were victims that day will not receive any compensation from the fund.
        How can Mr. Feinberg defend his position on the grounds that he can't
        violate state law? He is very willing to violate Federal law in distributing
        funds to illegal aliens! It is preposterous!

        It is just this sort of overt hypocrisy that continues to make me lack faith

        in our current leadership. Our Attorney General is undeniably using his
        own personal morality to justify this type of policy. As much as he claims
        to the contrary, Mr. Ashcroft and the Department of Justice (ironic)
        conduct themselves, AS PUBLIC OFFICIALS, from the bias of their own
        personal agendas.

        It is truly unfathomable to me that they believe this is legally
        justifiable,
        let alone, morally justifiable. Gay or Lesbian victims were just that.
        Victims.
        And, in several known cases, heroes. Can anyone deny that victim
        0001, NY Fire Department &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp , was not
        heroic? Patriotic? Moral? A good American? Upstanding? Deserving
        of having his loved ones supported just as much as any other
        victim of that terrible day?

        I, for one, am disgusted. So much for unity and tolerance for all. You
        know, for a "religious" man, Mr. Ashcroft clearly seems to have failed
        to grasp the most basic concept -- Do unto others as you would have
        them do unto you.  He ought to give it a try sometime.

        Individual Comment
