        To whom it may concern,

          My husband,           was lost in the September 11th terrorist

        children ages 5yrs and under.  I have received information from the Trial
        Lawyers Care on the September 11th victim compensation fund 2001, and I have
        some disagreements regarding the compensation fund distribution of funds,
        they are as follows:

        1)  Why do you want to limit what the compensation will be based on what has
        already been collected by the victims families (such as any benefits; life
        Insurance; State crimes victims etc.)?  This should not be based on the
        lawsuits that will occurr over time.

        2)  Osama Bin Ladens assests should not be attached to the September 11th
        fund of the package introduced by Congress, if victims families decide to
        sue Osama Bin Laden personally they should have all the right to due so,
        based on the evidence the United States took action on.

        3)  Why did the US put a freeze on Osama Bin Ladens assets or organizations
        that he is affiliated with,if he was not responsible for this terrible act
        of terrorism against the United States of America?

        4) Why should an average family be backed into a corner to receive whats
        rightfully due to the victims families, to accept this package?

        5)Any assets that the government freezes from Bin Laden should be
        distributed to the victims families with no strings attached.

        6)Why do middle working class people who lost loved ones have to be put in a
        position to make a decision on this package that Congress will be voting on?
          Don't you think we've been through enough already!

        7)  I am looking for the future for my children to be taken care of, 3 years
        from now this will be forgotten and I wonder how will I be able to take care
        of my children?  I will not forget so easely since my husband was taken from
        us for no reason at all? Can someone answer this for me?

        8) In order to releive some of the victims family woes is to waive estate
        taxesto all the victims families. Can this be possible?

        Individual Comment
        Staten Island, NY
