        I Am Outraged!

        To Whom It May Concern:

        It has recently come to my attention that you will not be automatically
        providing benefits to partners of gay and lesbian victims of September 11th
        due to the laws that do not recognize them as legal beneficiaries.  However,
        it is clear to me you are readily giving funds to illegal aliens who are in
        the country, breaking our own laws.

        How can this unfairness exists and why is this discrimination allowed, when
        we are encouraging all americans young and old to stand together against
        evil and terror.  Brave men and women of all races, religions, color, and
        sexual orientation died and many of their loved ones have been left behind
        in need of our support and understanding.  It is clear to me that you are
        continuing the cycle of hate and separating America with your discriminatory
        views and actions.

        I have donated a lot of money to this fund and feel that it should go to all
        of the victims' partners whether or not they were heterosexual, gay or
        lesbian.

        The guidelines you have decided to follow appear to benefit illegal aliens
        while leaving a percentage of the legal law-abiding citizens high and dry.
        This action is clearly immoral if not illegal in most states.  I regard this
        action as deplorable and pray you find the ability to rectify these wrongs
        before they come back to haunt you.


        Individual Comment
