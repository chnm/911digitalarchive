        9/11 Funds


        I cannot believe that, no matter what someone's
        personal beliefs, they could see not giving
        gay/lesbian partners and families their share of the
        9/11 funds as the right thing to do. By refusing their
        right to some of these funds, the American people are
        belittling and ignoring the sacrifices made by
        AMERICAN CITIZENS that just happened to be gay or
        lesbian. The fund is giving money to ILLEGAL ALIENS,
        people who have no right to anything else but will get
        their share of the 9/11 funds because they lost loved
        ones. So how is it wrong to give AMERICAN CITIZENS,who
        lost loved ones, their share of the 9/11 funds due to
        their sexual preferences. If we are to follow the
        laws, then apply them to everyone and refuse to give
        aid to ILLEGAL ALIENS families as well as gay/lesbian
        families. I thought we were a country that fought
        against discrimination, not encouraged it. I am
        seriously concerned that our government would not help
        its own citizens in the wake of this tragedy.
        Individual Comment
