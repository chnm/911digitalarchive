        Hello, I lost my husband in the WTC attacks on 9/11 and I have a concern

        estate would be divided, after the first $50,000, between myself and my
        husbands family...just because we didn't have wills. I want to make sure this
        compensation will be paid directly to me, not the estate, as his family was
        not financially dependent upon him.   I would have no problem splitting "pain
        & suffering" with them, b/c that is what we are all experiencing, but the
        economic loss should not be split because I am next of kin, we depended
        financially upon one another, no one else did. Please let me know how you
        presume this will carry out, thank you very much for your time.
