        UN-AMERICAN ACTIVITIES

        I am  outraged that gay and lesbian partners of those who died on

        by Congress.

        As a 72 year old woman, there has been discrimination in my life since
        childhood.
        First, I was taught that the Native
        Americans were bad people. Next,  it was the Japanese so, let's not buy
        their products. Then the Puerto Ricans were depicted as low class
        animals.

        Now, we get to the black-skinned people.
        They surely were at the bottom of the totem pole.

        SO THE MESSAGE IS THIS::: WHO SHALL WE FIND TO PICK ON NEXT?  OBVIOUSLY
        A GROUP THAT CANNOT OPENLY FIGHT FOR THEIR RIGHTS.

        Now, there is discrimination regarding Gay people who died on Sept. 11,
        2002.
        Their families must be compensated on an equal basis as other grieving
        families by receiving the same amount of money.

        WHEN THERE IS A WAR....WILL WE KEEP OUT THE GAYS, BLACKS, ORIENTALS,
        LATINOS & JAPANESE?
        OR WILL THESE PEOPLE BE NEEDED FOR EMERGENCIES ONLY.

        LET OUR CONSCIENCE BE OUR GUIDE.

        Individual Comment
