        To Whom It Concerns,

           The families of the people who lost their lives in both the buildings and
        the planes very much deserve help. Do the airline deserve help in the form of
        bail outs? Apparently so, and if so then why not the persons who are the life
        line of the airlines.
        The travel agents who have seen as much as a 75% drop in business. The self
        employed small travel agent business owner who can't apply for unemployment,
        who can't get state assistance because their business is an asset. The self
        employed who already work 50 hr. week keeping their business afloat, who
        couldn't find a part time job in this economy even if they could physically
        handle another job.
            I ask you to help the travel industry as a whole. To help the small
        business person who is being destroyed by 9-11-01. I am very saddened by the
        days events 9-11-01. The town of Hudson lost one family man to the
        terrorists. No amount of money will ever compensate the family for the lose
        of a loved one. But at least along with losing a loved one the family will
        not lose their homes, or their children's future. But is a person's life
        valued at 1.6 million or should quarterly request for money be submitted for
        an as need basis.
            I assure you the need for financial assistance due to 9-11-01 goes beyond
        the first line of horrible deaths and the families it touched.

        Individual Comment
              Hudson, NH
