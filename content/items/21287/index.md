        Dear Sir, Madam,

        From The Netherlands I write you this message, after I read that your Government does not equally compensate "hetero" families and "homo" families, who are struck by the horrible September 11 terrorist attacks. Personally, I think this is not right, not just and could not be the policy of a Government that continually states that there is freedom and equalty for all in their country.

        Here in The Netherlands, for example, gay partners can get married, just as straight people can, nothing less, nothing more.

        In times like these, I think the real "goodness and liberty" shines through all the words that are said.

        I hope you will be able to reconsider your policy on this matter.

        Best wishes, yours sincerely,

        Individual Comment
