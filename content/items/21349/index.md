        Wednesday, March 13, 2002 7:29 AM
        Supporting All Victims

        Mr Kenneth Feinberg:

        As a taxpayer, a citizen, a regular voter and an American Baptist, I am
        asking that you please take a moment to read this short e-mail.  I am
        sure it is not the only one you have received, and that you have many to
        consider.  Thanks for your brief attention.

        The terrorist attacks on September 11th affected ALL Americans.  The
        terrorists did not discriminate based on race, religion, ethnicity,
        socioeconomic status, divorce status, immigration status or sexual
        orientation.  It is unconscionable that the United States Government
        would discriminate in its relief efforts.

        You state in your public role that you will only follow state laws when
        addressing compensation for same-sex couples.  Yet you consciously
        override FEDERAL law in providing necessary relief to undocumented
        immigrants.  You are being clearly inconcsistent.

        Regardless of anyone's personal beliefs about homosexuality, the reality
        is that gay people suffer, gay people bleed, gay people die.  They lose
        their loved ones, their homes, their lives.  The fight bravely for
        others both in our military and in unselfish acts of heroism that
        prevented the loss of additional lives on that fateful day.

        The attacks of September 11th killed thousands.  It killed the dreams of
        thousands more.  Please do not contribute to the suffering by denying
        compensation to those who, unlike yourself, lost the people most
        precious to them in those few horrible hours.

        ALL people who lost their family members deserve compensation.

        Individual Comment
        Philadelphia, PA
