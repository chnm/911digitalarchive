        Thursday, December 13, 2001 1:11 PM
        Gay And Lesbian compensation

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing in regard to the Department's request for public comments
        about the September 11th Fund.  I want to express my strong feelings
        that in the spirit of this country's "equal opportunities" for all, that
        it is essential to make compensation available to all victims of the
        attack.  This should include the family members, partners and children
        of gay and lesbian couples.  Please do not turn away from these families
        in a time of such devastating grief and pain simply because they have
        chosen to live a lifestyle that differs from the "norm."  As a taxpayer
        and a contributor to those in need, I hope that this fund is used in
        fair and democratic ways, rather than to follow the narrow views of
        limited groups of people.


        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Wayland, MA
