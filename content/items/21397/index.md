        9/11 Fund To Discriminate Against Gay Families?


        Dear Mr. Kenneth Feinberg,

        I was very disturbed and disappointed to read that on
        "Meet the Press" on Sunday 3/10/02 you described how
        gay families will be discriminated against by the
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund and not
        receive any support.

        Just because most states treat gay people as
        second-class citizens does not mean that the 9/11 Fund
        should.  The gay families that have suffered losses
        because of the attacks have been hurt just as much as
        the straight families.  And the gay heroes, like            (one of the heroes of United Flight 93 on
        9/11), were as instrumental in preventing more death
        and destruction during the attacks and helping to save
        lives on the ground as the straight folks that day
        (don't forget that the first official casualty of the
        WTC attacks was           , a            onsite at WTC 1 to help with the rescue
        efforts).

        Remarkably, you've indicated that the families of
        ILLEGAL ALIENS will get compensation.  The families of
        non-citizens who are in the USA illegally will get
        compensation but the families of gay American citizens
        won't?  You indicated that the reason gay families
        won't get compensation is because the states don't
        recognize the relationships.  But the states also
        don't recognize the right of illegal aliens to be in
        the United States illegally and yet you find this to
        be an acceptable exception.  This is absurd and
        un-American.

        At a time when our patriotism and love of country
        transcend our differences and unite us all as
        Americans, the 9/11 Fund is choosing to divide us as a
        people, and send the message to all that some American
        heroes are more equal than others.

        Read the following excerpts from Senator John McCain's
        Eulogy for            on September 22, 2001 and tell
        me how it's OK to discriminate against            family
        while compensating the families of illegal aliens.


        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Atlanta GA
