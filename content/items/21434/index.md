        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 6:04 PM

        To:  Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        I am writing to urge that Justice Department regulations governing
        the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" ensure that
        compensation is available to all surviving families of those
        killed, INCLUDING the committed partners and non-biological
        children of gay and lesbian victims.
         Although I am straight I have many gay friends who are in commited
        relationships that date back before my marriage!  Surely they are just as
        entitled to compensation for their tragic losses as are the married victims'
        families.  How wrong it would be to provide funds to parents of adults who
        are in long term relationships instead of to the grieving partner!
        Please use your influence to ensure that justice is done without prejudice.

        Individual Comment
