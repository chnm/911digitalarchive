        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 12:06 AM
        9/11 Fund


        I have just been made aware that the plan for distribution of the September
        11 Fund to victims' families will exclude partners of gays and lesbians who
        died that day.

        I find this appalling.  You are willing to bend the law and grant
        compensation to illegal immigrants (something I don't object to), but when
        it comes to U.S. citizens who happen to be gay or lesbian, you draw the
        line.  This is unfair and, in the case of those who died helping others or
        making it impossible for the last plane to crash into the Capitol, a slap
        in the face.

        Please, honor all the dead equally by treating all of their living loved
        ones the same. Include gays and lesbians in the distribution of the
        September 11 Fund.

        Thank you.

        Individual Comment
        Gays Mills, WI
