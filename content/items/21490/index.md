        Friday, November 23, 2001 5:57 PM
        Comments regarding September 11th victim compensation fund

        In reviewing the proposed legislation and comments I
        would like to express my concerns.  The way the
        legislation reads presently, women who lived with men
        who would be their future husbands can not benefit.
        There are many women who lost the man they were going
        to marry but unfortunately they were killed before the
        wedding dates.  These women, including myself, deserve
        to be compensated separate from the "next of kin".  I
        have seen numerous comments addressing the same
        concern and know I am among many young women in the
        same situation.  I would ask that the legislation
        consider me and the other women as potential claimants
        and give us the ability to receive the financial
        support that their future husbands would have
        provided.  The majority of us effected in this way are
        young and would have had a life time of earnings, love
        and support.  Unfortunately, the love and support can
        not be replaced but our pain and suffering is
        unbelievable and should be compensated.


        Individual Comment
