        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 6:50 PM
        Sept 11th Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing to express my outrage at the recent
        public comments about the September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund.  It is completely unacceptable that
        the partners of gay and lesbian heroes of September 11
        will not necessarily receive the same benefits as the
        spouses of heterosexual victims of the horrible terror
        attacks.

        What a slap in the face to learn that while gay and
        lesbian partners of victims won't automatically
        receive equal benefits, the illegal aliens and fetuses
        of victims' partners will!  It is outrageous to
        believe that gay and lesbian persons are of lesser
        value to our society than are unborn fetuses and
        illegal aliens.  Those who believe in such injustice
        are completely prejudiced and homophobic.  It is an
        embarrassment that you represent the American people!

        Reconsider the decision to provide gay and lesbian
        survivors with unequal, disproportionate compensation.
         Certainly our relationships mean more than whatever
        percentage you have determined we deserve as compared
        to the rest of the victims' families.

        Individual Comment
        Bronx NY
