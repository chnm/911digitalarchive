        Compensation for partners of gay victims


        To whom it may concern:

        I read today that partners of gay people who were killed on September
        11, 2001 may not be eligible for victim compensation. I am outraged by
        this. How can anyone discriminate against one portion of the population
        like that? It is a dire insult to the heroes who died who were gay. How
        would you feel if you were discriminated against in this manner because
        even though you were a partner to a hero, you had brown hair or smoked
        cigarettes and somebody considered that sinful? This is a moral outrage.

        Do not allow this to be the policy governing our country's response to
        September 11. It is mean-spirited and bigoted. Gays are the last people
        to be included as human beings in our country.

        Thank you.


        Individual Comment
