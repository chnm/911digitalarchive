        Wednesday, November 28, 2001 6:10 PM
        Public Comment on Compensation Fund

        Letter to Department of Justice


        28 November, 2001

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice
        Main Building, Room 3140
        950 Pennsylvania Avenue NW
        Washington, D.C. 20530

        It is the duty of the Department of Justice to extend fair and humane
        recognition to all victims affected 11 September, with the inclusion of gay
        and lesbian partners and their biological or non-biological children.
        Individuals who can prove mutual interdependence with those whose lives
        were lost suffer equally with those who lost legal spouses, and deserve
        neither the distress nor exclusion with which they are faced.
        The Department of Justice would do well to imitate the state of New York in
        providing relief for families without concern for their definition.

        Respectfully Submitted,

        Individual Comment
        Oak Park, MI
