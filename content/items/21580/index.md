        Tuesday, March 19, 2002 1:03 PM
        Discrimination

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        It is absolutely pathetic that the 9/11 Victim's Compensation Fund slaps Gays
        and Lesbians in the face with such overt vengeance.  It is horrific that
        people lost their loved ones on 9/11 but then to have your government overtly
        discriminate against you based on your relationship is just as horrifying.
        Gays and Lesbians contributed to all the 9/11 Funds just like so many other
        people.  Our taxes support not only this fund but all the other innumerable
        government programs out there.  It is is reprehensible not to give us a fair
        shake by allowing Gay and Lesbian surviving partners to receive these funds.
        By relying on State law, this department knew that the pool of those eligible
        to receive benefits would be less than 1/10 of 1%, if that.  Yet the
        government was willing to overlook Federal immigration law by allowing
        survivors of illegal aliens AND their employers off the hook, looking the
        other way so that undocumented workers would be eligible to receive funds.  I
        think anyone who was in a committed relationship with someone that died that
        day should be eligible for the fund, regardless of their work status in this
        country or their sexual orientation.  But as an American, I would think other
        Gay and Lesbian Americans would be given the same consideration as foreign
        nationals.  I guess I shouldn't be surprised that this happened coming from
        Ashcroft's DOJ.  His level of hypocrisy is unparalleled.

        I would urge your department to review and amend this discriminatory ruling.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
        Denver, CO
