        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 11:48 AM
        HELP ENSURE COMPENSATION OF GAY AND LESBIAN VICTIMS' FAMILIES

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public
        comments regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing
        and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."
        I strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that ensure
        compensation is available to all victims of the attack,
        including the committed partners and the non-biological children
        of gay and lesbian victims.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic
        losses suffered by the families of gay and lesbian victims are
        no less than that of other families. To suggest otherwise is simply
        ridiculous. That anyone would even think to suggest otherwise
        makes my head spin. And the Christian fundamentalists accuse
        gay people of having an agenda? Then what do you call this attempt
        to deny surviving members of families of gay and lesbian people
        the same compensation received by familes of straight people?
        I call it out-and-out bigotry.

        Any legislation that is written to deny compensation to families of gays
        and lesbians killed by these attacks is as evil, vindictive, and heartless
        as the attacks themselves. I trust the Department of Justice will insure
        justice for EVERYONE, not just straight Christian people.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom
        left behind loved ones.  I urge the Department to honor these
        bonds by treating the families of gay and lesbian victims as you
        would any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Berkeley, CA
