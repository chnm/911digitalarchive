        To Whom It May Concern,

        The terrorist actions of September 11th have left an indelible mark on the
        American psyche.  How we react to these acts of hate and war will define
        our legacy for generations to come.  That said, I am appalled at our
        government's stance with regard to survivors of this tragedy.  While
        benefits are being extended to citizen, non-citizens, and even illegal
        aliens, gays and lesbians are effectively excluded from support.  We are
        NOT second-class citizens and there is evidence that were it not for one
        particularly BIG gay hero (
        ), another plane-bomb would have
        struck killing thousands more Americans.  In an age where it is
        unacceptable to make jokes dealing with the race, gender, or religious
        convictions of people, how can sexual orientation remain a valid
        target.  We are all human beings; I would have hoped that the events of
        9/11 would have convinced all Americans that all people deserve the same
        rights.  If not, then what are we fighting for anyway?  Only UNITED do we
        stand.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
