        Monday, March 18, 2002 12:45 AM
        Saddened and Disheartened

        Dear Sirs:

        I am overwhelmed by sorrow at hearing that surviving gay and lesbian partners
        of the horrific events of September 11th will be discriminated against in
        receiving financial compensation for their losses.

        How can you put a dollar amount on the death of loved ones?  Were their lives
        worth a dollar or thousands of dollars?  I am sure the survivors will agreed
        with me when I say that NO amount of money can fill the void that was so
        callously made by the terrorists.  As the dust settled in New York and the
        fires burned to embers at the Pentagon, hundreds of thousands of PROUD
        Americans gave  selflessly of their time, energy, and finances to those poor
        victims and their families..... families that INCLUDED gay and lesbian
        partners and their CHILDREN.  What makes them LESS OF AN AMERICAN than a
        heterosexual family unit?  Who decides to pick which of the hundreds of
        children robbed of family members receives relief and which are left to
        suffer without assistance from their country?    I would really like to know
        the names of those spineless people because there is NOTHING American about
        the pain and discriminate they will be delivering to those poor families and
        their defenseless children.  Haven't they suffered enough from these
        events...... Haven't we all?

        Shame on you, whoever you are.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
