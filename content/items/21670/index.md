        ----- Original Message -----
        From: "Comments, VictimComp"
        Subject: RE: Fundraising Efforts


        >
        > This is a  response to your e-mail of  November 7, in which you requested
        information regarding payments made by various charities in the aftermath of
        the September 11th attacks.
        >
        > Please be advised that the Department of Justice does not collect this
        information and is therefore unable to respond to your request.  The
        Department of Justice is in the process of drafting regulations for the
        implementation of the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001, which
        was established by Congress in the Air Transportation Safety and System
        Stabilization Act.  A substantial amount of general information regarding
        the Fund is included in the Notice of Inquiry and Advance Notice of
        Rulemaking and in the Press Release dated November 2, 2001, both of which
        are available on our website.
        >
        > If you have any additional questions, or if you require any additional
        information regarding the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001,
        please contact us.
        >
        > Website:
        > http://www.usdoj.gov/victimcompensation/
        >

        >
        > Dear Sirs,
        >      Re: Amounts collected for WTC victims and buildings
        >      The government has advised me that you are the appropriate agency to
        direct queiries to regarding the disposition of funds collected on behalf of
        the above.
        >       It occurs to me that the amounts collected far surpass the cost of
        rebuilding the WTC and of compensating each and every one of the victims
        appropriately.
        >       I wonder if I might inquire after a detailed itemization of the
        disposition of these funds with regards to whom received them.
        >
