        Thursday, March 14, 2002 10:24 AM
        Fund disbursement


        To whom it may concern at the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund:

        I am appalled at Kenneth Feinberg's statements on the Sunday, March 10
        broadcast of NBC's "Meet the Press" which indicate that gay and lesbian
        victims of the 9/11 attacks will not be treated equally with other victims
        of the attacks.

        The one thing that separates us from the terrorists and similar religious
        fundamentalists around the world is the belief that all humans, regardless
        of their differences, must be accorded the same rights as anyone else.

        I implore you, as has President Bush so often has done, not to "give in to
        the terrorists."  Please make your fund, and our government, something of
        which I, and every freedom-loving person, can be proud by including gay and
        lesbian victims, their partners and families, in just compensation received
        from your fund.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Carrboro, NC
