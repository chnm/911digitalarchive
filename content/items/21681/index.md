        Monday, March 18, 2002 10:17 AM
        Compensation

        I am concerned that we treat the loved ones of persons who are homosexual
        differently than those who are heterosexual.  The personal loss that these
        life companions have sustained is at least as great without also facing
        rejection from their government based solely on whom they love and share
        their lives with.

        Many corporations recognize the union of homosexual couples in their
        benefits.  For men and women who have given their lives - intentionally, as
        well as unintentionally - for their country, they and their families should
        receive the same compensation without regard to their orientation.

        Peace and Grace,

        Individual Comment
        Tulsa, OK
