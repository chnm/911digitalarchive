        November 29, 2001

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice
        Main Building, Room 3140
        950 Pennsylvania Avenue NW
        Washington, D.C. 20530

        Re: Comments on the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        As the Sept. 11th fund decision has been opened for public comment, I
        wish to add my own thoughts to the long list of statements I am certain
        you have already received.

        I hope you elect to distribute compensation to all parties affected by the
        Sept. 11th tragedy, and not just to the population who fits into the currently
        narrow definition of marriage. The idea of possibly discriminating against
        non-traditional families and partnerships will no little more than add insult
        to injury to many of those involved.

        We have entered a new era in U.S. history where there is a call for unity
        and collectiveness.  I ask, "what message is our government sending
        should it affords compensation only to legally married couples?" In order
        to stand as one, we must be treated as one. This is why I request you elect
        to extend compensation to unmarried couples, gays, lesbians,
        transgendered individuals, and their families.

        Thank you for your consideration.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Mesa, AZ
