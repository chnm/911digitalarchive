      text: |-
        Wednesday, December 26, 2001 10:29 AM


        To Whom it May Concern,

        After seeing some of the guidelines I am extremely disheartened and angered.
        The pain and suffering of the past few months is immeasurable in a dollar
        amount.  If it was however it would far exeed $100-500,000.  Let's be
        realistic.  We,the families, went through weeks of not knowing if our loved
        ones were alive, we are left to imagine the way in which they died, and we
        have been forced to make many exhausting trips into the city.  On top of that
        we have bee bombarded with the recognition of NYPD and FDNY.  Everyone died
        doing their job.  It is wrong to acknowledge people by occupation.  A BADGE
        DOES NOT MAKE A HERO. And the statue that will be erected with the firemen
        putting the American flag on the antenna of the towers should not represent
        the firemen who died. IT IS THE AMERICAN FLAG, IT IS WHERE MY FATHER DIED, IT
        SHOULD REPRESENT EVERYONE.
         Also, this is AMerica, land of opportunity.  Who is to say that one of the
        waiters, for example, working in Windows of the World was not saving money to
        go to college to be a doctor.  Congress passing laws granting FDNY NYPD
        families $250,000 along with the biased Mayor Guiliani's Twin Tower fund is
        utterly disgusting.
        I think that the mayor is WRONG and DOING A TERRIBLE JOB WITH ASSISTING THE
        FAMILIES OF ALL VICTIMS OF 9-11.
        Something needs to be done, because right now I am not proud to be an
        American.

        I would appreciate a letter in return to my comments.  I have made several
        attempts to contact the president and vice president and all I get is a
        response to donate money to the children of Afghanistan.  FORGET ABOUT THEM,
        LET'S CONCENTRATE ON OUR COUNTRY.

        Respectfully yours,

        son of the HERO (a carpenter)
        Individual Comment
