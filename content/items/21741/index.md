        Monday, January 14, 2002 1:51 AM
        Comment on Interim Final Rule

        January 13, 2002

        Dear Director Zwick,

        As a brother of a victim who was murdered in the World Trade Center, lets
        talk about what the special nature of this ?fund? should be. The fund should
        be set up because people like my brother were going about their business in
        the WTC and were murdered largely because the US government were
        unconcerned, uncommitted and about three months too late in protecting them.

        Furthermore, the fund is being set up because the US government didn?t act
        when the same terrorists attacked the same WTC tower in 1993 (didn?t the
        severity of this attack give you any hints, massive bomb, luckily few
        deaths). What about when American embassies were attacked in Africa, and
        American military interests were attacked (i.e. the Cole) in Yemen?

        If my brother, had died under different circumstances, he would have
        received his life insurance and other regular benefits, and that would be
        the end of it. But he didn?t die that way. He died a ?wrongful death? and
        government organizations (i.e. like the FAA) and companies the government
        wants to protect (i.e. the airlines) are culpable.

        So to summarize, the reason that the federal fund has been set up, is to
        compensate our family for this ?wrongful death?. The fund is there so that
        families like my own do not sue the various agencies, governments, companies
        and individuals responsible for their deaths.

        So why is the government offsetting benefits like life insurance (i.e. the
        collateral compensation) from the victims compensation payout? After all my
        family would have received this life insurance had my brother died a ?normal
        death?. Don?t let people like my brother - who paid life insurance premiums
        and who died because of significant negligence - bail you out. Instead
        determine an amount that we deserve for the ?wrongful death? of a 34-year
        old of significant worth, and don?t offset this by anything.


        Yours truly,


        Individual Comment
        Phoenix, AZ
