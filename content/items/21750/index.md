        Tuesday, March 19, 2002 7:36 AM
        Compensation for Gay Survivors


        It has recently been brought to my attention that disbursement of compensation
        from the 9/11 Fund may not be made to the surviving partners of gay persons.
        I further read that part of the rationale for this non-action is that the Federal Fund
        must adhere to the laws of individual states.

        While I do not advocate disregard for laws, it seems to me that Federal officials
        must demonstrate the same level of heroism and risk-taking as did those who died
        in the terror attacks.

        Please rethink the Commission's stand regarding payments to gay partners and
        treat all Americans with fairness and respect.

        Individual Comment
        East Setauket, NY
