        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 8:01 AM
        Aid To Victims

        Greetings,

        The quarreling about fund distribution must come to a speedy halt.  Those of
        us who have contributed...those of us who have grieved...do not want the fund
        or charities to conflict. We do not want our government to trifle with our
        tax dollars regarding this issue.  The fund should be distributed without
        regard to charitable donations received by a victims loved ones.  The rules
        regarding life insurance and pensions restrict the fund enough.  Please get
        on with it.

        I support the creation of a "fund master" (who has a history of fairness and
        compassion) who will interpret and enforce the fund's rules and decide final
        awards...without unnecessary time consuming appeals. Kenneth R. Feinberg
        would be a good choice.

        Thanks for hearing our plea.

        Kind Regards,

        Individual Comment
