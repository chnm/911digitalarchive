        March 17, 2002

        To whom it may concern;

        I find it appalling that you have chosen to exclude partners of gay and
        lesbian victims of the terrorist acts of September 11, 2001, from
        compensation under your current plan.

        Every life lost on that day is a life given in a war against the very
        things the United States was founded on; freedom, equality and JUSTICE. The
        fact that most parts of this country have denied basic freedoms to lesbian
        and gay families should not be held against the survivors who have lost so
        much.

        How can you justify the current plan of inclusion of all (even those in
        this country illegally) but deny the partners of the gay and lesbian men
        and women who died on that day? Their families are based upon the concept
        of Family of Choice, CHOICE being a  fundamental freedom guaranteed by the
        Constitution of the United States of America. UNITED being the key word.
        Today we must act and stand as UNITED citizens of this, the greatest
        country in the world, not divided by bigotry, religious fundamentalism or
        intolerance.



        Individual Comment
        New York
