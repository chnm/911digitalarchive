        Friday, November 23, 2001 10:59 AM

        Regulations re: Public Law 107-42



        Dear Sir/Madam:



        I am a solo practitioner in New York City that specializes in personal injury
        litigation.

        In order to ensure that the victims of the 9/11 tragedy and their  families
        receive fair and just compensation,and to the fullest extent  consisitent
        with the statute, I urge the adoption of regulations that  will judge each
        case on an individual basis. I have read with dismay that there is a push to
        have the victims' damages judged according to a chart or matrix.Having
        specialized in this area for over 20 years, I know that no  2
        cases are alike and that even when claimants suffer the same injury, so many
        other variables dictate a different result. This is especially so in the case
        of the victims who
        perished on 9/11.


        I am also a proud member of both the New York State Trial Lawyers Association
        and The Association of Trial Lawyers of America and signed up for their
        project in which
        I will be representing claimants pro bono. Please allow me and my fellow
        volunteer
        attorneys to render our expertise to the victims of the tragedy so that they
        receive the just compensation they are due. Thank you.



        Very truly yours,



        Individual Comment

        Kew Gardens, NY
