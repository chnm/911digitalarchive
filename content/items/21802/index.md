        Compensation Act

        Hello,
             I'd like to voice my opinion on this and say that I do NOT agree with
        this act at all. It is not the taxpayers duty to provide compensation when
        there's nobody left to sue. Now I respect the fact that the Airlines will go
        under if they had to provide the $$$ for these families and the market can't
        take them going out of business. But it is not right to take the taxpayers
        money and award it to the families of this horrible disaster. With the
        potential of 1.6 million dollars as an average, I feel it is just way over
        the top.
             I've been working for 16 years and I haven't even earned $80,000 yet.
        Now i'm married and am currently furthering my education with a scholarship
        i won in a competition. My wife is the only one with an income and believe
        me when i say that we watch every dime closley. We simply cannot afford
        this. Especially when they say that this is only the beginning. Will we
        continue to make victims millionaire's over night? And how about the victims
        at the Oklahoma bombing or the Embassies or even the father that get's shot
        down in Compton? What of them and their's? Will we always be liable? Me?
            I believe that there are other options available to compensate them
        with. The siezed assets of all of theterrorist organizations would be one
        way. Where's all of that money? How about all the charities? Where's all of
        that money? How about the airlines insurance companies because after all it
        is their fault for allowing the terrorists to get through their security
        gates? Where's that money?
            Let's face it. There is nothing in the constitution that guarantees that
        as american citizens and travelers to America, that they will be protected
        and secure against harm. And it most certainly does not read that in the
        case of such a disaster that the American citizens will make them
        millionaires. I can't believe the people we elect to come up with this crap
        and spoon feed it to us like we're a bunch of 2 year olds.
         We need to hold the countries and the financial orginazitions who harbored
        and supported
         the terrorist organisations responsible and make them compensate the
        families for this horrible situation. Just like Germany is doing for what
        happened there, and that was almost 60 years ago.
              Sincerley,
                        Broke!!!!
