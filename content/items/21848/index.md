        To whom it may concern:

        I am just writing this to go on record and express my outrage that gay

        automatically going to receive the same benefits as heterosexual
        spouses from the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund.  I understand
        that illegal aliens will be receiving the benefits.  In fact, I hear
        that federal law will be overlooked when it comes to illegal aliens and
        the companies that hire them in violation of the law.  How bizarre is
        that?!  How can the fund be so blatantly discriminatory?  This
        dishonors such gay "heroes" as &nbsp , who all died in the tragedy.
         If September 11 has taught us anything, it's that our patriotism and
        love of country transcend our differences and unite us all.  Enough is
        enough already.  Please stop the discrimination.



        Individual Comment
        Somerset, NJ
