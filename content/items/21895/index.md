        Compensations for 9/11 Victims

        Sir,
        It is unconscienable and beyond belief that your office and its regulations
        would deny compensation to longtime companions of loving gay and/or lesbian
        individuals otherwise deserving who perished on 9/11.  They should be treated
        with the same consideration, respect, and compensation as anyone else.  In
        fact, it is  simply not acceptable, and I will surely vote my conscience in
        this matter in every election possible.  Further, I will speak out against
        you -- and any administration which supports such unthinkable and grossly
        unfair behavior.  If you act from love and real human concerns rather than
        from fear of fundamentalist religious and political bigots, you will provide
        compensation where it is surely due.
        Further, I am urging all my friends and colleagues with extensive mailing
        lists to let you know of their feelings on this important matter.

        Sincerely,
