        December 4, 2001

        To: Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
         Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
         U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public comments regarding the
        forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund." I whole heartedly urge the Department to draft regulations
        ensuring compensation is made available to ALL victims of the attack, including gay and
        lesbian partners and all of their dependent children.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind loved ones.
        I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the families of gay and lesbian
        victims as you would any other.

        Sincerely;
        Individual Comment
        Wimberley, Texas
