        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 9:32 AM
        Compensation for victims


            Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
            Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
            U.S. Department of Justice

          Dear Mr. Zwick:

                I am writing in response to your department's request for
        comments for compensation to the victims and their families of the
        attacks of September 11th.  I would hope that the regulations for
        compensation do not include any exclusions based upon the sexual
        orientation of the individuals or their families.  I would hope that the
        regulations would stress need-based assistance, covering both natural and
        adopted children.

                The devastation endured by these families should not be magnified
        by some exclusionary government policy as the terrorists were
        nondiscriminatory in their attack and destruction of lives and futures.
        Most of the victims of the 11th had families that are in need of support
        now and they need to know that their government will do just that.  I
        urge you to create a just and equitable set of regulations that will
        allow these families to continue their lives without feeling
        discrimination.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
                        Columbia, Maryland
