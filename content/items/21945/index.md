        Attention:  Ken Zwick
                        Civil Division, USDOJ

        Dear Ken:

        I have read that gay and lebian partners of gay victims of the 911 tragedies
        will not automatically receive the same compensation as heterosexual spouses
        of victims from the Victims Compensation Fund created by Congress.  I am very
        distrubed by this.  After 911 there was great talk about there being no
        "hyphenated" Americans, e.g., Italian-Americans, Afro-Americans, etc.  We
        were all only Americans.  This was a rallying cry for uniting the country.
        In six months time, we have forgotten this rallying cry and are now
        discriminating against the surviving families of Gay-Americans who died in
        either the World Trade Center, Pentagon, or Pennsylvania tragedies by denying
        or threatening to deny them the same compensation as heterosexual suviving
        family members.  Many of these gay victims are acknowledged heroes such as
        &nbsp , &nbsp , and &nbsp .  Enough is enough.  The
        U.S. Government needs to do the right thing and compensate the survivors of
        all vicitims in a non-discrimantory manner.  This is the American Way.

                                                                 Sincerely yours,
