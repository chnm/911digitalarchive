        To Whom It May Concern:
        As a concerned U.S. citizen, I urge, with the strongest possible feeling, the
        United States Department of Justice to please include gays, lesbians,
        bisexuals, and transgenders in the qualified categories to receive assistance
        after the September 11th tragedy.  This is no time to make moral or political
        judgements on the righteousness of any particular way of living; regardless
        of whether one is gay or straight, if an individual lost someone in this
        disaster, they should be fairly and fully compensated, just like any other
        family member.  Please - as an American, a government employee, and a gay
        person, I urge you to include all affected Americans who need assistance in
        the relief fund.  Please do not further discrimination in this sad and tragic
        time when there is enough hate in the world already.  Thank you.

        Individual Comment
        Fullerton, CA
