            Just to place my remarks into some sort of perspective, I am a 62
        year-old heterosexual with a wife of 38 plus years, two daughters and a
        grandson.
            Upon reading of a conversation on one of Sunday's political talk shows, I
        became incensed.  A "gentleman" who works under the direction of the
        misanthropic U.S. Attorney General informed the moderator and the public that
        significant others and dependants of gay or lesbian 9-11 victims would
        recieve no help from the victims fund if the state involved (assuming the
        victim's home state) did not recognize same sex unions.
        That is disgusting!  Carrying Ashcroft's despicable discrimination into the
        dissemination of charitable funds points out just how un-Christlike this
        digusting self-styled musician/preacher has become.  It also underlines not
        only the total stupidity of the man who nominated Ashcroft to begin with, but
        how moronically sheep-like the Congress was to have confirmed the nomination.
            9-11 and its heroes notwithstanding, this administration has, once again,
        made me ashamed of being an American.

        Individual Comment
        Bristol, CT
