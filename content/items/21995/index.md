        Monday, February 11, 2002 12:30 PM
        Compensation greed

        I think the compensation of victims by taxpayers was a mistake from
        the beginning, but the greed that is being shown today by some
        victims is atrocious.

        Many people are hurt and suffer every day for no fault of their own.
        The taxpayer is not expected to compensate them, not should they.

        I think help for the victims of this attack should have been handled
        under existing law and precedent, with no special treatment.

        I am especially opposed to the idea that I, as a middle class
        taxpayer, should be expected to subsidize the lives of people who
        made far more than I do, and that the rich are being subsidized to a
        greater extent than the poor.Individual
        Comment
        Saint Paul, Minnesota
