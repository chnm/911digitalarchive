        Please Do Not Support Equal Access To Same ### Partners


        December  28, 2001

        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        As you may know, Amnesty International
        has recently issued a letter in support of same ### partnerships receiving
        the equal access to benefits for the September 11 Victims Relief Fund. I
        as a member of Amnesty International want to ask that you DO NOT SUPPORT
        equal access to the relief fund, giving same ### partnerships the benifits
        that would be granted to those who were married.

        Amnesty has called it an "arbitrary discrimination" to not grant rights to
        same ### partnerships. As a member, I am personally disappointed in
        Amnesty for trying to pursue an agenda under the words "human rights."
        They in themselves are pushing the same ### agenda, without any
        justification for it being a basic human right.  It is discriminating
        against the institution of marriage, and makes it useless.  By their action
        they are trying to redefine human rights by pushing an agenda that is not
        so fixed or assumed.  Please honor only those who have the legitimate right
        of marriage to be seen as a legal marriage partnership, please do not
        support Amnesty's push to redefine marriage.  These are rights that would
        not be considered for opposite ### partnerships that are outside of
        marriage, and so it is not discrimination to deny those who are of same ###
        partnerships.

        Thank you for considering my concerns. I look forward to hearing from you
        about this critical issue.

        With much Thanks

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment

        Las Vegas, NV
