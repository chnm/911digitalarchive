        Charitable contributions to victims funds

        I strongly object to Congress reducing award amounts less the charitable
        contributions received.  The victims of the 9/11 disasters are the remaining
        families left behind.
        How can our government even contemplate making these victims suffer even
        more grief at a time like this.
        I for one did not  contribute to those funds with the anticipation that the
        US government would seek to use those contributions as a resource.
        Those of us that gave, did so out of an act of humanity to our brothers and
        sisters.
         Please do not make a mockery of that by making these victims suffer more by
        having to beg for what is rightfully theirs.
        We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence then, is not an act, but a habit.
