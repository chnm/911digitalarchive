        Sunday, February 10, 2002 11:51 PM
        9.11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Hello,

        As an American citizen, I was very unsure exactly what to think about the
        compensation funds for the family of victims.  I had originally thought the
        compensation packages were fair.  However, I have changed my mind.  These
        families faced great turmoil because of these horrible terrorist attacks that
        could have been prevented.  The United States must admit that some people
        were at fault.  The security at the airports, the CIA, and many others should
        have been able to prevent this tradegy.  We owe this to the victims for their
        mistakes.  As a country at war, we should not be fighting over money.  How
        can anyone say how much a human life is worth?  A human life is priceless,
        you can never make up for their departure, no matter how much money may be
        offered.  But we must remember the thousands of children, wives, fiances,
        etc. who no longer have the of the financial support of their loved ones.
        The U.S. government should not deduct the money that people saved responsibly
        in pension plans etc.  In addition, the government should remember to include
        same sex partners and fiances in their aid packages.  The government should
        also remember to include uncles, grandparents, etc that may have raised
        victims, rather than their parents.  So please, just remember the pain and
        suffering of all the victims and their families, and do the right thing.

        Individual Comment
        Philadelphia PA
