        Victims Compensation Fund

        I don't believe awards should be determined by set dollar amounts.  Every
        case is different and should be looked into as the individual life it
        represents.  In my case, I lost my husband on September 11 and we were 5
        days shy of celebrating our 3rd month anniversary.  We recently were
        married on June 16th 2001.  We were married such a small amount of time
        that we had no children, it was our first marriage for both of us.  So
        there are no children involved but I am only 28 yrs old.  We wanted
        children and were already trying, I don't see that my case should be looked
        as a spouse and no children, so we'll give her this amount.  No, all the
        circumstances have to be looked and examined.  We were married for less
        than 3 months, we were both young, we were just getting started and we had
        many dreams that were cut short because of our government fell asleep.
        Every case has its own unique twist and as such they should be looked at
        very closely and individually.

        Don't make this a numbers game, these were innocent men and women who
        deserve much more respect and honor, as well as their families!

        Individual Comment
