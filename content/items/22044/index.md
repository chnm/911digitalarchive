        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 2:53 PM
        Victims Compensation Fund

        I am very confused about the purpose of this fund.  Is this in addition
        to the millions of dollars this country has donated to the families
        (September 11 Fund, etc)?  My heart aches for those who have lost loved
        ones and those whose livelihoods have been impacted by this tragedy.  I
        have contributed privately to those funds already established but I
        don't know that I understand or agree with why the government is also
        going to be compensating the same set of victims.  I think it is
        necessary to know how much each family has or will receive from other
        sources (other funds, Red Cross, life insurance, etc) before any public
        money is given.  I'm still stuck on the point of why  government monies
        are going to these victims when the private donations have been so
        generous.  We need to be careful with this.  Thousands of our innocent
        neighbors were killed on September 11 but thousands of our neighbors
        are being impacted everyday by job losses, etc.  Are they to be
        compensated, too?

        If this program does come to fruition, the following things need to be
        considered:

        What financial resources does that family/victim already have?

        Where else have they applied for assistance?

        A cap needs to be established so the distribution of money is equitable
        and, therefore, providing enough to go around.

        Lawyer fees are not compensable!

        Money goes to the family for direct needs:  food, clothing, shelter,
        education (within reason)


        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
