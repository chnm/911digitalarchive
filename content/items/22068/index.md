        Tuesday, February 05, 2002 10:51 PM
        VCF meeting

        Attachment 1

        I was invited to speak to a CPA seminar group in NYC today regarding the VCF.  During
        audience questions, one CPA indicated that he attended the Feinberg meeting about a
        week ago in Bergen County, NJ.  Feinberg appeared to agree that household service
        losses were being shortchanged and said he was amenable to considering such loss
        claims.  He also said that he thought the announced upper income cap did not do
        justice to some highly compensated victims and said he would not limit claims to these
        caps.

        Both of these statements, if true, would mean a significant shift in the plan's
        implementation. Still, claimants would give up their right to sue when following the VCF
        route but would not know of the ultimate award decision made by Feinberg or his staff.
        It would be very helpful and reassuring if the things he has said at that meeting and
        elsewhere were incorporated into the final rules soon to be released.

        Individual Comment
        Livingston, NJ
