        Monday, March 18, 2002 7:21 PM
        Be Fair to All Families



        I have recently learned that the Victims Compensation Fund will recognize
        families of all types, as long as those families are recognized under state law.
        I happen to be fortunate enough to live in California, where legislation now
        recognizes gay and lesbian "domestic partnerships."  Although this law does not
        create the same legal status that is enjoyed by all married heterosexual
        couples, it does create rights with respect to actions for wrongful death suits
        and presumably would allow the families of gay and lesbian victims who were
        residing in California to collect money from the funds.  Those victims who
        happen to live in states with less tolerant lawmakers deserve the same respect.
        Like their married heterosexual counterparts, they were living in committed,
        loving relationships and suffered a great loss in the tragic events of 9/11.
        Don't punish families from those states with less tolerant lawmakers by not
        recognizing their legitimate family relationships.



        Individual Comment
        Los Angeles, CA
