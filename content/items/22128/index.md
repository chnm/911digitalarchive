        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for comments
        about the pending regulations for implementing and administering
        the 9/11 victim compensation fund. I urge that compensation be
        available to all victims, including the committed partners and non-
        biological children of gay and lesbian people.

        the losses of gay and lesbian families are no less decastating or
        important then the losses suffered by other American families. It is
        more imperative than ever that we act as a democracy in esuring
        fairness for all our citizens.

        i urge the Department of Justice to act in a just manner by treating
        the families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        East Hartford, CT
