        FOR THE MEMORY OF:
        And so many more...

        I am sending this email because I want to voice my opinion about the appauling way the compensation funds are being handled for the vitcims of September 11th.

        Even though I am not American I feel it is my duty as a human being to speak out for the families and partners of the gay and lesbian victims.

        These people are, in most cases, not receiving any compensation. I think this is wrong, why should they be treated any different to married couples. Just because law says they couldn't get married doesn't mean they were in any less of a serious realtionship. The compensation policy is completly unfair, for gods sake, even the families of illegal immigrants were getting compensated, they were in this country illegaly, where in american law does it say that its illegal to be gay?

        It doesn't.

        I know my email on its own will not do anything to change the policy, but I only hope that you will bear this in mind, and let it be on your concience. At least I have done my bit to try.

        Once again, I felt it my duty as a human to write this, gays and lesbian are no less human than anyone else, please remember that.

        regards

        Individual Comment
         (Bradford, UK)
