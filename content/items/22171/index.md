        Victim inclusion


        Kenneth L. Zwick
        Director Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing to add my voice to those that support an inclusive definition of families in the Department of Justice's upcoming determination of those eligible for September 11th relief funds.  This administration has stood in support of families and, as such, should aid the families of gay and lesbian victims of this tragedy equally.  To do otherwise would be morally inexcusable, creating additional distress for Americans who are already traumatized by these events.  I believe this administration should stand for all victims equally.

        Cordially,

        Individual Comment
        New York, NY
