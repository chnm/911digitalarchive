        Sunday, March 17, 2002 9:47 PM
        Compensation for g/l partners

        As a Christian and an American,  I fully support offering the same (equal)
        benefits to same-sex partners and families of gay and lesbian victims of
        Sept 11 as those offered to heterosexual victims.  I am an associate member
        of a congregation that recognizes and affirms same-gender committed
        relationships, one of the more than one thousand American congregations
        that publicly affirms g/l committed relationships.  Further, as an
        elementary school teacher in these United States of America,   I've proudly
        led the pledge to the American flag each school day for more than 30 years.
        Let us not as a nation betray its promise,  "with liberty and  justice for
        all". Not justice for "straight" Americans...but justice for all!  It's the
        American way of justice to not discriminate against gay and lesbian Sept 11
        victims and their loved ones.  I believe it's also a matter of following
        God's law: Love your neighbor as yourself; do unto others as you would have
        them to do you.  I urge that the Victim's Fund not be used in such a way so
        as to discriminate against the same-gender partners and families of gay and
        lesbian victims of September 11.
        Thank-you.



        Individual Comment
        Wilson, NC
