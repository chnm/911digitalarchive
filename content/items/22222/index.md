        Monday, March 11, 2002 3:03 PM
        Compensation for Gay and Lesbian Victims' Families


        I am horrified to learn of Kenneth Feinberg's statement on NBC's "Meet
        the Press" (March 10) that the families of gay and lesbian victims of
        September 11 will not be compensated if their state does not recognise
        the relationships.

        This is a cowardly act of dispossession.  I deplore it.  State law
        aside, these people must receive full compensation from the Depart of
        Justice fund.  These lesbian and gay people are not lesser citizens than
        the heterosexual victims.

        America must recognize all of those who fell in this act of war.  Not
        just some.  To discriminate against lesbian and gay victims of September
        11 is an obscenity.

        Individual Comment
