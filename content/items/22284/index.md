        Tuesday, January 08, 2002 7:45 PM





        I do not agree with the victims' compensation law and I believe most other
        Americans don't either.  I am very sad about what happened on September
        11th, but I didn't cause it and I should not be paying for it.  No one in
        the United States is responsible for this act.  Who decided that it was
        right to pay out all this money to these victims?  This is not their money
        to give away.  Americans who want to donate have, through their charities
        and THAT IS ENOUGH!

        Where does this stop?  Will we pay out a million dollars everytime someone
        dies in a catastrophy?  My father was in the Army for 30 years and fought in
        countless campaigns, would I have gotten a million dollars if a terrorist
        killed him?  I couldn't even get adaquate military health care when he died
        of cancer.

        This law should be reconsidered before a single penny is given out.  I think
        that if you ask the American public how they feel about it, they would be
        against it.  The only reason we haven't said anything about it so far is
        that we didn't know what was being voted on.  Please tell me how I can get
        the word out to get this law changed.



        Individual Comment
