        Dear Sirs.

        As many American do,  I feel very sorry and sad about the Sept 11

        However I also believe that the government program to give millions to
        the families of the victims is a terrible policy and not appropriate.
        (min of $500,000 and max about $4,300,000--TAX FREE)

        Almost 3000 people lost their lives in the WTC that terrible day.
        However almost 6000 lose their lives every day in the USA.

        What the government give away program shows is how greedy some people
        can become.   No matter what you do and no matter how much Congress
        decides to give,  some people will complain and argue for more.   What
        about all the other people who die in the USA  every day and they
        receive no added compensation from the government.   And, as a taxpayer
        I feel this is a very inappropriate excess of payment.

        yours truly
        Individual Comment
