        Monday, January 21, 2002 5:36 PM

        Victims Compensation Fund

        I believe that the figures on this chart do not accurately reflect people who worked in the financial industry.  My husband was 49. He did not receive a 5 or even a 4% (after 51) raise each year.  He was what one would call a "late bloomer".  He was increasing his salary the last three years by 75%.  How can I possibly accept an award based on 5%.
        Also, the figures are based on "line of duty".  My husband did not sign up for "line of duty".  He went to work to support his family.  The Government did not protect him or anyone else who was in those buildings on 9/11.
        My children also deserve much more than $50,000 for their pain and suffering and so do I.  I have lost my best friend, my supporter, my lover, my life.  I have nightmares since 9/11.  I have to stop my mind from thinking about the pain and suffering my husband went through that day.  THAT IS CERTAINLY WORTH MORE THAN $250,000.
        Please reconsider these amounts.  I would give up every dime if it could be September 10, 2001 again.  I am sure all the victims would give up any amount of money to have their loved ones back.  BUT WE CANT.  Please do not victimize us twice.

        Individual Comment
