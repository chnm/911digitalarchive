        November 28, 2001

        Dear Director Zwick:

        I would like to ask that rules developed by the Department of Justice
        regarding the victims' compensation fund include gay/lesbian life partners
        and children of those partners.  In many cases, long-term committed
        partners are not able to jointly adopt children they are raising, and so a
        child may be, from a legal standpoint, considered a stranger to the parent
        they have lost.  And of course, gay and lesbian couples are not recognized
        by federal law in most cases.

        Thank you for your consideration of these issues.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Madison, WI
