        Victims Compensation


        I thought we gave millions to the various charities to compensate the victims of 9/11.  I just don't understand why the taxpayers are now giving them more.

        This sets a dangerous precedence.  The 9/ll victims are getting vast amounts of compensation and other victims of terrorists attacks received little or nothing.  Now all of them will be suing the government.  Also, we do not know what other victims there might be in the future and surely the government cannot afford to compensate them in a similar fashion.

        The victims of 9/11 are becoming multi-millionaires.  I pray there aren't any victims in the future because they will suffer more.  People won't be as willing to give to the various charities...where did that money go???  And the government won't have the money to be as generous as they were this time.

        Thanks for listening.    If you can still vote " No," please do.

        Individual Comment
