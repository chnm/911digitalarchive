        Republican voter requests equal treatment for gay and lesbian
        families


        December  19, 2001

        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        I write as a Republican voter, gay man and member of Amnesty International.
         I am fully in support of equal access to benefits under the September 11
        Victims Relief Fund for all victims, regardless of sexual orientation or
        marital status.

        Many lesbian and gay people were killed in the September 11th attacks.
        Since then, lesbian and gay surviving partners of those killed have had
        mixed results in obtaining equal treatment at the local level in the
        distribution of relief funds by private and state agencies.  I expect that
        your work will redress this grave injustice.

        Thank you for considering my concerns. It is long past time that gay and
        lesbian families received equal treatment under the law and by the
        Republican party.


        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Portland, OR
