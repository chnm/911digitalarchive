           September 11th compensation fund,

                I am outraged at the fact that gay and lesbian
        couples are not being compensated the same as
        heterosexual couples. What happened to all the love
        and support that was expressed after the tragedy. What
        is it going to take for people to realize that
        everyone is the same inside.  We all come in different
        forms, but we are the same inside.  How come it is
        okay that illegal aliens are being treated the same,
        but not gays and lesbians.  I was brought up to do the
        right thing and treat everyone as I wish to be
        treated. We have to stop being prejudice of one
        another and start loving eachother.

                Thank you for taking the time to read my
        thoughts on this matter.  I just hope that whomever is
        reading this letter will take the time to get to know
        the person inside and not judge someone for what he or
        she looks or acts like.  You may just make a new
        friend. Thank you.
