        Benefits for gays and lesbians


        March 11, 2002


        Victims Compensation Fund
        U. S. Department of Justice
        Washington, DC

        Ladies and gentlemen:

        It is unconscionable that the survivors of gays and lesbians, for the most
        part, will be denied benefits which will be awarded to other survivors of
        those who were killed in the terrorist attacks of September 11, 2001.

               the New York City Fire Department;        , one of the heroes of Flight 63 which crashed in Pennsylvania instead of into the U. S. Capitol;  and the        of the airliner which
        crashed into the Pentagon are only three of the scores of gay and lesbian
        heroes of September 11th.  To deny their loved ones the benefits which will
        be accorded routinely to others is an outrage.

        In the strongest possible terms, I urge reconsideration and repeal of this
        discriminatory practice.  Gays and lesbians who died in the terrorist
        attacks of September 11, 2001, must be treated equally with all others who
        lost their lives on that day.  If they are not, then the promise of America
        for "equal justice under law" died with them.


        Individual Comment
        El Paso, Texas
