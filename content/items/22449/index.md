        9/11/01 Victim Compensation Fund

        I am e-mailing today in response to the Department of Justice's request
        for public comments regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing
        and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."  I urge the
        Department to draft regulations that will ensure compensation is available
        to all victims of the attack, including the committed partners and the
        non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind
        loved ones.  I urge the Department of Justice to honor these bonds by
        treating the families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any other.

        Individual Comment
        Bellingham, WA
