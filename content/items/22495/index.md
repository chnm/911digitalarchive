        Thursday, December 06, 2001 5:47 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

             Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
             Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
             U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department of
        Justice's request for public comments regarding
        forthcoming regulations for implementing and
        administering the "September 11th Victim
        Compensation Fund".  I strongly urge the
        Department of Justice to draft regulations
        ensuring compensation is available to ALL victims
        of the attack, including committed partners and
        non-biological children of gay and lesbian
        victims.

        The devastating grief and economic losses
        suffered by families of gay and lesbian victims
        were no less than that of other families.  The
        terrorists drew no lines in choosing their
        victims, and the U.S. should draw no lines in
        compensating those who suffered the most personal
        of losses from this incident.

        All Americans who lost their lives on September
        11th left behind loved ones.  I urge the
        Department of Justice to honor these bonds by
        treating the families of gay and lesbian victims
        as you would any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        New Britain, CT
