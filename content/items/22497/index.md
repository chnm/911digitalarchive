        As per the solicitation of the Justice Department for public input on the
        rulemaking decisions for compensation to families of victims of September
        11, 2001, I am writing to express my opinion on the adoption of rules that
        are inclusive of gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgendered people and their
        families who suffered from the attacks.

        I am in complete support of laws that include compensation for the above
        mentioned groups as survivors of the victims.  Those who actually suffered
        losses as a result of the death of a loved one should share the right to
        compensation. I feel that this is the only humane response to what has
        happened.

        Thank you,

        Individual Comment
