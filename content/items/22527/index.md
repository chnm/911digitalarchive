        Wednesday, November 07, 2001 5:26 PM
        Comments to the DOJ

        I am a resident of Battery Park City.  I live in Gateway Plaza which has
        been described as "Ground Zero minus One."  I moved home recently after 6
        weeks of displacement.  During those six weeks, I lived in 5 different
        places.  The displacement from my home along with the trauma associated with
        watching the events of 9/11 as it related to the WTC from my living room
        window, has negatively affected me both on a personal and professional
        level.  This comment is directed more to the professional affects of 9/11.
        As a direct result of the attacks, displacement/homelessness, increased
        travel time, leaving work early to attend several meetings to determine the
        future of my living conditions and countless hours with various relief and
        charitable agencies, my work performance suffered dramatically.  That is, as
        a lawyer with a yearly billable hour requirement, I will be unable to meet
        that requirement this year.  The real result of that failure is immediate.
        I will not be considered for a raise.  The other result is failing to meet
        my billable hour requirement will not help my quest for partnership.  Thus,
        all the extra expenses that I have incurred, to date, as a result of 9/11
        will come out of my pocket as well as the HEPA filters that I plan to
        purchase to combat the almost daily headaches and chest pain that I now
        suffer from and possibly moving expenses are costs that I will continue to
        bear..

        Individual Comment
        Battery Park City, New York
