        Victim's compensation


        I hope that what I hear about your intent not to compensate the partners of

        compensate who they want in any way they want. Odd, isn't it, that more and

        aliens who died, as I believe you intend to, so what makes gay and lesbian
        deaths so much less important? I suppose red-faced rhetoric, which is where I
        am headed, has no place here, so I will conclude by requesting that you
        revisit this issue using some kind of common sense and decency. Sincerely, Individual Comment
