        Wednesday, November 28, 2001 4:35 PM

        Dear Director Zwick,

        Although I would write a formal letter, since time is of the essence I am
        choosing to send an email.
        I am aware the Deparment of Justice is inviting public comment regarding
        the rules governing the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund. I urge the
        Department to be inclusive of gay, lesbian, bisexual, and  transgendered
        people and their families who suffered from the attacks.

        As many individuals in the Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual and Transgender community
        have eloquently addressed -- the attack on September 11th did not
        discriminate according to sexual orientation. The sexual orientation of the
        American Airlines pilot, the man who helped foil the last hijacking, or the
        countless others killed -- did not matter during those moments and their
        aftermath. However, it does matter now because those who lost their life
        partners or de facto parents or children might be further victimized by
        current legal discrimination based on sexual orientation (e.g., marriage,
        adoption).

        I strongly urge you to be inclusive of all victims of September 11th and
        their families - and equitably distribute much needed funds.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Santa Cruz, CA
