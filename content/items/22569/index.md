        I agree, it would be discriminatory to withhold relief to these partners and children especially!

        ----- Original Message -----
        Thursday, December 13, 2001 1:24 PM
        FW: September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        As info, I found out about the Compensation Fund for the victims of
        September 11.  Below is the email I sent to voice my own opinion.  If you'd
        like to write your own email (or just copy this one), please do so.



        > -----Original Message-----
        > Thursday, December 13, 2001 1:16 PM
        > September 11 Victim Compensation Fund
        >
        > Fort Worth, Texas
        > December 13, 2001
        >
        > Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        > Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        > U.S. Department of Justice
        > e-mail: victimcomp.comments@usdoj.gov
        >
        > In late September, President Bush and Congress did a wonderful thing and
        > authorized the creation of the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" to
        > provide assistance to the surviving victims of the attacks and the
        > "relatives" of those who died.
        >
        > I was recently made aware that the Department of Justice is in the process
        > of writing the regulations that will govern implementation of the Fund and
        > has invited public comments to guide this process.
        >
        > Unfortunately, it seems, there are factions who are mounting a campaign to
        > request that the surviving partners and children of gay and lesbian
        > victims be excluded from the benefits of the Fund.  I find these views to
        > be unpatriotic, cruel and reprehensible.  How could anyone believe that
        > the emotional, financial and psychological pain of the September 11th
        > attacks and the loss of loved ones should be addressed based only on life
        > style choices?  Clearly, such a determination would have to be considered
        > a blatant exercise in discrimination.
        >
        > As The Justice Department considers the regulations governing the
        > "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" I urge you to ensure that
        > compensation is available to all surviving families of those killed,
        > including the committed partners and non-biological children of gay and
        > lesbian victims.
        >
        > Thank you for your consideration.
        >
        > Individual Comment
        > Grapevine, TX
