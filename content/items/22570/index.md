        particularly unfair. Discrimination is un-American. In a time when the country

        of New York, former Mayor Giuliani of New York, Attorney General Elliot

        survivors equally, I cannot comprehend how gay partners could be so
        egregiously overlooked.

        being insensitively ignored or treated as less worthy than others in the same
        situation. In such a time of grief, the government should not be treating gay
        Americans as second-class citizens.
