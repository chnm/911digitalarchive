        Friday, December 07, 2001 8:07 PM
        Sept 11 Victims

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing to encourage our government to do what is good and right in the
        treatment of the families of victims of the vicious attacks on September 11,
        2001.  Please follow the example of my very own New York Governor Pataki in
        extending full treatment of gay and lesbian partners as families of those
        who suffered and died because of that dreadful day.

        I know what that dread felt like, as my own boyfriend was at work that
        morning across the street from the Twin Towers that morning, when the planes
        hit, and I can assure you that my relief on the news of his escape was just
        as great as my heterosexual counterparts, and I am sure the sorrow and loss
        (both emotional and financial) for my gay and lesbian brothers and sisters
        is also just as great.

        In these most difficult times, they should not be made to feel excluded or
        ashamed of their lives, and should be comforted and assured that their
        families are as important in our society as any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        New Haven, CT
