                            Re:  Regulations Governing
                                 The September 11 Victims Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

             This relates to the September 11 Victims Compensation Fund (the "Fund") established
        upon the enactment of the Air Transportation Safety and System Stabilization Act (Public Law
        107-42), and the regulations to be promulgated to govern the administration of the Fund.

             The New York State Bar Association considers it very important that the Department of
        Justice obtain as much input as possible on the development of substantive and procedural rules
        to govern the administration of the Fund. The extraordinary circumstances surrounding the
        creation and administration of the Fund nevertheless do not lend themselves easily to
        conventional comment letters. Therefore, I am writing to offer the assistance of The Association
        and its members to work directly with the Department and the Special Master. It is the belief of
        many of our members who have expertise in the areas of personal injury litigation and
        compensation programs that the best way for us to assist the Department in this enormous task is to provide people with a wide range of relevant experience in order to help resolve issues and problems.

             In addition to the fact that the terrorist acts of September 11 have had a direct impact on the New York State Bar Association, it is important for me to point out that the Association is the larest voluntary state bar association in the country, with nearly 70,000 members engaged in every substantive and procedural area of law. Their expertise providers a formidable source of knowledge and information. I hope that there is an appropriate mechanism, such as paticipation in a working group, for us to assist in the development and operation of the Fund.

        Very truly yours,


        Comment by:
        New York State Bar Association
        Albany, NY
