        The Fund is Unfair!

        I am writing to formally protest the exclusion of Gay and Lesbians from

        If fetuses, illegal aliens and others can be compensated without
        discrimination (even when being here in blatent violation of US Federal
        law), then members of the Gay and Lesbian community, Americans all, should
        not be excluded.

        I was shocked when the Governor of Puerto Rico raised money "For Puerto
        Rican Families" and tried to get Governor Pataki to use the money raised
        here on the island for families of Puerto Rican descent only.  Thank God,
        Governor Pataki rejected her request and put the money into a fund that
        included all.  Now, it is time for the federal government to also reject
        discrimination and include ALL American citizens, as well as the unborn and
        those illegally here (and those who employed them illegally) in equality and
        equity.

        Sincerely and prayerfully,


        Individual Comment
