        Benefits for Gay Victims


        To whom this may concern:

        I thought that this was the country of the free and
        that we pride ourselves on being diverse.  In this
        terrible time of sorrow and war, why must we
        discriminate against gay Americans?  It absolutely
        disgusted me to hear that victims of 9/11's attacks
        that were gay would not be receiving the same sorts of
        benefits as their heterosexual counterparts.

        I urge you to strongly reconsider your stance.  All
        Americans deserve the same respect - especially those
        that are United States citizens, those that pay taxes,
        those that helped on the Philadelphia flight, etc.

        Thank you.

        Individual Comment
