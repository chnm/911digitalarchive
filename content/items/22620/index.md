        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 5:52 PM
        Victim Relief

        Regarding the victim relief.  I just received an e-mail indicating that those who are Lesbians or Gay may be left out.  I feel that in addition to the Presidential Prayer Updates.  This needs to be included:  that we do not leave out ANYONE based on sexual orientation.  It is time to heal all our divisions.

        In our Constitutions it reads "All people (MEN) are created equal"  it is time we act out of our very founding fathers very painstaking sacrifices to write. It came out of much prayer and discernment to believe and write this document.  So it is my belief that we honor the very truths of this document.  We ARE all equal, we are creations doing the best we can and it is not up to us to judge another persons life style.

        Even though I am heterosexual I can not imagine how difficult it must be to live in a society where people are still ostracized for beliefs, race and sexual orientation.

        Please act in consideration of the greater good for all people.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
