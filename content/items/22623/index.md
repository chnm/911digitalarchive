        Monday, March 11, 2002 3:28 PM
        survivor benefits appeal


        My name is           .  I am a resident of Sioux Falls, SD.  I have read
        that for domestic partners of gay men and women who have died in the
        September 11 tragedy, compensation may not be available.   As a sister of a
        lesbian with a life partner, I am writing to register my dismay at such
        discrimination.  While states may not recognize partners in unmarried
        domestic relationships, the fund can.  I encourage you to follow the
        spirit, not the letter, of the law.  Brave men and women died for our
        country in a moment of tragedy.  If we can include unborn children and
        alien residents in our compensation, we can include in that compensation
        the loving partners of gay men and women who have sacrificed their lives in
        this time of national crisis.

        I urge you to make this change a reality.  A hero is a hero.  Please honor
        the memories of all these brave individuals by refusing to discriminate
        against a few of them.

        Respectfully,
        Individual Comment
        Sioux Falls, SD
