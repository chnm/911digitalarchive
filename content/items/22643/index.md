        Friday, February 01, 2002 11:05 AM
        Compensation

        Dear Mr. Feinberg,

        I am deeply distressed by all the PR hype surrounding the compensation fund
        being driven by           . I see absolutely no reason or argument that 1.6 million dollars in taxpayer money is not enough. Not only is it enough,
        but it is much more than the average person paying the taxes to support
        those ungrateful slugs will ever even earn in their lifetimes. I have
        compassion for anyone's loss, but the disrespect being shown by           for the American people who he expects to provide BMW's and mansions for the
        victims of the WTC tragedy is simply too much for most of us to take. We all
        face personal losses in our live and no one expects the government to make
        us wealthy because of it. We work hard and continue to do so regardless.
        Maybe some of the thankless WTC victims need a lesson in the realities of
        life that the rest of us have to deal with -- like actually taking personal
        responsibility and going out and providing for their own families instead of
        always looking for the easy government entitlement.

        Individual Comment
        Charleston, SC
