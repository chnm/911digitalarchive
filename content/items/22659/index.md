        Friday, December 07, 2001 9:15 AM
        9-11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Mr. Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public
        comments regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing
        and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."

        The victims of this heinous act of violence against our country and way
        of life were as heterogeneous as the strong fabric of our nation itself.
        Men and women from CEOs to maintenance staff and from a multitude
        of ethnic backgrounds and religions were killed. I would hope the
        Justice Department will make every effort to see that no one is
        excluded when our nation reaches out in support of those left behind.

        I am writing because there is one group of loyal Americans who
        left loved ones behind that could be left out because they unjustly
        are deprived of the recognition they deserve as Americans. I am
        referring to the partners and children of Gay and Lesbian victims of
        the bombing. My fear is that these Americans who have already
        suffered so much may become victims of a misguided political
        agenda. Please do what you can to see that this does not happen.
        I strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that ensure
        compensation is available to all victims of the attack,including
        the committed partners and the non-biological children of gay and
         lesbian victims.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        New Britain, CT
