        Wednesday, March 13, 2002 10:24 AM
        9/11 Fund


        I am writing in regards to Mr. Kenneth Feinberg's remarks on Sunday's
        Meet The Press.

        I sincerely urge Mr. Feinberg to extend funds to all of the victims'
        families, regardless of the victim's sexual orientation. I am one of
        the many New Yorkers who witnessed the Trade Center collapsing (from 10
        blocks away), a site I will not soon forget. I also lost friends to
        this attack. And I am a gay man.

        Please do not allow politics to prevent the families of the victims
        from receiving some sort of closure. This is the only way for them to
        make some sense of all of this and to be able to grieve their loved
        ones. Do not deny them this decency, as it will only drag on their pain
        for an extended period of time as they fight for what was rightfully
        theirs to begin with.

        As the law has been bent to allow illegal immigrants to gain
        compensation, so should it be "bent" here. The reasoning behind the
        former is to allow ALL victim's families to receive compensation, not
        SOME. Otherwise this exception would not have been made. Be consistent
        in this ideal and allot all family members the necessary compensation.

        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
        Astoria, NY
