        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 10:25 AM
        Discrimination is Un-American

        Mr. Feinberg and like-minded others at the 9/11 Fund,
        I am writing to express my deep and profound
        disappointment and outrage at your recent statements
        regarding the 9/11 Fund's position on gay and lesbian
        victims and their partners and families.  You suggest
        that support for gay and lesbian partners should
        depend on state laws because of a fear of "local
        battles and lawsuits."  Particularly given your
        position on the inclusion of illegal aliens, how can
        you not see how hypocritical this is? On an emotional
        level, these events have triggered an incredible sense
        of coming together to help one another. Offering gay
        and lesbian partners equal assistance is not making a
        political stand or promoting an agenda.  It's about
        helping ALL individuals who have endured loss and who
        need support. So many heroes from that day are gay and
        lesbian citizens.  Many of them have been profiled in
        the news as truly symbolic of what it is to be a "good
        American." Why are you now making a conscious choice
        to lessen their memory and make them second-class
        citizens?
        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
        Providence, RI
