        To Whom It May Concern:

        I was disappointed and outraged to hear that the
        partners and families of gay and lesbian people who
        died during the September 11 attacks would not be
        allowed the same compensation as the spouses and
        families of the heterosexual victims.  To me, the
        message this decision sends is clear: gay and lesbian
        families are not valid and do not deserve the same
        consideration given to other families.  How can you
        disregard federal law about immigration and give
        compensation to illegal aliens and yet ignore the life
        partners of gay and lesbian citizens?  I urge you to
        reconsider this decision and extend help and comfort
        to all who lost family members and whose lives were
        forever changed by the events of September 11.  Thank
        you.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        New York, New York
