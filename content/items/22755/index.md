        To whom it may concern:

        I'm not one who normally signs petitions, but I was very upset when I
        heard that gay and lesbian partners of those
        who died on September 11 are not automatically going to
        receive the same benefits as heterosexual spouses.  Those partners
        grieve and suffer loss no different than heterosexual partners.  I would
        be willing to accept "the law is the law" if it were indeed the
        standard, thereby excluding illegal immigrants and others - but from
        what I read, they will receive compensation.  Either be consistent with
        the letter of the law or don't.

        Please make every effort to give full benefits to the American partners
        of gay or lesbian couples.  Make me proud to be an American to know that
        the right thing was done.

        Best regards,




        Individual Comment
        New Carlisle, OH
