        Monday, January 07, 2002 11:07 AM
        Victims Compensation

        I just want to voice my opinion that what started out as a goodwill gesture
        has turned the families of the victims into a greedy bunch.  While I feel
        that there should be some monetary help for interim use - I, as a tax payer,
        do not want to support these families for years to come.  That is why we
        have other avenues such as "life insurance".  I think we are setting a bad
        precedence when we value these lives over those of other "ordinary" American
        citizens and our active military.  We will see ramifications from this that
        we as taxpayers will not be able to afford in the future.  Sympathy for the
        victims is turning to disgust over their greed - this is not good.  We need
        to stop this before it gets any worse.  Give each family a like amount -
        regardless of family size or income - but do not make it an extravagant
        figure.  Does the family of a single person with no dependents really need
        $1 million-plus?  No amount will bring these people back.

        Individual Comment
        Gordon, WI
