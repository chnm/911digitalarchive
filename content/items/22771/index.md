        January 23, 2002




                                 COMMENTS

        IRA and 401k plans should not be considered to be pensions for collateral source purposes.


        In the determination of collateral source amounts of Workers Compensation and Social Security, only
        the annuity value of payments to be received should be used.


        Social Security paid on behalf of a victims children should not be collateral source.


        If Social Security paid on behalf of a victims children is deemed to be collateral source, then the amount
        should be reduced by a consumption factor in the same manner that is employed in step five of the
        tables to calculate Economic Loss.

        *Wife of victim

        Individual Comment
        Wyckoff, N.J.
