        Puritanical as garbage

        I am again reminded of the inequities shown toward the gay and lesbian
        community in regard to the 9/11 fund set aside for the relatives of

        What the community at large now wants to know is on what basis do you
        think you are eligible to "bend the rules" for aliens and others while
        excluding native homosexuals from your list of those whom you wish to
        say thanks to by giving them a government gift or stipend just for
        living and being a relative of those who died.

        This is an outrage at a time when we are trying to drop the hyphenated
        words of Italian-American, African-American Irish-American and all other
        hyphenated words and Gay-American is included.

        Gay Americans are just as real as any other person living on this planet
        and just as deserving of being given money as any other group of people.
        Especially so when                     , who was gay, showed his heroic and
        altruistic traits.

                             wasn't in league with the devil after all, was he?  Only rotten,
        foul                      would say he was.  Are you a rotten, foul                     ?

        So why are you discriminating on some flimsy basis of local State rules
        or City or County rules.  It is firmly felt there is nothing but
        prejudice involved here and we want it STOPPED immediately!

        You will treat ALL Americans in the same way. Your activities in
        discriminating against gays and lesbians are totally un-American and
        totally disgusting as foul, bastard activity.  You will cease from this
        thinking immediately!!

        To do otherwise is only going to fester this unrest to a point where
        those who are discriminated against will one day, not too far away,
        raise their heads in uprising in the same manner the blacks did.

        We are sick and tired of being discriminated against, said Martin Luther
        King.

        How dare you make these distinctions on the basis of your own personal
        prejudice and a book called the Bible that is currently under the

        The whole Christian movement is the work of the devil and in this case

        an atmosphere of hate towards anyone that wasn't of their skin coloring

        Mr. Feinberg, you have better change your thinking (and that of your
