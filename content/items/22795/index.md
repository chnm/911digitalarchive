        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing to you as the mother of a lesbian.  My daughter has opened my
        eyes to the diversity of our great country.  My blinders were taken off.  I
        certainly hope that you realize that you probably know gay, lesbian,
        bi-sexual and transgendered people - you might not know who they are.
        Every one of the GLBT people came from two heterosexual parents who had
        heterosexual parents.  They probably have sisters, brothers, aunts, uncles,
        and cousins who love them dearly.

        I implore you to let justice prevail and treat all people fairly and
        justly.  Don't let hatred and intolerance of others win.
