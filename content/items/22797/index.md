        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing to ask that the U. S. Department of Justice live up to its name "The Department of JUSTICE."  I emphatically urge the Department to draft regulations for implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" that include the victims who are committed gay and lesbian partners and their children, whether biological or adopted.  There grief is no less deep than any of the rest of the families.

        We are fighting terrorism; let us not create our own terror on ourselves.

        Thank you very much.
        Individual Comment

        Granada Hills, CA
