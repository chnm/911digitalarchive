        Friday, March 29, 2002 12:13 PM
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund

        I cannot BELIEVE the discrimination encoded in the definition of 'victim' and
        those allowed compensation through them! Law-abiding citizens being denied
        compensation - let alone recognition of their grief - because they are gay or
        lesbian, while ILLEGAL ALIENS, for heavens sake, are eligible?! With all the
        government fuss about illegal aliens, they are still counted as more
        'deserving' than a legal U.S. citizen?! This is the worst sort of
        conservative American hypocrisy!

         I am ashamed of and embarrassed for American leadership; out of an event so
        life-changing, a tragedy that nevertheless brought Americans closer and
        changed so many lives and policies, an event that brought appreciation and
        honor to our Fire, Police, rescue workers etc., an event that made people
        re-examine their lives and attitudes - after all this, the decision makers
        reduce it all to prejudiced categorisation. It tarnishes any good that may
        have come out of 9/11, and it in effect says "Americans - even unborn ones
        and illegal ones - have suffered a terrible tragedy which has changed us
        forever, and brought us together as a nation, and we recognise the suffering
        of all involved...... oh, except for the American citizens that happen to be
        gay or lesbian, because we don't count them so we're negating them as
        sentient beings who suffered through this too." How dare you do this to such
        a percentage of our nation?

        I am not, myself, gay - but so what? I am not Asian or African American
        either - but I don't condone prejudice; I am not disabled, but I do not think
        they are less viable; I am not elderly, but do not discount them as 'past
        it'. America talks of freedom and equality and understanding..... then
        something like this once again tarnishes that projected image. I cannot even
        express how disgusted I am.


        Individual Comment
