        January 16, 2002

        Mr. Kenneth R. Feinberg
        Special Master
        Office of the Victim Compensation Fund Program
        U.S. Department of Justice
        Main Building, Room 3140
        950 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW
        Washington, DC 20530

        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

        We are writing on behalf of our constituents throughout Connecticut who are the families of the
        victims
        of September 11, 2001.  Specifically, we are concerned about the deadline of January 22, 2002
        for
        submitting comments on the Interim Final Rule implementing Public Law 107-42.

        We have heard from many families since the proposed rules and regulations of the September
        11th
        Victim Compensation Fund were officially published on December 21, 2001.  Although the
        comment
        period is for approximately one month, many families feel the interruption of the holiday season
        has
        impeded their ability to comprehensively review the content of the Interim Final Rule.  Because
        the
        Final Rule will determine the rules and regulations by which compensation packages will be
        awarded to
        each victim's family, we feel it is imperative that a complete understanding of this information is
        critical
        to ensuring that families make a well-informed decision regarding their participation in the Fund.

        While we fully understand that your office is eager to move forward in settling claims for these
        families,
        it is also important to note that should a family choose to make a claim with this Fund, they
        waive their
        right to pursue most other legal options.  Therefore, we respectfully ask that you extend the
        comment
        period for a minimum of two weeks to give these families the time they need and deserve to
        make such a
        critically important decision.

        Thank you, in advance, for your time and consideration of our request.


                                      Sincerely,

        Christopher J. Dodd
        United States Senator

        Joseph I. Lieberman
        United States Senator

        Christopher Shays
        Member of Congress
