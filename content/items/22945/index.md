        Compensating 911 Gays & Lesbian.....

        March 17, 2002
        To the September 11 Victim  Compensation Fund,

         This is an open letter to the trusties of the September 11 Victim
        fund.  I am appalled
        that you are not considering compensation for the Gay and Lesbian
        partners of those who died on September 11 and are not automatically
        going to give the same benefits as heterosexual spouses.  And yet,
        you go against the state and federal laws and award compensation to
        ILLEGAL ALIENS and promise not to deport them and not to go against
        the company that hired them.  This is appalling, on the gays and
        lesbians you say you have to follow state laws and are not able to
        compensate them (by law), while on the other hand you ignore the
        federal and state laws on illegal aliens, also the companies that
        hired them.
         This is not the American way. These Gays and Lesbians are American
        citizens and you are surly are discriminating against them.  I am mad
        as hell and will be forwarding this letter to the President, my
        Senators and Representative for their action or we will need to shop
        around for better more compassionate law makers.



        Individual Comment
        San Jose, California
