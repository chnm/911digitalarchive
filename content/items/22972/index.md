        Discrimination


        To Whom It May Concern:

        I am saddened to learn that the September 11th fund
        will not be covering the partners of gay and lesbian
        americans affected by this tragedy.

        Have we learned nothing from this event about what is
        important and what is not?

        Would this not be the ideal time to take advantage of
        minds opening to the importance of human decency and a
        recognition of what is worth opposing?

        And what greater insult can we offer our fellow
        americans who happen to love people of the same sex
        who have been equally devastated by this terrible
        tragedy.  I am disappointed in my nation and its
        leaders.

        We should penalize people for whom they hurt, not whom
        they love.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
