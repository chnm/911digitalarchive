        Tuesday, March 12, 2002 3:37 PM
        911 Victim Compensation Funds


        To:  Kenneth Feinberg
        Date: 3/12/02

        Dear Mr. Feinberg:
        I am disturbed to hear about your ruling to disallow victim compensation funds to partners, children,  & other family members of gay & lesbians who lost their lives on Sept. 11, 2001.  Although many states do not legally recognize gay & lesbian relationships, your decision is both unethical & immoral given the events of that day.  Particularly disturbing is the discrimation against children of gay & lesbian marriages.  Those children are innocent victims of the terrorist attacks.  I believe you are using this tragedy in a political way which should have no bearing on how the funds are used.  You would be creating additional hardship & pain to the surviving families.  As a donor to the 911 events, I intended my money to be used for all survivors & their families.    Please reconsider your stance on this issue.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
