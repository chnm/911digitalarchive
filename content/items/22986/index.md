        9-11 compensation fund


        Why does the government, which is to serve the people, turn its back on homosexual couples who have built a life together?  It sounds like a corollary of socialism:  take from those who build and so you can give it to those you favor.

        When will our government understand the meaning of a free market and free association?  The older I get, the more  I realize that the only difference between a "conservative" government and a "liberal" one is who it favors.

        Those who lost their partners during 9-11 should be fairly compensated.  It shouldn't be that hard for the government to assess economic inter- or co-dependence in such situations.  To pretend otherwise is tragic.

        Since there is a fund for this, I urge inclusion.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
