        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 1:20 AM
        Disgusting Outrage in appropriation of funds

        I am disgusted that not ALL 9/11 victims are being included equally in the
        distribution of the funds.  I think it's proper that illegal immigrants are
        included without threat of deportation and that companies that hired them
        won't be in trouble either.  It's correct that the law be overlooked in this
        case.  But somehow, the fund administrators don't seem to be willing to do
        the same for partners of gay and lesbian people killed in 9/11.  The
        justification for this has been stated as fear of law suits from states that
        don't legally recognize same-sex couples, so stepping outside the bounds of
        law was not an option to take.  Why is this any different from immigration
        laws?  Hasn't 9/11 tought us anything about coming together as a nation of
        ALL people to protect eachother and our freedom?  This is a disgusting and
        sinful outrage and amounts to nothing but prejudice and discrimination.  As
        an American Citizen, I officially protest this exclusion of Americans who are
        gay and lesbian, and make my protest a matter of public record.

        Individual Comment
