        Enough is Enough



        You go to work, slip on some coffee, get mugged in the parking lot, get
        caught in a office fire, get buried in a earthquake and die.  That's it,
        your family moves on and what will be, will be.  But now you go to work and
        die in a terrorist attack, you are a police or fire man doing the job that
        you asked to do and die in a terrorist attack and we feel for you all.
        However, now it is apparent that this is not a tradigy, but a money making
        retirement plan to move to 90210.  Well, if you can get more money from
        suing the air lines, the govt., the company that made the box cutters and
        mace, the airline manufacture and anyone else remotely attached to this
        event, go for it.


        I regret I gave, I wish there was a way to take this money and give it to
        the GI's who were drafted and gave there life in the nations wars.  They did
        not make millions sitting at a desk, they did not ask for the job they
        received and they certently died in defense of their country.  Time to
        redefine hero and put mercy and compassion back in the heart and out of the
        checkbook.



        Individual Comment
        severn  md
