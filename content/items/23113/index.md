        Monday, January 14, 2002 12:58 AM

        I think that the rules and regulations that ken Feinberg has set up for the
        Victims Compensation Fund are horribly unfair.  Congress set this fund up so
        that we, the victims and their families would not have to go to court and
        could get on with out lives quicker.  ken feinberg took that whole idea and
        made a joke of it.  Made a joke of all of our loved ones lives.  We are
        giving up our right to sue, at least give us the opportunity to receive what
        can be a fair amount or close to it if we.  We could all get a lot more if we
        sued, but most of us won't, if treated fairly.  We would be doing the
        government a favor by not tying up the courts with 3000 law suits.  We are
        the victims, not the government, you don't know what it is like for us, to
        have this happen to us all.  We are the ones who have to live with this for
        the rest of our lives, our children have to grow up without parents, and
        mine, will never know her father, she is only 17 months old, at least give me
        the opportunity to offer her a wonderful life, money will not bring her daddy
        back, but it can help me to give her all the things that he would have wanted
        her to have.  Think about it...how would you feel if this had happened to
        you?  What if that was your spouse in that building and your child who will
        never know their parent?  HOW WOULD YOU FEEL?  Would you have made these
        rules and regulations had you been one of us?
        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Ridgewood, New York
