        Wednesday, November 28, 2001 6:26 PM
        Comments on the Department of Justice's Rulemaking Regarding
        the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001

        The outpouring of support from both public and private organizations to help
        the victims of the September 11 attacks and their families has been
        incredible.  But these efforts have also underscored the vulnerabilities
        that gay and lesbian couples face because their relationships are not
        legally recognized.
        Gay and lesbian partners and families have the same love and commitment that
        other families have and they have experienced the same horrendous grief and
        loss.  Please follow the example of the American Red Cross, Governor Pataki
        and Mayor Giuliani and do all you can to make federal relief efforts
        available to gay and lesbian partners and families.

        Individual Comment
        Marathon, WI
