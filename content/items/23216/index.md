        Dear Director Zwick:
        I am writing to ask for your support for the  National Gay and Lesbian Task
        Force's position  regarding disbursement of monies from the September 11
        Fund. I agree with the statements they made in their letter of November 21,
        2001 emphasizing  the need for the Department of Justice to  "include among
        relatives eligible for compensation those who lost their life partners or de
        facto parents or children, regardless of sexual orientation and marital
        status."
        The horrific loss of loved ones to a random terrorist act should not be
        compounded by our own government denying survivors whatever benefits may be
        available simply because we fail to have laws to protect all of our
        citizens.
        Thank you for listening, and for acting honorably in support of all
        citizens.
        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Denver, CO
