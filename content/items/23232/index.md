        Monday, March 11, 2002 5:40 PM
        Victim Fund


        To Whom It May Concern,


        I am quite disturbed to hear that the funds being disbursed to loved ones
        of the most tragic events of September 11 are being denied to those who
        were involved in a same sex relationship.  How horrific an event to deal
        with as a nation let alone the be affected directly by losing a loved one.
         All of the monies collected were to be disbursed to help the families
        financially and now you are going to deny them these funds.  Is it not
        enough that these families have had to endure the tragic loss and now have
        to endure more pain as you deny them the ability to financially recover
        from such devastation.

        I pledge to the flag each morning........ "..... and to the republic for
        which is stands.  One nation, under God, indivisible with liberty and
        justice for all."  It sounds as if you are dividing us and not enforcing
        justice for all.

        Please reconsider this action.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
