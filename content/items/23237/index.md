        Saturday, March 16, 2002 9:20 PM
        Outrageous Treatment of Americans

        To Whom It May Concern:

        I am writing as an outraged American taxpayer, having
        learned that partners and families of gay victims of
        the 9-11 terrorist attacks may not be accorded the
        same consideration given spouses and families of other
        9-11 victims.

        As explained to me, the federal program is obligated
        to follow various and conflicting state laws that may
        or may not accord any rights to the partners of gays
        and lesbians.  However, you are ignoring all federal
        immigration laws by providing compensation to the
        spouses and families of law-breaking victims who were
        in the United States as illegal aliens.  Furthermore,
        no steps are contemplated to deport these spouses and
        families.

        A number of the gay victims have been recognized as
        heroes for their bravery on 9-11.  To make them second
        class citizens, while handing over Americans' tax
        money to persons in this country illegally -- who are
        criminals for being here illegally -- just makes me
        sick to my stomach.

        I am not gay, but I am sure ashamed to be an American.

        Individual Comment
        Robinson, Kansas
