        Monday, March 11, 2002 2:16 AM
        September 11 fund


        Mr. President,

        I am writing you yet again.  I really do not want to, but, living in Puerto
        Rico where we have limited voice (and that voice only represents only a part
        of society in Puerto Rico, not the English speaking population, not the Pro
        Statehood population, not the Gay of Lesbian population) no vote in
        Congress.

        I truly believe that as long as one person is denied their civil rights, no
        one has civil rights or protection under the law.

        I know that you never read your e-mail, but that it is read by a staff
        member who makes check-marks on a card and you are given total numbers of
        people who write you by category, pro or con.  If you never read anything I
        have written, or respond to anything I have written before or since, please
        read this and respond.  The Autoresponder is so insulting.  If people take
        time from their lives to send you their thoughts and prayers, you should
        have someone on your staff to respond, but that would mean you would have to
        care, wouldn't it?

        I was shocked to hear Mr. Feinburg on Meet the Press yesterday.  He stated
        that our country is so compassionate that the fund will give money to all of
        the widowed, the children, born and unborn, even fetuses of those who were
        killed in the September 11th tragedy.  He then went on to say that illegal
        aliens will be eligible, without any repercussions due to their unlawful
        status, nor will their employers who illegally employed them.

        He then stated that Gay and Lesbian people will be excluded to the same
        extent their States Laws discriminate against them.

        Heroes like &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp and Flight Attendant &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp are not only having
        their ultimate contributions ignored, but &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp partner in life (as well as
        the partners of all of the other Gay or Lesbian folk who were killed either
        in the terrorist act, or who died saving others as fire fighters, police
        personnel or just plain folk trying to save others).  Senator McCain gave
        the eulogy at &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp memorial service.  He, by his actions on the
        plane, probably saved countless lives, many of them Republicans, and
        possibly your own, and sacrificed his own life in the process.  Why?
        Because he believed in the American way of life and justice.  He believed
        that, like hundreds of thousands of other Gay and Lesbian people who have
        died in war that he was protecting his own country.  Now this country is
        dishonoring the memory and humanity of &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp and others who just happen to be
        Gay or Lesbian.

        SHAME! SHAME! SHAME  on you and on congress for your bigotry and denial of
        justice to Gay and Lesbian Americans.

        Unborn fetuses can get justice!  Illegal Aliens who have no right to be
        here, no right to work here and no right to compensation and who broke the
        law, and their employers who broke the US Federal laws by employing them are
        all being shown "justice and compassion" "from a truly grateful nation."
        Gay and Lesbian Americans, most of us very patriotic, tax payers and
        supportive of the American way of life are yet again subject to the bigotry
        and prejudice of the Republican Party and it's mistress the Religious
        Wrong!.

        You will notice sir, that I am a clergy person.  In my denomination, and
        indeed in my mission parish, we discriminate against no one!  You would be
        welcome to worship here, but so would the drug addict, the old person from
        down the street, the married couple with their children, along with the gay
        couples and drag queens. God created all people.  The diversity in humanity
        is a reflection of God's infinite love of diversity in all people and all
        things.  Gay people are God's Children as much as all others.  God's grace
        and mercy extends to them as much to you.  Jesus died for their sins, just
        as he did for yours.  How then can you discriminate?  How can you approve of
        discrimination against fellow Americans (many of whom, I am ashamed to say
        supported you) while giving special consideration to law-breakers who were
        here contrary to American Law?

        Gay Americans are Americans whether you like us or not.  We have
        relationships (mine is now over 19 years, Christ-centered and faithful to
        each other) whether the government OF Some of the People, FOR Some of the
        People like it or not.  We will sacrifice, scrimp, save and even die for
        this country whether you like it or not. Our tax dollars and spending will
        help the country to recover from your recession whether you ignore us or
        not.

        It is time sir, to do the right thing.  Denounce the bigotry publicly and
        totally.  Demand that Gay and Lesbian Americans be included in the September
        11th fund.  Ask for laws to give civil rights to all American Citizens
        equally.  Ask for, and sign a hate crime bill that does include sexual
        orientation.  At present, you, Mr. Feinburg and Congress of guilty of the
        crimes of prejudice and discrimination! You will have to answer for these
        crimes in the next life, if not in this one as will we all.

        God Bless America and ALL Americans.




        Individual Comment
        Old San Juan, PR
