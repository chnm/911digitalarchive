        December  19, 2001

        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        I write as a member of Amnesty International, a U.S. citizen, and a
        registered voter in urging you to allow gay & lesbian partners and family
        members equal access to the September 11 Victims Relief Fund.

        As you consider rules for administering the relief fund, please apply a
        general principle of equality and compassion, by including among relatives
        eligible for compensation those who lost their life partners, as well as de
        facto parents or children, without regard to sexual orientation or marital
        status.

        It would be a terribly cruel to deny the loved ones of any of September
        11th's victims what is rightfully theirs - and would belie our supposed
        commitment to equality and freedom in the U.S.

        I look forward to hearing from you about your efforts to ensure that all
        surviving victims of the September 11 attacks have equal access to the
        relief fund.




        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Bloomington, IN
