        PLEASE CONSIDER STRUCTURED SETTLEMENTS


        Reasons:

         1. Payments are made over a period of time and designed to
        come at times when people need them - college funds, etc.

         2. History has shown that people are given large amounts of
        money they spend it all within five years -- nothing would then be left
        for children and they would   be looking at airlines and
        government to collect more later.

         3. We are in the DC area and can meet with families who
        were affected by the Pentagon disaster and let them know how structured
        settlement payments work.

         4. It would save the fund benefits because money would not
        need to be paid now, but could be paid monthly, etc.

          There are many other benefits and ways to help families
        and the fund using structured settlement annuities.

         We would welcome the opportunity to meet with anyone who is
        interested in exploring this option for these families or sending
        materials to anyone who is intersted in considering this option.  All
        the travel we do is at our own expense  and the fund does not pay us
        anything.  If a structure is done we are paid by the insurance company.
        Both the fund and the families should benefit from this financial tool.
