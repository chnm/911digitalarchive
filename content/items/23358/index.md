        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 3:07 PM
        Victim's Compensation

        To Whom It May Concern:

        First I would recommend that you read the New York Times' "Portraits of
        Grief"  section daily.  It puts a face on each and every victim of this
        horrendous attack.  Then I would ask you to reflect on the innocent children
        that have been affected for the rest of their lives.

        And, here is my two cents - the government should come up with a flat figure
        (for example, $250,000 per person - please feel free to go higher).  If a
        family has three remaining members they would receive $750,000.  In
        addition, the charitable organizations should compensate each family and the
        government should mind it's own business when it comes to these
        compensations.  The money the charitable organizations have received was
        sent in by the world public specifically for the families affected.  They
        should distribute EVERY PENNY!!

        The businesses affected should be compensated by the city, state and federal
        governments.  They should not be compensated by the charitable donations.

        Lastly, these compensation decisions should be made quickly.  The families
        have suffered enough - they should not have to worry how they are going to
        make their rent/mortgage payments.  Please show the world what a
        compassionate nation we can truly be.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        New York, NY
