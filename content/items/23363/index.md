        Sunday, November 25, 2001        1:30 P.M.
        Comment on victim compensation program

            I am writing to comment on the Victim Compensation Program.  I was a victim on September 11, 2001.  I was there on that sad day.  I saw some terrible things, like people jumping out of buildings, etc.  I will never forget it.  I was physically injured when the towers came down. I lost my job, and I am still waiting for my injury to heal so I can return to work.  So far, I have missed 10 weeks and it is hard to make ends meet with 3 children, a wife, and myself to take care of.  I think this program is a great thing for the victims of this tragedy.  I thank god that I am still alive.

        Individual Comment
        Staten Island, NY
