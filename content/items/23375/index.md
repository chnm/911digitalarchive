        12/24/01

        Kenneth L. Zwick. Director
        Office of Management Program
        U.S. Dept of Justice

        Dear Sir:
             I am writing to urge you to extend
        survivor benefits from the September 11
        catastrophe to all individuals who are
        committed partners of those who were
        killed at that time. Married people will
        be included, of course, but I am hoping
        you will include all partners in long
        standing stable relationships, whether homo-
        sexual or heterosexual, and especially to
        children of such relationships. The pain
        and loss is just as severe, perhaps more
        so, because there may be less community
        support for them.
             This has been a terrible tragedy for
        so many people. We need to get people
        strengthened and rejuvenated and back to
        some semblance of normal life, where they
        can survive emotionally and return to
        contributing to the community.
        Thank you

        Individual Comment
        Bartlett, TN
