        Thursday, March 21, 2002 4:31 PM
        Financial benefits of partners of 9/11 victims

        Dear Friend,

        I received this information in my e-mail, sent by a friend who has a gay
        daughter, and who is very upset. If it is true, I question the humanity of
        the following statement:

        In recent interviews, the head of the September 11
        Victim Compensation Fund (a fund created by Congress
        to help the families of those who died on September
        11) has made clear that the partners of gay and
        lesbian heroes of September 11 will not necessarily
        receive the same benefits as the spouses of
        heterosexual victims of the horrible terror attacks.

                  *                             *                          *

        I do not see how the sexual orientation of a hero should enter into the
        picture when it comes to financial benefits going to the partner of that
        hero. The tragedy of 9/11 was of such a magnitude that special dispensation
        should be given, and any law that bars rightful recipients of financial
        benefits is shameful and will arouse a great many people to take legal
        action. Of course, this may be merely a horrible rumor, but if it is not, you
        can expect a huge backlash if all the heroes families and partners are not
        treated equally. It will become a national shame and scandal, and rightfully
        so.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
