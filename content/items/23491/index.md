        Thursday, November 29, 2001 3:09 AM
        Sept. 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I understand that the Department of Justice is inviting public input on the
        rules governing the administration of the "September 11 Victim Compensation
        Fund."

        As a gay American, I urge the Department of Justice to include among
        relatives eligible for compensation those who lost their life partners or de
        facto parents or children, regardless of sexual orientation or marital
        status.  It's very important that all victims be treated equally and none be
        given special preference due to sexual orientation as would be the case if
        only heterosexuals who could legally marry were eligible.

        Moreover, as a gay Christian, I urge the Department to remember Christ's
        Commandment during this time of national crisis.  He told us we were to treat
        others as we would want to be treated.  Denying equal benefits to gay and
        lesbian families would not be in accordance with that Commandment; therefore,
        it would be immoral.  Immorality should not govern benefits to hurting
        families.

        Thank you.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Clayton, CA
