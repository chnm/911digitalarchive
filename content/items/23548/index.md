        9/11 Fund is discriminating against Gays


        Sir or Madam:

        This story has been passed along to me, and I must emphatically add my name
        to the list of people who think it an outrage that gays are being excluded
        from benefits of the 9/11 fund.  It is really counter to what we claim our
        stand to be as a country in this war, and is not justified by Mr.Feinberg's
        facile evasion and downright hiding behind state law.  It is not a
        justifiable position, legally or ethically, and I must protest strongly the
        policy decision that has been made.  And it is just that - a policy decision
        that has been made to explicitly exclude a group that is not popular with
        decision makers.  To support this policy certainly gives the strong
        impression that the President and Congress are adjudicating in a manner that
        is hypocrital in the extreme.

        I can only hope that either I am misinformed as to how benefits are actually
        being paid, or that something is being done to resolve this terrible
        injustice.  And one more thing: I would strongly recommend the immediate
        replacement of Mr. Feinberg with someone in possession of more political
        savvy and sensitivity.

        Best regards,

        Individual Comment
        San Francisco, CA
