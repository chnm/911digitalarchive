        Monday, January 21, 2002 1:52 PM
        Victims compensation fund

        Kenneth Zwick
        Department of Justice

        I would like to express my disagreement with the rules issued by Kenneth
        Feinberg, the Special Master, for the Victims Compensation Fund of 2001,
        P.L. 107-42.

        Mr. Feinberg is not satisfying the intent of this legislation. Asking
        victims families to sign documents that sign away future rights for an
        unknown settlement is ludicrous. Deducting insurance, 401K and other assets
        from this unknown settlement is an insult to the victims that acted
        responsibly to provide for their families.

        I hope you will see to it that these interim rules are reevaluated in the
        interest of fairness.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Glen Head, NY
