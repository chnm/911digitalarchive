        Hypocrisy on the highest level

        Although I grieve with those who lost loved ones on September 11th I am
        appalled that the U.S. government has decided to pay reperations to the
        victims. How dare the United States pay money to the victims of the WTC
        attacks when they refuse to even consider reperations to the descendents of
        slavery. Without question the horrors that were committed in this country
        and by this country under the name of slavery was far worse than those that
        occured on September 11th. It should also be noted that the crimes of
        September 11th were committed by individuals who were not citizens of this
        country while the crime of slavery was not only committed by U.S. citizens
        but encouraged and protected by the United States Government. The effects of
        the attacks of September 11th will live with those families for years to
        come but the effects of slavery, black codes and Jim Crow will continue to
        divide this country forever. In closing by paying money the the families of
        the WTC vicitms while refusing to acknowledge the horrific acts and effects
        of slavery is another blatant example of the racism and contempt for African
        Americans that the United States Government continues to foster.
