        Denial of benefits to gay and lesbian partners

        I have been following the issue of death benefits to survivors of the 9/11
        attacks.  In fact, yesterday I had lunch with a friend who knows a
        heterosexual woman who will be receiving approximately $10 million dollars
        from the Victim Compensation fund due to the death of her husband.  She and
        her husband were married for no more than two years.

        The denial of survivor benefits based on the sexual orientation of the
        partners is irrational, arbitrary and discriminatory.  I urge you to
        re-consider this position and treat all survivors - regardless of their
        sexual orientation - fairly, equitably and justly.
