        Wednesday, January 16, 2002 6:10 PM


        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

        As a widow due to the 9/11 attack, I find the regulations of the
        Victims' Compensation
        Fund totally inadequate in attempting to meet the family needs,
        make just compensation
        and live up to some form of moral responsibility.   The non-
        economic award of
        $250,000 and $50,000 for each dependent is an insult when one
        considers the
        economic bailout given to the airline industry.
        I truly believe my husband's death falls under the category
        of "wrongful death".
        After all, he and all the other victims, were killed to an
        action that should
        have been avoided had the airlines acted competently and
        implemented security
        measures already in place.   And now, that same industry is being
        bailed out
        by the government while the victim's are scratching for
        compensation while undergoing
        deep grief.   You're rewarding incompetence while at the same
        time penalizing
        the innocent victims.
        Mr. Feinberg, please allow yourself to not think of us in terms
        of numbers,
        but as people - wives, husbands, parents and many
        children whose lives will never be the same.   We have all
        suffered an unimaginable
        loss-the loss of a loved one due to a unprecedented terrorist
        attack.  The loss
        of a loved one who's only mistake was going to work on time or
        early.  A loved
        one who was working to support his/her family, and pay his/her
        taxes to the
        government.  The same government that is now turning its back on
        the families.
          Mr. Feinberg, the majority of the victims were white-collared
        workers.   Part
        of the corporate benefits is grou

        I sincerely urge you to modify the victim's compensation plan by
        excluding deduction
        of "collateral sources" and/or raising the monetary value of pain
        and loss.


        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Cortlandt Manor, NY
