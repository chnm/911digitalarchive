        Wednesday, November 21, 2001 9:07 AM

        Victim Compensation Fund



        Dear Mr. Zwick,



        In response to your request for comment concerning the September 11th
        Victims Compensation Fund established by Congress to provide assistance to
        those directly affected by the tragic events of September 11, I wanted to
        write to tell you about my partner, who I lost in this tragedy, and how this
        has affected my life.   My partner perished in the Pentagon crash, we had
        been together for nearly fourteen years.   We fully expected to live
        together for the rest of our lives.  I did not expect that he would be taken
        from me so soon.   Like so many other families who have written, my grief is
        deep and the uncertainty that lies ahead only adds to our desperate
        situations.



        In addition to emotional interdependence we shared all of our finances.   We
        owned our home together, shared bank accounts, and I was covered under his
        company health insurance.   I must now face surviving without the income
        from the primary income producer in our household.  I am asking you to base
        you eligibility requirements for compensation on broadly defined criteria
        demonstrating the financial dependence I have described to you.   This will
        ensure that equitable compensation is provided to all the families of the
        victims.



        Thank you for your consideration of my comments.



        Individual Comment

        Washington, DC
