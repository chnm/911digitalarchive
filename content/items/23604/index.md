        Proud of our Heros


        If the stories are true, that loved ones and family
        members of homosexuals lost to the terrorist attacks
        of 9/11 will not be compensated by the "funds" are
        true, the enemy at large have truly won over America!

        I am a heterosexual and am APPALLED at the idea that
        surviving loved ones and family members of homosexuals
        will not receive benefits from this fund because of
        the sexual preferences of the victims. I, for one, am
        still torn with emotion, that individuals made a
        choice on 9/11 to sacrifice themselves to the benefit
        of a larger population. I can only imagine the fear
        they must have felt, to never see or hold their loved
        ones again and yet they did what any good American
        would do.  They freely gave their lives so others
        would live.  All people!

        The events of 9/11 are sad enough and will forever be
        ingrained in my heart and soul, as they are to the
        rest of Americans in this country and to others
        abroad.  I am proud to be an American, am honored that
        so many other proud Americans died in heroic efforts,
        but am greatly saddened by a Government that would
        allow an additional atrocity to occur.  How can we be
        so ignorant to exclude "certain" people who gave their
        lives so willing for this country?  How is it that we
        can acknowledge some, including illegals, and ignore
        others?   How is it to hide behind State laws for some
        and bend them for others?  If you think about it, the
        enemy really doesn't have such a difficult job in
        destroying our Great Nation and it's People, they just
        have to sit by and watch us do it to ourselves.




        Individual Comment
