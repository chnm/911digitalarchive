        Victim's Fund Operation Suggestions

        To Whom it may concern:

        The fund should give everyone the same amount of money, as no life lost
        was more or less valuable in the eyes of the government.  Private sector
        charities are free to give money as they chose, and this must not be
        infringed upon by the government.  If the fund docks money from a
        victim's family who has received private sector aid, the government is
        in effect negating these charitable donations, instead of those given
        involuntarily (by taxes).

        No life lost was more or less valuable in the eyes of the American
        public, either, and therefore equity in distribution of charity should
        be achieved as well.  Political leaders should implore the organizers of
        charity funds to distribute the money donated evenly, as a means to this
        end.  We are not socialist, and therefore should not force equal
        distribution of money.

        Very Respectfully,

        Individual Comment
        Charleston, SC
