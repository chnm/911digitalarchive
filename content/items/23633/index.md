As you are aware, same-sex couples are not allowed to receive state issued marriage licenses, but both &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp and &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp blended their lives, finances, and families as if they were married.

        Thank you for your consideration,

        Individual Comment
