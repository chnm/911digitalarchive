        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

        I am simply appalled by the decision of the 9/11 fund not to provide
        compensation to gay and lesbian partners. The gays and lesbians on the planes
        and in the buildings are no less dead than the heterosexuals. The suffering
        of their partners is no less real than the suffering of heterosexual
        partners.

        Sen. John McCain praised            as a hero in his eulogy. Are we to
        assume that because he is gay            is less of a hero than the others who
        rushed the cockpit?

        Although you may blame state laws for the discrimination, we all know that
        the truth is simpler -- hatred. The 9/11 fund has even chosen to disregard
        national laws and grant financial compensation to illegal immigrants.
        Certainly illegal aliens are equally deserving of financial compensation, but
        if you are willing to set aside national laws, why not offer compensation to
        gays and lesbians regardless of state laws.

        I'm disgusted by this decision on the part of the 9/11 fund.

        Individual Comment
        Presto, PA
