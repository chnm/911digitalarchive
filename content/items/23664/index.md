        Mr. Kenneth Feinberg,

            The distribution of the  September 11 Victim Compensation Fund requires your immediate attention.
            The formula for distribution leaves too many individuals with little or no compensation.
            My neighbor, a young widow with two grade school children,  will receive nothing for the loss of her husband.  I don't think that was the intended spirit of this legistation.  The money should be distributed in a more equitable manner without regard to income from collateral sources.
            The victims' families have suffered enough.  Please spare them the ordeal of litigation.

        Individual Comment
        Manassas, VA
