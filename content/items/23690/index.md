        To Whom It May Concern,

         We represent over 100 families on Staten Island that have lost someone in t World Trade Center Disaster. I cannot believe that you are expecting these people to make a decision when they cannot even determine the entitlement. I have expressed this concern to both Congressman Fossella's office as well as the Attorney General. I would look to see this work sheet provided prior to next weeks meeting.
