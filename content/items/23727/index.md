        Thursday, November 29, 2001 1:27 PM
        Fair treatment for LGBT families



        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing to request that lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender families who
        lost a family member in the tragic events of SEptember 11 are equally
        compensated for their loss.  As a member of the LGBT community, I know that our
        families often do not get the legal recognition that they deserve.  I am urging
        you to make sure that those of us who lost loved ones are treated the same as
        our heterosexual counterparts.  Our loss is real and so are our families.  Thank
        you for your consideration.

        P.S.  Let's not forget that one of those brave souls on the flight which crashed
        in Pennsylvania was a gay man that risked his life to save many others.  Do not
        do him a disservice by allowing homophobic attitudes to prevent families from
        getting the compensation that they both need and deserve.

        Individual Comment

        Los Angeles, CA
