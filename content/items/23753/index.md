        Sunday, January 06, 2002 10:28 PM
        Taxpayers are not responsible!

        Mr. Feinberg,

        As a law-abiding and sympathetic taxpaying American, I am troubled by greed
        of other Americans.  The Federal Government is not responsible for
        supplementing any insurance payouts from Sept. 11.  It is a tragedy what
        happened.  We have all mourned the loss of life, but surely I would not be
        eligible for any monies besides insurance for my wife's or son's untimely
        demise.  If these people died in a hurricane or tornado or snowstorm or any
        natural disaster, then this compensation fund would not exist.  Does this
        fund apply to the Oklahoma federal building bombing?  How about the victims
        of the flood in Houston in June,2001?  It is only because these horrible
        things took place in New York and Washington,D.C. that so much is being
        dedicated to them.  Middle America doesn't matter to media and political
        types.  If charities want to donate, then I applaud them.  The victims'
        families should feel blessed that so many gave from their hearts to ease
        their suffering, but taxpayers should not be obligated via this fund to pay
        anything to them.  If so, then anyone that dies by whatever means will be
        compensated lest they be discriminated.  Let's leave this to the charities.

        Individual Comment
        Alvin, Texas
