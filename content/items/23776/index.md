        Tuesday, March 12, 2002 6:22 PM
        Kenneth Feinberg's appearance on "Meet the Press"


        I have heard about Kenneth Feinberg's appearance on the March 10 broadcast
        of NBC's "Meet the Press," and I am outraged.  Do you realize how far behind
        the United States is on the issue of recognizing gay relationships?  We are
        supposed to be the country of freedom, justice, and opportunity yet we lag
        behind countries such as Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Iceland, The Netherlands,
        France, and Germany who have already passed partnerships laws.  Even
        Finland, Portugal, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Belgium, and Spain are
        considering passing such laws.  What does it say about the land of the free
        that we still will not recognize the loved ones of gays and lesbians,
        especially when providing relief for such a tragedy as the deaths on
        September 11, 2001?  Feinberg said that the United States is willing to over
        look those who have illegally entered our country and those who illegally
        employeed them, but the our country will not provide relief for its gay and
        lesbian citizens.  How dare you to overlook them.  Even the American Red
        Cross recognizes the need to provide relief for the partners of gay and
        lesbian victims.  Some of those gay and lesbian victims are recognized as
        heros for their contributions and bravery that day including
        and           .  How could you deny the rights of your
        fellow citizens and their loved ones?  I realize that I am merely a voice in
        the crowd, but I swear to you that I will use that voice to continuing
        fighting for respect and protection of ALL people.  Do not miss your chance
        to honor the gays and lesbians lost.  Do not deny their partners and
        families of the respect that others will recieve.
        Individual Comment
