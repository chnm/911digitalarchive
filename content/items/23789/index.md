        Monday, March 11, 2002 10:28 AM
        9/11 Support


        Could you please explain to me why some gay Americans are being left out?  I
        really would like to know!  First, they are Americans!  AMERICANS!!  Should
        they not be allowed the same benefits under this Fund BEFORE an illegal
        alien?  Those illegal aliens, if lost loved ones, deserve the compensation
        just like everyone else.  However, I would think an American Citizen, someone
        who actually lives here LEGALLY, would get a nod and compensation long before
        someone living here illegally.  I sincerely doubt anyone is going to stop and
        say, "Let's sue because some gay person got benefits and our state doesn't
        allow that!"  Do you know what kind of ill will and basic dislike that person
        would receive?  Not just from gay americans (There's that word again -
        AMERICANS!!) also from other people who are heterosexual.  For someone, to
        actually attach a lawsuit again someone who suffered through the 9/11 with
        their loved ones dying, would be horrendous!  That person would probably be
        received as a terrorist in his own country for something like that.  All
        those people died for no reason whatsoever, and they will always be
        remembered.  However, why should some of them be considered lesser of a
        memorial than others?  Surely it would not be of their sexual orientation?
        Come on one would think this country, The Land of the Brave and the Free, is
        not going to let someone go unnoticed!  I don't know, maybe I'm just one of
        those people that think everyone should be equal.  If you stop and think
        about it, people that say gay people want gay rights....What they are simply
        asking for is basic human rights!  Is that hard to believe?  Right thats are
        given to every person that live in this country, are given rights, however
        some people are out there that wish to deny people that.  We like to call
        ourselves The United States, but how United are we?  Maybe that should be a
        question posed to every American (Funny, how that word keeps popping up -
        AMERICANS!!)  Maybe in a perfect world things will run smoothly, however,
        until then we should be making the best out of it, not trying to divide the
        country even further.

        Individual Comment
