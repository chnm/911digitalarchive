        compensation for terrorist victims

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I hope very much that those in your department who are drafting the
        guidelines for compensation to be given out to victims of the Sept.
        11 terrorist attacks will see to it that NO ONE is excluded from such
        compensation because they may be the surviving partners or children
        of gay or lesbian individuals who were killed in the attack.  Such
        discrimination against any innocent victims would be the grossest
        injustice. It's hard to believe that any government department acting
        as the agent of the United States would even consider such blatantly
        prejudiced action.

        Please pass my strong protests against such bias on to all those who
        may be connected with the victims' fund.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Austin, TX
