        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 3:16 AM

        Greetings,

           Like many of my fellow Americans my heart went out to the victims
        of the 9/11 tragedy - my wife and I donated generously in an attempt
        to assist the families affected by these horrible events.  Suffice to
        say that we certainly expected the overwhelming percentage of what
        we gave to go *directly* to those personally impacted by the
        disaster - when I hear reports that the American Red Cross will be
        distributing less than 20% of the money it collected to the actual
        victims I am sickened and angered.  All the hand waving and bureaucratic
        explanations in the world cannot shake my unswerving conviction that
        this outcome is simply, unequivocally wrong.

           You all are the "department of justice" well, where is the justice
        here?  My wife and I are kind hearted but we are not wealthy - had we
        known what a fiasco this would all turn out to be we probably would have
        explored other alternatives.  I never expected things to go the way they
        apparently have, it is a monumental injustice.

           The sad thing is - none of this actually makes life better for those
        families who have been ripped apart by this senseless violence ... if
        nothing else can be done, please publish a validated list of names
        and addresses and my wife and I will see what we can do to come up
        with something additional to help.  The aid situation is truly a
        travesty but that should not deter our and steadfast determination to
        truly bring whatever relief we can muster to the victims of the
        Sep. 11 attacks.

        Individual Comment
        Kingwood, Texas
