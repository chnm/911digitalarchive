      text: |-
        Thursday, March 07, 2002 5:16 PM
        Re: Victim Compensation Fund (9/11)


        I feel it is an absolute atrocity the people directly affected by the
        destruction of the Twin Towers are going to become rich over this
        tragedy.  I feel they, of course, are due SOME compensation, but
        referencing your own 'Loss Calculation Tables' many of these people are
        going to become INSTANT millionaires.  In my mind this is absolutely
        unacceptable!  This is a terrible precedent to set.  I say give the
        victims families one years wages and the children a fully paid college
        education. Period!!!  Of course, without prejudice, the fallen/injured
        police and fireman and their immediate families should get MUCH more.

        I am retired U.S Navy... I, by choice, gave my country the best 20 years
        of my life.  It is no secret that much of what was promised career
        military in the 50s and 60s has evaporated.  In truth, we haven't even
        been given a decent cost of living raise in the 14 years its been since
        I retired.  And our pensions are not near what our 'government
        employees' counterparts are.  Basically, guess what I am saying is,
        "Where is our million dollars?'  We certainly earned it.

        Also, giving the victims of 9/11 so much sets up a free for all for
        future incidents.  Are we going to henceforth make all families of
        tragedies of this type millionaires?  What about the families and
        victims of the Trade Center bombings in 1993?  Or the Oklahoma City
        Bombing?  Or the victims of the many tornados, earthquakes, and tornados
        in our country every year?  Or the families of  the people killed on our
        highways everyday?  Or the shooting victims families? The list is endless.

        And the victims of the Twin Towers were not the only ones hurt by the
        attack.  What about all the people laid off from their jobs because of
        it?  Perhaps we should give all them a million dollars also...

        In summary, giving these people so much is a BAD idea.  Let them make it
        in this world just like the rest of us. We all know this war against
        terror is going to be lengthy and costly both in money and lives.  Our
        kids (soldiers) are giving THEIR lives for this campaign and their
        beneficiaries only get $100,000.  Let's get realistic!

        Individual Comment
        Las Vegas, NV
