        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 4:40 PM
        9/11 Compensation

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public
        comments regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing
        and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."
        I strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that ensure
        compensation is available to all victims of the attack,
        including the committed partners and the non-biological children
        of gay, lesbian, or heterosexual victims.  In addition, it is important to
        remember those relatives of victims who were killed while performing jobs
        that many legal residents and citizens prefer not to do.  In other words,
        those who happened to be working without legal papers to do so still deserve
        compensation.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic
        losses suffered by the families of gay, lesbian, and illegal alien victims
        are no less than that of other families.  Just as the
        terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims, our country must draw no
        lines in compensating those who suffered the most personal of
        losses from these horrific attacks.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom
        left behind loved ones.  I urge the Department to honor these
        bonds by treating the families of gay, lesbian, and illegal alien victims as
        you would any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Austin, TX
