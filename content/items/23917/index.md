        December 17, 2001

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office fo Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        We are writing in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering
        the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."  My husband and I
        strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that ensure compen-
        sation is availabe to all victims of the attack, including the committed
        partners and the non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        It is hard for us to fathom the devastating grief and economic losses
        suffered by the families of gay and lesbian victims are not less than that
        of other families.  Just as the terrorists drew no lines in choosing those
        who suffered the most personal of losses from these horrific attacks.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind
        loved ones.  I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the
        families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any other case.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Westtown, NY
