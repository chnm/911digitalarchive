        hilt because he is off work and we are spending everything we have to keep
        our heads above water.  We have gotten no special treatment-no one is willing
        to help with our house payments- or pay our bills- until we get back on our
        feet!  We must pay or be out on the street. We are losing the battle- so
        don't expect us to feel sorry for someone who is getting a 1.4 million payoff
        for the loss of a loved one. If my husband died today from his disease I
        would get NOTHING except bankrupt!  Thank You.
