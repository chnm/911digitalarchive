        Sunday, December 16, 2001 12:35 PM

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice



        December 16, 2001


        Dear Director Zwick:

        I have friends in New York City that are two women who
        have been together for 15 years.  They are as married
        as I am in a heterosexual relationship.  Six years ago
        they adopted a child that was abandoned by a
        drug-addicted white middle class young woman.  This
        child has been raised in love, with two hard working
        parents.  One of her parents conducted tours at the
        World Trade Center and for some amazing reason was off
        on September 10th.  If she had died, restrictions to
        your program would have kept the child from receiving
        compensation for the loss of her parent.

        I urge you work for regulations governing the
        "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" which will
        ensure that compensation is available to all surviving
        families of those killed, INCLUDING the committed
        partners and non-biological children of gay and
        lesbian victims.

        They are all hardworking Americans.  They deserve to
        be treated as such.  Thank you.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
        Athens, GA
