        September 11th Victim's Fund

        Please take into consideration those very real
        "families" that do not fit into the "traditional"
        molds.

        I lost my life partner when she went to work September
        11, 2001 in the WTC buildings.  I have been crying and
        grieving everyday since then, the SAME as hundreds and
        thousands of other "spouses" who have lost their loved
        ones.

        We shared our home, shared bills, shared love and many
        happy times together.  We had made a commitment to
        each other and were indeed "family" to each other. We
        were first & foremost to each other.

        I firmly believe domestic partners whether same-sex or
        not, absolutely MUST be acknowledged and afforded the
        SAME rights, privileges and priority as would any
        other 'legal' spouse.

        Why should a brother or sister who did NOT provide for
        any type of support either financially or emotionally
        in addition to those who disapproved of their lost
        sibling's life-style now find themselves with a
        potential "windfall" simply because they are "next of
        kin".

        I am taking medication just to function normally, my
        work has suffered due to days missed because its
        difficult to know that she's gone and now I'm alone so
        subsequently I am docked pay.

        There is no amount of money that can give me back the
        person that I have loved.  She was taken from me
        because of the ignorance and hatred of others.  To
        provide financial compensation without consideration
        as to how and with whom a victim shared their life
        would be wrong.

        Please DO NOT DISHONOR HER - Please DO THE RIGHT
        THING.

        Do not insult or dishonor the many others who are in
        my very same situation.

        Thank you.

        Individual Comment
