        Wednesday, November 07, 2001 5:34 PM
        rules for victim's fund

        To be fair, there should be a limit on total aid received.

        I say this as both a taxpayer and a charitable contributer. Financial
        resources are finite and the victims eligible for aid are not the only
        people to suffer from the WTC disaster; unemployment is climbing and the
        economy is stalling.

        My opinion is that victims should receive the monies donated to charities
        first, including the Red Cross, and the government should fill in the gaps.

        The loved ones lost in the disaster cannot be replaced by dollars. While
        their families deserve financial assistance, they should not unreasonably
        profit at the expense of others.

        Individual Comment
        Fayetteville, AR
