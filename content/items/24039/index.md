        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 5:33 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation to Unmarried Couples

        As President Bush is finalizing his decision regarding the rules for
        distributing the September 11th victims' fund, I would like to add my voice
        to those who believe that all dependent survivors should be compensated, not
        just those with a marriage certificate.  While some argue that the sanctity
        of marriage can only be protected by providing relief money to the partners
        and children of victims who have a marriage license, obviously the true
        spirit of giving financial relief to those in need due to the September 11th
        tragedy  can only be achieved by examining ALL documentation and providing
        compensation to ALL persons and/or children who had financial dependence
        upon the September 11th victims.

        The ability of a man and a woman to obtain holy, matrimonial union will in
        no way be diminished by the government acknowledging and providing for all
        partners and children of all of the September 11th victims, despite the
        inflammatory, rhetorical arguments of some religious leaders.  Thank you for
        listening and doing the right thing.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Pacifica, CA
