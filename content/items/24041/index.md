        no double dipping

        The purpose of a government fund to compensate victims is just that:
        compensating victims.  It should function as a government insurance fund,
        compensating those who were not covered elsewhere.  Enriching victims (or
        vicitm families) should not be the fund's goal.  Charity money should offset
        fund payments.  Moreover, it is unquestionable that access to charity moneys
        is not equal for all (so many victims were low-paid immigrants), and we are
        risking making the well-connected (or at least the well-informed and
        well-versed in English) become the better-off yet again.

        Individual Comment
