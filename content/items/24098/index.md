        Compensate domestic partners


        Kenneth Feinberg:

        My husband and I contributed generously to the September 11 Fund with the
        understanding that our contribution would go to the survivors of the
        September 11 tragedy--period. I was deeply dismayed to read, in

        ' column on About.com, that you intend to discriminate against
        survivors based on their sexual orientation or domestic partner living
        arrangement--yet you sanction benefits for non-citizens, even those who are
        in the United States illegally.

        This is an OUTRAGE. Your claim that it is acceptable for the September 11
        Fund administrators to discriminate against domestic partners and same-sex
        couples because most of the states discriminate against these people is
        completely unjustified. Sexual orientation has no bearing on the pain of
        losing a loved one, especially in such a horrible tragedy. As fund
        administrator, you should be doing everything in your power to mitigate the
        suffering of the September 11 victims--not add to it. I am appalled and
        shocked at your decision, and regret that my husband and I contributed to
        your fund. You should be ashamed of yourself. I am very ashamed for you.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
