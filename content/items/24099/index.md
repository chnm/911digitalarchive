        Thursday, March 21, 2002 10:27 AM
        Comment on 9/11 compensation

        I urge you to be inclusive in the distribution of compensation for this
        tragedy, particularly toward gay and lesbian families.  Gays and lesbians
        are already suffering under the antigay discrimination of our marriage
        laws. They stand to have that discrimination elevated to the level of
        persecution if we deny the survivors of that tragedy compensation using
        those same discriminatory laws as justification!

        We are in our hearts a fair country.  Polls show that 75% of Americans feel
        that gay and lesbian families should be extended some form of
        recognition.  Our state laws have not caught up with this change in the
        will of our citizenry.  I understand your urge to comply with state law,
        but these extraordinary circumstances demand that you honor the will of our
        people, not the antiquated nature of many of our existing laws on
        recognizing same sex relationships.  Certainly the suffering caused by the
        events of September 11 and their aftermath have not discriminated against
        anyone.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Oakland, CA
