        Monday, November 05, 2001 4:09 PM



        The concept of "victim compensation" due to the 9/11 terrorist  attacks is
        good; the implementation will be a nightmare.  I would hope that  there is
        a maximum amount that can be paid out to any one person/family.   Frankly,
        I would think it would be equitable to pay a set amout, like $100,000  or
        $1 Million or whatever,  to the family of each victim.
        However, I can't help but wonder if we aren't setting a  precendent by
        doing this; in that after the next disaster, natural or man-made,  people
        will expect or think that they are "entitled" to victim  compensation.
        And finally, the first lawsuit that is filed having to do with  9/11 should
        be flat out thrown out and not even considered!  It is not one  company's
        fault or one person's fault; I mean who would have even  considered the
        possibility of what happened even happening (except  Hollywood)?  If
        anything maybe our own complacency and our misplaced need  for political
        correctness contributed.

        Individual Comment
        San Francisco, CA
