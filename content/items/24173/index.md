        Friday, December 28, 2001 8:54 PM
        Victim Compensation Rules

        This is a travesty of justice.

        As a "widow" of September 11th, I am outraged at this "Federal Compensation
        Program".  It does no more than victimize us again.  Because my husband
        chose to educate himself, work hard, build a career, PAY into an insurance
        program AND GO TO WORK ON SEPTEMBER 11TH, I am penalized?  What kind of
        justice is this?  You are considering the insurance that my husband paid
        into some kind of "reward" for me and my daughter?  I think not!

        We get up each day and wonder how we will get through it.  We pray to God
        for the strength to deal with all that has come our way.  We did not ask for
        this.  Our family was a very happy one.  It has been destroyed in a matter
        of moments.

        Our loved ones were left unprotected, they were told to stay put ... that
        all was under control.  The elevator mechanics left their posts which caused
        full elevators to be stranded.  There were elevators that were NOT working.
        The "heroes" were being called OUT of the buildings.  These were needless
        deaths.

        You want an opinion ... take those frozen terrorist assets and start to
        distribute them.  All the money in the world will not bring back my husband
        and my daughter's father.  All the money in the world will not cause the
        pain to go away.  However, someone is culpable!  THIS SHOULD NOT HAVE
        HAPPENED!

        Looking forward to hearing a reply.

        Individual Comment
