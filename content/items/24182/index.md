        December 5, 2001


        Mr. Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Div.
        U.S. Dept. of Justice

        FAX: 301-519-5956

        RE:  REGULATIONS FOR IMPLEMENTING "SEPT. 11 VICTIM
             COMPENSATION FUND - Equal Treatment for All
             Who are Affected

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        Thank you for inviting public comments re the above.

        I know that some people want to have these regulations exclude committed partners and
        non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims from this fund.

        It's inconceivable to me that human beings who suffered and suffer every bit as much as
        any others resulting from September 11 and are every bit as entitled to equal help would
        be excluded.

        I look to you to ensure that these regulations treat everyone involved with complete
        equality and basic human fairness.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
