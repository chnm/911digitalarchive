      text: I was at work as a U.S. Marine stationed at Camp Lejeune NC.  I was assigned as the Company Commander of Bridge Company, 8th Engineer Support Battalion.  I was returning to the company office from checking on platoon training.  The agitated Company Clerk informed me that the media was reporting a plane had crashed into the world trade center.  My immediate thought was it was a deliberate attack, but I quickly pushed that snap judgment aside in favor of waiting for further details and the possibility of other explanations.  I continued about my duties.  The Company Clerk continued to monitor the news via the web.  He announced, now on the verge of loosing his composure, that a second plane had struck the second tower.  That announcement removed all doubt about the nature of the tragedy; it was clearly a coordinated terrorist attack.
