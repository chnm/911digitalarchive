        Tuesday, January 15, 2002 2:37 PM
        victim compensation fund

        My name is     and I am writing to express my unhappiness
        regarding the current regulations for the victims of the September 11th
        attacks.

        My brother in law,     who was only 32 years old, was
        killed in the attacks. He worked for    , and was attending a
        meeting on the     floor of 1World Trade Center. My twin sister,    ,
        and her son,    , who was only 10 days old on the
        day of the attacks,born September 1, 2001, are left without a husband,
        father and best friend.

        Under the current regulations, my twin sister and godson will receive
        little if any compensation, because the current regulations allow for a
        set off for life insurance and other benefits, and puts a limit on the
        amount of earning potential, along with non-economic losses. With all
        the other devastating and stressful issues my sister faces on a daily
        basis, to watch her worry about these financial issues makes her life
        only that much more difficult.

        Specifically, the current regulations put a cap at $225,000 for yearly
        earning potential, which does not reflect current NY statistics.
        Furthermore, the cap for non-economic damages is unfair and heartless,
        given the lifetime of suffering for the families. There is no way to put
        a cap on this immeasurable pain and suffering, and the non-economic
        damages should be guaranteed, given the offsets already being applied
        against the total compensation. Furthermore, families should be fully
        informed in writing of what their compensation would be before waiving
        their rights to litigate.

        I am writing to ask your position on the current regulations, and to ask
        how you are representing the families who have been traumatized by this
        event. We need your help; and I urge you to represent the victims in
        these difficult times. Please participate in Peter King's campaign on
        our behalf by singing onto his legislative letter. Please also consider
        interacting with the families at meeting and scheduled press
        conferences. Please send a letter to Special Master Feinberg requesting
        these changes.

        I thank you in advance on behalf of my sister and nephew, and on behalf
        of all the families who's lives have been forever altered by these
        cowardly acts.

        Individual Comment
