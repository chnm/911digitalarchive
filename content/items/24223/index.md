        Monday, March 11, 2002
        Copy of note sent to Attorney General John Ashcroft

        Copy of letter sent to Attorney General John Ashcroft:


        Dear Attorney General John Ashcroft:

        I am very disturbed to read an article on
        about.com about the 9/11 Fund.  Here is part of the
        article that pretty much sums it up:


        "pretty much everyone who died - including
        people who aren't even American citizens and were living in
        the US illegally - will be honored by the September 11th Fund
        as deserving of America's special recognition and thanks.
        The sole exception will be gay and lesbian Americans, because
        Feinberg and the 911 Fund wouldn't want to do anything
        contrary to US law.  (Unless of course it involves an illegal
        alien who isn't even American - then apparently it's okay to
        bend the rules.)"


        So, I am angry that illegal aliens can get compensation, yet
        some US citizens can't?  What gives here?

        As a US citizen, I formally protest this, and I am very angry that
        you would come up with such a policy!  It is wrong!!

        I sincerely hope you get plenty of negative feedback regarding this
        policy and that something is done.  Illegal aliens do not
        deserve compensation, your US citizens do!

        Thank you,
        Individual Comment
        Denver, CO
