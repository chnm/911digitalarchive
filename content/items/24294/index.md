        Mr. Kenneth L. Zwick, Director

        Dear Mr. Zwick,
        As an American citizen and a military spouse, I am furious to learn that the
        partners and family members of the gay and lesbian victims of the tragedies
        of Sept. 11 will not be entitled to the same benefits as others.  This is
        particularly disheartening when gays and lesbians figure prominently among
        the heroes of that awful day, including &nbsp , who helped bring down
        the flight in Pennsylvania; &nbsp . &nbsp , who died in the line of duty when the
        first tower fell; and &nbsp , who was &nbsp of the plane that
        crashed into the &nbsp .

        When the tragedies of September 11 are used - occasionally even exploited -
        as a rallying cry for unity among all Americans, I find it sad and decidedly
        "un-American" that some of the loved ones of those who acted most valiantly
        to save the lives of others would be excluded or slighted in the effort to
        provide relief.  I find this particularly upsetting when I hear that even
        unborn fetuses and the families of illegal aliens will be entitled to full
        benefits, while the families of many full-fledged citizens in good standing
        will not.  This is wrong.  If we are providing benefits for ALL, then let us
        truly provide them for ALL.

        Thank you for your consideration in this matter.

        Very sincerely,
