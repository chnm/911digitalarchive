        Victim Compensation Fund at Taxpayer Expense

        To Whom It May Concern:

        I was both outraged and, once again, disappointed with this compensation to
        the victims' survivors.  I am currently unemployed and have been since early
        this year.  I am a baby-boomer professional and have not been able to find a
        job with proper compensation for my profession since September 11th. Many
        people are the unseen victims of this terrorist attack.  So, at my expense,
        some people will now be getting almost a million dollars or more  ( not
        taxable for two years) so they will not have to worry about money.  Well,
        this is absolutely ridiculous !  They should not be given anything!  People
        are killed all of the time for good causes and their families do not receive
        this.  What about other terrorist attacks in the USA or in foreign countries?
         What about those who have given their lives for their country?  Families
        lose loved ones all of the time.  These people do not deserve this money.
        This money could be well spent in this country taking care of the homeless,
        the elderly, or given for other causes.  I do not blame the people in
        Oklahoma City for example for being angry about this decision.
        These people should be given the monies that were raised for this cause - - -
        no more!!!!!
        They want to be rich and never have to work again at the taxpayers' expense.
        Well, life is tough and, although tragic that this happened, unfair deaths
        occur every day.

        What will happen when there is another attack? These people are not deserving
        of these federal funds and must face reality like everyone else.
        I am so angry and sick about this , it makes me want to move out of the
        country!!!!

        NOT WITH MY TAX DOLLAR. . .
        Individual Comment
