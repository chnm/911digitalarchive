        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 6:04 PM
        Fair Compensation for All Victims of 9/11

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering
        the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund." I urge the Department to
        ensure that all victims of the attack are compensated, including the
        committed partners and the non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        The grief and economic losses suffered by the families of gay and
        lesbian victims are no less than that of other families.  Just as the
        terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims, our country must
        draw no lines in compensating those who suffered the most personal of
        losses from these horrific attacks.

        All Americans who lost their lives on September 1 left behind loved
        ones.  I urge the Department to treat the families of gay and lesbian
        victims as you would any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
