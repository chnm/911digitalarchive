        Thursday, November 29, 2001 11:09 AM
        Victim compensation fund

        I understand that right now you are in the development
        stage of the rules that will govern the administration
        of the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund.  I
        believe the purpose of this Fund is to provide
        compensation to individuals who were injured, or to
        relatives of those who were killed, as a result of the
        events of September 11th.

        In my opinion, I believe it is essential that the fund
        includes compensation for those relatives who lost
        their life partners or de facto parents or children
        regardless of sexual orientation or marital status.
        Let us not compound this tragedy of Sept 11th by
        discriminating against those people who lost their
        loved ones.

        Please take what I have said in consideration as you
        write up the rules for the fund.

        Thank you,
        Individual Comment
        Chicago, IL
