        Friday, January 11, 2002 1:40 PM
        911 Fund comment

        Special Master Feinberg:

        As an attorney providing pro bono representation to a widow of a Port
        Authority emplyee killed in Tower One who left, an addition to his wife, five
        young children, please add these to my previous comments:

        1.  Presumed compensation for non-economic loss per dependant should be
        increased to a minimum of $100,000 per dependant.

        2.  No collateral source deductions should be made from any payments for
        non-economic loss.  To do so would be contrary to the law of virtually every
        state in a wrongful death litigation;

        3.  There should be a maximum dollar set off for collateral source benefits
        of $500,000.  Deductions in excess of that amount would emasculate the
        purpose of the Fund, and, as a result, thousands of claimants might well
        choose to forego claims from the Fund and thrust these claimants into what
        will be an overburdoned litigation system.

        Individual Comment
        Newark, NJ
