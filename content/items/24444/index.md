        Sunday, March 17, 2002 8:45 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        FOR THE MEMORY OF:






        And so many more...

        I am writing to express my outrage and to protest the blatant
        discrimination against gay Americans which appears to be fundamental to
        the implementation of the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund.

        In an appearance on the Sunday, March 10 broadcast of NBC's "Meet the
        Press," Kenneth Feinberg, the head of the September 11th Victim
        Compensation Fund (a fund created by Congress and run by the Department
        of Justice), said that gay partners of the heroes of September 11th will
        not necessarily be eligible for the same compensation as heterosexual
        family members who lost their loved ones.

        At the same time, in reading the details of the fund, I find that even
        illegal aliens will be receiving benefits under this fund. but "not
        necessarily" gay-Americans? Apparently, there's even a promise to
        overlook federal law and NOT arrest and kick out the families of illegal
        aliens, nor will American companies be punished for hiring illegals in
        violation of federal labor laws.

        At the same time, the law is being cited as the justification for
        discriminating against the loving partners and loved ones of the gay
        Americans and gay American heroes who died in the tragedy. How
        hypocritical and unjust! This is hardly the America I spent 20 years of
        my life defending as a proud member of the United States Navy.

        I call upon the administrators of the fund to implement in a way that is
        fair to all of the victims, not just some of them. I applaud the
        decision to waive law in the case of illegal aliens, but we should also
        waive the discriminatory laws that would prevent Americans, albeit gay
        Americans, from receiving the same benefits.

        Regards,

        Individual Comment
