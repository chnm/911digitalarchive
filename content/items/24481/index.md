        Monday, March 18, 2002 9:24 AM
        Discrimination in Victims' Benefits

        Dear Sirs:

        I am saddened that benefits won't be awarded equally to gay American
        citizens whose partners died as a result of the September 11 attacks,
        and outraged by the fact that illegal aliens will receive the same
        benefits as legal American citizens.

        Whose brilliant idea was that?  It is very clear that the INS is
        ineffective in keeping these illegals out of the country, but to award
        these aliens and not punish the companies that hired them while knowing
        full well they were here illegally in this country is an outrage.

        Maybe we should go backward in time and be treated like African
        Americans used to be treated.  We could have gay only restrooms, gay
        only drinking fountains and have to sit in the back of the bus, giving
        up our seats when heterosexuals need to sit down.  How about banning
        gays from attending heterosexual-only schools, forbidding us to look a
        heterosexual in the eye when we walk the streets or face jail time?

        By not awarding same sex partners the identical benefits as married
        couples, you are putting us in the back of the bus as second and third
        class citizens.  Is this the message you want to send to us, our country
        and the rest of the world?  I am totally embarrassed by your bias and
        bigotry and totally ashamed of my government because of this policy.
        And you should be ashamed too.  You call yourself the Department of
        Justice?  It's more like the Department of Just Us!

        Individual Comment
