        To Whom It May Concern,

        I am writing to you in response to requests for comments concerning the
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund.  I am a Rabbi of an upstate Reform
        synagogue that has an open and welcoming policy towards gay and lesbian Jews.
         It is unquestionably immoral to discrimate against gay and lesbian families
        by not affording them the same financial relief than non-gay and lesbian
        victims of the terrorist attack.  Does a child of a same gender family not
        need food or clothing? Does a gay or lesbian partner of a victim of terror
        not mourn? Does he or she not suffer from financial devastation due to the
        loss of an income?

        It is imperative that you compensate families justly and without
        discrimation.

        Individual Comment
