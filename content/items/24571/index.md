        America's love for our heroes


        I read an article about the 911 fund not going to gay families who lost their lives on that horrible day trying to save others.  I think this is ridiculous these people deserve it just as much as anyone else.  The 911 fund was here for the victims families and just because these people are gay you are trying to say they were not victims.  I am not gay I have a family and a husband (so I guess if I was the victim you would give me the 911 fund).  But my family consists of a lesbian (my sister) I love my sister she will always be my sister and she will always be an AMERICAN. I do not agree with her action but I do not have to live it.  The day of 911 everyone was proud to be an American not just the "straight" Americans but all Americans including the gays.  How do you explain to the children who's moms or dads are gay why they are excluded even if they are straight?  The 911 fund should be for all heart broken families.

        Please listen to this proud American Mom ( GOD BLESS)
