        Since September 11th, we've seen the uniting of the United States of America.
         It's made me proud to call myself and American... the way we've banded
        together across society's lines:  skin color, religion, sex... and even
        sexual preference.  Now those lines are being redrawn, and I'm ashamed.
        Every family that suffered losses deserve to be compensated, not just the
        families that a select group of people recognize.  Yes, laws are laws.  If
        that is to be the argument for omitting gay families from receiving benefits,
        then perhaps ALL laws should be followed to the letter.  It disgusts me that
        illegal aliens are recognized and receive help while legal Americans suffer
        without, simply because some parts of society don't recognize their kind of
        family.

        We all lost something September 11th.  Don't take away what faith we have
        left in the world by separating what took a tragedy to bring together... the
        AMERICAN people.  Gay, straight, black, white, Christian, Jewish-- we all
        lost.
        Individual Comment
