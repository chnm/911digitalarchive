        Sep 11 fund: A shameful ruling


        It is unconscionable to me, at a time when the administration is calling for
        unity among Americans, that President Bush and Mr. Feinberg would countenance
        a ruling that basically leaves the families of Septembere 11 many gay victims
        out in the cold.
        Gay people were among the most visible heroes of that dark day, so to learn
        that their partners of up to 30 years are being denied the compensation that
        the administration is even granting to the families of illegal aliens is a
        shocking and deeply cynical maneuver by an administration that seems more
        interested in throwing a bone to its right wing than in protecting the rights
        of tax-paying gay and lesbian citizens. This is shameful, and un-American,
        and runs counter to the very values that we are supposedly fighting to defend.
