        Tuesday, March 19, 2002 11:19 AM
        9-11 Fund Fairness and Non-Discrimination

        To 9-11 Fund Administrators;



         I am a heterosexual white male who works within what used to be the World
        Trade Center. I am saddened and deeply troubled to learn that gay and
        lesbian partners of those who perished in the attacks will not automatically
        receive the same benefits as heterosexual spouses.  I am not a "gay
        activist", but I DO believe in equal protection under the law and basic
        fairness without prejudice.  It would be an un-American outrage to victimize
        the families and partners of people who died at the World Trade Center based
        on their sexual orientation. We are not the Taliban.  This fund should act
        in non-descriminatory compassionate manner.  Please honor the memory of ALL
        who died by doing the right thing.  We must stand TOGETHER.



                     Respectfully,

                     Individual Comment
