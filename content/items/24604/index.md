        Effect on state workers' compensation awards

        Dear Mr. Zwick - I previously submitted a detailed article on the
        possible deliterious effect of a claimant accepting an award from the
        victims fund on their right to receive New York workers' compensation
        benefits.  I was gratified to see that this submission was acknowledged
        in the printed comments.  I want to again urge you to include in the
        regulations language that clarifies the effect of state workers'
        compensation benefits, whether they will be considered a colleteral
        source, and whether the workers' compensation insurance carrier will be
        in a position to claim a lien under NY Work. Comp. Law section 29.  I
        believe if the regulations state cleartly that the victims fund benefits
        are not to  be considered as either wage replacement benefits or medical
        benefits, and if the regulations clearly states that they are intended
        to be in addition to workers' compensation benefits, and finally if they
        state that the intention is to not require the claimant to seek consent
        to settle from the workers' compensation carrier, then the problem I
        have identified would largely be eliminated.  Again, thank you for your
        consideration.

        Individual Comment
        Syracuse, NY
