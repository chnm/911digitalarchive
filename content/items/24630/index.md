        Saturday, March 16, 2002 7:55 PM
        In pursuit of fairness

        Dear Sir or Madam:

        I am outraged that the fund for victims of the
        September 11 tragedy will not cover the partners of
        gays and lesbian who died that day.

        I know that there are many in our country who were
        still stuck in old and invalid predudices, but I am
        truly saddened to learn that they still exist at your
        level.

        I fear that this plea falls on deaf ears, but I am
        bound by my heart to make it.  I have a lesbian sister
        and many gay and lesbian friends, and I know that they
        are no different from the rest of us in the ways that
        matter.  And certainly not in the area of family.  A
        gay/lesbian family contains just as much love,
        commitment, and morality as any other family.

        Please reconsider your decision in this area.



        Individual Comment
        Auburn, WA
