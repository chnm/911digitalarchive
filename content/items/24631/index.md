        Monday, March 11, 2002 1:57 PM

        US Denial of 9/11 Fund Monies to Gay Americans




        To whom it may concern,

        I have just become aware that gay and lesbian partners of the Sept. 11
        tragedy victims are Not eligible for  benefit checks issued by the US
        government 9/11 Fund.  It is my understanding that even illegal
        immigrant partners are to step forward to receive their checks.  Our
        American Heroes who helped save lives, gave their own or were senseless
        victims are no less deserving because they were homosexual.  Kenneth
        Feinberg's denial of benefits by weakly deferring to individual state's
        treatment of gay and lesbian citizens is WRONG.  These partners are No
        Less traumatized by the loss of their loved ones than any other person
        and are No Less deserving of the same treatment as any other Human
        Being.
        This is an outrage and will not be tolerated by the US gay community and
        any other sane, feeling person who believes in Human Rights.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Durango Colorado
