        January 9, 2002

        Director, Office of Management Programs Kenneth Zwick
        USDoJ, Main Building, Room 3140
        950 Pennsylvania Avenue
        Washington, DC  20530


         Director, Office of Management Programs Zwick:

        as someone who lost my father in the world trade center along with many
        peole that my family knew that worked in the upper floors of 1 world trade
        center, i feel that the proposed details and stipulations of the fund are
        extremely unfair to many.  my mother, brother and I were very
        disssapointed when we heard the results.  my father was 54 years old, was
        planning on working for many more years, he loved his job, had worked
        there for about 20 years and was very successful at what he did.  he made
        much more that the cap of $225,000.  i do not see how one can cap
        someone's earnings at that rate.  then to take out what someone's family
        has received in life insurance and donations from the many charities.  one
        would think that at a time like this, that the government would give some
        kind of payback to those people that have been paying the majority of the
        taxes over the years.  basically as it works out, we would not see any
        such beneifts from the fund, we might even end up paying the government
        money.  if the government could spend all those billions of dollars to
        bail out the airlines after the weeks of sept. 11, you would think that it
        could assist those people better.  i could see if my father had died of
        natural causes, but this was an act or terrorism.  there should be some
        kind of better solution to this fund to make it equitable to those on the
        higher end of the scale who have been eliminated from the scale or capped
        out at $225,000.  some may think that if someone made all that money that
        they dont need any more assistance from the fund, but guess what , my mom
        has mortgages, she has cars, she pays taxes and she does have normal
        expenses like everyone else.  they may be proportionately higher, but they
        are still expenses. it should be interesting to see what eventually
        happens, i know many peole that have lost their spouse, and they are
        extremly unhappy. thank you for allowing me to express a small portion of
        my opinion.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
        West Palm Beach, FL
