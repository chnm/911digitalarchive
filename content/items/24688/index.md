        Thursday, December 06, 2001 9:11 AM


        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing in response to the Departrment of Justice's request
        for public comments regarding the forthcoming regulations for
        implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund."  I strongly, like so many other Americans, believe that
        the Department must ensure compensation is available to all victims of the
        September 11th attack, including gays and lesbians and their families.

        The losses suffered by the families of gay and lesbian victims are
        no less than that of other families.  Not compensating gays and lesbians
        would be unfair and further, would suggest that the federal government does
        not consider gays and lesbians to be equal under the law.

        I urge the Department of Justice to honor all victims of the September 11th
        disaster in an effort to treat all American's equally.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Silver Spring, MD
