        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 10:05 AM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund


         Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public
        comments regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing
        and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."
        My husband and I  strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that
        ensure
        compensation is available to all victims of the attack and we do mean all
        victims!
        All victims include undocumented aliens struggling to make a living as well
        as the
        committed partners and the non-biological children of gay and lesbian
        victims.

        We understand that many dependents of those workers whose papers were not in
        order are
        reluctant to come forward for fear they will be deported or suffer some
        other unwarranted reprisal.
        Their grief is no less severe and economic situation probably a lot worse
        than those with full
        citizenship. The losses suffered by the families of gay and lesbian victims
        are
        also no less than that of other families.  Just as the terrorists
        drew no lines in choosing their victims, our country must draw
        no lines in compensating those who suffered the most personal of
        losses from these horrific attacks.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom
        left behind loved ones. All are heros; all were part of the fabric that
        makes our great economy run.
        We urge the Department not only to honor these bonds by treating the
        familiesof gay and lesbian victims as well the
        aliens as you would any other but to actively make known their elgibility.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
