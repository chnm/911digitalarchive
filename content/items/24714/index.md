        Saturday, March 16, 2002 3:00 PM
        Compensation for all

        Fellow Americans:

        When terrorists attacked us, they did so
        indiscriminately. They did not murder just
        heterosexuals or secretaries or young people, their
        object was to kill as many Americans of all
        description as possible. Not even Muslims or foreign
        nationals escaped Osama's killing machine.

        If we are to heal, our compensation must likewise be
        universal. We cannot perpetuate the freedoms we claim
        to defend by dividing ourselves into factions
        according to our sexuality, religion or other
        perceived differences among us.

        Accordingly, let us resolve that all victims,
        including those not living the lifestyle that we may
        have chosen, be included in compensation funding.

        Let us strengthen ourselves in unity rather than
        weaken our society by exaggerating differences our
        enemies neither see nor acknowledge when they attack
        us!


        Individual Comment
        Etna,NY
