        Comments "The September 11th Victims' Compensation Fund."



          Kenneth L. Zwick, Director

          Office of Management Progams

          U.S. Department of Justice



        Lincroft, NJ.





        A  special agency should be established (simular to the Veterans
        Administration) to assure that the funds are distributed fairly and that the
        victims are protected financially from now until the youngest dependents have
        completed their education,
        after this the funds expenses should decrease.

          The dependents should have Medical and Dental Insurance coverage available
        to them,paid for by the fund.

         A social worker should be made available for each victims family to help
        with the maze of paper work, and this paper work should be standardized so
        that all agencies are using universal type forms.
        The victims children should have educational benefits made available for them
        until their education is completed at the "Funds" expense.
        Income taxes both Federal and State should be eliminated.
        Donated money was intended for the victims families and that's where it
        should go.  Charities and funding institutions should be held to strict
        accountability and should be required to publish administration costs to
        include executives salaries          When computing annual allowances these
        figures should not be decreased based on the deceased life insurance, 401K or
        IRA.


                                                                    Sincerely,




        Individual Comment

        Lincroft, NJ
