        Gay partner benefits


        Dear Sir/Madam,

        I just read an article that I hope is incorrect.  The article stated that the
        9/11 fund will not look at gay relationships in the same way as their
        heterosexual counterparts.  I find this approach to the fund distribution to
        be unacceptable.  Gay relationships should be treated like all other
        relationships.  The fact that they cannot marry is not due to lack of
        desire.....The excuse that the fund will defer to state law is just that, an
        excuse to exclude certain Americans from just compensation.

        I want you to know that I will not benefit in any way from this
        fund...however, I do hope that my fellow gay Americans who are the surviving
        partners will be treated as everyone else.

        Individual Comment
