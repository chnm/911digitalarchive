        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 6:21 AM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        My name is from.  I would like to voice my
        concern that the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund be administered fairly
        to all who were affected by family losses in the September 11 tragedy.  I am
        particularly speaking of those who are gay, lesbian, transgendered or
        bisexual who lost their partners in the September 11 attack.  Also, their
        children, whether they were their biological or adopted children or the
        children of their partner with whom they lived.  I implore you to ensure that
        these families are treated with dignity and that they are compensated the
        same as all other families.

        Also, I believe people who are not citizens of this country, but who were
        working in this country legally and were killed ... their families should
        also share in the distribution.

        Thank you very much.

        Individual Comment
        Richmond, VA
