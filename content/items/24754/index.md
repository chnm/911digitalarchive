        Sept 11 Victims Compensation Fund

        The offset provisions contained in the legislation should be eliminated.
        They are inequitable. They penalize some families and not others.
        Furthermore, offsets for life insurance paid for by an individual and
        offsets for 401Ks have no counterpart in the tort system. Certain rights
        under the tort system were taken away from us by the "Airline Bailout" bill.
        Ken Feinberg himself has stated that the statute "stacks the deck" against
        litigating. It is completely unjust that we should be deprived of our full
        constitutional rights as potential litigants, and also be deprived of the
        full amount of our damages when forced to submit to the supposedly equitable
        alternative-the 'Victims Compensation Fund'.

        Individual Comment
        Stamford CT
