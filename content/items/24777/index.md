        Saturday, January 19, 2002 12:39 AM
         comments on Victims' Compensation Fund

        In determining the size of the federal compensation awards, I
        urge you:

        1. Not to exacerbate the gap between the rich and the poor (which
        has been growing in recent years)

        2. To make sure that women and minorities are treated fairly.

        3. To retain the cap on lost earnings, as set forth in your original
        guidelines.

        Money alone will not make the victims' families whole. Also, we have
        other unmet needs in this country. So, I urge moderation, especially
        at the top end.

        Individual Comment
        New York State resident
