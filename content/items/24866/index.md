        Friday, December 07, 2001 9:07 AM
        To Mr. Kenneth L. Zwick

        TO:
        Mr. Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        United States Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

            As the Department of Justice determines how assistance will be
        distributed to victims and survivors of the victims of the September 11
        attacks, please remember that gays and lesbians - and their partners and
        children - are no less devastated by loss, sorrow, horror, and grief
        than anyone else.  They may, in fact, have even fewer opportunities for
        financial assistance because of the laws which sometimes impede their
        collection of insurance or other benefits.  Please take this opportunity
        to provide relief to ALL the victims of these unthinkable events.
            Please do not allow anyone's politics or social agenda to prevent
        aid going to all those who need it.

            Sincerely and respectfully,



        Individual Comment
        Traverse City, MI
