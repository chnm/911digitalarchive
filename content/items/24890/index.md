        Dear benefits personnel,

        As an American, I was appalled and horrified at what the terrorists did to

        be compensated from this fund, yet American citizens can not?  I donated to
        those funds, and you have no right to discriminate against any Americans who
        were harmed that day. I will not go quietly into this good night without
        joining the other voices calling on our own government to live up to it's
        motto of "Justice for all".  Do not let this policy stand, as an American I
        can tell you the "Whole world is watching"!.
        Sincerely,
