        we or are we not all equal?!  You know?  One nation under God, indivisible,
        with liberty and justice for all?!!  What ever happened to that?

                 Do you really expect all of us to believe that another American is
        worth less than even a bloody FETUS or an illegal alien, even after having
        been murder in the line of duty to their country, simply because of what
        they did/do with their genitalia?!?!?!  Are you all insane?!!!  What kind
        of a God do you people worship that would justify this kind of inhumanity
        towards one's fellow man?  It MUST be an extremely hateful, petty, violent,
        arrogant and selfish god!  But make NO mistake -- it is NO god of LOVE!!!

                 What I REALLY think that this all boils down to is just another
        vicious form of good old-fashion GREED!!!  I think that this irresponsible
        and selfish move is just another way of GETTING MORE MONEY FOR
        THEMSELVES!  I mean, you do the math.  Less money given out, means,
        ultimately, MORE MONIES LEFT AVAILABLE FOR THEIR OWN SELFISH INTERESTS AND
        DIVERSIONS FOR THE COMPANY, AS WELL AS THE GOVERNMENT, ITSELF!!!  It's all
        such a no-brainer, isn't it?!

                 Instead of trying to drum up support for our efforts in the war on
        terrorism, what we truly ought to be doing is trying to get ride of these
        disgusting, hateful, inhuman and arrogant hatemongers currently infesting
        our OWN great nation, irregardless of where, whom or what institutions,
        companies, or government that they stand for.  Apparently, these fools
        don't even wish to look at the fact that some of our country's greatest
        leaders were, themselves, gay or bisexual!  Try Abraham Lincoln and George
        Washington for starters!

                 How wise Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. was when he gave that:


                         "Injustice anywhere is injustice everywhere!!!


                 In my opinion, such as it is, what is most dangerous about any and
        all extremist groups such as the far right and the September 11th Fund, is
        not that they take themselves seriously.  Most of us do.  But rather that
        they consistently condemn any and all other sexual, spiritual, and moral
        choices, such as the Buddhist, the Muslim, the Jew, the gay, lesbian,
        pro-choicers, etc., and many others who consider themselves to be upright
        and good Christians.  And rather than LOGICALLY and COMPASSIONATELY
        discussing such issues as gay rights, gay adoption(as if the United States
        or the world needed anymore homeless children in it!  And a GOOD parent is
        a GOOD parent, you morons!!!), or abortion, they disown as vile, hideous
        and evil all those who favor choice and tolerance.  They are consistently
        disowning their own dark side, while simultaneously magnifying everyone
        else's, until at the extreme, doctors are murdered in the name of
        protecting life, all Middle Easterner's are seen as murderers and zealots,
        and gays and lesbians are STILL being hunted, dehumanized, murdered, denied
        their equal rights, and demonized!  And all in the so-called "Land of the
        free, and the home of the brave?!"  What a joke!!!  You'll kindly forgive
        those of us who are not laughing!

                 In closing I'll only say that hate is hate!  Irregardless it's
        vision, mechanics, machinations or religious, political or self-righteous
        institutions and convictions!  HATE IS HATE IS HATE!!!  Even as
