        Victim's Compensation Fund Comment

        Dec. 5, 2001


        Mr. Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice
        Washington, DC




        Dear Mr. Zwick:


        It is imperative that the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" include
        the same levels and access to compensation for all surviving families of
        those killed, INCLUDING the committed partners and non-biological children
        of gay and lesbian victims, doing less than that would be an ultimate
        hypocrisy.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind
        loved ones.  I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the
        families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any other.

        Thank you.


        Respectfully,

        Individual Comment
        Mount Vernon, NY
