        Saturday, March 16, 2002 5:16 PM
        9/11 Fund Exclusion of Gays

        I am a college student, and being young I am assumed to be
        cynical and have little tolerance or respect for our
        government.  But the latest actions of our government bring
        my respect for our government to a new low.  The hypocrisy
        of our leaders is absolutely appalling.  I have lost my
        faith.  How can our country, our government, our leaders,
        and ourselves truly believe in a free world if we fail to
        let that freedom encompass all people?  We can no longer
        seek to find division amongst ourselves.

        The policies of division are what have dictated the past
        and can no longer be supported.  Our countries policies of
        division have separated landowners from non-landowners, men
        from women, whites from Blacks (and every other race),
        Christians from Jews and Muslims, and straight people from
        gays and lesbians.  When will we learn?  I would have
        thought that we would have learned September 11th.  I was
        under the impression that since September 11th, we have all
        come together, to be united as a country.  But in fact the
        opposite is true.  From our government's policies targeting
        Arab-Americans and other immigrants to the 9/11 fund's
        exclusion of gays, our country, our government, our
        leaders, and ourselves (if we choose to do nothing about
        it) have once again resorted to a policy of divisiveness.

        It is time -- in fact it has been time for several years
        now -- to take a stand.  We can no longer let our
        governments policies of division separate us.  We are all
        Americans, and more importantly we are all humans.  No
        amount of misguided laws, divisive policies, prejudice or
        fear can deny that fact.  As a college student, as the
        future of this country, I ask you to end this countrys
        policies of division and help unite us all, gay and
        straight alike, under the banner of humanity.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
