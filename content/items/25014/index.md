        9/11 Gay Discrimination

        I do not understand the remarks recently made by your representative to Tim
        Russert.  In clarifying that you must abide by the States' laws you have
        provided a contradiction to your own decision to provide compensation to
        illegal aliens.  Do State laws permit this?  It is explicit that they
        recognize illegal aliens in terms of other services, etc.  I believe it is
        only right that we provide assistance to illegal aliens and I think the INS

        are safeguarded from potential deportation.  Nevertheless, to then turn our
        back on U.S. citizens who happen to be in same-sex relationships seems
        completely absurd.  One of the heroes,
