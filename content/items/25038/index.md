        issued an official policy of fair treatment for gay and lesbian families
        suffering from the loss of a loved one on September 11. The guidelines
        provide
        a list of 16 possible criteria that can show an interdependent and
        supportive
        relationship, including joint rental leases, bank accounts, or credit cards;
        joint responsibility for child care; designation as the beneficiary of a
        life
        insurance policy or retirement benefits account; and nonfinancial criteria
        such as registration as domestic partners and certification of a union
        celebrated overseas.

        citizens. Please do this out of respect for the families and friends of the
