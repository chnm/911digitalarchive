        Sunday, December 09, 2001 1:38 PM
        Victim's Compensation


        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Justice Department's request for comments
        regarding the upcoming regulations for implementing and administering the
        "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."

        I encourage the Department to include in these regulations assurances that
        compensation will be available to all victims of the attack, including those
        gay and lesbian partners in committed relationships and the non-biological
        children of gay and lesbian victims.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic losses suffered
        by all the victims of the September 11 terrorist attack was traumatic.  The
        families of gay and lesbian victims suffered no less than other families from
        this national tragedy.

        At this time of national tragedy, and also at a time when the nation seems to
        be uniting as in no other time, it is important that the Justice Department
        take the appropriate action, as its name implies, to perform an act of
        "justice" for all.

        All victims, including families of gay and lesbians, should be justly
        compensated.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Montgomery, Alabama
