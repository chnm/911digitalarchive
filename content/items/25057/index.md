        Complaints about compensation

        Hello, I am a Viet Nam war Veteran and fellow american.  I don't
        understand the complaints about any compensation to be received.  I

        cowardly act.  Please, let's put things in perspective!  Besides my
        family, I have be told thank you only once.  That was by a complete
        stranger who overheard a conversation about conditions in Viet Nam.  I
        cried when this stranger shoke my hand and said "Thank You."

        A fellow american.
