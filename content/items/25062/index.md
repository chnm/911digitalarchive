        To whom it may concern:

        On a day when all are paying homage to the victims of the 911 disaster on
        the 6 month anniversary, I would strongly object to Mr Feinberg's support of
        discrimination against the gay and lesbian victims of the disaster.  By
        saying he will do what the individual states do to recognise gay and lesbian
        couples is a very twisted way to circumvent the entire issue (as we all know
        that few states support or even recognise gay and lesbian couples) and
        support discrimination against one segment of society.  If he can
        financially support others, such as the partners of illegal aliens, then why
        is he and the fund not able to support the partners of gays and lesbians?

        Thank you for passing on my concerns,


        A concerned citizen

        Individual Comment
        Dayton OH
