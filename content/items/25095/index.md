        January 31, 2002

        To whom it may concern,

        Both my family and I have been greatly affected by the Trade Center Disaster on 09/11.  I,
        indeed, lost my father.  At the time of his passing, I was receiving financial support that was vital
        to myself and to the rest of my family, including my infant son,            .

        I turned to many private charities for their help, and must admit, that the Red Cross was that
        which was best able to aid us in this financial difficulty.  I have been going through a lot.
        Although the help received was great, the bills are once again beginning to pile up, and fast.  I am
        writing this letter asking for help.  Like I said previously, I have turned to many organizations for
        help, and only the Red Cross and Salvation Army was able to help me whatsoever.  Truth be told,
        I am a young parent, one who was still dependant on my father for financial help.  With his
        passing, came many hardships for my new family.

        I thank you very much for your time, and am hopeful that the reader of this letter will view it as
        more than just another letter, but instead as a person who needs help.  These trying times have
        caused much difficulty for many families.  It has been hard for myself as well as some of the
        other families that I have met, to ask for help, since we never have had to before.  I would like
        someone to respond as promptly as possible.  I appreciate that which I have already received, but
        as aforementioned, I need Help!!!
        Individual Comment
        Kenilworth, NJ
