        Thursday, December 27, 2001 6:41 PM

        Family's Compensation

        To Whom it May Concern:

        We all found out on September 11th that anything can happen at any time.  I
        am writing because I was told that the family of New York Police Officer
        would be receiving an amount that the family felt was
        insulting.  No one can put a value on a human life, and if they could it
        certainly wouldn't be an amount as small as the one in question.  I am a
        taxpayer and I want my money that I pay out of my own pocket each week to
        benefit families like the.  I don't want to spend billions of dollars
        fighting a never ending war.  "Charity begins at home," is a phrase I often
        hear from a coworker.  I feel it couldn't be a more accurate statement.
        While we are sending food packages to a country that ultimately condemns
        America, our own are struggling to make ends meet after they lost a loved
        one.  Officer  family saw how drastically their lives could change in
        an instant, and who is to say that something else could befall these people
        in the future.  What will    's survivors do if they lose another source
        of income?  How will his children go to college?  If he has a daughter, who
        will pay for her wedding?  Stop and think how much you would feel your family
        were entitled to if something else had happened in Washington on that day.
        What if was the DOJ instead of the Pentagon?  Care for our own, before we
        care for others.  Please help this family, this may be all they have.  Thank
        you for your consideration.  God bless you and God bless America.


        Individual Comment
        Pittsfield, MA
