        Sunday, December 09, 2001 10:27 PM



        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing regarding the Department of Justice's request for public
        comments about regulations for setting up and administering the "September
        11 Victim Compensation Fund."

        I strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that ensure
        compensation is available to ALL victims of the attack,including the
        committed partners and the non-biological children of gay and lesbian
        victims.

        Pain of losing a loved one was shared by ALL families affected by the
        attacks on September 11th -- including the families of gay and lesbian
        victims. To not recognize this fact is to make people victims twice --
        first of terrorists, then of American policies that discriminate.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each one left behind loved
        ones.  I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the families
        of gay and lesbian victims as you would any other. I see this as a matter
        of simple justice.

        Yours for Equality,

        Individual Comment
        Bennington, VT
