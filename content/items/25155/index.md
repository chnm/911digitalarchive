        November 12, 2001


        Attorney General John Ashcroft
        U.S. Department of Justice
        10th Street and Constitution Avenue
        Washington, D.C. 20530


        Dear Attorney General Ashcroft,

        On September 11,  many innocent people lost their lives in an act of terror that forever impacted this
        nation.  It is important that we not forget them,  and that has already been evidenced by the actions of
        this nation's citizens and government.

        In that spirit, President Bush recently signed the Air Transportation Safety and System Stabilization Act,
        which includes a provision called the "September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001"  a
        government-sponsored fund for those victims who do not sue the airlines.

        Recently the Department of Justice announced that it is seeking public comment on the implementation
        of the victim compensation fund.  To truly honor and recognize all the lives lost in the September 11
        tragedy, I respectfully ask that you include the most innocent of victims as "eligible victims"   the
        unborn.  It is known that at least one unborn baby died along with his or her mother during the attacks,
        and no doubt there were more unborn lives lost that have yet to be known.  The families of those lost
        umborn babies are grieving too, and they deserve that loss to be recognized equally by their
        government.

        Thank you for your time and your leadership at this time.  You remain in our prayers here at Focus on
        the Family.


        Sincerely,

        Issues Response
        Comment by: Focus on the Family
