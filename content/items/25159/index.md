        Thursday, March 21, 2002 5:11 PM
        Equal Compensation For All



             It has come to our attention that there is an inequality in the law
        governing the benefits of the 9/11 fund.  We support the fund and it's use,
        but we are very upset that spouses (i.e., partners) and households of
        same-gender families are not entitled to equal compensation under the law.
            We in same-gender households live as families, just as surely as those in
        heterosexual homes.  We love, care for, and depend on one another.  Many in
        same-gender unions are as dependant on one another's income as their
        heterosexual counterparts.   Unfortunately, we are unable to engage in any
        form of officially recognized union (i.e., marriage).  Please understand that
        this is not our choice, and we do not feel that those who lost loved ones
        should be penalized for this.  It is impossible for us to accept that fetuses
        and illegal aliens are recognized in the plans for compensation, yet we
        within an American community are not.
            In the wake of the tragedy, America rediscovered its spirit of unity.  We
        urge the 9/11 Fund to put action behind this administration's rhetoric and
        fully include our brothers and sisters.

        Individual Comment
        Missouri
