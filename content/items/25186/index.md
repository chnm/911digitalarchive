        Comment on Notices

        Dear Sirs:

        My son,           , was a victim of the WTC tragedy. My heart and soul have been ripped from me, as many other parents, wives, children,
        brothers & sisters. There is no compensation that can replace the life
        of my son.

        I am appalled by the information that the DOJ is considering deducting
        any insurance, charity & other monies received by the victims families.
        The insurance is a private organization which the company & or the
        victim pay a premium for such instances. The American people gave from
        there hearts to help the victims families. Several companies have
        collected funds on behalf of their victims. How sad that the government
        would even consider deducting this money from a settlement.

        I hope that you consider all the ramifications before you proceed with
        such an action.

        Thank you for your time

        Individual Comment
        Staten Island, NY
