        Friday, December 07, 2001 1:00 PM
        "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund"

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I strongly urge that families of all September 11th
        victims receive help from the "Victim Compensation
        Fund" regardless of race, color, creed, or sexual
        orientation.

        It truly makes me sick to my stomach that anyone would
        have to write a letter such as this--that any life or
        family isn't as important as another--that there is
        discrimination even in death; how discussing.

        As a first generation American, whose parents arrived
        at Ellis Island, I have always been proud that they
        came to this country and helped build it and have
        impressed their legacy on my children.  Now I'm not so
        sure of my pride if we allow a certain group (the
        American Taliban?) to cruelly encourage discrimination
        in the most horrific form.

        It should be remembered that all working people pay
        taxes to the government, not just the Christian Right.

        Individual Comment
        Hudson, MA
