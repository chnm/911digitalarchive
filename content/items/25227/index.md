        Thursday, December 20, 2001 7:56 PM
        Subject: equal rights for all 9/11 victims!


        December  20, 2001

        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        I am a member of Amnesty International, a worldwide human rights coalition
        dedicated to ensuring respect for the basic human rights of people without
        discrimination, in support of equal access to benefits under the September
        11 Victims Relief Fund for all victims, without regard to sexual
        orientation or marital status. I believe that the United States has been
        committed to upholding international human rights standards, which includes
        the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and the International Covenant on
        Civil and Political Rights, which prohibit arbitrary discrimination.

        I ask that you apply basic international human rights standards, and the
        general principle of equality, compensating life partners as well as de
        facto parents or children who have lost loved ones on 9/11, without regard
        to sexual orientation or marital status.

        AI reports that the State of New York has already adopted such a policy,
        granting compensation benefits "on a showing of mutual interdependence with
        the victim, in recognition that anyone who shared with the victim living
        expenses, day to day activities and the emotional bonds of family deserves
        help in this time of need."

        In the 9/11 attacks, many lesbian and gay citizens perished. Now the loved
        ones of those killed have had mixed results in obtaining equal treatment at
        the local level in the distribution of relief funds by private and state
        agencies.  If the government would create an inclusive policy, this would
        help establish an important principle for other such funds.  Real justice
        will be obtained only when all families  and all types of families who have
        suffered such unbearable loss on 9/11 are treated with dignity, equality,
        respect and admiration for their courage.

        Thank you for your consideration.  I will be anxious to hear about your
        efforts to ensure that all surviving victims of the 9/11 attacks have equal
        access to the relief fund.


        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Fairless Hills, PA
