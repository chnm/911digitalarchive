        Thursday, March 14, 2002 8:59 AM
        Victim's Compensation Fund


        I was just notified by a friend who watched the "Meet the Press" program on Sunday the 10th of March. This is one day before this country was to Honor the Six Month Anniversary of 9/11 when 2,830 human beings gave their life to their country, and Thousands upon Thousands lost a LOVED ONE.

        This was a catastrophe of unknown proportion and I send this note
        in disagreement, disbelief and disgust: That not ONE person who LOST a loved one on 9/11/01 at the World Trade Center, the Pentagon, Flight 11, Flight 175, Flight 93;
        suffer in a discriminating manner.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
