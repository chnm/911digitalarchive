        Tuesday, March 12, 2002 8:30 PM
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund


        Dear Government Officials and Others,

        It is with great sadness that I feel that I must write this e-mail.  As a
        gay man I can't believe that I am a citizen of lesser importance that any
        other man, woman, child, fetus or illegal alien yet this is what I am
        hearing from Mr. Kenneth Feinberg who head the Victim Compensation Fund that
        was created by our own congress.  In an article I just read quoting Mr.
        Feinberg's remarks to NBC's Meet the Press it clearly states that Mr.
        Feinberg regards gay and lesbian Americans as something less than others who
        are not gay or lesbian.

        I sincerely hope that those of you in a position to revise Mr. Feinberg's
        concept of humanity and fairness will address this matter with him and his
        organization.  Imagine that donations to the 9/11 Victim Compensation Fund
        were decreased by 10%.  This is the statistical amount that gays and
        lesbians have donated to this fund.  I hope that this matter is quickly
        addressed fairly and comprehensively.


        Individual Comment
        West Hollywood, CA
