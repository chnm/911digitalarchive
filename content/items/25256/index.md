        Monday, March 11, 2002 5:01 PM
        Regarding the 9/11 Victim Compensation Fund


        To Whom it May Concern,

        Can the attached text really be accurate? Did Mr Feinberg really appear on
        national TV and state that both the Attorney General and the 9/11 fund were
        willing to overlook the status of affected illegal aliens when determining
        victim compensation yet were not going to extend this leeway to gay victims?
        How can this possible occur?

        I have no agenda to advance regarding the status of either an illegal alien
        or a gay person affected by this tragedy. I am offended that any single human
        being who lost a life or was injured during the events of September 11 would
        be denied the compensation that everyone else will receive. If we are not
        about Freedom and Equality as a result of this horrific act, then we have
        learned nothing from it.

        Is it really acceptable to tell the nation that for one group, the laws are
        set aside while for another group, the people are the ones set aside?

        Please review the message that this selective policy sends to the country. I
        hope to hear that every victim will receive equal compensation under the 9/11
        Funds's guidance.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Auburndale, MA
