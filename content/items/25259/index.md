        Monday, March 18, 2002 1:18 PM
        9/11 Fund

        To Whom it may Concern:

        We are writing this letter to express our concern and our opposition to the
        decision by the Dept. of Justice to deny gay and lesbian victims their
        rightful compensation in the aftermath of the September 11 terrorist attacks.
        Beacon Fellowship is an Interfaith social justice organization based in
        Columbus Ohio. Our mission is to work to create change and promote social
        justice in communities thru Advocacy, Education, and Direct Action.

        We believe in fairness and equality for all citizens of this country. That
        must include gay and lesbian citizens, who pay their taxes, and are just as
        much a part of this nation as any others. Hate and bigotry have no place in
        our country or our communities.

        We encourage the DOJ and Kenneth Zwick to reconsider the decision and allow
        all victims of the horrible attack on our country to receive full and fair
        compensation for their losses. To offer anything less or nothing at all,
        would be truly un-American and would clearly violate the spirit of the
        U.S.Constitution as well as the Declaration of Independence, which closes
        with the words "Liberty and Justice for All".

        ALL means ALL, plain and simple. Don't allow bigotry and hate to dictate
        policy.

        Thank You

        Individual Comment
        Columbus OH
