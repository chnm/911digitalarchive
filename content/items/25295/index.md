        The heroism and the tragedy of 9/11 cut across the distinctions of race,

        distribution of charity in order to advance a narrow political agenda?

        so because they were stuck in a rigid "Us vs. Them" mentality and it
        caused them to act in brutal and cruel ways. We expect much better from
        the civic leaders in this democracy. Please do the right thing and take
        immediate steps to include ALL surviving children and families in the
        distribution plan.

        Individual Comment
        Parents of two teenage sons
        Portland, OR
