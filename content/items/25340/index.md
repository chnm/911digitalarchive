        Thursday, March 21, 2002 10:58 AM
        9/11 fund

        I am outraged that gay and lesbian partners of those who died on September 11
        are not automatically going to receive the same benefits as heterosexual
        spouses.  It amazes me that even after this nation has suffered such a great
        tragedy, that the families of "selected" individuals will have to suffer even
        more. "United we stand, divided we fall" seems to be more like "United we
        stand, with the exclusion of gays and lesbians ... until we need their tax
        dollars, support, etc."

        The victims of 9/11 worked in our country, paid taxes in our country, and
        supported our country. I do not feel that now is the time for our nation to
        turn our backs on those who paid the price of being an American with their
        lives, regardless of their lifestyles. This is a sad time for all of us, and
        now is not the time to make judgments.

        Respectfully,
        Individual Comment
