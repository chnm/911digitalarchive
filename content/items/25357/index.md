        Wednesday, November 07, 2001 10:56 PM
        Re: Survivors compensation for Terrorists' Acts

        Dear Sir or Madam:

         Our government's efforts to provide money to make the survivors of 9/11
        victims fiscally whole was admirable.  The generosity of the public
        contributions was surely far in excess of anyone's conception.

         Failure of local and state authorities to devise an organized,
        equitable means of disbursing donations is lamentable.  NO GOV'T FUNDS
        should be released until the private contributions have been unified and
         qualified survivors made fiscally whole with private funds, insurance
        policies, public servants' killed in action benefits, etc..

         Fiscally whole is neither enrichment nor double dipping.  Government
        funding should be used only if contributions do not meet the established
        needs of the survivors.   The remainder should be used to defray
        reconstruction costs, which always exceed initial estimates.

         Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Ponte Vedra Beach, FL
