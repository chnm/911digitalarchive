        Wednesday, February 06, 2002 9:21 PM
        Whine

        I think the survivors of the victims of September 11 are whining.  Nobody
        else gets any money from the federal government nor should they.  This has
        turned into a money grab.  I realize it is our own fault since we started
        calling them victims since day one.  They are not victims -- they are
        survivors.  In my day, when tragedy struck, you had a wake, a service and you
        got on with life.  The survivors are making their life a continuous whine for
        more money for nothing.  This is one of the worst things the Congress has
        done since the ADA, Family Medical Leave Act, EITC, and I could go on and on
        with giveaways to the whiners.  I am so sick of it it makes me want to throw
        up.  Sorry to be so graphic.

        I believe that Mr. Feinberg has done a very good job with an impossible
        situation.  He has tried time and time again to explain what is going on.  If
        it were up to me, I would scrap the entire thing and let them get their money
        from the Red Cross.

        Disenchanted with so-called American Citizens.

        Individual Comment
        Colorado Springs, CO
