        Monday, November 26, 2001 3:39 PM
        September 11 Victims

        Dear Attorney General Ashcroft,

        As a former St. Louis Missouri resident and long time watcher of you, I
        respect your track record and capabilities.

        The deaths on September 11th were the result of an attack on America and
        all of its citizens.  Please do not discriminate against any of those
        citizens. The unmarried partners of September 11th victims are
        particularly at risk.  In today's world, there are many definitions of
        relationships - especially in a city as crucial to the US as New York.
        Also in a city such as New York, the cost of living dictates a sharing
        of expenses. With one of those partners suddenly taken by the tragic
        events of September 11, many many people in such relationships are
        facing severe hardships. Thus I ask you to ensure that all unmarried
        partners, gay or straight, young and old and their dependents should be
        eligible for compensation.

        I thank you for your consideration of this important matter.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        San Francisco, CA
