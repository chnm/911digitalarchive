        Collateral Offsets


        It is a shame that the comments area has been "hijacked" by mass email
        spammers seeking to advance political agendas (however worthy they may
        be)  instead of allowing for open discussion of the merits of the
        program by those directly affected, namely families of victims like my
        sister in law, who lost her husband.  I trust by now you have heard from
        practically every outlet that reducing awards by "collateral offset"
        will be a certain deal-breaker for many families, as it uses the
        benefits that people had earned in their own right, or paid for out of
        their own pockets, as an excuse to deny compensatory damages for what I
        expect any court of law or sensible jury would find to be inexcusable
        lapses in security, preparation and judgment on the part of the many
        organizations involved (the airlines, the airports, the security
        companies, the WTC building management, etc.).

        I am hoping that the great public outcry against these conditions will
        be taken into account prior to the finalization of the settlement terms.
        When many potential settlements are being reduced to almost zero due to
        these conditions, many families will have no choice but to opt for
        litigation, as painful and drawn out as it will certainly be for all
        involved.
