        Saturday, December 08, 2001 2:29 PM
        Sept. 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for comments
        about the forthcoming regulations for the "September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund."

        I strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that ensure
        compensation is made available to all attack victims, including the
        committed partners and non-biological children of gay and lesbian
        victims.  The devastating grief and economic losses suffered by the
        partners and children of gay and lesbian victims are no different than
        that of other families. Just as the terrorists drew no lines in choosing
        their victims, our country must not draw lines of compensation.

        It is my fervent hope that careful consideration will be given to these
        concerns as the regulations are written.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
