        Consideration of Cost of Living Adjustments

        To whom it may concern,

        Please consider adding cost of living increases into  the assumed wages for
        the deceased victims up through the time they would have reached there
        retirement age.

        It is quite reasonable to assume that a person making $100,000 per year in
        2001 would be making, at least, the inflation adjusted equivalent of that
        amount, for example, 20 years hence, when that person might have retired.

        Of course, this amount , including inflation adjusted increases,  would be
        calculated before being discounted down to a present value figure.


        Thank you very much for your consideration.



        Individual Comment
