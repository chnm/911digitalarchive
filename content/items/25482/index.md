        Tuesday, March 19, 2002 9:48 PM
        September 11 fund

        Dear fund managers,

        I am writing to express thanks for your hard work in overseeing the victims'
        compensation fund for the September 11 tragedy, but also to implore you not
        to discriminate against gay Americans in the distribution of these funds.
        Although there are no definitive statistics, most experts agree that gays
        make up from 5 to 10 percent of our population. With approximately 3000
        deaths in the September 11 attacks, that translates to 150-300 gay victims.
        Many of these victims left behind partners, who would be spouses if only
        American law permitted them to be. You are not obligated to perpetuate this
        discrimination; indeed, I would say you are bound by a moral imperative to
        treat gay partners just as you would heterosexual spouses. These partners
        are just as bereaved, just as heartbroken, and in many cases, just as
        financially burdened by the deaths of their loved one. Please reconsider
        your policy, and include gay partners in the distribution of your fund.

        Thanks,

        Individual Comment
        Charlotte, NC
