      text: |-
        Friday, January 25, 2002 10:03 PM


        I think that $250,000 life insurance policy compensation for this
        disaster is appropriate.  If a natural disaster came in and wiped out an
        entire block or city, they aren't given this kind of compensation.  Yes,
        it is sad and unfortunate of the circumstances of September 11 but every
        day people die for no reason and not be compensated. You can't bring
        them back so the victims of September 11 will go on with their life as
        many other countless people have to after a love one dies, without huge
        monetary compensation.  It is not the nations or our government's fault
        but the cruelty of a select few. While this has affected numerous people
        besides the loved ones and families of the tragedy, it has also caused
        massive layoffs and slow growth to companies that are hurting and they
        are not getting help from the government.  How can an unemployed person
        survive on less than $250 a week for a family of 4 in order to pay all
        expenses?  Is the government helping these people?  No, they are not, so
        $250,000 is at least a boost to help these people to survive for a few
        years where the unemployed will be bankrupted and lose everything until
        they find another job.  $1.4 million for a death is unbelievable. I
        can't believe that a woman was complaining she wasn't getting anything
        considering she was fortunate enough to have life insurance, pension
        plans in place that still exceeded $250,000.  Let's put things in
        prospective.  Some of us don't have the money for life insurance or to
        provide for our kids and $250,000 would certainly allow for any person
        to get back on their feet either through training to get another job or
        to help with college funds for children. Let's spread the funds so
        everyone can eventually get back on their feet, not to profit for a
        loved ones death. They should be grateful that there is a fund that will
        give them that kind of money. Money shouldn't be used to measure a
        person's death because no matter what, nothing can bring them back so
        let's stop this excessive compensation to victims. Otherwise, how can
        companies survive or grow to keep our population employed.  Some people
        can not afford higher prices or premiums so that the greedy can be
        compensated.  If I had died in this tragedy, I would not want my
        children to be greedy.  Just given the opportunity to make their own
        way.

        Thank you..

        Individual Comment
        Hermitage, TN
