        Monday, December 24, 2001 7:05 PM
        Victim Compensation

        I don't want to sound unsympathetic, but I don't
        understand why taxpayer money is paying off the
        victims of Sept 11. We don't owe fellow Americans
        anything for an untimely death. When did we become
        responsible for the financial well being of any
        victim? Where do we draw the line? Do we pay for
        regular murder victims? Do we pay for traffic victims?
        I think not. Each person is responsible for their own
        financial planning to take care of their loved ones,
        as it should be. What happened to all the money that
        was raised for the victims? Why isn't it being used?

        I was taken aback when I heard one of the victim's
        relatives indicate that they didn't think the payout
        was fair.  This relative thought they were getting
        less than they deserved.  How greedy can a person
        be!!!

        I also expect the victims of the USS Cole, embassies,
        and Oklahoma to now come forth wanting their
        compensation package.

        The bottom line is no amount of money can replace a
        loved one.  Use the money that has been raised to pay
        the victim's families.  Don't use my tax money!!!

        Individual Comment
        Alexandria, VA
