        Tuesday, January 22, 2002 6:27 PM

        This is in response to the Victim's Compensation Fund that the U.S.
        Government set up for the victim's families.

        With all the donations that were received for the victim's of this terrible
        tragedy, we do not understand why the government has to use our tax dollars
        to pay the victim's families more money.  With all the charity funds and the
        insurance dollars, why should the government give these people more money?
        We do not understand this!!

        If the government is feeling responsible for this, why not for the Oklahoma
        City bombing?  Those poor people didn't receive a dime from the government
        and yet the person that committed that awful crime was a U.S. citizen.  What
        about the poor guy that we know who was just working and fell off the roof
        and it killed him?  Why doesn't the government help his family who doesn't
        have any insurance coverage?

        And why should the government bail out insurance companies?  Isn't that what
        we pay insurance premiums for?  So the insurance company can provide us
        coverage in the event of a disaster?  If the money isn't there, it is only
        because the "fat cats" at the top have taken the money that we have paid and
        put it in their pockets!!!    Just like Enron.......that fat cats got fatter
        and the poor employees were left high and dry.  We suppose the government is
        going to pay them something too!!  (Which we don't agree with either, even
        though we feel sorry for them!).  The government should make sure the fat
        cats pay the employees!!

        We hope that this gets through and is read by someone and not just dismissed.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
