        Friday, January 04, 2002 1:16 PM
        Fund support for ALL FAMILIES

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming
        regulations for implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund."

        I strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that ensure
        compensation is available to all
        victims of the attack, including the committed partners and the
        non-biological children of gay and
        lesbian victims.

        Every family impacted by the events of September 11th deserves all the
        support we can give,
        including financial support, especially when childeren are involved.
        Please honor these bonds
        by treating the families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any
        other.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
