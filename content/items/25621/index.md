        Thursday, November 29, 2001 6:36 AM
        Compensation or More Torture?

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice
        Main Building, Room 3140
        950 Pennsylvania Avenue NW
        Washington, D.C. 20530

        Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing to you to address the issue of compensation for all surviving
        family members of September 11th.  I have just one question:  so, only
        people who are married under the auspices of the Federal Government, are
        grieving and need help?  Your proposed policy is unjust and discriminatory.
        I really find it hard to believe that in the light of the tragic events of
        the 11th that you would really force people to undergo further trauma by
        making distinctions between families "worthy" of your assistance and
        families who are not "legitimate" in your agency's opinion.  I fully support
        the strong recommendations forwarded to you by Lambda, ESPA, HRC, GLAD,
        NCLR, and NGLTF.

        I look forward to your just and compassionate decision.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Rego Park, NY
