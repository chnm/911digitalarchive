        Victim Compensation Fund


                                             December 5, 2001

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for
        public comments regarding the forthcoming regulations for
        implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund."  I strongly urge the Department to draft
        regulations that ensure compensation is available to all
        victims of the attack, including the committed partners and
        the non-biological children of victims regardless of their
        sexual orientation.

        The emotional and economic effects on the families of
        victims is no less because the victims were "straight"
        or "gay."  The terrorists drew no lines in choosing their
        victims.  Our country must draw no lines in compensating
        those who suffered the most personal of losses from these
        horrific attacks.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of
        whom left behind loved ones.  I urge the Department to honor
        the bonds of family by treating all families equitably, and
        without any narrowing of our compassion by partisan ideology
        or homophobic discrimination.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Lansing, MI
