        Tuesday, March 12, 2002 2:23 AM
        9/11 Fund Benefits


        While I applaud the sympathetic approach to illegal immigrants in the paying
        of benefits for victims of the 11 September attacks, I'm appalled that the
        same generosity of spirit isn't being extended to the partners of gay and
        lesbian victims.

        Has nothing been learnt about tolerance in the past six months?

        If state laws can be circumvented in the case of illegal immigrants, I find
        no reason why they should be quoted as a reason for avoiding payments to gay
        and lesbian partners.

        As a heterosexual English resident of Australia, I realise it could be
        argued that this is none of my business, but as a citizen of the world I
        believe it is important to register my dismay at this turn of events.

        Individual Comment
        Upper Sturt, South Australia
