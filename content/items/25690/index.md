        greed



        Everyday in America people are victims of crime.  While I feel saddened by
        what happened to our wonderful nation and the peoople who lost a loved one in
        the WTC, I think it is completely riduculous how greedy these victims are
        being.  Getting over one million dollars for your loved one should be plenty
        of money.  Even if it was 100,000 dollars that is compensation enough in my
        eyes.  Victims of crime are compensated by their attacker, that is how it
        should be here.  Not only is the greed of these people upsetting me but also
        the feelings they have about suing the airlines.  The airlines are not
        responsible, the terrorist who hijacked the planes are responsible, it really
        is that simple.  What next, are you going to want to sue the passangers
        families on the planes who did not stop the plane from running into the
        buildings?  Give me a break and grow up.  I understand the pain you are in, I
        lost loved ones too (not in this tragety).  Money will not make your pain go
        away.  And what about all the people who lost their jobs due to layoffs and
        the falling economy?  They no longer have an income either but they are doing
        what adults do and find other jobs, not complain about only getting 1.6
        million.

        Individual Comment
