        I have to ask why the Federal government feels obligated to compensate the
        911 victims?  People die every day in this country from all sorts of things
        and the government is not expected to compensate their families.  Why is this
        situation any different to the greiving families of  family members lost to
        accidents, murder, illness, etc.?  Not only do I disagree with any Federal
        payment using my tax dollars, but the amounts are outragious.  No one needs
        insurance or compensation payments to last them for the rest of their life.
        The survivors could and should work sometime during their lifetime and many
        will remarry and bring large sums of unearned government give away money with
        them.  People are responsible to provide their own life insurance for their
        families.  Help to those in need is one thing, but giving many people more
        than a million dollars is stupid and unjustified in my opinion.  When I die
        my family will have to support themselves and settle for whatever insurance
        and savings I provide on my own.  The government will NOT provide any help to
        them.


        Individual Comment
