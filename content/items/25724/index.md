        Dear Sirs,

        Please accept this message as a show of disapproval for your intention to exclude gay and lesbian partners from receiving the full benefits that all other victims, both Americans and Non-Americans, will be receiving from the 9/11 fund.  The generosity of the American people should not be subjected to your personal agendas.  You say you can usurp the law in order to benefit those in this country illegally, but in contradiction you say your hands are tied by these same laws when it comes to aiding the gay families who have also fallen victim to this horrible tragedy.

        Hate and discrimination are un-American.

        This tragedy did not discriminate based on sex, race, nationality, age, sexual nature or religion and NEITHER should the 9/11 fund.

        Please reconsider your intentions.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Franklin, Tennessee
        Proud American.
