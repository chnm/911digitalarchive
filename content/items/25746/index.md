        Subject: ADMINISTRATION OF 9-11-01 FUNDS to Kenneth L Zwick

         Kenneth L Zwick, Director
         Office of Management Programs
         U.S. Department of Justice

         May I take a moment of your time to express my opinion of the
        administration of funds collected for the Sept 11 Victim Compensation Fund.
         I request you include all types of families which were affected on
        9-11 and treat them all equally.
         Please include among relatives eligible for compensation those who
        lost their life partners as well as de facto parents or children, without
        regard to sexual orientation or marital status.

         Please follow the example already set by New York Governor Pataki in
        granting compensation benefits "on a showing of mutual inderdependence with
        the victim, in recognition that anyone who served with the victim living
        expenses, day-to day activities and the emotional bonds of family deserves
        help in this time of need.

         Individual Comment
         Modesto, CA
