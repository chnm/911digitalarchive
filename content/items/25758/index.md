        Thursday, December 06, 2001 3:52 PM
        September 11 compensation comments

         Dear Mr. Zwick,

        Please ensure that rules drawn up for distributing
        compensation to families of victims of the Sept. 11
        attacks include all families, including the partners
        and non-biological children of gay and lesbian
        victims.

        They have suffered from the attacks just as much as
        other families and deserve just as much compensation.
        At a time such as this, when these families have
        already had to face down evil terrorists, don't let
        their own government, our government, become yet
        another terrorist they have to face.

        These families are every bit as committed to each
        other as any other family.  Their lives and deaths
        were just as real.  They suffered exactly the same
        emotional and financial devastation as any other
        victims.

        If we do not treat the partners and non-biological
        children of gay and lesbian victims the same as other
        families, the terrorists have won!  It would prevent
        this country from having any claim to "moral high
        ground" and we become just another terrorist
        supporting state.

        This country has already suffered great loss.  But
        please remember that additional lives are at stake in
        making compensation decisions.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Dallas, TX
