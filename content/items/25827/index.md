        Tuesday, January 15, 2002 3:22 PM
        victim compensation

        I very strongly oppose the compensation for the victims and/or their families
        from the Sep 11 attack.

        The American people gave many, many dollars to the various charitable
        organizations to help these people.  Also, I would think these people also
        had private insurance.

        I do not believe it is the role of government to compensate someone for a
        tragedy in their life.  You can call me hardhearted, but people die every day
        through no fault of their own, and the government does not give them a pile
        of money.

        If my husband was to die suddenly, would you compensate me?  I don't think
        so.

        It is up to each individual to prepare for the loss of a husband, wife,
        child, or any other family member.  Again, it is not the role of government!

        I sincerely hope you will rescind this.

        Individual Comment
        Milwaukee WI
