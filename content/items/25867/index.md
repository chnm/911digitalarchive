        To Whom,                              March 18-2002
          It seemed for but a second back in September, that
        if nothing else, we were all forced to re-evaluate
        what was important to us as a people, as a nation and
        as a member of the human race.
        It was a very brief second...
         America seems to back to its old self. While I should
        not be surprised, I do feel a profound sense of loss
        in that it appears we have not learned much or
        anything from such a horrific event. We are all in
        this together regardless of our views and yet there
        are those persistant individuals and groups with fear
        and loathing in their hearts that will go to the ends
        of the earth to deny the most basic of liberties,
        rights and dignity to even those American's directly
        involed in the tragety. Gay and lesbian American's are
        being denied the same benefits of their heterosexual
        counterparts; how can we begin to justify such blatant
        discriminitory practices in 2002 and in America of all
        places.
        The Land of the Free...for who?
        With Liberty and Justice for ALL.....with exceptions!
        Seems that theses basic American values that we are
        all raised to cherish have never been and continue to
        be denied to the population as a whole for various
        misguided reasons, usually masked under the name GOD
        or RELIGION.
        Some went as far as to say that the events of 9-11
        were caused by gays and lesbians.
        Our government officially sanctions such hatred and it
        is thus taught to our kids and others.
        Hatred is dangerous and if left unchecked, can bring
        down the mightiest of nation's.
        A Few Thoughts.
        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
        Cincinnati, Ohio
