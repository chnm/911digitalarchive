        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 12:59 PM
        9/11 Victims Compensation

        I feel strongly that the charitable contributions received by the families
        of victims should not be consider a "collateral source" in the distribution
        of federal aid.  I think that I speak for many Americans when I say that
        the intent of our donations was not just to make the families "whole".
        Given the senseless loss of their loved ones nothing could ever make them
        whole, emotionally or financially, but our hope was to make their lives
        easier by providing some financial security.  The combination of charitable
        aid and federal aid should do more than just cover their expenses and make
        up for lost wages, etc - it should provide an additional cushion that
        removes some of the day-to-day worries about finances which are normally
        borne by both parents.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
