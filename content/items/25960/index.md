        Compensation for partners of Gays and Lesbians


        Dear Mr. Feinberg,

        I find it appalling that the partners and children of gays and lesbians
        killed in the 9/11 attacks will not receive that same compensation as those

        themselves or their employers.  Surely the partners and children of gays and
        lesbians are no less worthy of our compassion.

        Individual Comment
