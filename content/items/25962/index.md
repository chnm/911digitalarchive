        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 3:22 PM
        Comments sought on victim fund

        The neediest should come first. Orphans, Widows and Widowers with
        children and low incomes. People who have been put in a desperate
        position due to Sept. 11th. People who have lost their jobs and cannot
        support themselves or pay their bills. The ones who need the
        necessities of life. Then if there is more money, distribute it
        according to what the fund will allow to try and compensate people.
        Give them welfare and food stamps to help. Allow the bill collectors
        to only collect on interest until the people can get on their feet
        again. And don't let it go against their credit. Help them to find
        jobs.

        Proof of neediness is probably essential or there could be corruption.
        Like what happened in CA. after the 1994 earthquake. Illegal aliens
        were collecting money and goods while the homeowners were left to
        struggle in a lot of cases. Many of the people walked away from their
        properties and it became a ghetto over night. I was one who had to
        sell at a fraction of the price because my company moved me to another
        state. Luckily, the lender forgave the loan. However, after six years,
        it still shows up on my credit. Things like this should be considered.

        Thanks,

        Individual Comment
        Irving, TX
