        Compensation for all?


        I'm appalled by Kenneth Feinberg's inhuman decision to offer 9/11
        compensation to the families and partners of everyone - including illegal
        aliens - except gays and lesbians.

                  , a gay man from San Francisco, has been singled out repeatedly
        for his heroics in preventing United flight 93 from destroying the Capitol
        or White House. One of the pilots of the four planes that were hijacked was
        a gay man. Many other gay and lesbian Americans were murdered that day, and
        their partners and families are grieving as deeply as the partners and
        families of all the straight people who died that day.

        Either payments should be made to everyone who deserves it because of 9/11,
        or no compensation should be made at all, to anyone. After all, that's what
        Mr. Feinberg is proposing for those he considers beneath him.

        Yours sincerely,

        Individual Comment
