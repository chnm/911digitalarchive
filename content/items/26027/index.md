        Survivor Benefits

        I believe it is good moral sense to include the partners of gay and lesbian
        families in survivor benefits. There are many religious belief systems in
        this great country of ours and although some are not supportive of these
        families, others are. Still, that is not the point really. The fact is that
        while these relationships do not have the same legal status, they do meet
        all the qualities of the truest esseence of family. They are loving,
        nurturing, supportive and in many cases quite long lasting. As such, the
        pain, anguish and loss is no less. To argue against survivor support for
        these poor people is to insult the heroism of the gay and lesbian people who
        died attempting to help others with this crisis. Let us not play God and
        determine whose suffering is more valid. Instead, let us seize this moment
        to show the world that our greatest strengths are not only in our military
        or government, but in our collective will to do what is right. Compassion
        and support for ALL suffering Americans would send the right message.

        God Bless ALL Americans.


        Individual Comment
