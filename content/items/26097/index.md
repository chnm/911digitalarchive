        Monday, March 11, 2002 3:45 PM
        911 Fund Eligibility


        I have just read the comments made by Mr. Feinberg on Meet the Press.  I am
        appalled and sickened that in this day, and age and after all that has
        happened, your organization plans on attacking Gay victims as bad as the
        terrorists that flew the planes into the World Trade Center Towers and Osama
        Bin Laden by not recognizing them as human beings.

        The partners and children of the Gay victims lost a partner that helped
        build a relationship in the face of hate and discrimination and now you have
        the audacity to say that you are willing to say they lost NOTHING because of
        the ignorance of the community they had to live in and educate.  You are
        willing to ignore national law yet will NOT stand up for basic humanity
        towards fellow Americans!

        I am deeply ashamed of you and am angered that many Gay and Lesbian people
        donated to your fund and worked for it only to be ripped off!!

        If you follow through on Mr. Feinberg's comments, I hope you never get
        another penny in donations and are sued by every Gay and Lesbian partner and
        family member of each Gay and Lesbian victim of the 9/11 tragedy!

        Please stand up and be decent, be Americans!

        Individual Comment
