        Saturday, December 15, 2001 2:55 PM
        domestic partner compensation for 9/11 attacks

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Please include those who lost their life partners as a result of the
        terrorist attacks on 9/11/01 in the group eligible for relief
        compensation. Those who lost life partners in addition to de facto
        parents or children among relatives must be made eligible for
        compensation without regard to sexual orientation or marital status.

        I recommend that the U.S. Government follow the  the example already set
        by New York Governor Pataki in granting compensation benefits "on a
        showing of mutual interdependence with the victim, in recognition that
        anyone who shared with the victim living expenses, day-to-day activities
        and the emotional bonds of family deserves help in this time of need."

        I am thoroughly disgusted that our government and George W. Bush states
        that America is united when it shows no compassion or responsibility to
        those domestic partners who loved ones died innocently. It's bad enough
        to lose someone you love and then have to fight to get what you deserve
        while trying to keep your house and finances since one breadwinner is
        gone. Is discrimination American these days? How are these people
        different?

        Thank you.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Prairie Village, Kansas
