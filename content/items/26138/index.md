        I am writing to ask WHY...  & HOW.... could you be so closed minded as to

        have human rights and some do not.  If we look back over history, there was
        another leader who felt this way about his country men.  That leader was
        Hitler.  Do you really want to be on the same history pages as Hitler?

        It amazes me, the lengths some people will go to in order to squelch
        another's human rights.  It is this type of behavior {discriminating against
        gay partners of those killed on 911} that makes me and countless others
        ashamed to be called American.  Trust me...  we will all be voting, and our
        numbers are large.

        I look forward to your response.
