        Friday, November 09, 2001 6:31 PM
        Battery Park City Residents

        Hi.  I just want to go on the record as being in support of the poor
        Battery Park City residents who have been terribly affected by the events
        of September 11th and who, for some insane reason, are not considered
        "victims" for purposes of receiving disaster relief funds.  This seems like
        a terrible injustice to me.  Many of those people are completely without
        homes or belongings while others are, at the very least, out the money they
        had to spend to find temporary shelter/belongings/transportation, etc.
        and/or to have special hazardous material companies sanitize their
        homes.  Some have to relocate alltogether.  Some are stuck breathing in all
        kinds of impurities in the air and are suffering physically as a
        result.  All are probably suffering emotionally/psychologically.  How can
        they NOT be considered victims?!?!  Please rectify this oversight as soon
        as possible.  Those of us who have donated money to these "victims'" funds
        have done so with the understanding that ALL the victims will be helped in
        some way -- it is terribly UN-American to just leave some of the victims
        out in the cold!!!   Respectfully submitted,

        Individual Comment
        Chester, NJ
