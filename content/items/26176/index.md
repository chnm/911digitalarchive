        Wednesday, January 23, 2002 10:40 PM
        Additional Comments


        At today's Family Meeting held in Ridgewood, NJ with Special Master Kenneth
        Feinberg, it was stated that the US DOJ can and will be a participant in the
        litigation against the airlines (the $6B insurance maximum) to compensate it
        for the money paid out of this fund.  This right is clearly stated in the
        statute regarding the "right of subrogation with respect to any claim paid
        under this title".

        The press has not clearly stated this and the American people think the
        money coming from this fund will be from taxpayer dollars!  Only amounts
        greater than $6B will be from taxpayer dollars and all calculations so far
        have the total much less. The American people have a right to know this
        fact.  The Final Rules should state this clearly.

        Individual Comment
        Paramus, NJ
