        Comment from a fiance

        Dear Mr. Feinberg,

        I am a fiancé of a wonderful 33 year old man who was killed while at
        work at &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp .  We happily lived together as husband as wife,
        however, we were not legally married and our chance to due so was taken
        from us on which is now the most tragic day in history.  I only ask for
        your consideration as a fiance/domestic partner and for your
        consideration for all domestic partners and couples who lived together
        and who's lives have been shattered at the loss of their spouses.  We
        that lived together were family and the compensation fund should be
        given to those that the deceased would have intended and financially
        supported while they were alive.  Please consider all cases accordingly
        with the realization that we fiancés soon to be married awaiting for the
        day to say "until death do us part", were literally parted by death
        having the chance to legally become married torn from us.  Please
        acknowledge us for our future as our fiancés and partners wishes would
        have been.  Please help us and protect us the way our loved ones wanted
        too and for so many of us all ready were.

        Many of us have spoke of you and the tremendous job you have ahead of
        you with so many lives in your hands, we hope for your strength during
        this difficult time for you as well as ourselves.

        Sincerely,
