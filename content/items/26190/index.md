        Taxpayers'Money

        Please bear in mind that this is not money "dropping from trees," it is
        taxpayers' money.
        This should not be a "gift" to the families of highly paid people.  If
        someone worked for a financial firm and their family will be receiving
        several millions in insurance, they do not need taxpayers' help.
        This should be limited to people who NEED help. I am thinking of the
        cafeteria workers, janitors, police, etc., whose families may very well be
        destitute.
        AND, something that has not been mentioned anywhere....What compensation is
        paid to the families of the REAL heroes, the servicemen/women killed in
        action?
        One last thought. If I am walking in Central Park and am killed by a
        lightning bold, what compensation will the government provide to my family. I
        don't mean to be sarcastic, but, do you see the precedent that is being set.
        Thank for you time.
