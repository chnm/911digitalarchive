        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering the
        "September 11 Victims Compensation Fund."  I strongly urge the Depaertment to
        draft regulations that ensure that compensation is available to all vicitms
        of the attack including the committed partners and the non-biological
        children of gay and lesbian victims.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic losses suffered
        by the families of gay and lesbian victims are no less than those of other
        families.  Just as the terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims,
        our country must draw no lines in compensating those who suffered the most
        personal of losses from these horrific attacks, particularly the children

        Please honor the bonds among lesbians, gays and their loved ones just as you
        would any other family.  The money administered to the victims should help
        those in need, and not be denied on the basis of politics.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Amenia, NY
