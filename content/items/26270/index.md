        Compensation Commets

        Dear Sir:

        The men and women who died in the WTC were for the most part private citizens
        arriving early to work. Because they took there jobs so seriously they were
        at there desks. Many of them were young with small children. It is my
        understanding that you are not only reducing there benefit by life insurance
        but also by social security. How unfair. There spouses and children will only
        receive these payments for a limited time. It was my understanding that
        social security was to help the survivors get through troubled times until
        they reach a certain age. This should not reduce any award.

        As too, pensions specifically 401 K funds.  The young have been encouraged to
        put away for there retirement. Very few today can live on Social Security
        alone. Are you penalizing them for saving for the future?

        And as to placing caps on either lost earnings or on the award itself, why
        did they pick the WTC? It was the heart of capitalism. For these victims to
        get to these positions they had to work long and hard hours. I feel the these
        caps should be raised and each family deserves something.

        Thank you
        Individual Comment
        Pompano Beach, FL
