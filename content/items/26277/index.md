        Thursday, March 21, 2002 12:21 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund


        Dear Sirs:
        As a proud American, I am writing to you with disgust regarding the
        allocation of funds distributed to those families who lost members on
        September 11, 2001.  I am in disgust because the gay and lesbian families
        who have lost their family members, partners and significant others are not
        getting any aid.  These are people like myself who work hard, pay taxes and
        struggle for equal rights even though we should not have to.
        As a member of the American society, all Americans should have the same
        rights, especially at a time when so much has been lost.  I am appalled that
        illegal aliens and their families are receiving compensation through  the
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund, while Americans, who just because
        they are gay/lesbian, are being left in the cold without any help from our
        nation.  They are being forced to feel as if the  terrorists who did this to
        them have more rights.  As you are aware, two of the terrorists on the six
        month anniversary of this tragic and most horrific event were granted access
        into the United States.  Since when did illegal aliens gain more rights than
        Americans, anyway? This is NOT FAIR and NOT AMERICAN!
        Many of the heroes of September 11th are gay and lesbian. For instance, &nbsp and so many more lost their
        lives on this horrific day. Should they not have offered their time and
        services to this nation because they are gay/lesbian?
        At a time when all Americans came together and supported each other, felt
        the others pain, volunteered their time and services, donated blood, goods
        and money and fought as one people, why, do tell, are gays and lesbians
        being overlooked by this fund? Putting all other laws against gays and
        lesbians aside, America should not be discriminating them at such a time of
        loss and pain.
        As a professional career woman who is American and very proud in being a
        Lesbian, I urge you to reverse this horrible and discriminating action!
        We are American. And as Americans we should  support all Americans for the
        simple fact that they are American.  We should not pick and choose who
        suffered more just because of their sexual orientation.
        I beg you to take action and provide for your countrymen and women.
        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
        Old Bridge, NJ
