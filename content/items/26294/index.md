        Thursday, February 07, 2002 10:35 AM
        lost forever


        Mr. Feinberg

                        I am writing you again regarding who is eligible to claim
        funds from the compensation fund. I have read many of the comments that have
        been posted . I have also written letters to The Attorney General of
        N.Y.,the Governor of N.Y., My State Senators and I have spoken to my
        attorneys. I am the fiancée/Domestic partner of a victim from the WTC
        attack. Apparently  there are many other people in the same situation as I
        am ,which is not knowing if we will be part of the claim process. The woman
        I love was brutally attacked and murdered just as all the rest of the
        victims were.    and I were as much married and dependent on one another
        as anyone else who actually was married. We supported each other
        emotionally,financially and spiritually!  We cared for each others families
        without hesitation, We lived life for each other as soul mates . We were
        inseparable except when we were at work . My pain my grief my loss is
        something only the other victims families could understand. There is no way
        to describe what has happened to all of us . The nightmares awakening me
        with screams the visions of death the ache in your heart in an instant
        perfect harmony changed into a tragedy. The loneliness the empty chair  no
        one to warm your feet on a cold winters night. You can almost say you are
        close to being catatonic. I am not judging the amounts to be given to the
        victims families I cant imagine trying to put an amount on pain and
        suffering or determining what someone may have made 10 to 15 years down the
        road. All I'm saying is that documented fiancée/domestic relationships must
        be eligible to receive compensation under your rules. You have told me in
        the past it's the state law that dictates who is eligible. I spoke to the
        attorney general and the Governor and they say the state law has nothing to
        do with who is eligible for the fund its strictly up to you Mr. Feinberg.
        Don't just hear the people listen to them they are crying out to you.




        Respectfully
        Individual Comment
        Bklyn, NY
