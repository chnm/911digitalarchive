        Friday, December 07, 2001 3:50 PM
        Victim Compensation Comments

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering the
        "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."  I strongly urge the Department to
        draft regulations that ensure that compensation is available to all victims
        of the attack, including the committed partners and the non-biological
        children of gay and lesbian victims.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic losses suffered
        by the families of gay and lesbian victims are no different and no less than
        are suffered by other families.  It is unthinkable that some people in the
        Department of Justice (!) should even hope to have the power to deprive
        these families of compensations given without question to more conventional
        families, of whose structure and makeup they approve.  The terrorists drew
        no lines in choosing their victims, and our country must draw no lines in
        compensating those who suffered the most personal of losses from these
        horrific attacks.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind
        loved ones.  It is easily imaginable that the marriages of at least some of
        the widows and widowers created on that day were not as happy or love-filled
        as at least some of the domestic partners who are left bereft.  Who is to
        judge that any one is suffering more than anyone else, and therefore more
        deserving of compensation?  Or is the motive for wishing to withhold funds
        from gay and lesbian commited partners simply punitive, a desire to punish
        them for lifestyles of which some Americans disapprove?  I urge the
        Department to treat the families of gay and lesbian victims no diffeerently
        from any other.

        Lest you be inclined toview this communication as perhaps coming from
        someone with a vested interest in any part of the 9/11 Victim Compensation
        Fund, I would like you to know that I am heterosexual, the biological mother
        of four children all of whom have the same father--to whom I was married for
        28 years--that I live in California and personally know no one who was in
        any way directly involved in the September 11 attacks.
        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Nevada City, CA
