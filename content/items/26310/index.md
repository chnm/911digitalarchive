        SEPT. 11 COMPENSATION

        I am outraged to learn that domestic partners of Gay and Lesbian people
        will not receive compensation for the loss of their loved ones in this
        terrible tragedy.
        Hasn't there been enough proof that the majority of Gays are fine,
        upstanding citizens who are patriotic and have a love for their country?
        How dare they be discriminated against in such an arbitrary manner
        simply because of their sexual orientation?
        I am an 81 year old straight, senior citizen who has been involved with
        helping the Gay and Lesbian Community for more than 20 years.  My life
        has been enriched and enhanced through my contacts with these highly
        intelligent, talented, caring and compassionate people. They do not
        deserve this unfair and inequitable treatment.  I urge you to reconsider
        your
        manner of thinking and  include domestic partners in receiving benefits
        they are entitled to regardless of their sexual orientation.
        Sincerely yours,

        Individual Comment


        Tarzana, California
