        Tuesday, January 22, 2002 5:41 PM
        Comments on proposed fund

        The proposed formula for distributing public money to victims of the Sept 9
        attack is both fair and very generous.

        I have personally contributed several hundred dollars to a charity for the
        victim's families. Employees in our company contributed several thousands.
        We did so out of compassion. We wanted to ease at least some of the
        financial impact of the losses suffered.

        It made those of us who gave to charity  feel good to help in any small way.

        Now many of the victim's families are complaining about the amount of money
        they will get. People who will be getting a $1,000,000 insurance payment
        complain that this reduces what they will receive from the government. The
        concern of many seems to have shifted to money. In many cases it sounds like
        greed.

        It makes those of us who gave to charity sad.

        Individual Comment
        Hauppauge, NY
