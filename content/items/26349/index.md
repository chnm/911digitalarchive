        Tuesday, January 22, 2002 2:40 PM
        Comments Regarding the Federal Victims Compensation Program


        Dear Special Master Feinberg:

        As a parent, my flesh and blood was murdered on 9/11. All who lost loved ones
        that day must be compensated in a fair and just way. Being a mother and a
        widow I can't say which loss is greater: The loss of a husband or the loss of
        a son.

        Have you asked yourself what would it be like not to see a future for your
        own family member?

        All give some but some give all.
        My son did that along with his fellow firefighters. We who are left behind
        also gave part of ourselves that day.

        All uniformed services families---including parents---should get fair
        compensation.
        We will not be forgotten. Hear our voices now.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
        Westbury, N.Y.
