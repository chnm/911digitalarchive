        Wednesday, December 19, 2001 11:42 PM
        Sept. 11th Victims Compensation

        Dear Special Master Kenneth R. Feinberg,

        I want to urge you to make sure that justice is served with the victims'
        family funds, by recognizing gay and lesbian partners of those killed on
        Sept. 11 and giving them their compensation, as well as other unmarried
        partners. Also, please make sure that no matter what the person's job, his or
        her family is compensated without prejudice. In other words, if a person was
        a dishwasher or janitor, his or her family needs and deserves just as much as
        a stockbroker's family. Please prevent any discrimination against poor and
        working class families of Sept. 11th victims.
        Thanks very much for your serious consideration of these issues.

        Individual Comment
        Santa Fe, NM
