        Saturday, March 16, 2002 9:28 PM
        Sept. 11 Fund

        Look at what we have in common.  We are all humans.  We love the same.  We dream the same.  We live the same.  We die the same.  Please treat us all the same.  Gay or straight we have all dealt with the tragedy of Sept 11.  We have all cried for the lost.  Felt joy for the saved.  Had resentment towards those responsible.  Prayed that it would never happen again.  And wept at the loss of talented, loving, caring people.  Gay and straight.  Don't sink to the level of those responsible for the tragedy of 9/11.  Don't discriminate against your own fellow American's.  Have we not all suffered enough?  Do we have to fight our own country for the equality that our own Constitution says everyone should have?  This war is already very personal, very difficult for those who lost loved ones in this terrible time.  Please don't make it harder for us.

        Individual Comment
