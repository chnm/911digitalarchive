        Unfair guidelines

        Good Morning Mr. Feinberg:

        Haven't we as victims been through enough?  Your guidelines for the victim compensation is unfair and inadequate.  I have lost my sister in law who supported her 75 year old Mom without a pension.  What is she going to do for the rest of her life.  You can never repay us for the lost of a vibrant, full of life hard working woman.  Atleast, do the right thing and represent the true heroes, the victims...rightfully.

        Victim compensation should compensate the families regardless of what they have receive.  It should not force us to sign documents and release our rights before know any details.  It is not the american system.....being forced to go against our better judgment.

        Regards,




        Individual Comment
        New York, N.Y.
