        Wednesday, November 21, 2001 1:20 PM
        Compensation to Victims of September 11th

        Kenneth L. Zwick,

        I write to ask that the federal government not discriminate against families
        which do not met the traditional definition.   We live in a multi-faceted
        nation where not everyone fits neatly in the a Judo-Christian model.  Many
        of those who died had established loving
        relationships without the benefit of marriage.  The partners of these people
        have suffered as grave a lose as those who formalized their love in a
        marriage ceremony.   To now deny financial support to these grieving people
        would be immoral.   I do not have to agree with
        someone's lifestyle to understand that their love is held just as dear as
        mine.  According individuals fair and just treatment even thought we
        disagree with them is the American way.   The people who died September 11th
        died because they were in the most democratic nation on earth.   That
        democracy requires that we treat people equally.

        Individual Comment
        Birmingham, MI
