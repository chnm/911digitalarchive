        Victim's of War

        I may not be understanding correctly when reading the article by &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp in the Associated Press titled 'Victim's Fund Met with Mixed Response' but if I
        am understanding correctly, I'm very disappointed with how we have turned out,
        as Americans.

        The first sentence says it all.  "NEW YORK (AP) - The government announced an
        aid program that would give families of victims of the terrorist attacks an
        average of $1.65 million - a plan that some families say doesn't go far
        enough."

        Were the 9-11 victims not victims of war?  Why do people think that they
        "deserve" more that what is being offered.  I don't get it.  Why does it always
        boil down to money.  For God's sake, these people lost their lives and we will
        never see them again.  What does that have to do with money.  Suffering,
        grieving, pain, depression, all of that is a very important and normal part of
        loosing someone that we love.  Never in a million life times will anyone be
        able to pay enough to make that pain go away.

        "What we are going to get out of it?  Who is going to support me now?  Who is
        going to take care of my kids?"

        These questions all sound like they are the American self centered greed.  I
        have a suggestion, how about your families.  Can't they help take care of the
        loved ones of their lost loved one?

        I'm not saying that we shouldn't help but AMERICA stepped up to the plate to
        help these people and we donated a lot of money.  OUR MONEY to help support
        these people in their time of trouble and to help make their suffering a little
        easier.

        This article is a slap in the face to all those that tried to help them and
        it's an embarrassment to our Country.  STOP BEING SO GREEDY!

        We all have our trails in life.  Some of us don't get the media attention that
        the 9-11 victims got so we don't get support and help from others when we have
        troubles.  We tough it out ourselves.

        Please think a little of other people before you complain about the help that
        you are given.

        Just my opinion.
        Individual Comment
