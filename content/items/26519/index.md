        Thursday, March 21, 2002 5:34 PM
        Compensation for domestic partner spouses

        As a physician and an insurance agent, we wish to go on record to protest the
        failure of the 9/11 fund to cover domestic partners and co-parented children
        of those who died in the September 11 terrorist attacks.

        First, two of the affected cities, New York and San Francisco, have for many
        years had legal status for registered domestic partners.  In New York, where
        we are registered domestic partners, both gay people and heterosexuals may
        register their partnership, as long as they live together and are not married
        to anyone else.  So for victims from those cities, there is legal precedent.

        Additionally, many employers nationwide have insurance coverage for domestic
        partners, and the biological or adopted offspring of domestic partners.  They
        realize what you do not:  that it is the right thing to do.  Love makes a
        family.  No family should suffer when the breadwinner dies.

        When we walked the streets of New York in those first terrible days and
        looked at the pictures of the missing, so many of them were under the age of
        40.  Many were in their 20s.  We had not made a will, when we were that age.
        These young people did not expect to die in an act of terrorism.  They are
        not to be blamed for not protecting their families.  You are, if you
        perpetuate the injustice.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
