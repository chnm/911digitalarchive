        Thursday, December 06, 2001 9:04 PM
        NY compensation

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Mr. Zwick,
        I urge the U.S. Government to follow the example already set by New York
        Governor Pataki regarding the inclusion among those eligible for
        compensation (for loss from the Sept. 11 attacks) of all those who lost
        their life partners or defacto parents or children. Decisions should be
        made without regard to sexual orientation or marital status. New York's
        standard, which grants benefits "on a showing of mutual interdependence
        with the victim," recognizes that "anyone who shared with the victim
        living expenses, day-to-day activities and the emotional bonds of family
        deserves help in this time of need," is a fair one.
        Our country has already been devastated by this unexpected blow. Now is
        a time to stand together, not to push us further apart.
        Thank you,
        Individual Comment
        Denton, TX
