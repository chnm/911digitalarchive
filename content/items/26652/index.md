        Astonished at Your Refusal to Gay and Lesbian Survivors


        Our president has called us to make a voluntary
        contribution to our fellow countrymen.

        I applaud him for making a first step.

        However, I see many of my gay and lesbian
        countrymen becoming increasingly disillusioned and even bitter because of
        unfortunate decisions like this one.  I suspect that the decision is based
        upon the facilitators' absolute ignorance in managing gay and lesbian issues,
        but I am kind.  Most of my g and l friends are much more suspect for
        understandable reasons.


        Get a clue.  Now that our numbers are
        reaching out to the ever burgeoning bisexual community, decisions like this one
        will continue to galvanize the resolve of some and the bitterness of
        millions.


        For this reason, I urge you to attempt to grapple
        with the apparent difficulties in administering such a fund.  It cannot be
        easy, but it must be done wisely and fairly if you expect such kindler and
        gentler attitudes to continue.


        I would ask not how many funds were
        appropriately placed by this decision, but how many funds were
        lost.

        Individual Comment
