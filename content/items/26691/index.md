        Instructions on Physically Injured Personnel in the Final Rule

        Mr Feinberg,

        Why are the instructions for physically injured personnel in the Victims
        Compensation Fund so vague and lacking in specific guidance?  With current
        rules, each case will be considered on an individual basis in determining
        the final compensation that will be provided.  With these vague rules, how
        does an injured person determine if  enrolling in the program will be in
        their best interest to cover potential medical bills that may arise at a
        later time and also give fair compensation in waiving your rights to sue at
        a later date? The current rules provide nothing in this regard.  I just want
        to make an informed decision that the Victims Compensation Fund will be my
        best option.  For the physically injured, there is no way to tell and no way
        to even estimate what the program might offer.  Additionally, why are there
        not rules for the non-economic loss for the surviving members of the
        families of the physically injured?  The final rule provides clear guidance
        for the deceased, but no specific guidance for physically injured for
        non-economic loss for family members.  I posted a earlier comment reference
        these rules and never got a response back.  I would respectfully request
        some feedback from my comments

        Individual Comment
        Springfield, VA
