        Wednesday, November 28, 2001 1:52 PM
        Victims of September 11



        Dear Sir/Madam:

        I am hoping that when the rules and regulations are set-up in regards to
        the financial support that is distributed to the survivors of victims of
        the disaster in New York on September 11, 2001 that consideration will be
        given to the loved ones of gay, lesbian, and transgendered partners.

        As gay, lesbian and transgendered people have no way of legally obtaining
        marriage licenses it is very hard for us to in cases like this to be
        treated equally as others.  We are not asking for special privileges, but
        equal privileges.

        In many cases, households that are set-up with life partners are devastated
        when a tragedy such as this strikes.  Many times insurance policies and
        wills can be contested by family members who have more rights than someone
        who may have been in a loving relationship for years with the deceased.

        I am just asking that gay, lesbian and transgendered citizens and taxpayers
        of the United States be treated fairly.

        Thank you for your consideration.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment

        Pittsburgh, PA
