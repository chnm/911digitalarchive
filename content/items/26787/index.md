        Monday, March 11, 2002 8:38 AM
        9/11 Justice for Gays


        Dear President Bush,

        I am extremely angry and saddened by the position taken by the Justice
        Department regarding victim compensation for gay, lesbian, bisexual, and
        transgender persons who died during the events of September 11th.

        Kenneth Feinberg,the head of the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund,
        has stated that for all intents and purposes the domestic partners and
        children of gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgender persons who died on
        9/11 will not be compensated in the same manner as the partners (spouses)
        and children of heterosexual persons who died in the events of that tragic
        day.

        On this, the six-month, anniversary of that terrible day, I respectively
        urge you and your administration to do the just and humane thing: fully
        compensate the families of all victims in the same manner, regardless of
        their sexual orientation or gender identity. Do not engage in a blatant act
        of discrimination and injustice. Do not increase the pain and suffering
        people have already suffered. You are the President and leader of ALL
        American's.

        My life and the work of our organization is completely devoted to achieving
        full and complete rights for all American's regardless of race, ethnicity,
        disability, sexual orientation, or gender identity.

        Respectively,

        Individual Comment
        Louisville, KY
