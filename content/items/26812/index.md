        To discriminate against gay and lesbian families is wrong.  When two people

        emotional, mental, and financial support.  When the 9-11 tragedy happened,

        families from 9-11 will have to go on and rebuild their lives.  I contributed
        money to many of the groups collecting money.  I never thought for a minute
        that in the light of a great tragedy you would deny money arbitrarily and
        capriciously.  In doing so you are making a gigantic moral mistake.
