        Benefits for Partners and Relatives of All 9/11 Victims


        I was astonished to read that some partners and relatives of the 9/11
        victims may not be compensated from the fund. Specifically I am referring to
        the surviving partners and relatives of gay and lesbian 9/11 victims.

        While I oppose discrimination against gay and lesbian Americans anytime,
        discrimination at this time is particularly odious in view of what we know
        now know about the deeds of heroes such as &nbsp and &nbsp , who happen to be gay men.

        Now is the opportunity for Americans to unite and heal together. It is not
        the time to perpetuate artifical divisions among Americans. Those brave
        heroes of 9/11 didn't, we shouldn't either. Let's reach out and take care of
        their loved ones and show how much stronger we can grow as a nation in the
        process.

        Sincerely,


        (a straight woman who believes in the spirit of our constitution)
