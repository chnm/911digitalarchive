        Monday, March 18, 2002 2:45 PM
        Gay and lesbian partner compensation

        To Whom It May Concern,
        It's an outrage that the 9/11 Compensation fund excludes partners of
        gays and lesbians.  There were many gay heroes on that day, including
                             (who helped bring down the plane in PA) and                      (who died in the line of duty when the first tower fell),
        and countless others.  We shame their heroism and their memory by
        neglecting their spouses who, just as their straight counterparts, are
        left to take care of their families and grieve in the wake of this grave
        incident.  Rest assured that the gay community and the majority out
        there who support equity in compensation among all spouses and families
        will make their voice heard if this issue is ignored.  This is simply
        prejudice, not just a legal matter.  Because if were simply a legal
        matter, we wouldn't be giving compensation to the families of illegal
        immigrants.  Do the right thing and include all families who lost loved
        ones that day.  It's less than American to do anything else.

        Thanks,
        Individual Comment
