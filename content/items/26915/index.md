        children ...  some of us stay home to care for those children ... If there is
        one person who was a co-parent to a child (possibly adopted) and his/her
        partner was a stay at home mom/dad what would this financial aid mean to that
        child?  This is very important... please don't let the morally judging groups
        out there dictate our countries future...  we are real, decent, socially
        viable people who deserve the same rights as our fellow contry men and women.
         Please, take this chance to stand up for all Americans.....
                 Together We Stand.... United Or Not!
                         Please..... begin the journey toward uniting this country
        even further.....
                                                 Thank you,


        Individual Comment
