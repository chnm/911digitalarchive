        Monday, January 07, 2002 7:51 PM
        I JUST DON'T BELIEVE IT!

        AFTER READING YOUR ARTICLE ON THE WTC VICTIMS RESTITUTION LAWS I JUST HAD TO
        PUT IN MY TWO CENTS. BEING A MOTHER OF TWO MYSELF AND RAISING THEM ALONE INTO
        THE GREAT ADULTS THEY ARE TODAY I TRULY DO NOT UNDERSTAND THIS EXTREMELY
        LARGE AND UNFOUNDED RESTITUTION AMOUNT FOR THE SURVIVOR OF THE VICTIMS THE
        WTC. I WILL BE THE FIRST ONE TO SAY HOW SORRY AND UNBELIEVABLE THIS
        CATASTROPHE IS NOT JUST TO THEM BUT TO ALL AMERICANS AND FELLOW HUMAN BEINGS
        ON THIS EARTH. ALBEIT A GENEROUS OFFER OF SUCH A LARGE SUM OF MONEY FOR THE
        SURVIVING FAMILY MEMBERS IT SEEMS A BIT OUT OF LINE FOR THE REST OF AMERICA
        THAT LOST SOMEONE IN WW1, WW2, VIET NAM, CIVIL RIGHTS MOVEMENTS, HOLOCAUST
        VICTIM SURVIVORS ETC. I JUST DO NOT UNDERSTAND WHY AMERICA IS SO RESPONSIBLE
        FOR THE TRAGEDY OF SEPT. 11 SURELY THESE PEOPLE NEED FINANCIAL HELP BUT DOES
        THE LIFE OF ONE OF THE VICTIMS IN THE WTC WARRANT MORE VALUE THEN THE LITTLE
        GIRL THAT WAS KILLED BY A DRUNK DRIVER? DO THE LIVES OF ALL THE SURVIVORS OF
        FIRES AND EARTHQUAKES, TORNADOES, HURRICANES, FLOODS AND MANY OTHER DISASTERS
        NOT WARRANT SOME FORM OF LIFE LONG FINANCIAL FREEDOM AS THE GOVERNMENT IS
        GIVING TO THE WTC SURVIVING FAMILY MEMBERS? WHAT ON GODS GREEN EARTH MAKES
        ALL AMERICANS, AMERICANS? WE ARE A NATION OF PRIVILEGE AND PRIDE, A NATION OF
        LOVE AND DEMOCRACY, BUT WE ARE NOT A NATION OF MAKING MILLIONAIRES OUT OF THE
        REMAINING DISASTER SURVIVORS. THE RESTITUTION FOR THE WTC FAMILIES IS WELL
        ABOVE AND BEYOND THE CALL OF DUTY TO ALLOW AMERICANS TO PUT MORE VALUE ON ONE
        DEAD AMERICAN THEN THE OTHER. I WAS UNDER THE IMPRESSION ALL AMERICANS WERE
        CREATED EQUAL, THEN WHY DO THESE PEOPLE DESERVE TO BECOME MILLIONAIRES MORE
        THEN ANY OTHER SURVIVING FAMILY MEMBER OF ANY OTHER DISASTER. YES, THESE
        FAMILIES NEED AND DESERVE HELP BUT DOESN'T ANY AMERICAN UNDER ANY DISASTER
        DESERVE THE SAME RESTITUTION'S? MY TAX DOLLARS DON'T EVEN PUT A DENT IN A
        SODA POP CAN BUT I HAVE TO SAY IF IT IS TO BE DONE FOR ONE IT SHOULD BE DONE
        FOR ALL, OR AREN'T WE STILL AMERICANS, AND AREN'T WE STILL EQUAL?
        Individual Comment
