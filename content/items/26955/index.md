        Thursday, November 29, 2001 6:48 PM
        victims and partners/family of victims of 9/11

        Dear Mr. Zwick,
        I understand you are accepting comments regarding the compensation fund for
        victims of the September 11th attack.  I would like to voice my concern for
        acknowledgment of gay and lesbian partners of the victims.  Please recognize
        the significance of their position, and their importance in the lives of the
        victims.

        There is no legal standard for partners of gay and lesbian Americans, and
        often, the significance of these couples is simply not considered when
        tragedy strikes.  Please include among relatives eligible for compensation
        those who lost their life partners or de facto parents or children,
        regardless of sexual orientation or marital status.

        Thank you.

        Individual Comment
        California
