        Help ALL Victims Of Sept. 11

        In late September, President Bush and the Congress authorized
        the creation of the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" to
        provide assistance to the surviving victims of the attacks and
        the "relatives" of those who died.  The Department of Justice
        is in the process of writing the regulations that will govern
        implementation of the Fund and has invited public comments to
        guide this process.  Sadly, there are those on the Radical Right
        who would like to see the surviving partners and children of gay
        and lesbian victims excluded from the benefits of the Fund.
         SUCH CRUEL AND REPREHENSIBLE VIEWS MUST NOT PREVAIL!

        I urge you to write regulations governing
        the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" that ensure that
        compensation is available to all surviving families of those
        killed, INCLUDING the committed partners and non-biological
        children of gay and lesbian victims.

        Individual Comment
