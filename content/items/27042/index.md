        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 5:03 PM
        Compensation Opinion

        I strongly believe that 9/11 victims should be compensated from either
        charities or government funds, not both. I disagree with the sentiment that
        those contributing to charity will be disinclined to donate in the future if
        their funds do not go directly to victims. On the contrary, many donors
        might be disinclined to donate again if they knew their donations were going
        to victims who had also received taxpayer support. Given our economy and the
        many negative repercussions of September 11, I believe double-compensation
        would be horribly wasteful.

        Thank you for soliciting public opinion.
        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Portland, Oregon
