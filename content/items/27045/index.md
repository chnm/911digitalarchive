        Thursday, March 28, 2002 11:26 AM

        I am appalled that consideration is being given to excluding partners of gay
        and lesbian heroes and victims of the 911 tragedy from receiving
        compensation funds. Perhaps all gays, lesbians, their families and other
        supporters should request that their contributions to any and all 911
        compensation funds be returned.

        It's wrong for members of our government to choose to lump all gays and
        lesbians into one group based on the actions of a few by excluding partners
        of these heroes from well-deserved compensation. If overlooking good works
        and contributions of a whole group based on the actions of a few is the
        logic applied, we should elect an entirely new government because of the
        actions of those in government who have committed immoral acts.

        It's time to take a step back, recognize the contributions of every
        individual for what they provide and build on those contributions to
        strengthen our country for the challenges going forward. Please, it's time
        to apply common sense!

        Respectfully,

        Individual Comment
