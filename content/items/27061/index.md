        Imperative to be equally compassionate

        To whom it may concern:

        It is imperative to acknowledge the loss and tragedy suffered by all of the
        victims of September 11, 2001.  That means the families as identified by the
        ones who survived the devastation of losing a domestic partner; whether that
        partner be the same or other gender, be equally compensated.  While the
        federal government has yet to recognize domestic partners, state
        jurisdictions as well as hundreds of city municipalities have done so.  I
        encourage the federal government to equally compensate those significant
        others of gay, lesbian, and heterosexual domestic partnerships as evidenced
        by shared households, expenses, and joint responsibilities.  I have complete
        confidence that the individuals making these important financial decisions
        will be just and fair minded.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Baltimore, MD
