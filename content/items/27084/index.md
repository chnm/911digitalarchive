        Dear Mr. Zwick:

             Thank you for the opportunity to submit comments on the Notice of Inquiry and
         Advanced Notice of Rulemaking for the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001
         ("the Act"). I offer comments and recommendations in the following three areas.

             1. It was not the intent of Congress or the President to preclude those who proceed
         with claims under the Act from also filing suits against the assets of Osama Bin Lidin, El Queda,  the Taliban or any other terrorist individual, organization, or government involved with the  events of September 11th. Such "frozen asset" suits should be specifically excluded from the  waiver provisions under the rules implementing the Act.

             2. The economic loss amount should not be reduced or offset by amounts received
         from life insurance proceeds or other benefits or assets owned by the September 11th victims.

             3. The rules should be very clear as to the rights of those who dismiss civil actions
         within the ninety day period in an effort to file a claim under the Act, but then are subsequently  deemed ineligible under the Act or elect not to file a claim.  Those individuals should be returned to the "status quo" so that they may, if they so choose, file in another forum.

             I trust that these comments and recommendations will assist in your development of the
         rules implementing the Act.  Please contact me if I may be of any further assistance in this  matter. Thank you.

                                           Sincerely,

                                           Individual Comment
                                           Washington, DC 20005
