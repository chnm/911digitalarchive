        Saturday, March 16, 2002 3:41 PM
        9/11 Fund's Treatment of Gays and Lesbians

        Kenneth L. Zwick

        Mr. Zwick

        I was amazed to learn of the way in which the 9/11 fund is discriminating
        against gays and lesbians.  Unborn children and illegal aliens are being
        included without regard to the state and federal laws applicable to those
        groups.  However, gays and lesbians will only be included to the extent
        applicable state laws allow.

        It is disheartening to discover that prejudice and hate can continue to live
        so strongly  in a time when unity is so important.  First, in the comments of
                    , who actually blamed gays and lesbians for causing God to allow the attacks to occur in the first place.  And now, in the unfair treatment of gay and lesbian citizens of this country when it comes to their participation
        in the fund.

        The enormous amount of national pride that has been generated and displayed
        since 9/11 is an amazing and wonderful thing.  So wonderful that the American
        public has been willing to focus less on our country's deteriorating
        financial condition.  A fact no doubt welcomed, and probably encouraged, by
        the current administration.  That's smart politics.  But to also use recent
        events as yet another vehicle for discriminating against the gay and lesbian
        community is unconsionable, and has much more to do with hate and intolerance
        than it does with politics.

        ALL of the deaths caused on 9/11 are tragic, and I mourn every one of them.
        It is heartbreaking to learn that the U.S. Government mourns those gays and
        lesbians who died on 9/11 less than the other victims.

        Individual Comment
        Atlanta, Georgia
