        Comments to interim final regulation as published

        Dear Sir:  I have already submitted my detailed comments on the interim regulations and your lack of enabling authority to in any way
        compromise money due the victims who you continually strong arm into accepting your position that they have no choice but to accept the tithing you propose to compensate them by.  This submission is on a different topic and is specifically directed to your election to publish the names on this site of persons forced to accept your meager terms due to financial exigencies.  For all the world to see, you now see fit to broadcast who has come begging to you in an apparent effort to get other victims to learn of their friend's plight and to get on board with you as well.  In public you emphatically tell people to trust you; that you will treat them fairly.   You also tell them that this is a totally anonymous process and that the specifics of the claim and their identities will be protected.  You then publish their names.  Why should non-joining people trust you to look out for them in bigger ways?  Now that you've told the world who the claimants are every scam artist, salesman and needy relative will be lining up.  Shame on you Mr. Feinburg.


        Individual Comment
        Skaneateles, New York
