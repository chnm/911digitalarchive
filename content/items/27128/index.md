        9-11 victim compensation fund

        3-18-02

        To the committee for the Victim Compensation Fund for 9-11 families:

        I am absolutely outraged that you consider partners of gay/lesbian
        couples not to be equal to the partners in heterosexual relationships and
        thereby will be denied any of the funds that have been collected for the
        families of the victims of the 9-11 attacks.
        I suspect you weren't forthcoming with your narrow-minded, bigoted policy
        because you knew it would cut back on contributions.  Had I known I would
        have looked elsewhere to donate my money.
        Partners and families of gay and lesbians have no less loss or grief than
        heterosexuals, and for you to deny them money that was contributed in
        good faith only wounds them a second time.
        I would respectfully request that you re-think your distribution policy
        of the money collected for these families to include ALL families.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
