        Tuesday, March 19, 2002 10:08 AM
        Fwd: 9/11 Fund to deny gays benefits

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        Recently I have seen you on television talking about the September 11th
        Victims Fund and I was outraged to hear that the fund that Congress
        established is discriminating Gay and Lesbian Americans.  Today, I received
        an email providing more detail on this fund and that has prompted me to
        express to you my concerns.

        First, I have to tell you that this decision tears down the statements made
        by our Government shortly after 9/11 telling us that we are no longer
        hyphenated Americans  we are Americans.  The tragedies that occurred the
        morning of September 11, 2001, were an attack on our entire nation.  When
        the attacked occurred, as a nation we transcended our differences and
        united as Americans.  In the days after September 11, 2001, our nation
        stood together and displayed to the world that a terrorist organization
        could take down buildings; however, they could not break the spirit of
        Americans.

        The tragic events of September 11th did not discriminate when it took
        victims; the fund our Congress has established to help the victims loved
        ones should not either.

        Sincerely,





        Individual Comment
