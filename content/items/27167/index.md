        Re:
        To Whom It May Concern:

        This letter is no voice comments with regard to the Victim Compensation with regard to my
        son,         .

        I do not believe that we should have to agree to and sign for a Settlement without the knowledge
        of the settlement amount. Also, I do not believe that the contributions from the American
        citizens of the United States should be deducted from the settlement. Nor do I believe that the
        insurance that my son paid for should be deducted either.

        My son was my carekeeper and I depended upon him in more ways than one and certainly not
        only financially. He did little things for me that people take for granted every day. He did little
        things such as open a window or pour may water. These are things that I am unable to do for
        myself without huge efforts. There is no price that you can put on these things.

        It is imperative that you reconsider the condition that you are abiding to with regard to this
        matter and sincerely considered changing the conditions to our benefit.

                                                Sincerely,

                                                Individual Comment
             Yonkers, NY
