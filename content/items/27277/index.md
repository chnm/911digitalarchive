        compensation

        As callous as this may sound, I am opposed to any compensation to the victims
        of the WTC.  I believe that ample donations have been received from various
        sources to compensate those affected.  If our government is responsible for
        every accident and every loss of life, then every person should be afforded
        the same consideration when an accidental death or injury occurs.  The loss
        of a child, husband, wife, father or mother, sister or brother is just as
        catastrophic to those who sustain this loss due to reasons other than the WTC
        catastrophe is just as real and devastating to those as it is to those who
        lost loved ones in the 9-11 disaster.  Any compensation afforded should be
        the same as any other loss due to accident, such as insurance, retirement,
        job related compensation etc.

        Individual Comment
