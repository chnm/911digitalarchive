        9/11 VICTIM COMPENSATION FUND

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am touched by the outpouring of support from our country to the victims of
        the tragedy of September 11th.  I understand there may be some question as to
        whether to include the partners and families of the gay and lesbian victims.
        I am shocked that such a question should even arise. Certainly every person
        who suffered or died at the hands of the terrorists deserves equal treatment,
        including financial benefits to surviving partners and family members.  The
        hatred that hijacked those planes believes that for reason of birth, life
        style, nationality or religion, certain people do not deserve to live.  I
        pray we do not perpetuate that divisive thinking within our own country.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Sherman Oaks, CA
