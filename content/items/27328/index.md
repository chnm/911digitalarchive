        The unfair reality of the Victims Compensation Fund

        I lost my husband     on 9/11. I have three children ages 4 and 2 and one
        born one month after the attack.  His company     is only giving
        health care benefits until April of this year.  How can I afford health care
        when the fund is deducting life insurance, pensions (which I do not have) and
        charitable donations as well as placing an economic cap on the salaries
        earned?  How can I manage when I will walk away with a little over a years
        salary?  I do not and will never have the earning potential my dear husband
        had not to mention that I was a stay at home mother.  Please I implore you to
        revise the rules to not include life insurance which we paid for and
        sacrificed to have.  I feel as though we have already paid the ultimate
        sacrifice...a lifetime without my husband, a daughter who never met her
        father and two children who will only have vague memories.  Haven't we been
        robbed and raped enough?  Please do what is right and just for our children
        in the name of our loved ones.
        Thank You.
        Individual Comment
