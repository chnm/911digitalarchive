                                      Re: Comments on Interim Rule

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

             I was unable to access the e-mail address for comments
        "victimcomp.comments@usdoj.gov."

        1.   The interim rule fails to make provision for possible
        conflict arising out of disparate interests of various survivors of
        a deceased victim, where one survivor does not wish to accept the
        benefit offered by the Fund but intends to sue but another survivor
        desires to accept the benefit and does not intend to sue, where
        both survivors would qualify as "heirs" under state law.

        2.   The interim rule fails to make clear whether the Fund
        award is divisible between multiple survivors and whether, if it is
        divisible, how the division is to be made and whether one
        survivor's claim would preclude another survivor's determination
        not to claim but to sue (see comment 1).

        3.   The interim rule fails to make clear whether the Fund
        award is the property of the decedent and accessible by creditors
        of the decedent or whether the Fund award is the property of the
        survivors and therefore immune to claims of creditors of the
        decedent.

                                 Yours sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        New York, NY
