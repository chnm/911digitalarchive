        TO:  Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
           Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
         U.S. Department of Justice

        FROM: &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp

        DATE: Wednesday, December 12, 2001

        SUBJ: Compensation of Gay and Lesbian Victims' Families

         This is in response to the Department's request for public comments regarding
        forthcoming regulations regarding the September 11th Victims Compensation Fund. I strongly urge
        the Department to develop regulations that guarantee equal compensation to all victims, regardless
        of sexual orientation and/or marital status. Until federal and other applicable laws fairly treat same-
        sex relationships, gay and lesbian families must receive equal treatment from funds such as this.

         Thank you for your attention.


        Individual Comment
