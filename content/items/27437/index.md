        Wednesday, December 12, 2001 2:58 PM
        Fairness in Funds Distribution

        Please ensure that the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund is
        distributed in the fairest way possible. To that end, I have two major
        concerns.

        Gay and Lesbian families should be treated the same as married couples in
        that domestic partners and children of those unions should receive
        compensation for the lost of their loved one.

        Also while I understand that funds will be distributed based on lost
        earnings, care must be taken so that all families receive at least a minimum
        level. The family of a corporate CEO and the family of a janitor will both
        be struggling with unforeseen expenses and this fund should make sure that
        they are able to buffer themselves from unpredictable crisis.

        Thank you for considering my comments.


        Individual Comment
        San Francisco, CA
