        Saturday, March 16, 2002 10:54 PM
        Regarding September 11th victim partner benefits

        re: September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Discrimination goes against the values that I hold as an American. I am
        outraged that gay and lesbian partners of those who died on September 11 are
        not automatically going to receive the same benefits as heterosexual spouses.
        Is the grief and loss of a gay or lesbian any less than valid than that of a
        heterosexual person?


        were all gay citizens who lost their lives on Sept 11th. It is extremely
        upsetting to me to read that unborn fetuses and illegal aliens will be
        recognized and receive benefits, yet our government cannot bring itself to
        recognize gays and lesbians for such a great loss.

        Can we not break down the boundaries of discrimination in this time of
        national grief and need? I call upon our government to declare that gay and
        lesbian partners will receive equal benefits to heterosexual spouses from
        this fund. Gays and lesbians deserve equal treatment.

        Respectfully

        Individual Comment
        Highland Park, NJ
