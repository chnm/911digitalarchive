        Wednesday, November 07, 2001 6:49 PM
        victim and victim family compensation

        We believe that a common sense approach with some guidelines on amounts and
        needs would be the best.  People all over the country cope all of the time
        with the loss of the breadwinner in their families and with insufficient
        life insurance.  If families planned well, and they have life insurance
        coming if they have lost a loved one their need may be less than those who
        didn't plan ahead.  Does that mean they don't deserve to receive some of
        the money that other Americans have given through the goodness of their
        hearts?  Of course not, and I don't think any of us who have donated
        believe that.  But the money wasn't meant to make anyone wealthy.
        The money can never compensate for the loss of a loved one.  If someone was
        injured they may need more money than the families who lost a loved
        one.  Children should not have to worry about paying for their education
        when they've lost a parent in this tragedy.  Older people who were being
        helped financially by a child should not have to do without.  Thanks for
        your attention.

        Individual Comment
        Rapid City, South Dakota
