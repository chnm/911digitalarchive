        Equal Access for Gay and Lesbian partners


        December  25, 2001

        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        I write as a member of Amnesty International, a worldwide human rights
        movement committed to ensuring respect for the basic human rights of people
        without discriminatio. In October the US congress passed wonderful
        legislation that would lead to the  creation of a federal fund to
        compensate the family members affected by the Sep. 11th tragedy.  I write
        to you, hoping that the lesbian and gay partners are not forgotten in the
        distribution of these relief funds. International human rights standards
        like the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, which the US is held
        accountable to, prohibit discrimination. The Justice Department must stick
        to the principles of equality and follow the example of New York state by
        granting compensation, "On a showing of mutual interdependence with the
        victim, in recognition that anyone who shared with the victim living
        expences, day to day activities and the emotional bonds of family deserves
        help in this time of need."  All of the victims deserve our love and help
        at this time. I am asking that the Justice department bring real justice
        with this legislation and include the surviving lesbian and gay partners by
        giving them equal access to crises and relief benefits. Thank you for your
        time.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Mankato, MN
