        Thursday, January 03, 2002 12:20 AM
        Comment on Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        Though the Department of Justice has established the Victim Compensation
        Fund for the victims of Sept. 11, it seems as though many of the survivors
        have been forgotten.  The injuries sustained by many of us were not harms to
        our physical body but to our minds, to our emotions, and to our souls.  Many
        of us realized after we were fortunate to return to our homes that we have
        been permanently disabled by this attack.

        The 24 hour physician window discriminates against those of us who were able
        to run for our lives during this catastrophe.  There are still many victims
        that are just now coming to terms with the psychological implications of
        this experience.  It should be considered that the hospitals were overrun
        following the attack and you must realize that for many of us the life
        altering injuries were not apparent within the first 24 hours.  Please
        consider this when re-evaluating your criteria for compensating the
        victims.. ALL OF US!!

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Jackson, MS
