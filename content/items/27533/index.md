        To whom it may concern:

        It is with great sadness that the United States government cannot stop discriminating during such a time of need among my fellow Americans.

        Discrimination based on sexual orientation or any other status is wrong.

        Whether a person is gay or lesbian should have no difference on the fact that they have lost someone very important and financially dependent.

        Please allow the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund to give money toward gay and lesbian partners and/or families as well as the heterosexual ones that have lost loved ones.

        Thank you,
