        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 5:23 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Attorney General Ashcroft and DOJ Staff,

        Thank you for the opportunity to submit comments on the rules for the
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund.  I am very concerned that this
        fund does not seem to have any caps for the maximum dollar amount per
        victim or for the total fund.  I believe that such caps are necessary
        to keep the fund within reasonable limits.

        Most Americans support offering some help to the victims of the
        September 11 terrorist attacks.  However, these victims do not
        deserve a blank checkbook!  We do not offer unlimited compensation to
        victims of natural disasters, or even to the families of our dead
        veterans or to families of winners of the Congressional Medal of
        Honor.

        I also believe that the fund should not compensate victims for
        expenses that have already been covered by insurance or by private
        charities.  This kind of policy discourages people from buying
        insurance or giving to charity.

        Regards,

        Individual Comment
        Dearborn, MI
