        Tuesday, January 22, 2002 6:38 PM
        comments on interim rule

        Dear Mr. Feinberg -
        I lost my brother, &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp , in the WTC attacks.  I am urgently requesting
        you to reconsider that portion of the fund distribution plan which deducts
        collateral sources such as life insurance and pension/social security
        benefits from the amount awarded.

        My preference is for a straight amount for each person lost - regardless of
        age, sex, number of children, or other sources of income - this fairly
        compensates each for the true value of their loss - the person not the
        paycheck.

        My sister in law will be raising two small children without a father for the
        rest of their lives.  To deduct for life insurance, pension, and other
        benefits my brother worked for is to doubly punish them for my brother's
        prudence.

        The purpose of the plan was to prevent lawsuits against the airline industry
        and others (the city of Boston, New York, the airports, the security
        companies, etc.) if the amount under the settlement is too low, there will be
        no incentive to settle and lawsuits will be the inevitable result.

        Please reconsider the 'collateral source' deductions!

        Thank you and God bless.

        Individual Comment
