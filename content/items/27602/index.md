        Saturday, March 16, 2002 3:45 PM
        Be a good American role Model - don't discriminate

        Im writing to encourage fairness in the wake of the
        September 11 attacks.  Discrimination is un-American.
        It didnt used to be and then, after September 11, our
        government told us, loudly and clearly, that
        discrimination actually was un-American.  I have
        always believed that. However, in recent interviews,
        the head of the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund
        stated the partners of gay and lesbian heroes of
        September 11 will not necessarily receive the same
        benefits as the spouses of heterosexual. That is
        discrimination and that it is un-American.  I am
        saddened and outraged that gay and lesbian partners of
        those who died on September 11 are not automatically
        going to receive the same benefits as heterosexual
        spouses.  Please be fair.  A Human being is a human
        being.  The human families of those human beings who
        died in the tragedy deserved to be treated equally. If
        the fund will be used to compensate fetuses, illegal
        aliens, and heterosexuals then it must also be used to
        compensate homosexuals and other unmarried partners in
        exactly the same way.  Please be good American role
        models and do not discriminate.  Compensate all of the
        humans affected by the tragedy equally.

        Individual Comment
