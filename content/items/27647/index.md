        13  December 2001


        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice
        FAX   (301) 519-5956




        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I write in response to the Department's request for public comments regarding regulations
        for implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."  I urge the
        Department to draft regulations that ensure compensation is available to all Americans and innocent
        victims of the attack, including partners and non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        One cannot doubt that the grief and economic losses suffered by the families of gay and lesbian victims
        are no less than that suffered by other families.  Under law in most of these United States, gays and
        lesbians are prohibited from giving their unions legal status equivalent to that of marriage.  Regardless,
        our country must draw no lines in compensating those who suffered the most personal of losses from
        these attacks, just as the terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims.

        Many Americans, homosexual and heterosexual, acted heroically on and in the aftermath of September
        11.  Many Americans, homosexual and heterosexual, lost their lives on September 11 and left behind
        loved ones.  I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the families of gay and lesbian
        victims as you would any other.

        Particularly during this season and in these circumstances, it behooves the United States of America to
        do the right thing rather than that which may be popular.

        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
        Lubbock, TX
