        comments on the victim fund

        TO:        Special Master Feinberg

        FROM:      Widow of World Trade Center Victim

        How can you determine whose life is worth more than another?

        It does not matter if your are a bond trader or a janior, 20 years
        old or 80 years old no mathmatical formula could ever equate to the
        loss of a loved one.

        To be fair and equitable each victim's family should be given the same
        amount of compensation.  Your position in life should not determine
        your value to your family.  Its not the size of your wallet but the
        size of your heart that should determine your standing in life as
        well as in death.

        It is highly unfair for any deductions of life insurances or pensions
        payments to be made.  What that would amount to, is a penalty for
        preparedness!

        Thank you for your time and consideration in this very important matter.
