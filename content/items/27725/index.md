        Friday, December 07, 2001 6:15 PM
        Victim Compensation Fund

        To Whom It May Concern:

        I am aware that you are soliciting comments regarding the Victim Compensation
        Fund for the World Trade Center Disaster Relief Fund.  I am writing to
        request that homosexual and lesbian individuals who lost their life partners
        be included among the relatives eligible for compensation, as well as their
        de facto children of the relationship.

        It is my opinion that the relief fund should be administered without regard
        to sexual orientation or marital status, and there is no reason for the
        federal government not to follow the example already  set by New York
        Governor Pataki in granting compensation benefits "on a showing of mutual
        interdependence with the victim, in recognition that anyone who shared with
        the victim living expenses, day-to-day activities and the emotional bonds of
        family deserves help in this time of need."

        Thank you for considering my opinion.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        New York, N.Y.
