        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 11:28 AM
        9/11 Victim Compensation fund

        Mr. Feinberg,

        It is an outrage to me and millions of other conscionable Americans that, in
        the midst of one of the greatest tragedies this country has ever known, such
        blatant discrimination would continue towards ANY HUMAN BEING who was
        personally effected!

        Your rationale for denying benefits to gay & lesbian survivors is so lame as
        to be ludicrous; your actions, nothing short of immoral.

        It is time for you and anyone else responsible for the distribution of these
        funds to come out from behind the proverbial veil of state disapproval.
        Each & every one of the survivors, regardless of race, creed, color, or
        sexual persuasion, is entitled to the same level of benefit.  To handle this
        fund in any other manner demonstrates a gross misuse of power and lack of
        humanity!

        Individual Comment
        Villa Park, Illinois
