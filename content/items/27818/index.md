        I strongly urge the Justice Department to draft regulations that ensure compensation is available
        to all
        victims of the attack, including the committed partners and the non-biological children of gay
        and lesbian
        victims.  The sorrow and economic losses suffered by the families of gay and lesbian victims are
        no less
        than that of other families.  As the terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims, our country
        too must
        draw no lines in compensating and supporting those who suffered the most personal of losses
        from these
        terrible attacks.  Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, and each of them left behind
        loved
        ones.  I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the families of gay and lesbian
        victims as
        you would any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
