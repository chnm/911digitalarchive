        Dear Mr Zwick:

        I am writing to you about the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund. As a public High School
        teacher, September 11th brings back horrible memories and images--- I  am sure you can imagine.
        During a time of such severe tragedy, it is up to us to make sure that all people are provided

        As a member of Amnesty International I ask that the Justice Department is consistent with
        international human rights standards, and all victims including gay and lesbians families are
        eligible for compensation. Real justice is served when all types of victims' families are
        treated equally.

        Thanks for your time,

        Individual Comment
