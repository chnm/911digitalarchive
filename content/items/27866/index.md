        Saturday, March 16, 2002 5:26 PM
        9/11 Releif Fund

        On September 11, Americans pulled together in a way that has not happened in
        my lifetime.  I collected and donated money in order that those who directly
        lost a family member in the tragedy would be able to financially survive
        their loss.  And I am outraged that the benefits that are being extended to
        illegal immigrants and unborn fetuses are being denied the families of gay
        and lesbian victims!  It is about time that we as a country, and you as the
        disbursers of aid to the families, stop discriminating against the
        gay/lesbian/bi/trans community!  A family is a family is a family; what you
        imagine as a family does not apply to all Americans.  The loss of a gay or
        lesbian spouse hurts no less than the loss of a heterosexual one, and the
        support of family, friends and country is greatly needed.
        So please, reconsider your allocation of funds to include the families of
        gay and lesbian victims.  Do not pass on this chance to show the world that
        all Americans are equal, and barriers can be broken down.
        Thank you,

        Individual Comment
