        Sunday, March 17, 2002 11:10 PM
        September 11th

        I just read an article indicating that Attorney General Ashcroft has granted
        immunity from deportation to illegal alien families of September 11th
        victims. These families can come forward for financial assistance from the
        September 11th fund without fear of deportation. I am happy to hear it -
        these people are humans and are undoubtedly in need of support.

        Kenneth Feinberg, the director of the Sept. 11th fund, seems happy to be
        able to sidestep federal law to help these victims. However, he indicated
        that he will only grant funds to partners of homosexual victims in
        accordance with state law because he is afraid of lawsuits. Since almost no
        states recognize homosexual partnerships/marriages, it is likely that many
        people will be excluded from funding. Does this really seem fair to you?

        I would like to hear that our government is at least considering granting an
        "immunity" similar to the one for illegal aliens. Perhaps Kenneth Feinberg
        and the September 11th Fund could be immune from being sued by the states if
        they help ALL the victims of September 11th.

        I am a 35-year-old, Caucasian, heterosexual woman. This letter is written
        not in support of my own rights, but to hopefully help a group of people
        from being victimized twice by the tragic events of September 11, 2001.

        Thank you for your time.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
