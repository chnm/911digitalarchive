        To Whom It May Concern:

        I recently learned that there is a possibility that the lesbian and gay
        partners of people who we lost on September 11th will not be receiving the
        same compensation as heterosexual partners.  I truly hope that this is
        misinformation, but felt compelled to voice my opposition to this.

        Whatever you think about homosexuality, these people have lost important
        people in their lives.  Whether the law recognized them as married or not,
        they were just as much a part of their lives as the husbands, wives, children
        of the other victims of the tragedy.

        This is not an issue of "special rights" or any other nonsense that the
        anti-gay forces seem to assert.  This is about a loss.  And, while money can
        never compensate anyone for their lost memories and future dreams, why add to
        their grief with blatant homophobia?

        The gay and lesbian victims are just as dead as the heterosexual ones.  And
        their families feel the loss just like anyone.  We've all heard the stories
        coming from the tragedy, including those of             and            .  These were real people, not gay rights activists.

        I truly hope that the news stories are incorrect, but fear that they might
        be.  For what it's worth, I hope that my voice is heard and that you
        reconsider the decision to treat these human beings as second class citizens
        (or re-affirm your commitment to ensuring that the families of these victims
        are treated equally).

        Thank you,

        Individual Comment
        Philadelphia, PA
