      text: I was on my way to work in Alexandria after dropping my son off at school after an early-morning dentist appt.   I had been listening to a book on tape and hadn't heard the news.  My husband called me and I put on the radio and I thought at first that it must have been a small plane, a ridiculous accident, didn't yet understand the scope.  By the time I got to Alexandria, there was a report about the Pentagon, mention of a bombing at the State Dep't; it felt like the world was coming to an end.We could see smoke from the Pentagon from the dock behind the art center. Several people were watching TV in one of the studios, the building was not yet open and it was very quiet.  We watched the footage of the planes hitting the buildings and were stunned by the collapses.  It felt like we watched for hours.  I spoke to my husband, tried to reach my brother in Manhattan, and went home.
