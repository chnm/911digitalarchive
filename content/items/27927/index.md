        Sunday, December 09, 2001 1:23 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        To whom this concerns:

        I applaud your efforts in creating the September 11th Fund to provide
        compensation for the losses of relatives of those who died, and those who
        were injured.

        Please be aware that many of the grieving families are committed partners or
        non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims of the terror attacks.
        The compensation given by this fund must also include families of these
        victims as well or the whole idea behind the fund will be promoting a gross
        inequity.

        I urge you to include among relatives eligible for compensation those who
        lost their life partners or de facto parents or children, regardless of
        sexual orientation and marital status.

        Thank you for your consideration.

        Individual Comment
        Sacramento, CA
