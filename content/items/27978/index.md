        December 7, 2001

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I send you this e-mail as a request to consider including among those
        eligible for victim compensation those who lost their life partners as well
        as de facto parents or children, without regard to sexual orientation or
        marital status.

        Please follow the example already set by New York Governor Pataki in
        granting compensation benefits "on a showing of mutual interdependence with
        the victim, in recognition that anyone who shared with the victim living
        expenses, day-to-day activities and the emotional bonds of family deserves
        help in this time of need."

        Justice should be for all citizens of the United States and should not
        exclude those who fall into a minority that may not be accepted by certain,
        small and very vocal groups.

        Please consider my request, along with the many others similar to it, and
        include life partners of heros like                       .

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Chicago, IL
