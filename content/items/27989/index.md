        As a personal injury attorney, I have heard the objections of many who
        feel that the interim final rule is unfair.  I do NOT agree.  I think
        that the plan which has been worked out, while not perfect, is an
        excellent and fair system.  It is easy to understand and for 98% of all
        people, extremely generous.  No system is perfect including the present
        tort system.  I completely endorse the proposed rules.

        One comment.  I do not understand why anyone would choose track A in
        lieu of track B.  Under A, you can ask for a hearing.  Under B you
        immediately go to a hearing.  Since you have NOTHING to lose by having a
        hearing (they will not reduce the award that you receive under track A),
        everyone will end up with a hearing anyway.  So why not just have a
        hearing for everyone (just track A or B).

        Thank you.

        Individual Comment
