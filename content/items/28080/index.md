        The world is watching us!

        The entire globe is watching the United States to see how we pull together
        in the wake of September 11, 2001.

        While they will see many shining examples of heroism, they will also see
        that the US government deems 25 million of its own taxpaying citizens as
        less worthy of aid than illegal aliens.

        If people here illegally are to be given humanitarian aid, so be it.  But,
        do not grant such assistance then turn around and deny it to gay and lesbian
        American citizens who simply seek to nurture and support their families!

        Our European allies especially must be watching us with quizzical faces,
        wondering at our line of reasoning.  Give money to illegal aliens but not to
        American citizens in the same need?  It is no wonder they shake their heads.

        In the sight of the world, do the right thing.  Provide needed aid to ALL
        American citizens.




        Individual Comment
        Alpine, NY
