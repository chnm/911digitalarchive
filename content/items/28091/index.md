        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 10:41 AM
        Just Distribution of Sept 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Members of the Justice Department,

        As you finalize the details of the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund, I
        write to urge you to take all kinds of families into account as you consider
        who should receive compensation.  Please be sure to include among relatives
        eligible for compensation those who lost their same-sex life partners, as
        well as de facto parents or children, without regard to sexual orientation
        or marital status.
        Please consider the example already set by New York Governor Pataki in
        granting compensation benefits "on a showing of mutual interdependence with
        the victim, in recognition that anyone who shared with the victim living
        expenses, day-to-day activities and the emotional bonds of family deserves
        help in this time of need."
        Thank you for your just and equitable distribution of these funds in this
        time of terrible tragedy.  The pain and loss of gay, lesbian, bisexual and
        transgendered partners, their parents and children is no less than that of
        married couples and their families.  I give thanks for your serious
        attention to their needs.
        With Prayers for Peace,

        Individual Comment
        Chicago, IL
