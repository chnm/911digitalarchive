        Dear Sir:

        I am outraged that gay and lesbian partners of those who died on September
        11 are not automatically going to receive the same benefits as heterosexual
        spouses of those who perished that horrible day.
        The Victim Compensation Fund Web site is for ALL Americans!
        To exclude this group, the partners of gay and lesbian heroes of September
        1, from always receiving the same benefits as the spouses of heterosexual
        victims of the horrible terror attacks is a blatant INJUSTICE.
        This arbitrary decision is especially egregious given the other decisions
        related to the fund, such as compensating fetuses and illegal aliens.     If
        the issue is one of law (as I have heard it said) then the arbitrariness of
        the decision is underscored by the fund's decision to overlook federal law
        and NOT arrest and apply Federal law in expelling the families of illegal
        aliens, nor punish American companies that hired so-called illegals in
        violation of federal labor laws.
        The message is crystal clear.  It is all right to die for America if you are
        Gay, but once again if you are Gay, you do not have the same rights or
        PRIVLIDGES as a heterosexual American.

        It is a sad day to be an American....

        Respectfully,
