        Compensation for Gays/Lesbians

        To those that are deciding who is eligible for victims' compensation:

        Your idea that the partners of gays and lesbians do not qualify for
        compensation is very disturbing.  By deciding that compensation can be paid
        out to other people, including illegal aliens and unborn children, in essence
        says that your opinion of gays and lesbians falls somewhere lower on the list
        than all of those other groups that do qualify.

        Now, if this is the plan that you intend to continue to implement, then
        perhaps we gays and lesbians of the world should abandon all good intentions
        when it comes to risking our lives to save the lives of the people that you
        love.  This would certainly make sense, since we wouldn't want to lose our
        lives knowing that our partners and our children will be left in this world
        without any hope of financial support from you.

        Furthermore, you can rest assured that the money and votes that would assist
        anyone in perpetuating this type of discriminatory practice will dwindle to
        nothing as more and more people understand the hatred for others that you are
        fostering in our world.

        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
        Long Beach, CA
