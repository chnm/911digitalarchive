        Saturday, December 29, 2001 1:50 PM
        September 11 Victims Compensation Fund

        To Whom It May Concern:

        My husband &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp was an employee of &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp who died in the World Trade
        Center attack. I would like to object to the proposed compensation formula
        presented by Special Master Feinberg. In order for the compensation fund to
        work, it needs to provide compensation in line with comparable wrongful
        death actions. By requiring offsets for collateral funds, this fund is
        unable to come even close. Under the provisions, my family would stand to
        receive $0, even though Mr. Feinberg would have the public believe that most
        families would receive substantial compensation. Why should the insurance
        companies and pension funds bear the responsibility for the failure of so
        many parties to protect the tenants in the World Trade Center? I would like
        to propose that all offsets be cancelled and the non-economic portion of the
        compensation be amended to reflect the amounts awarded in similar wrongful
        death actions.

        Individual Comment
        No. Massapequa, N.Y.
