        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 2:58 PM
        Compensation for Families of Sept 11 attack

        Kenneth L. Zwick
        Director Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick,
        I am writing to express my support for including gay/lesbian/bisexual and
        transgender families in the "Victim Compensation Fund." As an American
        citizen who has lived in a family relationship with another man for nearly
        seventeen years, and as the father of a two-year old boy, I believe that
        our family is no less deserving the protection and support of our nation
        than any other. And should we personally experience a tragedy of the
        magnitude of September 11, I would hope that the nation would come to our
        aid as fellow Americans.

        My hope is that one day our nation will rise above the petty divisiveness
        we so often see and hear, and recognize the great wonder and diversity of
        the families that weave together the fabric of our society.

        Thank you,

        Individual Comment
        Charleston, SC
