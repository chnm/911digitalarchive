        Dear Sirs:
        I am a gay man who has had a 12 year relationship with a fine and caring

        in circumstances like mine.  It would be a  travesty if the loved ones of
        these people are ignored in the national effort to heal the wounds of that
        horrible event.  I contributed money to the fire fighters' families who had
        succumbed in the disatrous collapse of the twin towers.  Please don't make me
        regret that contribution by petty and small minded treatment of the gay
        victims.  There are bound to be few of these couples, but treat them with the
        same regard you would the other family members.
        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
