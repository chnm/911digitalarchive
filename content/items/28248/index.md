        Saturday, March 16, 2002 1:01 PM
        Sept 11 Victims' Compensation Func

        Sir

        I am utterly stunned and outraged that the Victims Compensation fund
        includes fetuses and illegal immigrants but excludes gays and lesbians.  The
        last time I checked, fetuses and illegal immigrants pay no taxes (from which
        this fund is taken), yet I, as a legally single man (despite being partnered
        for four years) pay the highest percentage of my income to the Federal
        government and people like me who lost loved ones in the attacks get
        nothing.

        I demand an immediate and acceptable expanation for this.  Deferring to the
        States' laws is a huge "cop-out."  The States are not paying for this, the
        federal government is, so the States have nothing to say about it.  If it
        were up to the States, would it not make sense to disburse the funds to the
        States in proportion to the number of people each State lost?  If it were up
        to the States, would illegal immigrants from California not be excluded
        because of ballot propositions over the past few years (as shameful as that
        is)?

        If this "public commentary" actually has any effect, I'm sure I won't be the
        last you hear from on this.  Change your mind.  It's not up to the States.
        It's up to you, and you alone, and failing to be fair about this fund will
        be on your shoulders alone.




        Individual Comment
        Pittsburg, CA
