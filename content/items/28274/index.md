        RE:  September 11, 2002 Victims' Fund:

        It has come to my attention that the September 11. 2002 Victims' Fund
        established and funded by Congress is not being fairly administered in terms
        of discrimination against survivors and beneficiaries of victims who were
        lesbian or gay.

        I trust that this is not the case, and if it is that this discrimination is
        addressed and the fund is distributed with equity and fairness.

        To have lost loved ones in such terrible ways is difficult enough, to add
        insult to such injury by not respectfully acknowleding the loved ones,
        survivors and beneficiaries of all of the victims is unfair, unethical and
        immoral.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
