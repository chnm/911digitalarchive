        Comment on Interim Regulations for 9/11

        Comment on Interim Regulations for 9/11
        Victim Compensation

        I am in full support of this regulation going forward as is. This victim
        compensation is an attachment to the AIR TRANSPORTATION SAFETY AND SYSTEM
        STABILIZATION ACT."
        There is no indication that the action of terrorists were directly related
        to lax security or breech of any law or regulation. This is a generous perk
        to families who have suffered from their loved one being a victim of a
        tragic event. People are tragically killed every day whether it be in the
        workplace, public transportation or act of war or terrorism. That's life and
        the result of being in the wrong place at the wrong time. It's wonderful
        that some people had the resources to purchase abundant life insurance,
        others did not have discretionary dollars to do so. This should be about
        need and not greet.

        Individual Comment
