        Tuesday, January 15, 2002 8:20 AM
        Victims Compensation

        Dear Sirs:

         I am writing due to the methods being imposed for compensation upon the
        victimized families that have been affected by the September 11, 2001
        attacks at the WTC, Pentagon, and Pennsylvania.

         With an understanding of our immediate desire following the attacks to
        assist business, I appreciate the sentiment behind the Victims
        Compensation Fund P.L. 107-42.  I take strong exception however to the
        interim rules issued by Kenneth Feinberg, The Special Master.

         My town of Mahasset, NY suffered greatly with everyone else on that day
        that brought solidarity and resolve to this great country. As anxious as
        we may be to put behind us the litigation that will surely result from
        this tragedy, I feel strongly that the establishment of arbitray income
        limits is inconsistant with the justice we all strive to achieve.

         I would request that you make amendments to the interim rules to the
        extent that income caps be removed when computing economic loss and that
        compensation for pain and suffering more accurately reflect the
        suffering.

        Individual Comment
        Mahasset, NY
