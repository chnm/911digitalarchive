        Fiances' as claimants

        We along with dozens of parents who lost sons or daughters are outraged at
        some fiances'  who feel they should be qualified claimants and who also feel
        that parents are better off financially than they are. This is an outrage and
        a contradiction to our American traditions. Many parents live on fixed
        incomes and are in need of long term care and medical assistance . Many of
        these same fiances' were earning more than the deceased.The state statutes
        clearly indicate that the next of kin should be the qualified claimant. We
        parents sacrificed our entire life for our children only to be dealt with
        forever pain and suffering that cannot be experinced by any other
        relationship.

        Fiances' would be better served if they worked out their differences with the
        families of the deceased . Parents are the most qualified claimants both
        under the law and under moral principles.

                                                                Thank you,
                                                                Parents
        Individual Comment
        New York City,NY
