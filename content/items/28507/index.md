        Greetings--
        I am writing you to beg you to honor the memory of all of those killed

        extend benefits to all the families and spouses of those victims.  All
        Americans, straight and gay alike, who lost their loved ones that day

        and rather than
        being Italian-Americans, African-Americans or gay-Americans, we were now
        simply Americans.  There is no room for politics here.  Please, please,
        treat everyone respectfully and equally.

        in Christ's name--

        Individual Comment
