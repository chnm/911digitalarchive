        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 11:06 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund Comments

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing to urge the Justice Department to live up to its name by
        administering the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund in a fair and just
        manner by including among relatives eligible for compensation those who lost
        their life partners as well as de facto parents or children, without regard
        to sexual orientation or marital status.

        In the midst of this great national tragedy, it is time we truly became a
        nation of justice in the way we treat our own citizens.  Gay and lesbian
        people and their families suffered no less, lost no less, and must have no
        less access to equality and justice in its aftermath.  I urge you to follow
        New York Governor Pataki's example in granting compensation benefits "on a
        showing of mutual interdependence with the victim, in recognition that anyone
        who shared with the victim living expenses, day-to-day activities and the
        emotional bonds of family deserves help in this time of need."

        I look forward to a fair and compassionate response from the Administration
        in regards to this issue.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Richmond, Virginia
