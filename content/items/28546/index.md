        Non-compensation for gay and lesbian partners


        Dear 9-11 Fund,
        I am outraged that partners of gay and lesbian people who lost
        their lives in the 9-11 attacks are not being allowed to
        receive money because of their sexual preference.  I know that
        one argument is that "we are following the state law".  I feel
        this should not even be considered.  If the government is
        looking the other way for illegal immigrants then how can it
        not do the same here?  Just because someone has a different
        sexual preference doesn't make them any different than the
        rest of us.  They work hard, pay taxes and make contributions
        to society.  They, just like all the other victims of 9-11,
        have suffered.  They are no different.  They should not be
        treated differently.  They must be allowed to receive a check
        like everyone else.  Thank you.
        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
