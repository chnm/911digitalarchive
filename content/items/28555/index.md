        Friday, March 22, 2002 2:16 PM
        exclusion gay/lesbian partner in Victim Compensation Fund

        While I am so glad to hear of all that is being done
        to assist families of Sept. 11 victims, I am outraged
        to learn that partners of gay and lesbian victims will
        not be assisted in the same way as family members of
        heterosexual victims. A victim is a victim, and grief
        is grief. Though there may be no legal bond between
        two partners of the same sex who have chosen to love
        and support one another and spend the rest of their
        lives together, that is only because current laws do
        not allow them to create the same legal bond as a man
        and a woman. Gay couples support one another in every
        way heterosexual couples support one another, and one
        of those ways is financially. In fact, as many gay
        people have been alienated by family members, their
        partner and other friends, often fuction as their only
        family. In short, I feel this causes a great sadness,
        that, in many ways, still America refuses to be
        "united" and to agreed that "all men [and woman] are
        created equal." I ask that you please reconsider
        exclusion of gay and lesbian partners in the September
        11 Victim Compensation Fund. Otherwise, you are deeply
        dishonoring these victims' lives, love, and place as
        Americans lost in a terrible tragedy.

        Thank you,
        Individual Comment
