        Please treat all 9-11 victims equally. Its only right.


        Dear 911 Victims Fund:

        I've recently become aware* of the fact that your fund will not be
        recognizing same sex partnerships in the same what it will be recognizing
        heterosexual partnerships.  I understand that Mr. Fein berg is willing to
        overlook federal laws regarding illegal aliens and their unlawful employers.
        Yet when it comes to recognizing same sex partnerships Mr. Fein berg tries
        to lay blame on the lack of consistent recognition of same sex partnerships
        by the states and their laws.

        The majority of Americans believe in equal treatment for everyone -- even
        gays and lesbians.  Please reconsider your position and provide funds
        equally to same sex partners as you do for heterosexuals.  Just because we
        can't legally marry doesn't nullify the existence of same sex partners.
        Please make us proud that we're all part of the human race and TREAT
        EVERYONE EQUALLY.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Indianapolis, IN
