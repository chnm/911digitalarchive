        Dear USDOJ:
        I am writing you to ask you to please reconsider you exclusion of
        gay/lesbian partners from the compensations fund.
        I have no connection to the fund, nor did I lose anyone I love on
        September 11th, nor do I have any connection really to the gay rights
        community.  But it just breaks my heart to think of the many partners who
        lost not only their loved ones, but the financial, emotional, and
        physical support, and to think that their will be no help for them. And
        even worse, no acknowledgement that their loss is real, and as difficult
        to suffer through as any other.

        Thank You,
        Individual Comment
        Boise, Idaho
