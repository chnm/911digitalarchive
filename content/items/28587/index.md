        Wednesday, November 28, 2001 6:01 PM
        September 11th Compensation Fund of 2001

        Dear Kenneth L. Zwick,

        I would like to state as a supporter of the National Gay and Lesbian Task Force that it is unfair to deny the life partners and families of gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgendered people the right to part of the compensation fund. They should be considered with the same amount of sympathy and compassion as a heterosexual person who died on that fateful day.

        The life partners of those who died need support, especially if there are children involved. We need to include them in the relief effort because they are part of this country, and it is unlawful to 'forget' them when it is time to hand out the help that they need so badly.

        Please consider the consquences of your actions.

        Individual Comment
        Davis, CA
