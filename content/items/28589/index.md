        hello
        I find it difficult to understand how anyone can measure the degree of gief
        and pain suffered by the tragic loss of a loved one--whether it be a
        child-parent or spouse.The survivors anger or disappointment in the lack of
        better security cannot measure pain.Therefore I firmly believe every. victims

        spouse receive the biggest portion (perhaps based on family size and
        potential earnings-)-then all others receive an equal amount for the pain of
        losing a child or mother or farther.
        It seems to me that some representation on the decision making committee
        should be those that have suffered the most--to be able to bring the true
        feeling of loss to the committee.
        respectfully
