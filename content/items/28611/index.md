        Tuesday, March 12, 2002 2:27 PM
        Denial of 9/11 funds to domestic partners


         I am ashamed that my country would condone discrimination in the
        distribution of funds that have been donated by people all over the world
        for victims of September 11.  I donated money to various victim funds with
        the intention that it would be distributed to all the families of anyone
        killed or injured by the horrendous terrorist attacks.  I did not anticipate
        that my donations would be denied to domestic partners, whether same sex or
        opposite sex, of victims simply because they are not legally married.  Many
        would be married if they could be.  There are many reasons why two people in
        love do not marry but the lack of a legally married status does not render
        them any less in love, any less dependent upon each other, any less of a
        citizen, or any less deserving of my money.  Most egregious are laws that
        forbid the marriage of same-sex couples.  How can my country give these
        donated benefits to the families (mostly illegal alien themselves) of
        illegal aliens who have flouted the law to reap the advantages of living in
        this country and yet deny them to law-abiding citizens who have contributed
        to this country but who simply have not legalized their partnerships,
        especially those who are forbidden to legalize their partnerships?  How can
        my country ignore the domestic partner of a gay man (and possibly of other
        men and women, straight or gay, we do not know about) who quite likely saved
        the lives of the very people who are now denying him this money that I and
        other far more fortunate people (more fortunate because we survived) donated
        to help all the families now bereft of their loved ones?
         I demand that the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund headed by
        Kenneth Feinberg return my donations if this heinous injustice is allowed to
        continue.  The terrorists did not single out only certain people to be
        killed; they targeted everyone and anyone, gay and straight, legal citizens
        and illegal aliens, men and women, good people and bad, young and old.  Mr.
        Feinberg, you are misappropriating my money.  Your intended actions will
        misappropriate this money by perpetuating moral judgments against victims of
        events that defied morality.


        Individual Comment
        Dayton, Ohio
