        Sunday, January 06, 2002 6:55 PM
        Sept 11 Funds

        I don't think its fair that tax dollars are used to
        compensate for the victims' families. What about
        future terrorist acts?  Is our country plan to
        compensate everyone for every terrorist acts?  How
        will we determine terrorist acts? The killings on Long
        Island train or massacre at various high schools some
        years ago, weren't they terrorist acts? or were they
        classified as "insane acts"?  What difference does it
        make? result is all the same! Deaths and grave
        tradegy!
        Furthermore, to have the compensation based on
        victim's earning potential is just obsurd!  I have  a
        lot of compassion for helping people in needs but I
        certainly don't see the need to use our tax dollar to
        help maintain the life style of victim's families!
        (compensation based on earnings is doing just that!).

        I have been a hard worker all my life and paying taxes
        all along!  Our countries need the money to fight the
        war on terrorism! People die of various causes all the
        time!  These other people were not "compensated" by
        our country for their death, however wrongful!

        I understand that this is a part of the airline bail
        out package to avoid victims' law suits.  I wonder if
        our great country will be bankrupted by all the law
        suites!  Our culture has become a culture that abuse
        their rights to sue! Frivolous law suits are
        everywhere. From the McDonald's coffee being too hot
        to a thief breaking a leg while robbing a house.. All
        these idiotic law suite should have been thrown out
        before they get to the court!  And to know the outcome
        being even more absurd than the lawsuits themselves.
        one can understand why living costs have climb so much
        and so fast!  I am absolutely behind law suits as a
        result of real wrongful death and believe in "punitive
        damage" as in the case of the PG&E's dumping of toxic
        waste.  But what "wrong" had the World Trade Buildings
        owner or leaser done on Sept 11th?  No Wrong! It's not
        the fault of our government, not the fault of the WTC,
        not the fault of the employers who had chosen offices
        inside the WTC! I think compensation by the outpouring
        of private donations is enough! Goverment should bar
        lawsuites against the WTC owner and leaser, lawsuites
        against airlines. In the case of Sept 11, the airport
        security wasn't breeched! The knifes the terrorist
        carried were allowed on and before that date.  What
        failed was our own perception about hijacking!  Had we
        adopted the policies of Israeli Airline of 'no entry
        to cockpit' then the terrorist couldn't have taken
        control of the plane.
        Now we have all learned a great lesson! Painfully yes.


        Individual Comment
