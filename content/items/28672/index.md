        Friday, March 15, 2002 10:07 PM
        Equal Compensation

               I must say how sorry I am to have heard about your Final Policy, which
        effectively disbars gay and lesbian partners and families of 9-11 victims
        from compensation.
               The tragedy of 9-11 effected everyone in the World Trade Center, the
        Pentagon and Flight 93. The terrorists had little concern as to whether their
        victims were gay or straight. Why should our government have such concern?
               I understand that illegal aliens will be able to come forward to gain
        their just compensation. I have no complaint on that front-they are just as
        injured as anyone else. It is strange, however, that their path to
        restitution has been smoothed, while gay and lesbian Americans have had
        barriers placed in their way. Surely similar accomodation to that granted to
        the illegals can be found.
               I would expect that our government would be as concerned to mitigate
        the suffering of all Americans who went through the horror of 9-11 as it is
        to wage the war on terrorism. Please reconsider your policy so that true
        justice can be served.


         Yours truly,

         Individual Comment
