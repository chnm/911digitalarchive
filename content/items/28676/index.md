        Missing my father

        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

        On 9/11, my father &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp was tragically
        killed while he was at work in the WTC.
        He worked in the private sector as did the majority of
         the people who worked in the Twin Towers.
        The DOJ, however, elected to use outdated and obsolete
        military and civil service data and has applied it to
        the private sector.
        We have become painfully aware that the interim rules
        of the plan are fraught with many deficiencies which
        need to be amended. The most egregious of which are:
        1) underestimating the earnings of civilians in the
        private sector (2) using inappropriate data that
        underestimate earnings growth in the private sector
        (3) not allowing for recovery for lost household
        services (4) penalizing families who exercised good
        financial planning and (5) signing away our rights.
        A full review and revision based on up-to-date data
        would be greatly appreciated by the undersigned.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Plainsboro, NJ
