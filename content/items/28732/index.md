        As a heterosexual woman, I would like to voice my disappointment in our government's decision to include illegal aliens in the compensation of 911 and exclude gay and lesbians. Especially when they have died saving the lives of the very people who are denying their partners what is rightfully theirs. If one vote counts, I vote to give ALL the victims their due and forget the archaic prejudices of yesteryear.

        Ashamed for you,
