        Thursday, March 21, 2002 6:36 PM
        9/11 victim compensation fund

        Dear Kenneth Feinberg and colleagues,

        I am dismayed to hear that the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund will
        not benefit ALL people who lost loved ones in the terrorist attacks on the
        World Trade Center and Pentagon.  It seems less than civilized to refuse
        benefits to same sex partners in this time of national mourning.  Every
        single person who lost a loved one deserves the same treatment, regardless
        of their race, nationality, religion, gender, or sexual
        preference.  Extending benefits to illegal aliens and unborn fetuses and
        refusing the same benefits to gays and lesbians is atrocious.  The rights
        of Illegial aliens and fetuses are not protected under the laws of this
        nation or in states any more than the rights of homosexuals; therefore,
        granting compensation to the former groups and denying them to the latter
        is blatantly unfair.

        Please make sure that all people are treated the same way in the wake of
        this disaster.  The attacks themselves were devastating enough, the gays
        and lesbians who will be hurt by your policy do not need any further reason
        for grief.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Providence, RI
