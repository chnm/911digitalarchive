        Dear Dept of Justice Victim Compensation staff,

        I'm writing to urge you to include all families in your plan for
        compensation, including gay, lesbian, bisexual, and trangendered parents,
        life partners, and children.  Their pain is no less valid or meaningful
        just because the state does not recognize their relationships.  We've all
        already suffered enough, why cause these families even more pain by denying
        them the compensation that other victims and families are recieving?  Take
        a stand and make a program that is inclusive of ALL who were harmed or lost
        someone on Sept 11!



        Thank you for your time and attention!



        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment

        Middletown, CT
