        Protest 9/11 Fund Exclusion of Gays

        To whom it may concern,

        I recently read that the benefits received from the
        9/11 Fund are different for heterosexual partners of
        victims than gay and lesbian partners.  I am outraged
        to hear that after all the pain we have been through
        as a country, we are still inflicting pain on each
        other.  "Family" has many definitions and all families
        affected by this horrible event should be supported
        equally - regardless.  It is incredibly sad to me that
        one family's suffering is considered more just than
        another's.  The 9/11 Fund has the power to be part of
        the healing of this country or perpetuate
        discrimination and hate.  I hope you choose to help
        unite us.

        Individual Comment
        Seattle, WA
