        Thursday, January 17, 2002 5:42 PM
        Victims families

        I am writing in regard to the unfair distribution of the monies to the
        victims of Sept. 11ths families.   My Uncle
         died in the World trade center attacks.   He took care of, and supported my
        Grandmother.   I think that not only is it unfair that  you will deduct any
        insurance money and other donations...but you won't tell us the amount we
        will receive.    How can we decide to accept a sum of money , when we don't
        know what the sum is.   That is like agreeing to buy a car without knowing
        the price first.   I just think that the families should be told how much
        they will receive if they don't sue...and should also get all of that money,
        with no deductions.    It is hard to put a price on someone's life.   No one
        , and no amount of money can take away the pain that our family is feeling.
        But don't you think my Grandmother at least deserves to have a quality life.
         If you deduct money from the amount she should receive you will seriously
        infringe on her quality of life.   Don't you think our family has suffered
        enough already?   Please take this into consideration and change the plans
        that are in existence.
                              Sincerely,
