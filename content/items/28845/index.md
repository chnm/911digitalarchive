        Monday, December 03, 2001 2:39 PM

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Mr. Zwick

        This is regarding the  development of the rules that will govern the
        administration of  the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund"
        established by Congress in the Air Transportation Safety and System
        Stabilization Act.  I urge you to adopt rules that are inclusive of
        gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgendered people and their families
        who suffered from the attacks.

        Let us overcome our national prejudices during this time and give aid
        to ALL people whose spouses, parents, or children were lost or
        injured in the attacks,  including life partners and de facto parents
        or children.  These people and families are suffering regardless of
        sexual orientation or marital status, and your fund should benefit
        them regardless.

        I support the letter sent to your office by Lambda Legal Defense and
        Education Fund, National Gay and Lesbian Task Force, Human Rights
        Campaign, Empire State Agenda, National Center for Lesbian Rights,
        and Gay and Lesbian Advocates and Defenders.

        Thank you for your time and attention.

        Sincerely

        Individual Comment
        Tucson, AZ
