        Saturday, March 23, 2002 12:55 PM
        September 11 Victim's Compensation

        Sirs:
        It has come to my attention that the September 11 Victim's Compensation Fund
        will not be paying any compensation to life-partners of any of the gay and
        lesbian victims of the attacks on September 11, but there will be
        compensation for the families of illegal aliens and foreigners who were
        victims.

        Had I known that this was to be the case, I would never have made any
        contribution to the fund.  Those victims who were lesbian and gay and who
        have life partners are being discriminated against outright, and as the body
        who enforces justice in this country, the Department of Justice is committing
        a grave error.  This discrimination has completely destroyed any feeling of
        patriotism that the attacks of September 11 instilled in me, an American gay
        male who votes.

        I will find out who is responsible for including such language in the
        compensation fund's charges, and I will do everything in my power to see that
        they are not reelected, if indeed they have been elected.

        I also want to know why, as the justice enforcement arm of the government,
        your department does not seek to end discrimination in this country which
        denies basic rights supposedly enjoyed by all Americans, but which
        short-sighted and narrow-minded politicans continue to curtail.

        I am completely diillusioned and disgusted by our government.

        Individual Comment
        Pawleys Island, SC
