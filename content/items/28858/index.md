        Monday, March 18, 2002 6:30 PM
        Post 9/11 Patriotism

        To Whom It May Concern:

        As an American citizen, it comforts me to think that our congress has set
        monies aside specifically to care for the families of 9-11 victims.  As a
        queer woman, it angers me to think that had I died my loved ones would not
        be compensated for my death.

        Honestly, all of the post-9-11 patriotism has stirred my faith in "American
        Values" in democracy and freedom and liberty.  Words that for so long seemed
        empty have regained meaning for me.

        To find that the Victim Compensation Fund is discriminating against the
        life-partners of queer victims... breaks down the wall of hope that I have
        been building within myself.  Only by using the democratic system, by making
        my voice heard can I keep pessimism and cynicism from flooding back in.

        Please, show our nation and the world what American Values are.  Please show
        that Americans are Americans without qualification.  Please treat the loved
        ones of all Americans fairly.



        Individual Comment
