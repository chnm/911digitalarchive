        My fellow Americans:

        If the news item, as published in About.com's 3/11/02 edition, by           , is true (and I have seen no evidence to the contrary), then the Department of Justice is about to embark on a tragic miscariage of justice in forging policy and a response to Gay Americans (and perhaps non-American, illegal aliens) that survived or were victims of the terrible disaster of 9/11.
