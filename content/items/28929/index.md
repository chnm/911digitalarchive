        Friday, November 16, 2001 1:02 PM
        Comments on Sept 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001

        My comments on the processes and regulations for implementing the Victim
        Compensation Fund are:

        1.  Collateral sources which are deducted from the Fund compensation should
        only be life insurance, pension funds, death benefit payments and payments
        by Federal, State or local governments, as stated in the law.  Funds
        received from charitable organizations should not be deducted.

        2.  A claimant should be required to waive their right to file a civil
        action only after a determination is made as to the amount of compensation
        that they will receive from the Fund, and the claimant accepts this
        determination.  If the claimant is dissatisfied with the amount they would
        receive from the Fund, they should be able to waive their right to receive
        this amount and be allowed to file a civil action.

        3.  The claimant should be able to file an appeal of the amount initially
        determined to paid to them from the fund.  If they are dissatisfied with
        the results of the appeal, they should be able to waive their right to
        receive this amount and be allowed to file a civil action.

        Thank you for your consideration of my comments.

        Individual Comment
        Newport, RI
