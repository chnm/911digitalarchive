        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        Civil Division
        U.S. Dept. of Justice
        Main Building, Room 3140
        950 Pennsylvania Avenue

        Re: VICTIM COMPENSATION FUND


        Dear Mr. Zwick:



        We lost our only son in the World Trade Center attack on September 11, 2001. He was
        38 years old.

        While we truly believe that most of the compensation should go to his wife and children,
        however, as the law stands now, we have no right as parents to receive
        compensation for our horrific pain and suffering. Our pain is devastating and will
        last a lifetime. Our son can never be replaced.


        A parents' Pain and Suffering should be part of the non-economic fund's regulation.
        Our Pain & Suffering should be separated and apart from the compensation that the wife
        and children are going to receive.

        Thanking you in advance, we remain


                                      Sincerely yours,

                                      Individual Comment
                 Monroe Twp., NJ
