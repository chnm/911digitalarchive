        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 9:43 AM
        Sept. 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Kenneth Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U. S. Department of Justice

        Dear Sir,

        As I understand, the Justice Deparment will soon be
        setting guidelines for deciding who is eligible for
        compensation under the "September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund." I would ask that those guidelines
        include the life partners of any victims, as well as
        children they may have shared with their life
        partners, regardless of marital status or sexual
        orientation.

        As you no doubt know, New York Governor Pataki granted
        benefits through the state of New York "on a showing
        of mutual interdependence with the victim, in
        recognition that anyone who shared with the victim
        living expenses, day-to-day activities and the
        emotional bonds of family deserves help in this time
        of need."  I ask that a similar standard be used by
        the Justice Department.

        Sincerely

        Individual Comment
        Winston-Salem, NC
