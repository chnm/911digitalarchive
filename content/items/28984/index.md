        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 2:48 PM
        Victims Fund Comments

        I recently read the article by the AP inviting
        comments on the victims compensation fund. Here are my
        comments

        1)  Victims should not be compensated for lawyer's
        fees. This will create a situation for profiteering by
        lawyers from this event and future events. Victims
        should seek compensation on their own or have others
        help, but not paid through the fund.

        2)  The program should set up local field offices at
        or near the site. In addition, they should be allowed
        to apply for compensation at local Welfare or other
        type offices.

        3)  Victims should have the right to appeal if they
        are not satisfied, but not through the courts. An
        independent mediator should be used.

        4)  Compensations amounts should not be effected by
        insurance payments, pensions or gifts from charities.
        The victims have suffered enough and don't need the
        strain or complexity.

        =====
        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        San Diego, California
