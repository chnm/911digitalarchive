        Thursday, January 24, 2002 4:36 PM
        Questions regarding september 11 fund

        Dear Sir/Madam:

        I have a few questions regarding the calculations and distributions from the
        September 11 fund.  They are:

        1)  Will housing allowances, and other cash benefits received by military
        personnel killed in the Pentagon attack be included in the annual salary used
        to determine the range listed in the compensation chart?  This was considered
        by individuals as a very important part of the individuals compensation plan.


        2)  If an expecting mother delivered a child 1 1/2 months after the attack,
        would this child be considered a dependent and accounted for on the
        compensation chart?  The surviving spouse will claim this child as  dependent
        on their 2001 tax return.

        3)  Will survivor benefits (veterans administration benefits and social
        security) be considered collateral offsets?  This is not a pension as the
        victim did not retire from the military.

        4)  For a victim that was a Military Officer, retiring in less than 4 years,
        expecting to go into private industry in a professional/executive position
        earning an average of 50%-100% more annually would you consider this private
        industry salary when calculating his future earnings.  If so how do we
        calculate his compansation range on the chart? If not, why not?

        Thank you for your response.

        Individual Comment
