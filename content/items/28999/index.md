        victim compensation

        I am in favor of restricting the monies given to
        victims of the September 11th tragedy based on funds
        they receive from elsewhere.  In a city like New York,
        how can we justify over compensating peoples while at
        the expense of victims of other "daily" tragedies...
        hunger, homelessness, disease, etc.? Our city needs
        resources to rebuild, jobs to be created and funds
        dispersed throughout the needy population.  To pay out
        excessive funds to the victims of 9/11 would not be
        equitable to all the people of New York who need assistance.

        Individual Comment
