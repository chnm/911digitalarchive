        Wednesday, November 28, 2001 6:17 PM
        Support Victim Compensation for Lesbians and Gays

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing to express my support for inclusive victim compensation for
        partners and families of  Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual and Transgendered people
        injured or killed in the devastation of 911.  These individuals and families
        have suffered the same physical, emotional, psychological and material losses
        as their heterosexual counterparts.

        If we as a nation choose to compensate people who have suffered in this
        horrid situation, then all partners and families deserve to be compensated in
        an equitable manner regardless of marital status or sexual orientation.

        I hope you will see that justice is done in compensating victims of 911.

        Individual Comment
        Grand Rapids, MI
