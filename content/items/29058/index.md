        Sunday, March 17, 2002 4:35 PM
        9/11 Fund Exclusion of Gays

        To Whom It May Concern,

        I am outraged that families of gay and lesbian victims of the 9/11 tragedy
        will not be automatically compensated in the same way as families of
        heterosexual victims.  Americans have pulled together as never before as a
        result of this tragedy and denying equal compensation to families of gays
        and lesbians is to alienate queer and queer friendly Americans (and there
        are quite a few of us).  Discriminatory policies are not new to gays and
        lesbians and their families, however this policy is particularly
        offensive.  The tragedy of that day was felt just as much by families of
        gays and lesbians as it was by families of heterosexuals.  The entire
        country felt an extreme sense of loss on that day, one which will last for
        many years to come.  I find it disturbing that there are people who feel it
        is not appropriate to compensate ALL families who lost loved ones on that
        day.  I think it is tragic that people with such sentiments are in charge
        of the 9/11 fund.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
