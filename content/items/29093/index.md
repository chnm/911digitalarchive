        Thursday, December 13, 2001 2:16 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund


        December 13, 2001

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice
        e-mail: victimcomp.comments@usdoj.gov

        In late September, President Bush and Congress did a wonderful thing and
        authorized the creation of the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund" to
        provide assistance to the surviving victims of the attacks and the
        "relatives" of those who died.

        I was recently made aware that the Department of Justice is in the process
        of writing the regulations that will govern implementation of the Fund and
        has invited public comments to guide this process.

        Unfortunately, it seems, there are factions who are mounting a campaign to
        request that the surviving partners and children of gay and lesbian victims
        be excluded from the benefits of the Fund.  I find these views to be
        unpatriotic, cruel and reprehensible.  How could anyone believe that the
        emotional, financial and psychological pain of the September 11th attacks
        and the loss of loved ones should be addressed based only on life style
        choices.  Clearly, such a determination would have to be considered a
        blatant exercise in discrimination.

        As The Justice Department considers the regulations governing the "September
        11 Victim Compensation Fund" I urge you to ensure that compensation is
        available to all surviving families of those killed, including the committed
        partners and non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        Thank you for your consideration.

        Individual Comment
        Fort Worth, Texas
