        Saturday, March 23, 2002 9:51 AM
        Compensate equally

        Mr Zwick:
        I have recently learned that you plan to descriminate against gay and
        lesbian victims of the 9/11 attacks. You state that you would not
        compensate the surviving partners of gay victims if the state in which they
        lived at the time of their death currently practices discrimination against
        them by not recognising their partnership. Does that mean that if a victim
        lived in NJ where descrimination is not legal their surviving partner would
        be compensated but if they lived across the river in Yonkers NY where
        descrimination is legal they would not be compensated? What if the victim
        lived a few miles south in NYC where descrimination is illegal under city
        law but legal under state law? You will have created a monstrous and
        fundamentally unfair system that will be mired in controversy and court
        challenges. I urge you to compensate the families of all victims fairly and
        equally. There is no other just and moral solution.
        Individual Comment
