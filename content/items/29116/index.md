        Monday, January 21, 2002 11:52 PM
        Comments Federal Victims Compensation Program



        I attended the rally on 1/18 at the Park Avenue Armory in NYC. Below are my
        comments regarding the aforementioned subject:

        As the spouse of FDNY/Paramedic                   , who gave his life to save
        others on September 11, I am disappointed on the ruling of the Federal
        Victims Compensation Program, specifically on how the amount was determined
        for each family affected by this tragedy, especially the Uniform personnel.
        Why should Uniform personnel be penalized for having a good pension and/or
        life insurance when their type of job puts them at risk every day?  In order
        for them to protect and make sure their families are taken care of, they
        would need to have this pension and/or life insurance.  Why then should
        pension/life insurance or any government funds be deducted from the amount
        being compensated to prevent the families from sueing the airlines. With
        this ruling, many families are not being given a choice. It gears families
        who are being penalized to go towards a lawsuit, but at the same time they
        don't want to go thru the emotional anguish of a lawsuit. Eventhough, money
        would not replace the 3,000 lives lost on 9/11, reality is that all the
        families need to be compensated fairly and equally.
        Personally, I have had a great loss and I would never be able to fulfill all
        my dreams with my love one the way we had planned but knowing that my
        husband die as a hero doing the job that he loved for 17 years and the
        country that he love; I feel that the right decision will be made to justify
        for the country's great loss!



        Individual Comment
        N. Babylon, NY
