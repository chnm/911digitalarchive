        Equality Now!


        December  18, 2001

        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        On behalf of those who died on September 11, I urge you to legislation that
        does not discrimate against their surviver.  As you consider rules for
        administering the September 11 Victims relief fund I beg that you include
        among relatives eligible for compensation those who lost their life
        partners, as well as de facto parents or children, without regard to sexual
        orientation or marital status. Many lesbian and gay people were killed in
        the September 11th attacks. Since then, lesbian and gay surviving partners
        of those killed have had mixed results in obtaining equal treatment at the
        local level in the distribution of relief funds by private and state
        agencies.  An inclusive policy on the part of the federal government would
        help establish an important principle for other such funds.  In
        compensating victims, real justice will be served only when all families -
        and all types of families - impacted by the human rights tragedy of
        September 11 are treated with dignity and equality. Don't abandon a bunch
        of orphans just because their the person who had supported them was gay or
        lesbian.  Think of them, if no one else

        Thank you.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Washington, DC
