        Dear Attorney General Ashcroft,

        The deaths on September 11th were the result of an attack on America.
        Please do not discriminate against the unmarried partners of September 11th
        victims.  All unmarried partners, gay or straight, young and old and their
        dependents should be eligible for compensation based on a set of minimum
        guidelines.

        These guidelines should be based on the length of relationship, the fact
        that the couple was living together at the time of an individual partner's
        death, established joint finances, existing dependents such as a
        child/children, etc.  If one or more of these factors--and others along the
        same lines--were in place, then unmarried partners should have the same
        compensation rights as married partners.

        Individual Comment
