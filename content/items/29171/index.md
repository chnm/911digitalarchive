        Monday, January 14, 2002 5:55 PM
        Fiancee's concern

        I lost my fiance in Tower 1 of the WTC on September 11th.  We were in a
        committed relationship for almost 6 years and shared an apartment in NYC for
        2 1/2 years, along with our dog.  Our wedding date was set for October 19th,
        2001.

        There are many other fiance/es left suddenly left behind from this tragedy.
        We all seem to be feeling less significant than we truly were in the
        victim's life.  Why is the intent and plan to marry seemingly viewed as the
        same as being 'single'?  It is not, and being left out has added to the hurt
        and loss.  We should not be penalized by employers, insurance companies, or
        other compensation sources for not yet being legally married.  It is a form
        of discrimination.

        I would like to see the circumstances of fiance/es, whether they were living
        together yet or not, more fairly recognized in the compensation fund and
        general laws.

        Thank you for reviewing and considering my comment.

        Individual Comment
        NYC
