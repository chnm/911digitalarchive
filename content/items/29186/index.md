        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 10:33 AM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice


        Dear Mr. Zwick:
        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering the
        "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."   As the majority of Americans, if
        not all would support, the Department needs to draft regulations that ensure
        compensation is available to all victims of the attack.  However, in the
        word ?ALL? victims, I strongly urge this compensation include the committed
        partners and the non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic losses suffered
        by the families of gay and lesbian victims are no less than that of other
        families. Just as the terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims,
        our country must draw no lines in compensating those who suffered the most
        personal of losses from these horrific attacks.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind
        loved ones. I urge and ask that the Department to honor these bonds by
        treating the families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any other.
        After all, isn't that what the United States of America is currently
        fighting for...to protect our freedom and those who live under it.


        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
