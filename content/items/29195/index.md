        Friday, January 18, 2002 11:21 AM
        WTC Compensation



             Please do not further victimize the families of those who perished
             on 09-11-01.  To withhold federal money because a family received
             other compensation such as insurance is wrong. Most people buy
             insurance with their own hard earned money. It is not right that
             the survivors be penalized by a victim's untimely death simply
             because that victim tried to do the right thing for his family by
             buying insurance while living.

             Additionally, why should the families of the victims give up their
             constitutional right to sue private entities because they received
             federal compensation funds? Benevolence on the part of the government
             should not be administered on the condition that grieving families
             relinquish a fundamental legal right.

             Thank you for this opportunity to comment. Respectively submitted
             in loving memory of &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp.
