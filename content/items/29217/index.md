        I am the mother of a victim &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp from the World Trade Center.
        Here are a few comments.
        I believe all vicitms from Sept 11 should be treated equally by the govt.    Rumor has it (although perhaps incorrectly) that the airlines victims are receiving $2million for their pain and suffering, whereas the WTC victims would be receivng $250m

        I do not think the families of victims who were financially responsible should receive less from the govt.  Specifically, life insurance proceeds , for which victims had to pay premiums, should not be deducted.   In some cases, the life insurance deduction would result in virtually a $0 payment.
         Likewise, 401k funds should not be deducted.  This was the victims own money!

        I have heard Mr. Feinberg speak that he cannot play Solomon.   So I believe if you take a victims earnings potential and proceed on an actuarial basis,  and account for dependents. it will be fair.  While &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp did not have dependents, I believe that her beneficiaries should be compensated for the loss....in this case of their only child.
