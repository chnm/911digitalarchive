        Unacceptable discrimination

        It is unconscionable that the September 11 Victims Compensation Fund has
        adopted regulations that discriminate against the life partners of gay and
        lesbian victims of the attack.   Many gay men and women lost their lives and
        lost loved ones.  Their lives were disrupted as horrifically as those of
        every other victim.  They paid the same price  as every other victim.  How
        can you possibly see fit to denigrate them now in this sordid manner.
        Illegal aliens, fetuses, common law heterosexual unions -- all are
        recognized, and rightly so.   But where is the justice for our gay brothers
        and sisters, sons and daughters?   This is a shameful step that must be
        reconsidered and altered.

        Individual Comment
