        Dear Victim Compensation Fund for 9/11,

        I am writing to you today to ask you to compensate all partners and families

        -- without concern for race,  sexual orientation, religion or any other
        variable in which people tend to be divided.

        This is not a time to discriminate -- it is a time to show compassion for all
        Americans, and a time to act from the heart for human rights, rather than
        from  some other political, religious or discriminatory reason.

        The terrorists acted from religious, political and discriminatory reasons --

        I urge you to provide automatically all partners (e.g., spouses/domestic
        partners/gay and lesbian partners, people of color, etc...)the same benefits
        as caucasian, heterosexual, spouses.

        Thank you for your time and consideration.



        Individual Comment
        San Diego, California
