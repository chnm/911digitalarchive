        To Whom it May Concern:

        As a mother of four small children who lost their father on September 11, I
        find it offensive that the fund deducts collateral sources of income from
        insurance etc..  What type of message are you sending to the public when you
        penalize those who may have done the prudent thing and actually gone out and
        paid for some level of life insurance.  No amount of life insurance is going
        to compensate for my husband's earning potential over his lifetime.
        Furthermore, to cap the salary level at such a low level also does our family
        a diservice.  Finally, the fact that the fund only allows $250,000 for pain
        and suffering knowing full well that a jury would award a much hicher level
        is insulting.

        I am not trying to win lotto here.  I am just trying to ensure a lifestyle
        for my children that we worked all of these years to attain.

        Sincerely,



        If nothing else, you should pay the families that had life insurance the
        present value of the cash stream which supported that policy over the life
        that they paid for it.

        Individual Comment
