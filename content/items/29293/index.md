        Dear Mr. Zwick,
        I am writing to the Department's request for public comments regarding the
        forthcoming reulations for implmenting and administering the "September 11
        Victim Compensation Fund." I strongly urge the Department to draft
        regulations that ensure compensation is availabe to ALL victims of the
        attach, including the committed partners and the non-biological children of
        gay and lesbian victims.

        It is incomprehensible to me that the Department would consider otherwise.
        The devasting attack was on ALL U.S. citizens and those suffering the worst
        of these consquences should receive aid -- regardless of race, religion or
        lifestyle preference.

        I am not gay, but I believe in our constitution and equal rights for
        EVERYONE. There are many outstanding gay and lesbian citizens employed by our
        Government throughout this great nation and  many, many more making great
        contributions of all natures to ALL people throughout the world. It is almost
        2002.  How could there even be a question about any of this?

        Thank you for your time and consideration.

        Individual Comment
        Townsend, TN
