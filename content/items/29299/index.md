        To Whom it May Concern:

        Please be inclusive of gays, lesbians, bi-sexuals and transgendered
        persons when deciding who will benefit from the Compensation Fund for
        victions of September 11, 2001.

        Many Americans have lost their loved ones, life partners, sons,
        daughters, children, etc.  Please remember to include members of the
        GLBT community when deciding benefits.  We have all suffered as a
        result of this terrible tragedy, and this will help to ameliorate in a
        small way the terrible scars left from this day.  Please draft the
        legislation to be inclusive of all partners still surviving.

        Thank you for your time.


        Individual Comment
