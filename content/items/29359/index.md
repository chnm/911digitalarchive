        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 7:23 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        This letter is in response to the request for public comments regarding the
        regulations for implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund." I strongly urge the Department to draft regulations that
        ensure compensation is available to all victims of the attack, including the
        committed partners and the non-biological children of gay and lesbian
        victims. This should be a time of coming together in this country and
        partisan politics should not play a role in deciding who deserves to get
        compensated and who doesn't. Each person lost or injured and their families
        were effected in the same way.

        The families of gay and lesbian victims' grief and economic losses are no
        less important or worthy than any other person's family. To deny the right
        of compensation to these families would be inhumane. To afford rights to
        some victims and not others would be going against the fundamental rights we
        espouse to other countries about freedom for all people.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind
        loved ones. I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the
        families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any other.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Coppell, TX
