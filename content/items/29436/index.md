        Sept. 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        US Dept. of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

         I am writing regarding the forthcoming regulations for administering the
        compensation fund for Sept. 11 victims.  I strongly urge the Department to
        draft regulations that make compensation to all victims of the attack,
        including family of gay and lesbian victims.

        There can be no doubt that the loved ones  of ALL victims of these attacks
        suffered equally both emotional and economic losses.  It is the duty of our
        government to honor these people equally for their sacrifice on our behalf.
        Thank you very much for considering all of these people who have lost much.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Sarasota, FL
