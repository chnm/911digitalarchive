        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 1:24 PM
        Sept 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am a former US DOJ attorney.  I recently heard that there is a proposed
        disparity in how the Victim Compensation Fund is to treat the loved ones of
        gay and lesbian victims as opposed to other victims' loved ones.
        Specifically, I was informed that the fund may not include the committed
        partners and non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.  With our
        national message of unity and compassion being the banner behind which we
        fight the destructive and evil acts of international terror, I can't believe
        that such a polarizing and unfeeling possibility exists.  We must stand
        together to relieve the pain of those who have suffered direct loss as a
        result of the terror attacks of Sept 11, regardless of the sexual
        orientation of the victims or their loved ones.  Please be sure to extend
        the compensation fund's coverage to include the committed partners and
        non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.  Thank you.

        Yours sincerely,
        Individual Comment
