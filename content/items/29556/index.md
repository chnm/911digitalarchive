        9/11 Fund - Don't Discriminate!


        On NBC's "Meet the Press" this past Sunday, Kenneth Feinberg indicated that
        gay and lesbian partners of 9/11 victims and heroes will not necessarily be
        eligible for the same compensation as heterosexual family members who lost
        their loved ones. Feinberg's statements were a total cop out and a
        horrendous disappointment.

        Federal agencies should provide leadership and set positive examples--and
        not kowtow to individual states' decisions to discriminate against gays and
        lesbians--by including gays and lesbians in 9/11 compensation programs. The
        events of 9/11 were an equal-opportunity tragedy, and we have ALL suffered.
        I hope your organization will reconsider its decision to discriminate
        against gay and lesbian Americans.


        Individual Comment
