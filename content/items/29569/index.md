        Saturday, March 16, 2002 2:23 PM
        Please DO NOT Discriminate

        It has come to my attention that the 9/11 victim's fund is not
        automatically allowing the same benefits to the survivors of same-sex
        relationships as ALL survivors of heterosexual relationships will
        receive.  I do not understand why this should be the case.



                    This is even more disturbing when it is announced that
        illegal aliens will receive the benefits and that companies who have
        hired illegal workers will be overlooked so their employee's survivors
        can receive these benefits.


                    I am NOT against allowing "illegals" to receive these
        benefits, but I am definitely outraged by the fact that you consider
        Gays as a totally separate group that is NOT necessarily worthy of these
        well-deserved benefits.



                    Why are illegal aliens more worthy than survivors of a safe
        sex relationship?  Why can the law be overlooked for "illegals" at the
        same time that you insist the fund operate to the "letter of the law"
        when it comes to gay spouses?



                    A hero is a hero is a hero; Gay, straight, legal, illegal,
        Black, White, Christian, Jew etc.  All the heroes that died on 9/11 are
        the same.  PLEASE, treat them equally.



                    Do not denigrate the names and heroic acts of these brave
        men and women based solely on their sexual orientation.  Doing so is
        blatantly hateful and unfair and not worthy of a country as great as the
        United States of America.


                    Thank you for your consideration of my concerns,



        Individual Comment
