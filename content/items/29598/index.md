        September 11

        My brother was killed on September 11th.  He was one of the unlucky employees
        of Cantor.

        I am writing because I'm horrified by the was Congress has handled this
        situation so far.  I can only image if a member of Congress lost a family
        member how different the outcome would be.  You have no idea the pain and
        suffering that is torturing my family. How can the government say that there
        is one person who represents the family?  My parent lost their only son, and
        my sisters and I have lost our only brother.  My sister-in-law is left to
        raise her three children by herself.  9, 7 and 3 year old.

        What is this about "double dipping?"  The government is afraid families are
        going to get "rich" from this tragic event.  I invite anyone in congress to
        go live with my sister-in-law and her three children for one week, and live
        with their heart ache, live with their despair.  There is not enough money in
        the world to help heal my family.  But, since there is no other way the
        families can seek justice, except by filing lawsuits, that is what we, as
        Americans, should have the right to do.

        I thought the government was here to protect us.  Instead you hire airport
        employees who have criminal records to "protect us."  Where was the Homeland
        Security during all of this?

        On top of all of our pain in suffering, this should be the last thing my
        family along with (5000 other families) should have to dealing with.  I hope
        in the end the government does what is right.

        Thank you,

        Individual Comment
