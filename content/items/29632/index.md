        Wednesday, December 12, 2001 4:41 PM
        Sept 11 Victims

        Dear Mr. Zwick

        I am writing to let you know that I am completely disgusted with the
        notion that anyone would want to limit relief efforts to anyone.  As I
        understand it at the moment, there are those who would dis-allow relief
        compensation to families, partners and children of gays and lesbian
        victims.  This is simply un-acceptable.

        The victims of the horrible attacks on our country were from every walk
        of life and left behind loved ones that are hurting and in need of some
        security and compassion.  By excluding one group of these victims is to
        add insult to injury.  These people have had their loved ones ripped
        from their lives and now there are those that would have these victims
        shunned!  Please do not let some small minded individuals minimize our
        country's response to this tragedy.

        All of the victims of this horrible attack should be eligable for
        relief and compensation, with-out regard to race, creed, marital
        status, sexual orientation etc.  Please do what you can to protect
        these victims families.  Compensate them all!

        Thanks you for your time
        Individual Comment
        Anchorage, AK
