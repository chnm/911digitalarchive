        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 10:27 AM
        Victim Compensation Fund

        I want to express my outrage, my hurt at the anti-gay bias in the
        distribution of September 11th Victim Compensation Funds. I just want to
        remind those involved that someone they know and care about is gay. You may
        not know who, may be surprised at who they are, but you know and interact
        with them, possibly every day.

        Would you treat your son, daughter, parent, cousin, niece, nephew, aunt or
        uncle this way? Think about your circle of family and friends and consider
        the stab in their hearts as you take this action.

        Brave people died in this terrible tragedy. Gay people, out and closeted,
        died; gay people who were parents, children, spouses, partners, brothers and
        sisters; gay people who were loved.

        To support bias, bigotry and hatred raising the banner of religion as our
        foundation, rally cry or excuse, flies in the face of the freedoms our
        forefathers worked so hard to create including the right to be free of a
        government whose laws were based solely on religious grounds, and the right
        to pursue happiness.

        Or to throw up your hands in powerlessness to debatable laws and use them as
        crutches that support bias and bigotry and discrimination of any sort - that
        is not what our country is made of.

        Worse still, it smacks of the same intolerance that breeds monsters like
        those that perpetrated this horrible event.

        On September 11th, our beliefs were attacked, our belief that all humans are
        equal and our moral obligation to stand up for the underdog. Do not let them
        destroy our values. Do not subscribe to their discriminatory fervor to
        foster a spark of hatred for any group of people.

        So I encourage those who can make this decision to stand up for all of the
        victims and those that relied on them and loved them. Stand up for what they
        know in their hearts is the right thing to do.

        Show the world that in the United States there are no second-class citizens.



        Individual Comment
