        To Whom it May Concern:

        I am very much AGAINST the victim's compensation program that was voted on by our legislature.  Please stop this program from being enforced!  Please consider:

        1) Never before in the history of this country, have victims' families been compensated in this way!  (Pearl Harbour. World Wars, terrorist attacks, etc.)

        2) None of us would be able (as this compensation program attempts to enable victims' families) to maintain our current standard of living, after the death of a wage earner.  Why should these people be allowed this?   It's ridiculous!

        Please vote a big "NO" in your public comment survey, on my behalf!

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
