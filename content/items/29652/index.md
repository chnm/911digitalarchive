        sept. 11 injustice

        We cannot believe the terrible injustice that is happening to our fellow
        Americans in the gay and lesbian community.  They are as much American as
        anyone else in this nation and we need to make sure they are treated as such.
         How can we treat people who are not American citizens here illegally better
        than we treat our own fellow Americans? Every one who died on Sept. 11 is a
        hero and we need to treat each and every one of them as such.  How dare you
        even think about separating the heroes based on their sexual orientation.
        The gay and lesbian people who died on that tragic day served their country
        just the same as any heterosexual did despite all the ridicule and harassment
        they have endured their whole life from other American citizens.  Everyone
        says that this tragedy brought the country together as one, but obviously it
        has done nothing but tear it apart more that it already is. How dare you
        treat the heroes of 9-11 with such disrespect.

        Individual Comment
