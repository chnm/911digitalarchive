        I can't imagine that in a "time of unity" that there would be such blatant discrimination. It only serves to further embarrass me to be an American Citizen. God forbid thanks should be given to Gay Americans for saving thousands of lives. So being gay is akin to being invisible?
        I look forward to your immediate response in this matter.

        An Open-Minded American Citizen
