        Thursday, December 06, 2001 8:00 PM
        Victim Compensation Fund

        On September 11, thousands of people lost their lives in the most tragic of
        events my generation has known.  Countless people have been affected and
        left to wonder what they will do now.
        I am writing to express my support of including domestic partners and
        children of gay/lesbian partnerships in the plan for distributing the fund.
         Gay/lesbian relationships are no less loving, no less supporting, and no
        less valid than any other relationship.  Those gay/lesbian victims of
        September would want their families to be included in the Victim
        Compensation Fund no less than any other victims.  Although gays and
        lesbians do not have the same legal protections as other partnerships,
        their families should be treated no differently.  As one nation--as one
        human race--we all lost something on September 11.  Please do not exclude
        the families of those gay and lesbian victims.  They have suffered as much
        as anyone else.  The pain does not need to be worsened by discriminating
        against or excluding these families.

        Individual Comment
