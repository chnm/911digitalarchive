        Friday, December 14, 2001 1:50 AM
        Sept. 11 Compensation Fund

        Special Master Kenneth R. Feinberg
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

        I am writing to ask you to carefully consider the way in which you
        choose to administer the compensation to the families of all those who
        died on September 11.
             If you compensate based on lost wages, then the survivors of the
        bond traders will receive millions, while the survivors of the janitors
        will receive very little. But whose salary will be more sorely missed?
        The bond trader probably had both the funds and the forethought to
        insure him- or herself, while the janitor's family was probably living
        paycheck to paycheck. I think it would be more fair to at least set a
        minimum compensation level across the board, so that these glaring
        inequities don't happen.
             I also urge you to take into consideration that there will be
        nontraditional families without a marriage license who nonetheless
        deserve compensation for their loss. These include gay and lesbian
        families who don't have the option of marriage, plus those who chose not
        to formalize their relationships by legal means. The Red Cross and the
        State of New York have chosen not to discriminate against these
        families; I urge you at the Department of Justice to follow suit.
             Thanks for listening. I look forward to seeing your actions in
        handling this formidable responsibility.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Van Nuys, California
