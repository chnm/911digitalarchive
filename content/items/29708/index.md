        The September 11 Fund is Unfairly Discriminating

        Dear Mr. Feinberg,

        I am writing to express my disappointment and outrage in your exclusion of
        gays and lesbians from the September 11th Fund.

        It is disheartening to see that your fund, which includes all heterosexual
        partners and children of victims (including fetuses and illegal aliens), has
        decided to exclude same-sex partners on the basis of a flimsy excuse of not
        wanting to get sued for giving benefits to people in States where they
        aren't required.  It is obviously a transparent attempt to discriminate
        against gay men and lesbians, since illegal aliens, which are not entitled
        to financial benefits (and are in fact supposed to be deported) are being
        given full benefits and universal immunity if they come forward.  There is
        no justification for giving a blanket exemption in one case, and not the
        other.

        Not only are you sending a message saying that people in America who are
        present illegally have more rights than citizens, but you are effectively
        spitting on the graves of such heroes from Sept 11th as             .

        I implore you to reconsider this reprehensible, un-American decision.

        Respectfully,


        Individual Comment
