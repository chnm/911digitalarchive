        9/11 Fund to Discriminate Against Gays


        Reading the article "9/11 Fund to Discriminate Against Gays" by            at About.com,
        (http://uspolitics.about.com/library/weekly/aa031102a.htm), left me
        shocked, upset and angry that an opportunity to respond with a gesture
        of goodwill and charity to all the unfortunate victims of the 9/11
        tragedy and their survivors was consciously lost by the administrators
        of the 9/11 fund.

        By denying domestic partners of gay and lesbian victims of the 9/11
        tragedy an equivalent distribution of the charitable funds given for all
        victims, the September 11th Victims Compensation Fund and the Department
        of Justice sullies and denies their profoundly meaningful acts of
        courage, bravery and altruism.  I'll be certain that all my friends and
        acquaintances, regardless of sexual orientation, know about this most
        recent act of cowardice and do all that is possible to inform their
        Congressional representatives to bring what collective voting and
        funding power we have to bear upon changing this misguided and
        ultimately immoral policy decision.

        Individual Comment
        Oakland, CA
