        Dear Sir/Madam:

        Your discriminatory policy on compensation to the
        partners of gay men and lesbians killed in the attacks
        of September 11th was brought to my attention by a
        firefighter who risked his life at the WTC that day.

        He (and I) are outraged that you would duck your moral
        responsibility to compensate ALL the victims of that
        attack. How can you justify bending the law to take
        care of the families of illegal aliens--which is a
        noble gesture--and ignore those gay and lesbian
        Americans whose lives were devastated when their
        partners were killed that day?

        If the proposed policy sounds un-American to a
        firefighter who went rushing into the towers that day,
        then I have to agree.

        It's not so much about the money as it is about
        respect for your fellow Americans. I hope you will be
        able to look everyone in the eye when it comes to
        discriminating between those who you say are deserving
        and those who you say are not.

        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
