        You ask if awards from the victim's compensation fund should be reduced by
        the amount of charity a victim's family accepts? Absolutely not. Donations
        are just that. Gifts and only gifts given freely by those who chose to. These
        donations should have absolutely no bearing on the compensation a victim's
        family receives as a result of these horrific acts. As the wife of a victim,

        was incredibly difficult and painful. Please do not make obtaining victim's
        compensation for the loss my husband and my children's father even more
        painful by even considering the reduction of this compensation.
