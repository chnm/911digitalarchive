        Wednesday, November 28, 2001 2:22 PM
        September 11th Fund

        To: Department of Justice

        Re: Compensation to Same-Sex Partners and Family Members

        As the Department of Justice makes its decision about who is eligible
        for the September 11th Fund to receive compensation for the losses of
        relatives of those who died, and those who were injured, I urge you to
        include among relatives eligible for compensation, those who lost
        their life partners or de facto parents or children, regardless of
        sexual orientation or marital status. The pain felt by gay and lesbian
        families is no different than the pain experienced by every other family
        who had someone brutally taken away from them on that day.  It is my
        hope that you will do the morally right thing and grant funds to all
        families who lost life partners, parents, or children.

        Individual Comment
        Princeton, NJ
