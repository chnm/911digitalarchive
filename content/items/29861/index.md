        Monday, January 07, 2002 11:05 AM

        I can't believe what is going on with all the fuss about compensation.
        People die EVERY day and their families don't become instant millionaires at
        the expense of the American taxpayer! I have no sympathy for the             widows, with their $5000 a month mortgages, two BMWs in the garage, private
        school tuitions and stay at home moms... where is their life insurance? If
        the husband died in a car accident or even in his sleep - how were they
        planning on paying the bills then?

        Charity is great for those who need it, but it shouldn't be used to make
        families rich. Americans gave generously to charities expecting to help
        families with immediate expenses, not to make instant millionaires. Also,
        the government should not use taxpayer's money to fund the bank accounts of
        victim's families. If anything, pay for a few months expenses, and then
        expect the families do what they would do if their bread-winner died any
        other way, get on with it. Get a job and get on with life.

        Our country and all Americans have survived many, many needless deaths and
        tragedies without victim's families "winning the lottery". God help us all.


        Individual Comment
