        Discrimination is wrong

        hello,

        I am dismayed to hear that the families of lesbian or gay victims of the Sept
        11 calamity are going to be treated differently -- and in fact, as in
        ordinary day-to-day life, are going to be treated as unimportant and "less
        than".  An extraordinary amount of money was given by generous Americans, to
        be distributed to the families of victims. This is no time for
        government-sponsored homohobia to determine how much a grieving family member
        has to watch go to other "real" families.  I think this is disgraceful, and
        truthfully I don't know any of you can sleep at night, if you are part of
        this injustice.  It is time to redefine 'family', to include, not just
        fetuses and illegal aliens as well as conventionally married people, but
        people whose strong bonds of love and commitment DEFINED them as "real"
        families, regardless of the legalities.

        Individual Comment
        York, PA
