        I believe

        I believe that Congress wanted to help the families when they signed this
        Bill.

        I believe that in signing this Bill they want to protect the Airlines AND
        take care of the families.

        I believe that Me. Feinberg is trying to save the government money and that
        perhaps he thinks he is being fair.

        I believe that the current system penalizes those families that did the right
        thing and put money aside for insurance, retirement and other savings.

        I believe that the People of the United States want the families of the
        September 11th Tragedy to be supported financially.

        The way the system is currently set up the current salary of the family
        member who perished would not be factored into the calculation rather an
        average of the last 3 years. What about those who just received a huge
        promotion?

        I believe information to the public is inaccurate and therefore the public
        believes our government is financially supporting the families. THIS IS WHAT
        WE WANT!!!! Please do not penalize families that did the right thing. Please
        make sure every family is taken care of by this fund.

        Don't subtract non-economic loss. The pain and suffering these families have
        endured is real. They will live with this loss for the rest of their lives.
        Since it has been determined that families should be compensated for their
        loss then why turn around and DEDUCT it from their economic loss? Preserve
        this compensation and protect it.

        The amount of $50,000 per child for pain and suffering is way too low. These
        children have to live with the tragic loss of their parent for the rest of
        their lives. Increase this amount.

        Please recalculate your formula. Give to each family; don't subtract
        anything! Make sure each family will be taken care of financially. This is
        what we want, this is what we believe you are doing and this is what you
        SHOULD do.

        Please take care of the families!

        Individual Comment
        Stafford, VA
