        terrorist attach and 9/11 compensation

        Hello,
        I am an ordinary citizen from Canton, Ohio, in the center middle America.  I
        am outraged that even illegal aliens are being compensated for the September
        11 attacks, and our own citizens who have been in a committed relationship
        are not!
        Any relationship is worthy of compensation if illegal aliens can be
        compensated.  These people are not even citizens, and our own citizens are
        being ignored.
        The worst part is the justification used to make this decision.  Illegal
        aliens will not be prosecuted or sent back if they come forward.  This is
        obviously against the law, but to do that and then turn around use the law
        (does your state have benefits for committed couple relationships?  If not,
        then that victim does not qualify for benefits!)  That I feel is totally
        wrong!
        Furthermore, even if the victims had no spouse or children, certainly the
        relatives or friends of the victims who may be in their will, are deserving
        of the benefits allowed to the married couples.  Is one human life worth more
        than another?  I ask you where is the fairness in this?
        Please reconsider your decisions.




        Individual Comment
        Canton, OH
