        Sunday, March 31, 2002 2:09 PM
        VCF: Including Domestic Partner Benefits

        Dear Mr. Feinberg,

        I have just reviewed your Victim Compensation Fund plan and I am disgusted
        that you would exclude domestic partners to your compensation plan.
        Domestic partners have the same level of commitment to and love for their
        partners as heterosexual couples do and to deny them compensation for their
        painful loss is to deny this reality.  I appreciate that you are trying to
        follow state and federal guidelines regarding same-sex relationships,
        however these laws are antiquated and do not truthfully reflect the diverse
        families of Americans.  Please reconsider excluding domestic partnership
        funding for families that can prove their commitment to each other (ie:
        joint accounts, shared mortgage, auto loans, etc).  All American families
        have been hugely affected by the September 11th attacks - don't exclude some
        families because they don't look like straight middle/upper class, white
        families...  Show compassion for ALL American families.

        Thank you for your consideration.  Please contact me if you would like to
        discuss this further.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Denver CO
