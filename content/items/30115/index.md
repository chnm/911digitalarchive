        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 7:10 PM
        Sept.11th Fund Comment

        TO: Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        U.S. Dept. of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I heard that you are accepting comments about the use of the September 11
        Victim Compensation Fund. I would like to say that I believe your fund should
        follow guidelines similar to the ones established by Gov. Pataki of NY. The
        NY guidelines allow compensation to all those who were "sharing living
        expenses, day-to-day activities and the emotional bonds of family" regardless
        of marital status or sexual orientation.

        My home state of Virginia is refusing to allow any money to go to the gay and
        lesbian life partners of victims in the Pentagon crash, and I know that is
        causing great hardship (on top of horrible grief) to a number of g&l families
        left behind. One woman is losing her house because she can't make the
        payments. It's so unfair. People build lives together and do all they can to
        support each other, and then not only are their lives torn apart by
        terrorists but our own government refuses help. That's just one of the
        reasons I voted against the current state administration in the election four
        weeks ago.

        Please be fair. Recognize ALL the families, not just the ones
        and want to recognize, and take care of ALL the Americans
        bereaved and financially deprived by the attacks.

        Thanks.

        Individual Comment
        Richmond, VA
