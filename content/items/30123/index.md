        Friday, January 18, 2002 11:07 AM
        Comments of the interim rules

         The DOJ and the Special Master fashioned the yearly income to be the
        average salary of 1998,99,2000 this is
          contrary to the spirit of the law.

         This procedure is grossly unfair (and should be repealed) to young
        people who are at the early stages of their
          careers in business.

         For these young people, the salary can double from year to year as
        their labor skills and working knowledge are
          mastered.

         The fair and just process would be to calculate the economic loss on
        the victim's actual pre-tax earnings salary of
          Sept. 10,2001 INSTEAD OF the three-year period 1998-2000
        consideration.

         It would be a crime to maintain the three-year consideration,
        because you victimize once again those dead people
          who through education, extra work effort,. or both, strived to
        improve their economic remuneration.

        Individual Comment
        Staten Island
