        Monday, January 14, 2002 11:20 AM
        Victim Compensation

         1)  When Personal Representative files a claim they should be advised
        of the amount of the award that will be offered and then elect to accept
        this amount or reject it and proceed in other venues. Filing initial
        claim should not waive right to file future lawsuits. This should be
        waived only on acceptance of award.
        2)   Insurance policies as recieved by dependants and  taken out on
        private basis  (premiums fully paid by the deceased) should not be
        deducted from award as this penalizes those who had planned ahead.
        3) Charitable contributions were made to dependents to help pay for day
        to day expenses and should not be deducted from award. This was
        originally stated in the government Presumed Economic and Non Economic
        Loss Tables in paragraph II-C.

        Individual Comment
        Yonkers, NY
