        Another Complaint

        I to cannot believe the surviving family members of 9-11 on the television talking about filing bankruptcy, when I would suspect that most victims of the attacks carried a healthy life insurance policy.  I also do not approve of the proposed plan that the amount dispersed would be in proportion to the income, (position) of the deceased.  Is one life worth more than another?
        I have lost family members (brother at age 18 and father at age 42) unexpectedly, (not related to 9-11)  so I understand the grief and pain of the families, but I do not understand this feeling that they should all become millionaires because of this tragedy.  Maybe fighting over the money gives them a direction to point the anger and grief that they feel.
