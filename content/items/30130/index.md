        Final Rule re: Gays and Lesbians

        While I understand and respect the idea of honoring state statutes about
        domestic partner benefits in relation to 9-11 aid funds, I think the federal
        government can and should, in this case, take the high ground and see the
        big picture. There are many cases in which federal policies have been ahead
        of states, and while big government is not the goal, a just visionary
        government is. I do not believe you will be sued by states if you give aid
        to those who lost loved ones on 9-11. If on the off chance you are, the
        suits can have no merit simply because a one-time financial aid contribution
        is not in violation of anything. Thank you for reviewing this matter once
        again, with a wide open heart.

        Individual Comment
