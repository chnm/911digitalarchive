        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        As you develop the rules by which the funds provided in the Air Transportation
        Safety and System Stabilization Act, please make sure that funds are made

        children and/or parents of lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgendered and
        heterosexual people, who have suffered emotional and financial loss or hardship.
        Sexual orientation or marital status should not be used to exclude people who
        are in need.
