        Wednesday, January 02, 2002 10:32 PM
        comment about the unfairness of the fund

        Dear Special Master:
               I think the way this fund is going to be distributed is unfair.  I
        feel that the government should let the victims know the amount of the award
        before being made to sign anything.  If a person settles out of court they
        know the amount of the settlement before they have to sign anything.
               All people who have life insurance have to pay monthly premiums as did
        my husband.  Because they were cautious about their future and paid money out
        of their own pocket, not the governments, they are being penalized.  Also
        everyone who has a job on the books has to pay social security, it is not
        just given to them, so this should not be deducted either.  If the government
        fells that accepting workers compensation is "double dipping" I do not think
        the money already received should have to be given back.
               I also strongly believe that there should no be a cutoff age for a
        child when it comes to pain and suffering.  A thirty year old living on their
        own does not have less pain than a child under the age of eighteen, if
        anything they have more pain because they got to spend more time with the
        lost one.  There should not be a clause that only a dependent has pain and
        suffering.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Staten Island, NY
