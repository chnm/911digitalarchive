        Dear Sir:  Tragic  as  it  is,  I  do not feel that the  US  taxpayer is
        in  no way responsible  for  the  events  of  9 11. If  so, how and  in
        what manner?  I feel these awards  at our expense  are  nothing  more
        than a "bail out"  for  the insurance  companys.  The insurance
        companies are in the business of insuring so let  the taxpayers  stand
        aside and  let them do their job.  These  "awards" set a very bad
        president. We did not place any awards for the Oklahoma bomings,the USS
        Cole, or at any other event in history of which I am aware.  Where does
        it end? My son is a policeman. If he is killed by a terrorist , do my
        grandchildren receive  1.65  million dollars?  How many people have to
        be killed at one time for their relatives to receive awards?  Are
        one,two,two hundred lives any less precious  than any others?  I feel
        the victim's relatives should receive only what they would be entitled
        to if they were killed on the job  and not one cent more, and not one
        cent of taxpayer's money. Please don't do this to the taxpayer.
        Respectfully,
        Individual Comment
        Soddy Daisy, Tn.
