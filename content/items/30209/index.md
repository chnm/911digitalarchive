        9/11 Compensation


        I understand why the government giving compensation to the victims families of 9/11.
        However, it just doesn't seem right.

        Here we are, paying for high energy and healthcare costs.  Higher interest rates, as well as higher taxes for the lower, and middle income people.  Just what are *we* supposed to do?  Ever our groceries have gone up.

        I am a victim of nuclear fallout, since I grew up in St. George, Utah.  We have not been
        compensated.  Only those that can show they have certain cancers.  Anyone that can prove they lived there at the time of the above ground testing, should be compensated, without having to prove anything else, period.

        Perhaps you should take care of this country's blunders', before you pay for some other tragedy. Things would get better in this country if the government took care of it's own mistakes.

        Individual Comment
        Las Vegas, NV
