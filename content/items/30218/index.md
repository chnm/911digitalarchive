        Tuesday, March 12, 2002 8:26 PM
        Gay and lesbian exclusion in 911 Fund distribution


        I am appalled by the recent information I received regarding the distribution
        of 911 Funds.  Having contributed significantly myself through various
        sources, I now come to find out that resources that I helped make available
        will not be made available to the partners and families of gay and lesbian
        men and women victimized by the events of September 11th.

        It is equally unfathomable that the families of illegal aliens may receive
        benefits, but that this gay discrimination remains in place.  If the concern,
        as stated, really is going contrary to state laws that already discriminate,
        it seems odd that federal and state immigration laws could be ignored for the
        sake of the families of these victims.

        I am ashamed to think that my country would draw lines so contrary to the
        basic human kindness and fairness.  I am also angry that a fund that I
        supported is now restricting access of those same funds to families that have
        an equal right to share in the support offered by this country's citizen's.
        The states did not give this money and therefore it is inappropriate to
        follow state imposed restrictions and discrimination in its distribution.

        This policy must change.  Human decency dictates nothing less.
        I expect a response regarding this matter.

        Individual Comment
        St. Louis, MO
