      text: |-
        Wednesday, January 16, 2002 8:00 AM
        Victims Fund


        Monday, January 14, 2002

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        The purpose of this email is to provide you with my concerns relating to the
        September 11th Victims Compensation Fund. My  brother     was
        murdered in the September 11th WTC disaster.

        He was a very special person to our family in many ways.  The loss of my
        brother has deeply affected me, as well as my brother and sister. The loss to
        my mother is devastation.  This loss will be with us until the end of our
        days.

        I have reviewed the rules of the fund and have a number of issues with it.  I
        believe the rules fall far short of the clear intent of what Congress
        expressed.  I believe you merely accepted a statue that was handed down  in
        creating a process or a program that would be accepted by those touched by
        this terrible act.

        This fund was created for only one reason...to create a compensation program
        that would replace what otherwise would be overwhelming litigation against a
        variety of businesses, industries, etc.  The guidelines, as provided in the
        Victims Fund, in the "simplest terms" are unfair, unrealistic, flawed and are
        insulting.   The method that was applied to derive the base tables to compute
        the awards are not valid and do not reflect the actual costs and future
        earnings.

        How can YOU measure the worth of an individual life based upon his economic
        loss?  It is a very "slippery slope"" one travels down when determining which
        life counts more than another life.  I believe a base amount should be given
        to each family---that amount as indicated in the document submitted to you by
        the Family Committee---is $2,000,000.  Provide this amount across the board.
        Equally.
        The deduction of insurance and pensions should be removed from the award
        computation!!! When was the last time you heard of a jury making an award
        settlement subject to subtraction of insurance and pension???????

        The Victims Compensation fund was established for the correct reasons. Please
        return to the original purpose of creating this fund. Fairness and Equality
        for the Victims Families. Change the current guidelines NOW.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Scituate, Ma
