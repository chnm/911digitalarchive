        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

             Re:  September 11th Victim Compensation Fund Interim Rule
                  28 CFR Part 104

        The Victim Compensation Fund is designed to provide a fair and expeditious means of
        compensating families so they
        do not have to enter into protracted legal battles.

        Developing a fair system is a very daunting challenge, but I want to draw your attention to two
        areas that require
        your attention.

        Please review the compensation amounts to ensure they are reasonable.  I have been informed
        that you established
        payouts equivalent to those given to police officers and members of the military who are killed
        in the line of duty.
        Payments traditionally given to police officers and members of the military may not be suitable
        in this situation
        because the people who lost their lives on September 11th lived drastically different lifestyles
        with significantly
        lower risks.

        Although Public Law 107-42 requires you to deduct life insurance and other similar payments ,
        please modify the
        proposed rules to guarantee that every claimant will receive a fair payment.  I urge you to
        establish general
        guidelines explaining how you will use your discretionary power to make awards in
        extraordinary circumstances to
        help those who do not receive fair payments.

        The finalized regulations must make sure that the victims' families receive the full and fair
        compensation they were
        promised by Congress.

                                           Very truly yours,


                  COMMENT BY:
                                           Patsy T. Mink
                                           Member of Congress
                  Washington, DC
