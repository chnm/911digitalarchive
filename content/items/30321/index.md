        Monday, March 18, 2002 9:19 AM
        Compensation for 9-11 victims

        I am horrified, outraged, and ashamed to learn that the U.S. government is
        planning to withhold compensation for the domestic partners of homosexual
        victims of the 9-11 terrorist attacks.  Did the gay and lesbian victims of
        9-11 suffer any less than heterosexual victims in the final minutes of their
        lives?  Do their surviving partners experience any less grief, or economic
        loss, than the family members of heterosexual victims?  Is the U.S. Dept. of
        Justice so completely callow and so rigid in its heterosexist morality that
        it will refuse to treat all the victims of this tragedy equally?

        As an American citizen, and a person of conscience, I demand that the DOJ
        treat all surviving spouses and domestic partners equally in its
        compensation plan.  To do otherwise is to make a mockery of justice.

        Individual Comment
        Dallas TX
