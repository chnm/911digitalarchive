        Wednesday, February 06, 2002 8:15 PM
        Victim Compensation Fund

        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

        I listened to your discussion with a representative from one of the
        family rights groups on the Nightly Newshour this afternoon.  It is with
        much sadness that I have to say I disagree with you about whether the
        victims families are greedy.  I truly believe some of them are,
        particularly people such as the representative on today's discussion.

        We have never compensated victims of any other disaster in this manner
        and I am very concerned about the prescience this may create.  We did
        not have any special fund for the victims of the Oklahoma City bombing.
         We do not have special funds for the victims of natural disasters.

        While I realize that no amount of monetary compensation will replace a
        lost loved one, I do not believe that the families of these victims
        should be made wealthy at the taxpayers' expense.  In any other
        situation, persons receiving compensation for lost wages would have to
        pay income tax on the money received.  These persons will have to pay no
        income tax on any of  the funds.  I believe that the proposed
        settlements are fair and equitable.

        I want to compliment you on your handling of this difficult situation.
         I find your proposal to be be fair and equitable.

        Very truly yours,

        Individual Comment
        Berkeley, CA
