        September 11 Victim Compensation

        Mr. Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I strongly urge the Justice Department to adopt a policy of fairness and
        equality toward the bereaved survivors of September 11 victims.  New York's
        Governor Pataki has already recognized that families in today's America
        include many varieties of relationships.

        Given the intolerance of the monsters who brought this atrocity upon us, our
        own government should give a shining example of tolerance by recognizing that
        surviving same sex partners suffer just like everyone else and deserve the
        same assistance.  I cannot overemphasize how important the Department's
        decision will be.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
        Rancho Mirage, CA
