        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 2:05 PM
        Stop Benefit discrimination

        To:  Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
         Office of Management Programs
         Civil Division
         U.S. Department of Justice
         Main Building, Room 3140
         950 Pennsylvania Avenue
         Washington, DC 20530

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        Re:  Stop benefit discrimination from the September 11 Victim Compensation
        Fund

        I believe that discrimination is un-American.

        I find it unbelievable that the families and partners of some of the women
        and men who were held up as heroines and heros of the September 11 tragedy
        are being denied benefits from the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund, as
        those heroines and heroes have been discovered to have been lesbian or gay.

        These partners, or when there is no partner, the family, of these lesbian and
        gay heroines and heros should automatically receive the same beneifts as
        heterosexual spouses or family members.

        I ask that you use the power of your office to see that this un-American
        discrimination ceases immediately, and the partners and families of lesbians
        and gays who died September 11th are compensated.

        Thank you.

        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
        Monterey, CA
