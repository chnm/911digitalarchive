        Sept. 11 "compensation"

        To Kenneth L. Zwick, Director,
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division,
        U.S. Department of Justice

        In the aftermath of the horror of September 11, I urge you to disregard sexual orientation or marital status in decisions on eligibility for compensation -- as well as any such program can "compensate" -- for the loss of life partners or children.

        It shouldn't matter, but in case it does: I am a (voting, taxpaying, church-going) heterosexual mother and grandmother. But my sexual orientation and marital status don't preclude a sense of justice as regards my gay and lesbian peers.

        I hope that sense of justice is alive in the department of same.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Walnut Creek, CA
