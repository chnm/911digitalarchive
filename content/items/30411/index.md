        Saturday, March 16, 2002 3:05 PM

        Hello,

        I have heard that you will not be giving the same reparations to families
        and partners of gay and lesbian relationships. I think this is disgusting
        and people like you make me ashamed to be an American. It is not your right
        to judge people, but you were elected to treat them fairly, that is your
        job, that is what we pay you for. Even people who are not Americans, illegal
        aliens are going to be given more than these gay and lesbians...I am glad
        that we are taking care of people who are not American who suffered in this
        attack, but to omit or treat differently a group of people because you don't
        like who they sleep with is frankly unconstitutional. Many gay and lesbian
        lives were lost, many of whom fought bravely to save us from even more death
        and disaster. I would think that we as Americans should embrace our
        differences and have more compassion for our peers as we would the the
        people we love in our own lives. I will continue to protest this and I will
        make it known on my website and I will march on Washington if I have to. You
        are not God, you are the government, you are acting a lot like the people
        who caused this tragedy to begin with. The Taliban executed gay and
        lesbians, you are acting much like they did executing their inalienable
        rights, trying to make them invisible and unimportant, remember this...gays
        and lesbians are a part of your community whether you like it or not, they
        vote and they contribute significantly to our economy. And contrary to what
        you might think no one knows the word of God, no man has the right to judge
        another human being as God would, just who the hell do you think you are?
        This is the meat of what revolutions are made of.

        Individual Comment
