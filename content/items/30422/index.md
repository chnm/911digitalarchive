        Monday, March 11, 2002 9:01 PM
        Why is Mr. Feinberg being a bigot?


        I just heard that a very anti-American member of your staff, Kenneth
        Feinberg, is planning on limiting what kind of compensation goes to gay and
        lesbian victims of the September 11 terrorist attacks.  And the way the
        rules read, gays and lesbians will probably get nothing.

        I was sad to hear that you even had such an obviously unAmerican scumbag of
        this type on your staff.  To think that there are people who would deprive
        any victim of 9/11 assistance reminds me of how this country does not treat
        everyone equally.

        Please tell Mr. Feinberg that if indeed he plans on limiting what kind of
        compensation goes to gay and lesbian victims of 9/11 he must also limit
        compensation for all other Americans.  I have gay friends who are victims
        and to see that the very help they need will be deprived because of bigotry
        and hatred, especially in American where these victims were killed BECAUSE
        THEY WERE AMERICAN.  I guess Mr. Feinberg now has the power to decide who is
        "more" American than another.  It's amazing he ever got into the role he is
        in, was he hired by idiots?

        If he can't do that, tell him I highly suggest he go step down and let
        someone American handle the job.

        Sincerely,




        The List
        March 11, 2002
