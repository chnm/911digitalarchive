        Insurance Offset

        Some people have objected strongly as to the fairness of offsetting
        insurance proceeds against compensation from the fund.  Since the purpose of
        the fund is to make survivors of victims insofar as possibly financially
        whole, and not to make the survivors of the foresightful better off than the
        survivors of the less prepared, this argument is without merit.  However, to
        assuage their sense of unfairness I recommend that the formula be altered so
        that for those who are the named beneficiaries of life insurance policies,
        their award include the cost of premiums paid.

        Individual Comment
        Golden  CO
