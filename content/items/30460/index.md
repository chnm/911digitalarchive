        We were extremely upset when we heard about this "quiet" act to compensate
        the victims of the World Trade Center.  This is unprecedented and in
        consideration of the amount of money that many of Americans sent to
        different organizations to help, we are wondering why the government would
        take more money out of our pockets - without our permission - to do this?
        We have heard the amount of money many are being given.

        On a personal note, my husband was without a job for a year because of
        cancer.   We had bills we incurred through this, not to mention the money
        we lost due to his not working, which caused great hardship on us.  We gave
        to those in NYC even though we didn't have a lot, but now the government is
        stepping in and doing even more.  This is not a wise move in our opinion
        and we feel it is wrong.
