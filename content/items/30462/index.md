        9-11 Fund, please do not discriminate

        Dear Sir,

        I have recently been informed that the partners of
        gay-Americans who were killed in the 9-11 terrorist
        acts are not going to be receiving compensation from
        the 9-11 fund.  I AM TOTALLY OUTRAGED BY THIS.  I am a
        gay-American, and I contributed $100 of my money to
        help ALL Americans affected, not just the ones you see
        fit based upon your personal bias.  If you are willing
        to overlook the laws regarding illegal aliens, then
        you must overlook the biased laws regarding partners
        of gay-Americans.  You cannot have it both ways; that
        is simple discrimination, and is horrible given the
        times we (ALL of us) face as Americans today.

        In recent interviews, the head of the September 11
        Victim Compensation Fund (a fund created by Congress
        to help the families of those who died on September
        11) has made clear that the partners of gay and
        lesbian heroes of September 11 will not necessarily
        receive the same benefits as the spouses of
        heterosexual victims of the horrible terror attacks.
        EVEN FETUSES AND ILLEGAL ALIENS ARE COVERED!!!
        Even fetuses and illegal aliens will be receiving
        benefits under this problem - they've even promised to
        overlook federal law and NOT arrest and kick out the
        families of illegal aliens, nor will they punish
        American companies that hired illegals in violation of
        federal labor laws.  But when it comes to gay and
        lesbian heroes of the day -              who helped
        bring down the flight in Pennsylvania,             who died in the line of duty when the first tower fell, and
        who was             of the plane that crashed into the
                    (there are many more) - we are told the law
        is the law and no exceptions can be made.

        If September 11 has taught us anything, it's that our
        patriotism and love of country transcend our
        differences and unite us all.  We were told that
        September 11 was the day that "hyphenated-Americans"
        were no more, and rather than being Italian-Americans,
        African-Americans or gay-Americans, we were now simply
        Americans, all of us together as one American family.

        Please do what is right:  distribute funds to ALL
        those affected by this terrible act of hatred.  Please
        do not commit a second act of hatred by discriminating
        against other fellow Americans.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
