        Discrimination Against Americans

        To whom this may concern:

        I find it appalling and offensive that all victims of 9/11 are not treated equal by those
        in charge of the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund.  If Mr. Kenneth Feinberg thinks he can hide behind his cowardly statements of abiding by State Law when it comes to domestic and same-sex partnerships if it serves the purpose to deny rightful compensation to gay
        spouses and partners while breaking Federal Immigration Laws to allow compensation
        to illegal aliens who are not even American, he is sadly mistaken. The good citizens of
        this country will not sit by idle while this jack-ass makes the rest of the good people of
        this country look like backward goons of a past intolerant era.

        All victims deserve equal compensation because that is what donors set out to accomplish. After September 11th
        there is no more room for intolerance in our Society, like it or not.

        If Mr. Feinberg wants to politicize the victims issue and further divide us, he should not be part of this Fund.




        Individual Comment
