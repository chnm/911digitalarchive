        I am terribly saddened...


        Sirs,

        When I gave money to help those Americans involved in
        the tradegy of September 11th, I certainly did not
        intend for you all to take my generous contribution
        and use it to further promote hatred and bigotry. In
        an appearance on the Sunday, March 10 broadcast of
        NBC's "Meet the Press," Kenneth Feinberg makes it so
        terribly clear that his agenda is just that; cut
        anyone who is not "heterosexual" out of the fund.  His
        ramblings about state law do nothing to sway me and
        only further sadden me at the terrible mishandling of
        our generously donated funds.

        I donated so EVERYONE personally effected in the
        heinous act would be somehow compensated for their
        loss.  You now, in your ingnorance, wish to deny those
        Americans who happen to be Gay or Lesbian the same
        rights to my donation as even illegal immigrants will
        receive.

        Shame shame shame on you.  If I had known then what I
        know now, I would think twice about sending you one
        dime.  ALL THE VICTIMS OF SEPTEMBER 11th DESERVE TO BE
        TREATED EQUALLY.  EVERYONE OF THEM.

        Sadly,

        Individual Comment
        Los Angeles, CA
