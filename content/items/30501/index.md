        Saturday, March 16, 2002 6:29 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        I have just read, with indignation, if what I read is true, that
        homosexual partners of some September 11 victims will not receive the
        same compensation as heterosexual partners of victims.  Again, if this
        is true, it is an outrage.

        If what is needed is proof of a legal, civil, or religious commitment to
        the victim, that is clearly denied homosexuals, no matter how hard they
        try to obtain the right to marry or enter into a legal civil union.
        Since the state and federal government prohibit that action of legal
        connection, it only adds insult to injury to further denigrate this
        group by rejecting its fair claim to legal compensation.

        I am incredulous.  Again, if what I read is true, I only hope that
        discriminated against partners of victims decide instead to sue and
        financially shut down the airlines instead!  That will be their only
        recourse left by the 50 States of the Union in denying legal marriage,
        and the US Government in its ridiculous Defense of Marriage Act.

        If there are other reasons for denying these partners of US heroes, what
        can they be?  It couldn't possibly be blatant discrimination--that is
        un-American.

        Pay them.  Give them my tax dollars.  My heart goes out to the loved
        ones, even gay loved ones of our September 11 heroes.  May we keep the
        memories of             and             alive in our American consciousness.

        Most sincerely,

        Individual Comment
