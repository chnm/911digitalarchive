        Saturday, January 26, 2002 9:17 AM
        government paying of victims

         I really am bothered that the US government sees fit to pay victims
        families for Sep 11th. These people were killed. It certainly amounts to
        wrongful death. But how can we draw a distinction from Sep 11th or being
        shot to death in a gas station holdup, a bank robbery, a home robbery, or
        even killed by an automobile accident ( about 110 Americans die every single
        day from automobile related incidents). How can our government tax us to
        help some but not all families of wrongful death or murder ? I believe it is
        unconstitutional.
         I feel very strongly, and am very angry over what has happened to these
        fellow Americans, but how can we compromise our constitution, by having our
        government giving this one group cash? I want to see Bin Laden's head on a
        post outside the capitol as a warning to all who might attempt such a thing.
        But financial meddling by our government should not happen.

        Individual Comment
