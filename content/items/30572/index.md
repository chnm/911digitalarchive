        Saturday, March 16, 2002 9:49 PM
        Discrimination against gays

        Mr Feinberg,
        I would like to take this opportunity to express my outrage at the
        discrimination displayed by the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund
        against the gay and lesbian heroes of the tradegy that befell our nation. You
        claim that you cannot go against state laws, and that equal compensation to
        ALL victims would do just that. Yet, you have no problem bending United States
        laws in your compensations for illegal alien. I fail to see the justice in
        this. How can you place certain victims into second-class status just because
        of their sexuality? Does it make them lesser heroes? September 11th brought
        the nation together as one. Differences did not matter any more. We are all
        Americans. Yet, through this discrimination you dividing this country once
        again. You are telling the people of this great nation that yes, you are all
        Americans, except for this one group - they are lesser Americans and do not
        deserve the same treatment. This should be a time where we look beyond race,
        gender, class, and sexual orientation. I am ashamed to have giving my money to
        this fund. If there were a way, I would take it back. Through the
        discrimination against gays and lesbians, the September 11th Victim
        Compensation Fund has become decidedly anti-American. The discrimination you
        show will only cause more discrimination, which will lead to more hatred. If
        you call yourself American, you will stop tearing this country apart.
        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
