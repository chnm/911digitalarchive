        Monday, March 18, 2002 9:49 AM
        9/11 Fund Compensation Awards

        I am writing to express my concern over the comments made recently by
        Kenneth Zwick regarding the potential of payments being made to partners and
        families of gay and lesbian victims.  It seems that if you're bending the
        law to include illegal aliens and their families, you should not USE the law
        as a way to discriminate against gays and lesbians.  Using state law as your
        guide is not a fair way to determine the eligibility of victims' families
        for money that has been received from around the country and the world.
        Many of those donations have come from gay and lesbian donors, some of whom
        have been personally touched by this tragedy, most of whom haven't, but are
        showing their support and their patriotism toward the 90% of the population
        that are straight AND the 10% who are gay.

        Please reconsider using state law as your guide; the states have NOTHING to
        do with this issue.  Please do use the same compassion that is driving you
        to ignore federal law in some cases to include people who have committed to
        their partners within a system that doesn't ALLOW them to be legally
        recognized.

        Thank you for your attention.


        Individual Comment
        St Louis Park, MN
