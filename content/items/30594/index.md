        Fair benefits for 9/11 victims


        To whom it may concern:

        I understand that the September 11 fund is not planning to pay benefits to
        domestic partners of people who died in the September 11 attacks.  I
        understand that US law does not protect homosexual relationships, but it
        also does not protect illegal immigrants who are living and working in the
        United States IN WILFUL VIOLATION OF FEDERAL LAW.

        It is an outrage that CRIMINALS get better treatment than HONEST TAX
        PAYING CITIZENS.  If you deny benefits to gay couples on the basis that
        their particular state's laws do not protect them, you must also deny the
        same benefits to the illegal immigrants.  In fact, if you REALLY intend to
        comply with the law, you should DEPORT the illegals instead of giving them
        money, but you're not doing that because you know it isn't right.

        Why not do what's right in the case of homosexuals too?

        Keep in mind that if not for the gay man who helped to down flight 93, the
        US Capitol could be just as flat as the WTC right now.


        Individual Comment
        San Francisco, CA
