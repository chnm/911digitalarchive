        compensation for significant others/same sex partners

        Pain and suffering damages traditionally are added to the victim's
        Estate, distributed under a Last Will and Testament.  This legal
        tradition may create a way for the Special Master to compensate
        significant others and same sex partners who cannot collect on the
        Wrongful Death claims for economic damages, which ordinarily are
        distributed to dependents.  (For a discussion on legal treatment of
        Wrongful Death and survival damages, see any Prosser, Wade and
        Schwartz text!)

        You may find that same sex partners tended to have made Testamentary
        dispositions, because that has always been a method to assure
        financial security for one another.

        Although there would tend to be a disparity in treatment between
        spouses and partners, under current laws this would at least assure
        that individuals received some compensation.

        Individual Comment
