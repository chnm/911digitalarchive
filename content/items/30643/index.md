        Tuesday, December 11, 2001 3:07 PM
        Domestic partner benefits re: Sept. 11

        To:

        Kenneth L. Zwick
        Office of Management of Justice
        United States Dept. of Justice

        December 11, 2001

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        Recently I have learned that money collected for the
        victims of the Sept. 11 attacks has not or will not be
        distributed to gay, lesbian, or bisexual partners of
        those who perished in the attacks.

        I am very disappointed and horrified that those who
        have lost loved ones would not receive the same
        treatment as other family members of the Sept. 11
        attacks.  Please support fair and equal treatment of
        all citizens of the United States that have been
        traumatized from the loss of a significant partner
        from these attacks.

        In Virginia, Governor Gilmore is not supporting equal
        treatment for partners of those who were killed on
        Sept. 11.  I understand that Governor Pataki of New
        York has supported and will give financial support to
        partners of victims from Sept. 11.

        These events have been very difficult for all citizens
        in the United States.  Please consider my comments in
        making the decision to give financial support for all
        people throughout the United States who lost partners
        and family members due to these attacks.  Thank you
        for your time and consideration.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Richmond, VA
