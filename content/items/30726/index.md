        Sunday, March 17, 2002 1:00 AM
        A Hero Is A Hero

        After having read the story that domestic partners of
        gay and lesbian survivors in the 9-11 tragedy would
        not receive the same consideration as those of their
        heterosexual contemporaries, I must say that it
        sickens me to know that such discrimination STILL
        continues.

        Gay, straight, black, white, protestant, jew...GET
        OVER IT.  People are people.  A loss is a loss.  And
        it is only fair that everyone be treated equally
        during these trying times.  Instead of trying to find
        MORE ways to segregate the human race, we should try
        to Unite.

        I also believe we are doing the correct thing by
        retaliating, but we shouldn't retaliate against our
        own.  Get over this narcissitic attitude that it's
        either "your way or the highway."

        A loving, gay patriot - with a domestic partner of 12
        years (This has lasted longer than both of my sisters
        straight marriages and my brother's)
