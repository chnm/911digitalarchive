        Sept. 11 Victim compensation fund

        Kenneth L. Zwick
        Office of Management Programs, Civil DIvsion
        US. Dept. OF Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwixk

        I am wriitng in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering the
        "Sept. 11 Victim Compensation Fund."

        I strongly urge the Department to ensure compensation for ALL victims of the
        attack, including the committed partners and all non-biological children of
        gay and lesbian victims.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic losses suffered
        by the families of gay and lesbian victims are no less than that of other
        families. Just as the terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims,
        our country must draw no lines in compensating those who suffered the most
        person of losses.

        I urge the Department to honor the bonds of love in families and treat all
        the victims equally.

        Sincerely


        Individual Comment
        New Boston, NH
