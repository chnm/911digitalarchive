        Thursday, November 29, 2001 3:18 PM
        Please include consideration of my concerns!

        Dear Members of the DOJ & Director Kenneth L. Zwick,

        I urge you in the strongest language of which I am capable to include
        among those who may receive compensation from the September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund those who lost their life partners or de facto
        parents or children, regardless of sexual orientation or marital
        status.  In other words, please include lesbian, gay, bisexual, or
        transgendered Americans among those who deserve recognition for their
        losses.

        All of us as human beings are affected by death & loss; do NOT set
        aside the love & family units created by human beings whose sexual
        orientations might be other than heterosexual. Please recognize their
        families as deeply affected by these tragedies, please recognize
        their financial situations as being of EQUAL merit to ANY OTHER
        citizen of this nation who lost a loved one or was injured in the
        heinous attacks of September 11, 2001.

        This is an historic time, after events that were never imagined
        before September 11th.  I urge you as strongly as I can to act with
        compassion & common sense.  Please include language that includes ALL
        AMERICANS & their families as worthy or compensation by the September
        11 Victim Compensation Fund who can document domestic & financial
        interdependence.  Thank you.

        Emphatically yours,

        Individual Comment
