        Monday, March 11, 2002 2:35 PM
        benefits for Gay and Lesbian surviving partners


        To the September 11 Fund,

        I am writing to find out if gay and lesbians are to be denied
        benefits from the September 11 Fund -- if surviving partners and
        their children will be excluded from payments  made to the families
        of the victims of this tragedy.

        I can only begin to imagine how complicated the process of disbursing
        payments might be, and I am hoping that the comments of  Kenneth
        Feinberg on NBC's "Meet the Press," March 10, 2002, were incorrect
        (see below for comments).  If changes to state law are being made to
        include illegal aliens (a step I find completely appropriate, and in
        line with the sentiments of unity that moved through us as a nation
        in the wake of September 11), then I am certain we possess the both
        the will and the ability to extend the help to all who need and
        deserve it.

        My partner and I made a contribution to the September 11 Fund
        believing that all who needed help would receive it.

        I look forward to your reply.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Durham, N.C.
