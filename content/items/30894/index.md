        Compensate gay domestic partners of 9-11 victims

        Please compensate the gay domestic partners of 9-11 victims in the same way
        you?d compensate a grieving spouse.

        The U.S. Department of Justice?s September 11 compensation fund is
        compensating illegal aliens, who are breaking our federal laws, but not
        compensating the domestic partners of gay 9-11 victims.  I have just spoken
        with
         at the DOJ, who confirmed this fact.  DOJ cites as their policy
        justification that state laws don?t acknowledge domestic partners.  In fact,
        this is homophobic logic.

        Yes, most states don?t yet legalize the unions of gay partners.  That doesn?
        t change the facts.  Americans use the freedom we have in the ?pursuit of
        happiness? in many ways.  Many millions of Americans choose to commit
        themselves to partners of their same gender.  Both
         at the DOJ and myself
        have such unions in our families.  Even Dick Cheney has such family members.
        When you love someone, you can feel the love and humanity in their families.
        You know that their loss upon a death is as deep as anyone?s loss at the
        death of a spouse.

        The September 11 fund is supposed to compensate people for their grief, not
        cause grief.  The Department of Justice should stand for justice.  Here, the
        justice should be for those who lost their life partners (in fact, not
        according to changeable laws).  Without compensation for gay domestic
        partners, the ?Department of Justice? stabs a knife into the already-open
        emotional wound of their loss.

        Respectfully submitted,

                                        Individual Comment
                                        San Francisco, California
