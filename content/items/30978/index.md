        911 compensation fund


        It has come to my attention that partners of gay and lesbians victims of
        the 911 attack on the United States will most likely be unable to recieve
        compensation for the tragic loses of their loved ones.  However, other
        groups of relatives - including those considered ILLEGAL ALIENS - WILL be
        allowed to recieve benefits, with the government turning it's eye away
        from the laws regarding legal citizens of the US.



        I find this policy absolutely unacceptable, and demeaning to the memories
        of those gay and lesbian vicitms - some who which were absolute heroes in
        bringing down the airliner thought to be heading for Washington D.C.



        I ask your administration to reconsider this policy decision, and allow
        those partners of gay and lesbians who were killed on September 11th to
        also recieve equal financial compensation recieved by ALL other familes of
        vicitims of this tragedy.




        Thank you very much.



        Individual Comment
        Seattle
