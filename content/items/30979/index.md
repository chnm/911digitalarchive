        9/11 compensation

        To whom it may concern:

        I would like to express my feelings on the compensation plan for the 9/11 victims.  I saw a report on FOXNews today that I thought was pretty disturbing.  A woman wanted more than the alotted 250,000.00 that she would get for her sons death (whom was 21,had no wife or children and they never depended on his financial support), because that was not enough money to cover pain and suffering.  I have a daughter and a husband and God forbid anything happen to them, but I would never be able to put a dollar figure on pain and suffering.  I don't care if you gave me all the money in the world, that would not fill the place of my loved one.  People lose loved ones to all sorts of different circumstances and most are not refunded of there families lives.  And as horrific as this event was, I don't feel as if anyone should get money unless they lost someone that they were financially dependent upon.  I think that 250K is more than enough for people like the above mentioned.  They are just money hungry!  Keep everything the way that it is and give the most money to those who have children and wives or husbands.  Thank you for your time and I hope the rest of America is with me.

        Thank you,
        Individual Comment
