        Thursday, December 20, 2001 8:39 PM
        RE: COMMENTS REGARDING SUBTRACTIONS for the Victims


        Dear Sir or Mam,

        I for one am totally appalled with the fund for SUBTRACTING
        any life insurance or pension benefits from anyone who was
        injured or killed during the Attack.

        You totally screw any family who planned for their future.
        I thank God did not lose any friends, acquaintances, or
        family members in this attack, but I feel totally angered at
        these subtractions for those families who did.

        It seems to me it is the governments' cop-out in the failure
        to protect our citizens from terrorists in the first place.
        And to further compound the terror, you mess with their
        future.  You want to be fair, or so you say, BUT if that is
        true then how on earth could you take away and devalue their
        life or lives the way you are doing?

        I feel that if the FBI and CIA had been paying attention
        instead of sitting on their laurels this type of attack
        wouldn't have been able to take place in the first place.
        You don't want to be sued or the airlines sued so you
        cheapskate your way out by such a paltry funding and expect
        the grieving families to be happy with all these take a
        ways.

        I wonder....Is the government so callous, hardened, and
        uncaring, that if it were each of your family members being
        screwed by these subtractions, would you be so quick to
        implement them?  You all by rote, spiel out all the words of
        being upset by these actions, yet you crucify a family for
        having pensions or life insurance...  How is that fair??


        A furious taxpayer
        and Concerned American,




        Individual Comment
        Starford PA
