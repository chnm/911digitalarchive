        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        I am concerned by the attitude of the US Government and the victims of the
        9/11 attacks.  Additionally, I would like to protest the fact that my tax
        dollars are being spent in this manner.  As terrible as the loss was for the
        families of the victims, they are not ?entitled? to money from the US
        taxpayers.  I agree they should be helped in their time of need.  That is
        exactly the reason I have given money to charities and blood to the Red
        Cross.   However, I do not believe it is ?deserved? as many outspoken
        representative claim.  Nor do I believe I should be forced, de facto, to
        give more of my money.  It is simply contrary to what this country stands
        for?individual responsibility.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Ardmore, AL
