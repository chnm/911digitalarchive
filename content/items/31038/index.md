        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 3:48 AM
        September 11 Victim Compenstion Fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        The Justice Department requested public comment on the upcoming regulations
        for implementing and administering the "September 11 Victim Compensation
        Fund."  I URGE the Department to draft regulations that ensures compensation
        is available to ALL victims, including the committed partners and the
        non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        There is no question that the terrible grief and economic losses suffered by
        the families of gay and lesbian victims are no less than that of other
        families.  The terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims, and our
        country must draw no lines in compensating those who suffered the most
        personal of losses from these horrific attacks.

        Many Americans, human beings all, lost their lives on September 11, each of
        whom left behind loved ones.  I URGE the Department, once again, to honor all
        bonds by treating the families of gay and lesbian victims as you would any of
        the grieving human beings.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Chico, CA
