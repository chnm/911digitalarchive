        Pre-Conference

        As I recall Mr. Feinberg's comments on PBS he
        indicated that he wanted the beneficiary to know, with
        a fair degree of accuracy, the benefits they would
        receive under the program.  To this end he mentioned
        that there would be a right to come into his office
        and discuss the matter before submission of the actual
        claim form.
        The proposed regulations do not make provision for a
        pre-submission conference. A pre-submission conference
        is extremely important to my client as they have
        special circumstances that would not normally be
        addressed in a typical application and the proposed 21
        days to file supplemental information may not be
        sufficient time to develop the data.  If a
        pre-submission conference were held it would be
        possible to submit data appropriate from the Master's
        viewpoint in the original claim.  Specifically, my
        client has a child that is severely mentally impaired
        but not legally incompetent.  Guidance from the Master
        regarding what would help him understand our position
        may not be possible to obtain in the 21 days allowed
        by the current regulations to file supplementary
        information.

        Individual Comment
