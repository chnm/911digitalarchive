        Jan 6, 2002
        Dear Mr. Feinberg

          I am writing to urge you to ensure fair treatment for all surviving families of the tragedy on Sept. 11,
        including the children and families of low-wage workers and gay and lesbian families.

          Please  set a high minimum compensation level for all families of victims, instead of following traditional
        lost wage rules. It would be horribly unfair for families of  low wage workers who were already
        struggling financially to watch most of the tax payer money to go to families of  wealthier workers. The
        Red Cross & the State of New York have announced that they will not discriminate against gay &
        lesbian families. Please do the same.


        Individual Comment
        Portland, OR
