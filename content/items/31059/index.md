        In response to an email I received from a friend:

               "To protest the exclusion of gay and lesbian partners from receiving
        U.S.government benefits for their September 11 losses, send a brief e-mail to
        victimcomp.comments@usdoj.gov"

        I would personally like to add my opinion to this matter. These people, like
        any of the other family/marrige partners who lost a loved one in the tragedy,
        also feel the pain of loss just as deeply as anyone else. To exclude them is
        to show a prejudice against American citizens in a time of need.  It isn't
        constitutional to exclude any American from the same rights that his or her
        countrymen benefit from.
