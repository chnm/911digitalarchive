        Relief Fund finds no Relief

        My husband was killed on 9/11 leaving behind a 4 year old son, a 2 year old daughter and a seven week old daughter whom he never had the chance to meet.  I am writing because I am very angry with the way the government has set up the 9/11 relief fund for families.  My husband and I had made a conscious decision to purchase life insurance.  I do not feel that we should now be penalized for having been forward thinking.  If I felt as though I could sue American Airlines (m my husband was on flight 11) I would not be so angry.  I feel that the government took away my option to do so when it capped each plane at $1.5 billion.

        Individual Comment
