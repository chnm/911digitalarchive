        Equal treatment for 9/11 compensation

        FAO:
        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice


        In formulating the rules that will govern the September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund, I would urge you to ensure that those who lost their life
        partners or de facto parents or children, regardless of sexual orientation
        or marital status are included in the category of relatives deserving of
        compensation.

        Please do not make this tragedy a further reemphasis of the inequalities
        those in same sex relationships must face regarding their long-term,
        committed relationships.

        Thank you for your consideration.

        Yours,

        Individual Comment
