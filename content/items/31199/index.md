        I am saddened once again by your lack of equality


        It has come to my attention that everyone, including illegal aliens (who will

        rather see you dead.

        sad day when lawbreakers like illegal aliens and the businesses that hire
        them are receiving better treatment than law-abiding citizens.

        I always knew John Ashcroft was trouble, but I really knew something was
        wrong when the works of art (the statuary) around the Dept.of Justice
        building were covered up.  Why didn't he just order them blown to bits like
        the Taliban did to those ancient Buddhas?

        You insult all Americans and all that America stands for when you constantly
        maneuver every way you can to deprive gays and lesbians the equal right to
        employment protection, housing protection, and every other protection that
        every other citizen enjoys.

        We're all waving flags, alright, but the country has not become closer and
        more united, except in fighting the common enemy at hand.  There is no room
        for bigotry in this country, and the role model against it should be how our
        government presents itself.  You are doing a dismal job so far.

        Individual Comment
