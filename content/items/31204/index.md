        Wednesday, December 05, 2001 4:59 PM
        Attn: Kenneth L. Zwick, Dept. Justice

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I am writing in response to the request by your office for public feedback
        concerning the drafting, implementation, and administration of the
        "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund." I think it is very important for
        the language included in forthcoming regulations to include compensation for
        the widows of gay and lesbian partnerships, as well as their children,
        biological or not. Family is defined more broadly today than it ever has,
        and I think it valid that those who considered themselves the family of a
        victim or victims in this tragic event be included and compensated equally
        as those in a more traditional scenario.

        Furthermore, as the supervising manager of a restaurant that proudly took
        part in the nation-wide "Windows of Hope" charity day, a day where a portion
        of our proceeds and those of hundreds of other restaurants for one evening
        went to the families of those restaurant workers that were killed in Windows
        on the World, I strongly lend my voice to the families of those victims as
        well. Many of those people who worked for Windows on the World as well as
        those that worked in the World Trade Center, were undocumented residents of
        this country. Therefore, their families, many of whom are either living
        elsewhere in the world or are also undocumented, have not recieved the kind
        of governmental aide that those who are citizens of the United States have.
        I feel that your Compensation Fund also ought to take the stance that the
        American Red Cross has by encouraging those who are victims and may be
        illegal to come forward for help without the fear of arrest and deportation.
        In this trying time, we must look past such petty laws and look into the
        lives of those that have lost so much. This a human issue, a global issue,
        and one that supercedes, for the time being, immigration law.

        Thank you for your time.

        Respectfully,
        Individual Comment
        Coral Gables, FL
