        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 3:50 PM
        Sept. 11 Victims

        To Whom It May Concern:

         I write in order to advocate that ALL the victims of the September 11
        attacks be able to participate in any governmental funds available on their
        behalf.  Domestic partners and the children of gay parents should be
        allowed to receive benefits; their loved ones' sexual orientation and
        marriage licenses should not be the defining measure of who is eligible to
        receive benefits.

         Please do the right thing.  It was a gay man who was instrumental in
        saving American lives by fighting the hijackers on one of the American
        airlines' flights (the one that crashed in Pennsylvania).  Surely anyone
        who believes in justice and fairness and equality for all will recognize
        that the domestic partners and children of gay people are just as
        deserving, and just as grief-stricken, as those in traditional heterosexual
        families.

         Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
         Valdosta, Georgia
