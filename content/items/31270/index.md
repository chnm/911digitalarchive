        December 13, 2001


        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        Civil Division
        United states Department of Justice
        Main Building , Room 3140
        950 Pennsylvania Ave.
        Washington, DC 20530

        Dear Mr.Zwick:

                  As you consider government relief for the victims of the attacks on our country on
        September 11, please consider two things:

             1)   Aid to the families of the victims should not be based solely on the victim's last
                  paycheck. We should not be giving away public funds only to the wealthy. A
                  strong minimum payment for all victims' families should be established.

             2)   New York State and the American Red Cross have stated that families of gay and
                  lesbian victims will be compensated the same as others. I urge your office to
                  certify this policy as well. Those families are just as much in need as any others.

             Thank you for your attention.

        Sincerely,
        Chicopee, MA
