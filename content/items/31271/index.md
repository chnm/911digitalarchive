        administration of funds for compensation of families of tragedy

            I hope that the funds will be equally distributed among ALL the families of the victims of Sept. 11th. Discrimination would be another tragedy. Gay life partners and unmarried heterosexual partners have had the same emotional and financial devastation as the "traditional families". As a stay at home lesbian mom I know  the loss of my partner in such a sudden and sensless way would  be emotionally devastating, but the loss of income and benifits would make it more than I could cope with. It would compound the tremendous impact on the children.  They would then have to deal with the added burden of no income, and immediate life style changes as I would have had to get a job and leave them a soon as possible, on  top of the loss of a parent  . Regardless of someones religious or moral beliefs,  this country is based of equality  for all. That is what separation of church and state is all about. Please remember these unfortunate victims when deciding on the
        rules for the fund. A victim is a victim. And there has been more than
        enough sensless  tragedy in this country.

        Individual Comment
