        Thursday, March 21, 2002 10:33 AM




        Mr. Feinberg:

        I heard today, with more than a little dismay and disgust, that your
        organization intends to deny partners of homosexual vicitms of September
        11 the benefits that are to be paid to illegal immigrants and
        non-citizens who also lost family members in the attacks.

        Can you explain to me how you justify this? I was under the impression
        that the September 11th Fund was created to ease the financial burden of
        victims' spouses and loved ones. Are you and your collegues suggesting
        that the gay spouses and loved ones are somehow less deserving of our
        help and compassion? That homosexuals who died on the 11th due to the
        intolerance and hatred of a group of people are of less value? Are you
        really prepared to perpetrate intolerance and demonstrate such a
        stunning lack of compassion? What drives this decision of the Fund?
        Misguided and narrow-minded morals? A personal agenda? Finances? Please
        tell me how you can turn your back on Americans like &nbsp , one
        of the heros of Flight 93, and his loved ones.

        I am saddened and angered by the September 11th Fund's policy. I urge
        you to search your heart, and change your mind.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
