        Dear Friends,

        I am writing to protest the unfair treatment being given to lesbians
        and gay men in the administration of the September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund.  I find it deplorable that the partners of lesbians
        and gay men who were killed or injured in the attacks will not be
        receiving the same benefits as heterosexual spouses.

        This is especially disturbing because illegal aliens will be receiving
        benefits, and will not be subject to arrest and deportation.  If the
        USA can extend help to these people, can we not help our American gay
        and lesbian brothers and sisters who have lost partners?

        Also disturbing in this is that it is a slap in the face of heroic
        Americans on that terrible day: &nbsp , &nbsp , and
        &nbsp among others who are gay or lesbian.

        It is time for our government and country to stop finding ways to
        divide Americans into Us and Them, and to realize that we are all part
        of this time of trial.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Coventry, CT
