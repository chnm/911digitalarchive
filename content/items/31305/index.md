        Sunday, November 25, 2001 4:54 AM

        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001




        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director

        Office of Management Programs

        Civil Division

        U.S. Department of Justice

        Main Building, Room 3140

        950 Pennsylvania Avenue

        Washington, DC 20530



        In Re:  Public Law 107-42





        Thank you for inviting public comment on the above-cited Public Law and its provitions.  I wish to specifically comment on the Victims' Fund.



        There has been great public discussion and no small debate over who exactly constitutes a victim for purposes of eligibility for a portion of
        this Fund.  I wish to express my earnest belief that a victim should herein be defined as any person with an accountable relationship to any of
        those who perished in the national tragedies at the Pentagon and the World Trade Center.



        I particularly wish to fully endorse the provisions contained in the Congressional letter of 16 November authored by Mr. Frank and signed by 44
        Members of Congress, including Mr. Nadler, whose district includes the site of the World Trade Center.



        I wish to note that Governor Pataki has already signed a compensatory program which reflects the broad and inclusive interpretation we are
        asking the federal government to apply to the victims of this tragedy.  I hope and pray that my nation's government is as compassionate as that of the city and state of New York.



        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment

        Providence, Rhode Island
