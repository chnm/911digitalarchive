        Thursday, November 08, 2001 9:38 AM
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund of 2001

        I have been displaced from my apartment

        It is inconceivable to me that any one directly affected by the events of
        9/11 would not be considered to be a victim even under what appears to be
        the very narrow definition provided.

        I have been displaced now for almost 2 months now and I have no date from
        the landlord as to when I will be able to move back to my home.  For a
        number of weeks following the attack access to my apartment was extremely
        limited as the whole area was designated a crime scene. Even now access to
        my apartment is limited to business hours and I have to sign in before
        entering.

        The cost of finding alternative housing and the clean-up of the apartment
        (HazMat decontamination) not to mention the emotional cost is staggering.

        Individual Comment
        New York, New York
