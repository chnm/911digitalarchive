        December 6, 2001


        Dear Mr. Zwick,
        Please follow a completely inclusive policy in distributing compensation to the victims of the Sept 11 tragedies.
        I understand that there is a movement to exclude members of non-traditional families such as lesbians and gays and non- biological children of victims.
        These individuals are suffering in similar ways regardless of the legal/civil defination of their family relationship.
        This is not the time to be divisive and discriminatory with judgments.
        Please take a strong stand for inclusiveness and non-discrimination in this matter. The whole world is watching.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Traverse City, MI
