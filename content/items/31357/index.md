        Victims Comp Fund Comments



        Dear Attorney General Ashcroft,



        In response to your request for comments regarding the
        Sept. 11th Victims Compensation Fund providing
        assistance to those directly affected by the terrorist
        attacks, I would like to tell you about my partner
                  , who died in the Pentagon attack; and how
        deeply this has affected me.



                   loved and lived life with humor and practicality.
        She was prior military (10 years U.S. Navy); she
        completed her Bachelor?s in 1998 and was contemplating
        starting her Masters.  She had just completed a yearlong
        mid-level management course the week prior to Sept.
        11th.  She belonged to Toastmasters.  She loved nature,
        valued friendship and was a terrific photographer.



                   and I were together 17 years and had figured that
        we could celebrate our golden anniversary together.  We
        co-bought our current house and lovingly rehabbed it to
        become our home.  We co-owned a car, had joint bank and
        credit card accounts and were the beneficiaries to each
        other?s wills.  We were in all respects, a true and
        committed partnership.



        Mr. Attorney General, it makes no difference whether one
        calls a relationship a marriage (traditional) or a
        partnership (non-traditional); the coming together of
        two people, to love and laugh and cry together; to
        provide constant support and camaraderie for each other,
        to share the bills and heartache and good fortune
        together; that is the meaning of a true relationship.
        That is what            and I shared.             mother said
        it best when she stated that            had been married
        twice before (conventional) and it didn?t work; yet it
        worked for us. I am grateful for every minute of the
        years we had together.  For our partnership.



        I am asking you, when defining the criteria for
        compensation to the victim?s families, to recognize same-
        sex partnerships as a valid and true relationship.   I
        sincerely hope your committee can be as open and
        accepting as the Red Cross has been with me.  I am
        thanking you in advance for your
        consideration.




        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment

        University Park,  MD
