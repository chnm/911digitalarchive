        Dear Sirs:

        The federal government has found a way to put aside laws regarding the

        11, 2001. This is our government at its best.

        If our government can do the right thing for illegal immigrants killed in
        the attacks and their loved ones, then it absolutely must do the right thing
        for all American citizens killed and their loved ones.

        The federal government can find a way to put aside laws regarding the rights
        of Americans who happen to be homosexual in order to grant compensation to
        the loved ones of those gay and lesbian American citizens killed in the
        terrorist attacks. It would be shameful to do otherwise.

        Don't hide behind "states' rights" rhetoric. No state is asking you to deny
        compensation to anyone. Just do the right thing and do it now.

        Individual Comment
        An Arizonan living in California
