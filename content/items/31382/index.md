        Dear President Bush,

        If the quotes attributed to Kenneth Feinberg from his appearance on "Meet the Press" are accurate, I am outraged.

        As disturbing as the past six months have been, it was heartening to see that when push came to shove, even the most liberal of us could rally behind your leadership.

        I hope for a speedy revision of this policy.

        Will you offer support and relief to every person affected by the bombings in New York, Washington D.C., and western Pennsylvania?

        Will you include the partners or gays and lesbians?

        Or, will bias and discrimination erode the tenuous recovery that Americans are just beginning to muster.

        Please let me know.


        Sincerely,


        Individual Comment
        NYC NY
