        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 11:02 AM
        Comments on Victims Compensation

        I am very concerned about the fact that the Government will provide
        compensation to victims of the September 11, 2001 disaster. While this
        was the utmost invasion of US soil by terrorist, I am very concerned
        that the government will not take into consideration all the facts.
        First of all the victims families should be compensated to the fullest.
        The companies that employed known victims should be the first to make
        sure families of the victims are taken care of. The Red Cross is also
        involved in this process I am told. I however do not know the full
        involvement of the Red Cross.
        There will surely be law suits against parties. The law suits should
        move forward and just compensation given.
        My point is that the Government should be very careful and let the just
        and normal compensation by employers and outcomes of lawsuits take place
        before a fund established by Congress be used for compensation.
        This was a great tragedy. There is no denying that. However be ever
        vigilant of what is happening and do not throw money at something that
        will be taken care of in a prudent manner.

        Individual Comment
