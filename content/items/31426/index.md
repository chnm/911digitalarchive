        My name is                      and I have become aware recently of the inequity of
        financial help available to families of victims of the September 11th
        terrorist attacks. I am deeply saddened and outraged at the recent statement
        made by Kenneth Feinberg on Meet the Press that he will defer to state
        governments in terms of whether domestic partners of gay and lesbian victims
        of the 9/11 tragedy will receive any kind of compensation.  As you know,
        there is no federal law protecting domestic partners although they pay the
        same taxes as heterosexuals, contribute to society, and even were recognized
        as playing an instrumental part in the 9/11 events (                    , NY Fire
        Department                     ).  It is an injustice, on top of
        such suffering, to deny compensation or benefits of any kind to domestic
        partners of gay and lesbian victims, who are not eligible for social
        security benefits, survivor's insurance and other compensations allowed to
        heterosexual couples.  I ask you to direct Mr. Feinberg to make an exception
        for domestic partners in the same way the 9/11 fund made an exception for
        families of undocumented persons.  Please do not contribute to further
        victimization by leaving these families more vulnerable after the tragic
        loss of their loved ones.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        San Francisco, CA
