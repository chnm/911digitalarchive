        Firefighter and police inequity

        Dear Mr Feinberg,  NY Policemen and fireman, who heroically perished at the
        wtc disaster,and who saved the lives of 99% of the people in the buildings
        below the floors struck by the planes, are entitled to absolutely nothing
        according to your plan. They  risk their lives every day and their pension
        plan is their reward for this risk, not their meager salaries, as they make
        30% less than surrounding communities. Their rights to a lawsuit have been
        stripped by Congress as nothing will be left of the airlines insurance when
        the creditors and government get at it. My son perished in this disaster and
        it is great to know that you consider his life to be worth ZERO. No amount of
        money can replace him. The rich airlines were protected and the little guy
        gets stung again. It is truely sad that I will never see his smiling face or
        know what it like to have a grandchild. I am a firefighter and work at Ground
        Zero in the recovery effort. If you could come here for one day and work by
        my side, you would see  terrible and horrific sights which would enlighten
        you and make you very sympathetic to the 3,000 heroes who perished that day.
        The government (FAA) is wrong to have allowed people to bring knives and
        boxcutters on board. Step up and take some responsibility for this
        disaster.Don't give ZEROES to HEROES.

        Individual Comment
