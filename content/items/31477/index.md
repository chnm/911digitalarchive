        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 7:32 PM
        Just Compensation to All Survivors


        December 4, 2001

        Office of Management
        Dept of Justice
        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I write in support of offering benefits and
        reimbursement to the surviving domestic partner and
        any children of gay or lesbian victims of the
        September 11 tragedy.

        There are already enough horror stories of people
        being denied any recognition of their relationship to
        their loved ones killed in this act of terrorism.
        Need we add more suffering to their lives by denying
        them the recognition and just compensation needed for
        them to reorder their lives?

        The City of New York and the State of New York have
        set an example of what is the right and decent thing
        to do in this matter.  I hope and trust that the Dept.
        of Justice will follow this good example and show the
        world that the American people unite in times of
        suffering and hardship.

        It would be terribly ironic and the worst possible
        message to send if the Dept of Justice decided that
        discrimination based on religious prejudice should
        govern acts of charity and justice for the survivors
        of a tragedy motivated by the same kind of self
        righteous religious fanaticism.

        We are a people of diverse ethnic, religious and
        political persuasions and backgrounds.  In such
        circumstances it is only just that we be generous to
        all our people.  That is our greatness as a people and
        I trust the Dept. of Justice will make us proud of our
        heritage by acting generously and compassionately
        toward all the survivors of the tragedy.

        Sincerely yours,

        Individual Comment
        Storrs, CT
