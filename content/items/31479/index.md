        Friday, December 07, 2001 5:58 AM
        concerning regulations governing the "September 11th Fund"

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I appreciate very much the chance to share my comments concerning
        regulations governing the "September 11th Fund", as recently requested
        by the Justice Department.  I strongly urge the Department to draft
        regulations that ensure compensation is available to all victims of the
        attack, including the committed partners and the non-biological children
        of gay and lesbian victims.

        In this time of grief for our nation, I feel that it is necessary for
        our government to lead the way in supporting unity and tolerance.  Gay
        and lesbian families, which consist of committed partners, sometimes
        raising children, function just as all American families do: they depend
        on one another for emotional and financial support.  The terrorist
        attacks have destroyed the family support systems of everyone involved,
        and all victims are in need of help.  By supporting all of the families
        devastated by these attacks, the "September 11th Fund" will not only
        show boundless compassion, but will quicken the healing of our citizens
        and children, and our nation.

        I urge the Department to honor all families damaged by the attacks on
        September 11, 2001, showing no discrimiation against gay and lesbian
        victims, just as they would not discriminate against people of color or
        families with different religious backgrounds.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Mount Dora, FL
