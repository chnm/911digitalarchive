        Thursday, March 14, 2002 10:06 AM
        911 Victims Fund and Gays


        At this time of trial in our country, I am appalled by
        this country's possibly refusing financial assistance
        to all segments of our population.  It doesn't matter
        to the victim's life partners and family what their
        sexual preferences were.  I find it very troubling
        that we will assist illegal aliens no questions asked;
        but totally ignore our citizens with alternative life
        styles. Kenneth Feinberg, the head of the September
        11th Victim Compensation Fund (a fund created by
        Congress and run by the Department of Justice), said
        that gay partners of the heroes of September 11th will
        not necessarily be eligible for the same compensation
        as heterosexual family members who lost their loved
        ones. The federal government can and should be fair in
        this matter. I don't care what the laws of the
        individual states might be. There have been many
        "Heros" during and since the events of 9/11/2001.
        They came from all walks of life.  When the 911 Fund
        was set up,I was proud to know that we would take care
        of our people. As a heterosexual, I never gave a
        thought to the fact that someone might not be assisted
        because of their life choices.  Please do the right
        thing, and take care of all our citizens.

        Thank you,

        Individual Comment
