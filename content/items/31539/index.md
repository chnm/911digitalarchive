        Issues for the Special Master to Consider

        I am the son of one of the deceased victims of the WTC attack.  I am now
        24 years of age.  My father (the deceased) was widowed 12 years ago, of
        which I am the only child.  Four years ago my father remarried.  My
        step-mother has two children (one 23 and one 21) who are not related to
        my father by anything other than marriage.  I am concerned that since
        she is the personal representive, I will not be protected in the
        Compensation process but her children may be.

        I believe in circumstance like this, the Special Master should take the
        following into consideration:

        1)  The direct (blood) relation of claimants in determining the split of
        funds.
        2)  The length of time associated with the decendent (ie 4 years of
        marriage as opposed to 24 years of life)
        3)  Amount of support the decendent gave to each benificiary.
