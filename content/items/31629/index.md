        Sunday, March 24, 2002 2:29 PM
        discrimination of the 9-11 fund

        To Whom it May Concern:

        I have to express the outrage i feel towards the 9-11
        fund regarding the non-eligible distribution of funds
        to gay and lesbian families and loved ones.

        How dare you pick and chose who may and may not be
        eligible based on someones sexual orientation? Why not
        base it on someones's religion or perhaps their
        ethnicity or even their gender???? Simply because you
        realize how ignorant and outrageous that would be not
        to mention totally unacceptable.
        Basing distribution based on ones sexual orientation
        is also ignorant, outrageous and totally unacceptable.
        As an America i am continually amazed at how our
        government can no doubt take something and turn it
        into chaos with no basis except for bias and
        ignorance.
        how stale is that?

        THESE PEOPLE ARE U.S CITIZENS!

        I do not believe the people who contributed to ANY
        effort regarding 9-11 included in their distribution
        their preference of who was to receive that money.
        What is just as sad as the 9-11 attacks is the remorse
        some people have over contributing their money to the
        hands of mismanagement.

        I wonder if next time i pay my taxes i may elect
        specifically where my tax money is to go and depend on
        the government to distribute accordingly.

        Individual Comment
        New Hope Pa.
