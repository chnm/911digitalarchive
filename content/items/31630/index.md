        Hello,
        I am providing feedback on the September 11 Victims Compensation Fund
        I would like to ask that eligibility to receive compensation be inclusive of
        ALL Americans impacted by this tragedy. This includes those who lost their
        life partners as well as de facto parents of children, without regard to
        sexual orientation or marital status.

        New York is granting compensation benefits 'on a showing of mutual
        interdependence with the victim, in recognition that anyone who shared with
        the victim living expenses, day-to-day activities and the emotional bonds of
        family deserves help in this time of need'.

        Thank you for the opportunity to provide feedback regarding the
        administration of the fund.
