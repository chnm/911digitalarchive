        Sent: Thursday, December 06, 2001 12:23 AM
        Subject: NO descrimination
        Dear Mr. Zwick,
        I am a 19 year old art student in Brooklyn. I saw the Towers Fall on
        September 11th. It was a numbing experience. Later that day, as my friends
        and I were desperately searching for a place to donate blood, we passed
        in front of the Brooklyn Bridge. It felt like someone had knocked the wind
        out of me. There were people, as far as the eye could see, streaming across
        all lanes of the Brooklyn Bridge. They were all covered in dust and ashes,
        and some had blood on their clothes. They all looked confussed and defeated.It
        was then when the reality of it hit me, This Really Did Happen. Not only
        did it happen, but it happened to all people from every walk of life. Not
        just Whites, Blacks, Hispanics, Homosexuals, Chinese, but it happened to
        everyone.
        Now when I ride the subway I constantly see missing pictures of Fathers,
        Mothers, Sons, Daughters, and loved ones. I wish I could give them answers,
        but I know I can't. But I did give them all of my money in my wallet as
        I passed by My college's Donation Containers. I put that money in thinking
        that I had done a good thing.
        Now Imagine my shock when I found out that the Religous Right (and other
        groups) are trying to dictate that some famlies of those victims don't
        deserve that money simply because they're Gay.
        This goes against everything that America symbolizes, and is discrimination.
        It undermines the Constitution and Mocks the BIll of Rights. In this time
        of New Found Patriotism, if such a thing is allowed to occur, Then I am
        truly Disapointed in my country.
        Please do not destroy my Love of This Country, and all that it is supposed
        to symbolize.
        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
