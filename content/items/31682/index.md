        Gay Americans lost to on September 11th.


        I think it's the gravest injustice I've ever heard
        regarding the interview on Meet the Press that aired
        on March 10th. According to Kenneth Fienberg, head of
        the victims compensation fund for the September 11th
        event some Americans are likely going to be left out
        because of who they are. I think it's a travesty of
        justice and the personal beliefs of attorney general ,
        John Ashcroft, and his department that will likely
        isolate the gay and lesbian citizens from recieving
        some compensation for the heartache and sorrow that
        so many Americans experienced on September 11th. In
        short Mr. Fienberg's comments that it will be
        ultimately up to each state to decide if gay and
        lesbian Americans will be compensated is a cop out.
        What will it take for this country and in particular
        our leaders in Washington to wake up and see that
        regardless of thier own personal feelings that all
        Americans suffered and lost on that day in September.
        Here's one gay American who hopes our leaders wake up.

        Individual Comment
