        Monday, March 11, 2002 2:25 PM
        Sept 11 Discrimination


        To Whom It May Concern:



        I understand that Kenneth Feinberg has decided that the September 11th
        Victim Compensation Fund will not give any money to gay partners of any
        of the victims of the 9/11 tragedies unless those victims happened to be
        lucky enough to live in a state which already recognizes gay
        relationships.  I find this absolutely APPALLING!  You are
        discriminating against a group of people who were as affected by the
        tragedy as any other group - a group that includes many of the heroes
        you tout as having saved so many lives that day (including           ,
        who helped prevent flight 93 from crashing into its intended target,
        which may have included all of you).



        I know that up until now, you have taken public input to reconsider how
        these benefits will be applied.   Please reconsider this decision as
        well.





        Let's not make Sept. 11 about discrimination or politics.  That day was
        a tragedy for everyone, gay and straight.  You have the ability to make
        the situation a little bit better, rather than a whole lot worse.  Thank
        you for your time.



        Yours truly,



        Individual Comment, taxpayer and concerned U.S. citizen

        South San Francisco, CA
