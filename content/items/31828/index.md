        March 17, 2002

        Special Master Kenneth R. Feinberg
        September 11th Victim Compensation Fund
        U.S. Department of Justice
        950 Pennsylvania Avenue NW
        Washington, DC 20530

        Mr. Feinberg,

        While I by no means consider myself to be an "activist," I feel I must take
        issue with  comments made by you on "Meet the Press" on Sunday, March 10,
        2002. The comments I make reference to are those directed at Gay and Lesbian
        AMERICANS whose partners perished in that terrible, unforgivable act of
        terrorism on September 11, 2001.

        During that interview, you indicated that surviving partners will not
        necessarily be eligible for the same compensation as heterosexual family
        members who lost their loved ones. Your rational behind this position seemed
        to be that you were simply following guidelines on how individual states
        addressed "domestic partnerships."

        As I understand it (and please, by all means, correct me if I am wrong),
        surviving family members of ILLEGAL IMMIGRANTS  will be fully compensated,
        but because you are following "State Laws," surviving gay partners are not
        afforded the same consideration.

         So let me get this STRAIGHT (every pun intended). This country, this land
        that I love, the home of the free and the brave, gives more consideration to
        an illegal immigrants surviving family members that it does to the surviving
        partners and children and gay and lesbian men and women who perished?

        Pray tell, how is it that a gay man or woman's life is  less valuable than
        someone who has entered this country ILLEGALLY??? If you want to follow laws
        when deciding who deserves what compensation, why not take into consideration
        FEDERAL LAW??

        You sir, with all due respect, are an idiot.

        Let me assure you of this. The gay men and women (with the exception of the
        illegal immigrants) who went down with the towers that paid their taxes, bled
        red the same as all the other victims. And their survivors hurt just as badly
        as all the other survivors.

        Actually, thanks to you and your draconian way of thinking, they now hurt
        much worse.

        I suppose this is just another example of "dubyas" philosophy of
        "compassionate conservatism."

        Thank you for you time.



        Individual Comment
        Gay Taxpaying American
