        Survivor Benefits re: lgbt 911 victime

        I am incredulous to read that surviving partners of gay, lesbian and
        transgendered victims of the 911 tragedies are not automatically included in
        the benefits program for other surviving partners/spouses.

        The root problem, of course, is that lgbt Americans cannot marry in this
        country.  That very un-American discrimination will eventually end; however,
        excluding lgbt survivors now only compounds a travesty of equal justice that
        our lawmakers seem slow to rectify.

        Laws seem to be getting bent all over the place in order to qualify
        survivors, including those of illegal aliens.  I find it insulting that it is
        ONLY lgbt survivors who are told that their benefits must be determined
        according to the letter of the law.  Did our laws really anticipate a 911
        situation?

        Think about what you are doing and saying.  Upon reflection I am sure you
        will finally do the RIGHT thing.

        Thanks for listening.  Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Providence, RI
