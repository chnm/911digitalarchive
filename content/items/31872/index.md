        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 5:30 PM
        equal compensation to ALL families

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs, Civil Division
        U.S. Department of Justice
        via E-MAIL victimcomp.comments@usdoj.gov

        Dear Mr. Zwick:

        I am writing in response to the Department's request for public comments
        regarding the forthcoming regulations for implementing and administering the
        "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund." I strongly urge the Department to
        draft regulations that ensure compensation is available to all victims of
        the attack, including the committed partners and the non-biological children
        of gay and lesbian victims.

        It is unquestionable that the devastating grief and economic losses suffered
        by the families of gay and lesbian victims are no less than that of other
        families.  Just as the terrorists drew no lines in choosing their victims,
        our country must draw no lines in compensating those who suffered the most
        personal of losses from these horrific attacks.

        As the adult daughter of a lesbian mother, this issue is particularly
        important to me. I know I'm not alone-- there are an estimated 4-6 million
        sons and daughters with gay and lesbian parents in the United States, and we
        are organized, active and passionate about protecting the rights of our
        families.

        Many Americans lost their lives on September 11, each of whom left behind
        loved ones.  I urge the Department to honor these bonds by treating the
        families of gay and lesbian victims as you
        would any other.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        NYC, NY
