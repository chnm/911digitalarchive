        Discrimination against gays


        On the day of September 11, 2001, the nations heart was broken.  However,
        never before have I felt more bonded with my fellow Americans.  I like many
        Americans, began to realize how much I loved my country.

        However, with the news that gay domestic partners will not be getting
        finacial aid while illegal aliens will has once again made me feel
        disenfranchised.  Through the first few weeks after this terrible tragedy, I
        saw so many good gay people volunteer in downtown Manhattan.  I watched as
        gay residents of Chelsea and the Village cheered the police and firemen.  I
        also learned about many gay men who gave up their lives on that day.  I ask
        that you reconsider.
