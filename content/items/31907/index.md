        Saturday, March 16, 2002 5:25 PM
        9/11 Fund Exclusion of Gays

        To Whom This May Concern,

        I am writing to you out of great disappointment for the decision to limit
        compensation to the gay/lesbian partners of 9/11 victims.

        It is absolutely shameful of you to exclude these domestic partners.  Unborn
        fetuses and illegal aliens are covered in you plan, but not gay/lesbian
        partners??!!  Shame on you!!

        All victims of 9/11 gave and suffered equally.  And still, you have the
        audacity to dole out compensation in an unequal manner.  Discrimination is
        un-American.

        I strongly urge you re-visit your discriminatory policy regarding gay/lesbian
        domestic partners.

        I have already my multiple donations to organizations that benefit the
        victims and families of 9/11.  However, I feel so strongly about your
        discrimination towards gays and lesbians that I will be certain to only give
        future donations to organizations other than your own.  I will be certain to
        place my charity with those who are truly practicing it.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Madison, WI
