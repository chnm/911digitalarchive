        I have just received an email stating that gay partners/spouses/families of

        is still a family unit, even if those same people are all the same sex.  It

        our country and the people who live within its bounds.
        This type of bias and small-mindedness makes us look backward, bigoted and
        unintelligent to the rest of the world.  Why would the rest of the world
        leaders want to work with a nation of backward primitives who have no desire
        to address  anything different and/or forward thinking.  No wonder we are a
        prime target.  We can't even come together as the diverse families that make
        up our nation.  It is the same as bombing another country for not having the
        same religious beliefs as the bomber.   Both Hitler and bin Laden would be
        proud!  Are we not reflecting their tragic closed mindedness by doing the
        same ourselves?  What a shame!  We should know better by now!  Perhaps
        people who feel this way should take a good look at themselves to see where
        their fear comes from, and then take a look at the great leaders and heroes
        of our world. Lifesavers come in every color, as the saying goes...
