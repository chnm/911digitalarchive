        Friday, December 21, 2001 4:35 PM
        Victims Compensation Fund Eligibility

        Gentlemen:

        I have two concerns about the distribution of the
        money to the "victims" of 11 September:

        1.  Whether an individual had life insurance or not
        should have no bearing on the amount they receive.  If
        the premiums were paid with their disposable income,
        you should not penalize that family since they gave up
        the use of those funds for additional protection.  The
        family's present financial situation should not
        influence the amount to be received.

        2.  I have great concern with using taxpayer's money
        for compensation to what is called "undocumented"
        aliens.  If this is just a politically correct way of
        saying "illegal" aliens, I object strongly to giving
        taxpayer's money to someone who has broken US law.  It
        is rewarding bad behavior with our money and should
        not take place.  Do you intend to send money to the
        hijackers' families as well?  They too were in our
        country illegally and should have no monetary benefit
        from illegal actions.

        Thank you for your consideration and remember, this is
        not your money, it is ours (taxpayers).


        Individual Comment
        Falls Church, VA
