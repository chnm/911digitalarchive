        Monday, March 11, 2002 3:19 PM
        Compensating Gays and Lesbians


        Dear Sept. 11 Fund:

        I am writing today to express my dissatisfaction with recent news reports
        concerning the disbursement of survivor's benefits from the 9/11 attacks. I
        sincerely hope that gay and lesbian partners will also be obtaining full
        benefits from the Fund and that news reports to the contrary are false.
        Americans were not asked to help provide money for straight spouses and
        family members of the terrorist attacks. We were asked to contribute
        generously to a fund that would help everyone affected by the evil act that
        will forever be remembered as the Sept. 11 tragedy.

        Please let the Fund organizers know of my discontent and to alert them to
        the fact that while I am not a representative of any organization or group
        of individuals, I no doubt speak for millions of other Americans when I say
        "Rid the 9/11 Fund of political and religious nonsense and compensate each
        and every survivor equally without regard for their sexuality."

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
        Fishers, IN
