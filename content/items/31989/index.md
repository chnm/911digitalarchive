        Compensation

        After reading many articles and comments about the Victim's Compensation Fund

        full of greed and will continue to expect more and more if you are held
        hostage to their requests.  If their family member would have been killed in

        cold-hearted person and have shed many tears over this tragedy; however, I
        request that you stand firm in your plan and not offer any more money or
        benefits to the survivors.  I am sickened by the behavior and requests of
        many of the survivors.
