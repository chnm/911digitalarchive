        Thursday, March 14, 2002 1:24 PM
        Article: 9/11 Fund to Discriminate Against Gays

        Dear Mr.Zwick,
        It has come to my attention that the September11th Victim Compensation
        Fund, of which I am one of the millions of contributors, does not give
        assistance to the vast majority of partners and families of those 911
        victims who were gay men and lesbians.  THis is mind boggling and I
        strongly disapprove of your policy. I am also deeply disturbed that my
        donation turns out to be discriminatory toward a significant group of
        Americans.

        I find your reasoning faulty, and wonder if this is a homophobic bias on
        the part of your department.  Surely, if exceptions can be made for the
        families of undocumented aliens (a move that I approve of heartily), you
        can find a way to face the potential individual lawsuits you fear? For
        God's sake, you are part of the Justice Department, after all!

        By way of information, I have copied the electronic article where I've
        learned about this issue. If it is in error, I would appreciate your
        response and send you, ain advance, my apologies. I am not one to spread
        incorrect information.

        Thank you for your time,

        Individual Comment
        Port Townsend, Washington
