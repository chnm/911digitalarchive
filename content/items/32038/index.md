        Wednesday, March 13, 2002 10:05 PM
        protest treatment of gays/lesbians


        At a time of healing and a time of broad support for all victims of the
        atrocious attacks on 9/11...it is inconceivable to me that you would not
        extend your support to the partners of gays and lesbians that lost their
        lives in the twin towers and pentagon and aboard the planes.  To
        discriminate nationally against such persons because their particular
        state might not yet recognize their relationships while at the same time
        recognizing illegal aliens is so counterproductive to be unbelieveable.
        I urge you to reconsider. I do not think that you will get an
        overwhelming response from members of the gay and lesbian community.
        But that should not stop you from soliciting them as equal in suffering
        before the law. Shame on you for ignoring these people.
