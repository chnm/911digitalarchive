        Saturday, January 26, 2002 2:41 PM

        I have the greatest sympathy for the losses suffered by those whose loved
        ones died on 9/11.  However, I believe that the $1.63 million offered by the
        federal fund, which is TAX FREE, is a generous offer and one which the
        federal government has no obligation to make.  I have heard the complaints
        about the unfairness of deducting pensions, life insurance policies, etc.,
        but each family will still end up with $1.63 million even with these
        deductions, albeit not necessairly from the federal government in full or
        even in part.  Let's not also forget the multi-millions of dollars collected
        by various charities which will be distributed to these families and the
        fact that President Bush has excused these survivors from paying federal
        taxes on any income gotten in 2001 and 2002.  If any family believes they
        have been treated unfairly, or that they are being used as scapegoats to
        save the airlines,  they are always free to take their chances with a
        lawsuit.  I hope that reason will prevail, and that the emotionalism of the
        situation will not be exploited.

        Individual Comment
        Dix Hills, NY
