        Saturday, January 12, 2002 1:08 PM
        Fairness

        I am not convinced that the Victim Compensation Fund is an appropriate
        function of our federal government, but I do recognize the validity of
        arguments on either side of the issue. Since the proponents have apparently
        already prevailed, I can accept  the inevitability of the program.
        What I cannot accept as reasonable or equitable are the provisions to reduce
        beneficiary payments by the amount of existing life insurance and/or
        retirement plan benefits received. Those victims who loved their families and
        had the character to buy adequate life insurance, and who had the discipline
        to forego present wants in order to contribute to retirement plans for their
        own and their families' future comfort, are being dishonored when their
        government disregards those prior commendable traits and in fact penalizes
        their families because their deceased providers did the right thing!
        I urge you to remove the proposal to reduce Fund benefit payments by life
        insurance and retirement proceeds available to beneficiaries. That would be
        doing the right thing!
        Thank you for your attention and consideration.

        Individual Comment
        Houston, TX
