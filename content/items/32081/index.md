        Sent: Monday, March 11, 2002 8:48 AM

        I am extremely disturbed by Mr. Feinberg's comments on Meet the Press, 3/10, in which he
        says that benefits for gay and lesbian partners of victims of 9/11 will be based on their
        respective state laws regarding domestic partner benefits.
        Such a stand makes these people victims a second time and serves only to further isolate and
        discriminate against the gay community. Having made donations to two different funds, I am
        very disappointed by this news.
        There should be no distinction here. If benefits are to be extended to illegal aliens, and I'm glad
        they are, but suspicious of Ashcroft's promise of no recrimination, then surely we should offer
        these same benefits to survivors and partners of those gay individuals who gave their lives on
        9/11.
        Please reconsider this very unfair and discriminatory position.
        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
