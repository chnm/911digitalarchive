        "Victim Payment" Plans


        You have no justification for not accomodating partners, be they
        of the same or of differing sexes, just because of a sexual
        orientation or lack of paper document denoting their dedication
        to each other.

        Normally federal money dispensers do so without relying on state
        laws, many based on religious purposes, in order to avoid both
        electoral and judicial confrontations. Blaming the lack of "gay
        benefits" on the lack of state support is, in reality, blaming
        the Bible (et al) and the religious origins of the laws in those
        states.

        Our United States was founded on a body of law. Part of that law
        proclaims the separation of Federal and State governments,
        venues, powers, and so forth. You are disbursing FEDERAL FUNDS,
        not drawn from State budgets; therefore, under Federal Law,
        there can be no sensible, truthfull grounds for denial -- only
        the 'play politics' denial that loses elections. People are
        notorious for not voting for people who kick dogs, animals or
        those perceived to be of a different class.

        Your attention is drawn to the commentary "9/11 Fund To
        Discriminate Against Gays" Commentary, by             , wherein "Kenneth Feinberg, the head of the September 11th Victim
        Compensation Fund (a fund created by Congress and run by the
        Department of Justice)," claims the applicable states' laws are
        being observed. Oh? Since when has our federal government seen
        fit to parallel state laws?

        If Mr. Feinberg is to be taken seriously, then the DOJ will stop
        its harassment of Oregon doctors who are obeying state law by
        providing for painless suicide.  If Mr. Feinberg is to be taken
        seriously, then the DOJ will stop hasseling marajuana users in
        the 11 or so states where MJ is legal medicine.

        On the other hand, if Mr. Feinberg is LYING, then the DOJ
        spokesperson is saying "We will play God. We may deny there is a
        God, daily except Sunday mornings; but that's okay, we'll play
        God's Hand as we see fit."

        The next step, of course, is the ballot box; if the GOP allows
        this to proceed, they will be handing both House and Senate
        msajorities to the Democrats on a gold platter. It's very
        difficult to vote for people who oppose one's own standards.

        Individual Comment
        Drain, Oregon
