        Wednesday, March 13, 2002 1:51 PM
        Exclusion of Gays from Benefits


        Dear Mr. Feinberg:

        I am disgusted that you have effectively excluded the survivors of gay and
        lesbian Americans who died on 9/11 from receiving benefits due them from the
        9/11 Compensation Fund.

        It is completely unacceptable for you to defer to state law on this issue.
        This is not a state issue.  It is a federal issue.  You are fully aware that
        only two states have laws that recognize gay partnerships. Your deferral to
        state law is clearly nothing more than a thin fabrication to avoid
        responsibility for your morally sadistic decision to dishonor the survivors
        of gay men and women.

        Your decision brings to mind too clearly the decision of some of the
        commanders of the allied troops which liberated the concentration camps
        after the Nazi collapse.  They ordered that gay concentration camp victims
        be held in the camps - among the smoldering and rotting dead - because under
        German law their homosexuality made them common criminals.   That decision
        was fairly quickly reversed,  and I hope you will soon reverse yours.

        Yours Sincerely

        Individual Comment
        San Diego CA
