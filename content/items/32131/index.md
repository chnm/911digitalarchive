        Monday, January 28, 2002 11:29 AM

        I am deeply saddened and disturbed by the backlash of comments about victims
        compensation. It seems as though we are giving the terrorists yet another
        victory with all this bickering back and forth.


        While it is true that all of us at one time or another have experienced loss
        of loved ones, I for one, would not want to walk in the shoes of any of
        those who lost loved ones or had family members injured in the attacks. The
        grief and anger would be more than I could bear. No price is high enough to
        replace the lives of those lost, or mend the shattered lives of those
        injured.



        However, the opportunity afforded by this site as a forum for free speech
        serves as a reminder for me, that our way of life and basic freedoms are
        something the terrorists can never take from us. For the sake of our
        country, I will continue to pray that an equitable solution can be arrived
        at for all concerned.


        My thanks to the Justice Department for allowing all Americans to be heard.
        God Bless America.



        Individual Comment
