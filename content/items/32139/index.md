        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 11:23 AM
        Sept. 11 relief

        To whom it may concern:

        As an American, I feel strongly that all individuals who died and
        their families deserve equitable support from our country.  Our
        highest ideals are "Liberty and Justice for all"  In this crisis all
        Americans have stepped forward to help others.  We must stand to our
        highest ideals in this time of national crisis and compensate
        families of gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgendered people in the
        same manner we would others.  We have many models for how this can be
        done with same sex partner insurance present in many progressive
        companies today.  In addition we must support the families of
        undocumented immigrants in the same way.  Our economy has benefited
        from their contribution and their families will feel the loss of
        their presence.

        In the plan that was crashed in Pennsylvania by courageous Americans
        seeking to protect the capital, it was even a gay man that was
        involved in overtaking the hijackers.

        As an American who believes in our ideals, I urge all of you involved
        with this compensation effort to remember the core values that make
        our country what it is.

        Respectfully,

        Individual Comment
