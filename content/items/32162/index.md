        To:  Kenneth L. Zwick, Director

        Dear Sir:

        The people who died on September 11 all died equally, male and female, young
        and old, gay and straight.  The killers didn't discriminate and neither
        should We, The People, discriminate in denying benefits and compensation to
        the survivors of these victims.  A same-gender partner is still a partner.  A
        child of a homosexual is still a child.  Their grief is no less  than the
        grief of survivors of other classes of victims, their problems are no
        smaller, the obstacles they will have to overcome are no easier.
        Please--don't compound the crimes of the suicide bombers by treating any of
        their victims as less than worthy than others.

        Individual Comment
        Chicago, IL
