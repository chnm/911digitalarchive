        Dear Sir or Madam:



         I write to thank you for your comments concerning the September 11
        Victim Compensation Fund of 2001 (the "Fund").



         As you know, Congress passed the legislation that created the Fund
        and gave the Department of Justice, acting through a Special Master
        appointed by the Attorney General, the responsibility for drafting
        regulations that will govern how this important program operates.  The
        Department and I are committed to creating a program that is an efficient,
        simple and fair method to compensate eligible individuals.  Your comments
        are a crucial step in this process.  They help to ensure that our decisions
        are well-informed and are based on the valuable input of those most
        affected.



         Your comments have been entered in the official record.  I am
        grateful for the opportunity to consider your views.



               Sincerely,

               Kenneth R. Feinberg

               Special Master
