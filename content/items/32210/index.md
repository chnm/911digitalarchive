        Wednesday, March 20, 2002 9:50 PM
        Discriminiation is Un-American

        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director
        Office of Management Programs
        Civil Division
        U.S.Department of Justice

        Mr. Zwick:

        As countless Americans continue to recover from the impact of September 11, I
        join in protesting the fact that gay and lesbian partners of those who died
        are not automatically going to receive the same benefits as heterosexual
        spouses. As a gay man, I find it unacceptable that gay-Americans are not
        entitled to the same treatment under the Victim Compensation Fund as any
        others who lost their lives that day.

        Gay and Lesbian heroes of that day, both known and unknown, leave behind life
        partners who have the same emotional and financial needs as other Americans.
        I urge that changes be made to assure equity for all Americans who died that
        morning. It is said often that life will never be the same. That is so, and
        let us not perpetuate discrimination that was irrational before September
        11th and remains irrational today.

        Thank you,

        Individual Comment
        Branchville, NJ
