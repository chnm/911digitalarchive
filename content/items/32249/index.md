        Thursday, December 06, 2001 11:17 AM
        FW: Sept. 11th Victim Compensation Fund

         To:
         Kenneth L. Zwick
          Director of Management Programs, Civil Division


         Dear Mr. Zwick,

         This letter comes in reference to the Department's request for public
        comments regarding the regulations for implementing and distributing the
         "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."  I am asking that you and your
         department draft regulations that will treat  ALL victims of this attack
         with fairness and honesty, including domestic partners and the
         non-biological children of these gay and lesbian couples.

         Please help send a message to those that would seek to further separate
         and divide us as a nation and a free people that we
         are a great nation because of our diversity not in spite of it.  Surely,
        the losses suffered by the families of gay and lesbian victims are equal
         to those suffered by other families and should be compensated accordingly.
         On a day when so many innocent people lost their lives, let us say to
         those people, we honor your life and your memory and the lives you left
         behind.

         God bless you with the task ahead and God bless the World.

         Best and kindest regards,

         Individual Comment
         NY NY
