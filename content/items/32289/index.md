        To whom it may concern:

        I am extremely dismayed and angered by remarks made by Kenneth Feinberg on
        "Meet the Press" that benefits of the September 11th Victim Compensation Fund
        will not extend to the partners of gay and lesbian victims.  This is even
        more angering when families of illegal aliens will be eligible to receive
        benefits.

        Mr. Feinberg states that he cannot go against state laws or regulations
        concerning same sex partners, yet it's no problem to bend the rules in the
        case of illegal imigrants.

        I disagree strongly with this decision by the Department of Justice to
        restrict eligiblity of same sex partners to the benefits of the September
        11th Victim Compensation Fund.  I demand that this restriction be eliminated,
        and that same sex partners be made eligible for these benenfits.

        Don't you agree that all law abiding, tax paying citizens of the United
        States should have, at the very least, the same rights and benefits as
        illegal aliens?

        I'm sorry that I contributed to this fund.

        Sincerely,



        Individual Comment
        New York, NY
