        Wednesday, November 07, 2001 7:44 PM
        Victims Fund comment

        Regarding the dispensing of public (tax monies)  money to victims of any
        disaster, be it a terrorist attack, or any other disaster, I feel much
        attention should be paid to the victims REAL and JUSTIFIED need for such
        assistance. I feel there are many "professional  victiims", people who
        ALWAYS seem to be unable to get out of the way of disasters and make no
        effort to take care of themselves. There are also always quite wealthy folks
        who seem to get wealthier by way  of government giveaway programs. I feel
        the government should always make sure of the legitimate need, first.
            The governments giveaway  programs should always, also, take into
        consideration any funds these supposed victims may  have received from other
        sources. Sources such as any of the many organizations that use gift money,
        begged from givers,  supposedly to help victims of disasters.

        Individual Comment
        Jefferson City, MO
