        Dear Director Zwick,

        I am writing to you today to tell you how outraged I am that partners or children of gay men and women who died as a result of the terror attacks on September 11 are not entitled to the same benefits as heterosexuals.

After all, we are ALL Americans, race, gender, or sexual orientation should not matter.  As a country, we should do nothing less.  As a society we can do nothing less.

        Thank you for your time.

        Sincerely,
