        Sunday, January 27, 2002 4:25 PM
        Compensation Fund

        I am so grateful for this opportunity to contact you. First I must tell you how deeply grieved my heart has been and still is for your loss and now the struggles to move on. It seems that the act of terroism that led to this is beyond human comprehension. I pray for all of you, though I do not know your names. God does.
        Secondly, I can't believe that you should have the difficulties you are encountering while trying to get money to survive this loss. My husband and I sent money to the Red Cross, thinking it would be immediately distributed to you. How shocked and disappointed we were to find that they were holding it and had not given as we felt they should. We contacted them to let them know how we felt but needless to say, have never received a reply. The money is yours and should be given with no strings attached. Whatever you get over and above that does not bring back your loved one or ease your pain but it is yours. You should not have to battle this while you are trying to grieve over your loss, a senseless loss.
        My prayers are with you. May God bless you during this time. I know He is with you daily, despite the difficulties.
        In Christian love,

        Individual Comment
