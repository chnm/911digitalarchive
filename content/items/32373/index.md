        Compensation

        The criticism is going to continue as long as the families of the victims
        think they "deserve" this money.  This money is because we want to help
        families in need.  My husband was killed by a drunk behind the wheel of a
        car. There are millions of widows and widowers like myself that know EXACTLY
        how hard it is to on and to raise our children in these circumstances.  I
        contributed what I could because I wanted to help other families in their
        time of need.  I did not give this money to set up all these peoples
        finances for life!  Furthermore, I do not agree that my taxes should take
        care of these people for life.   The difference between their loss and my
        loss is what?  The woman complaining about how little she will get because
        she has 1.4 million in insurance should thank her lucky stars she has that
        and not take money from the fund set up for the people that are in need of
        help.

        They should be ASHAMED!

        Individual Comment
