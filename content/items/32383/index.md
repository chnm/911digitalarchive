      text: |-
        Monday, November 12, 2001 1:31 AM
        sep 11

        First off I need to say that the people providing you comments are not
        fully aware of what is REALLY happening with their heartfelt  donations.  I
        know personally, because I lost my MOM in the tragedy.  People see a figure
        of $1 billion donated and think....OK 1 billion divided by 5000 equals 200k
        per family.  Seems like we have been taken care of.....right???  WRONG!!!!
        I would like to let the pubic and the DOJ know that I have received a total
        of 9,000 from the Red Cross to help pay ALL of my Mother's expenses.  So
        PLEASE don't think we are walking away millionaires.  These charities need
        to be investigated to see what they really do with the extremely generous
        donations made by warm and caring individuals.  And let me also mention
        that to ask the opinion of people who have no idea what our family and the
        thousands of families like us, have personally gone through, is
        irresponsible.  They do not have the slightest idea what this has done to
        us.  My sister had to be hospitalized for several weeks due to this
        tragedy.  She lost 25 lbs and was vomiting and passing blood.  My other
        sister left a 125k and year job because she cannot even function anymore.
        These people who are making these opinions on MY life and what I am
        entitled to, should try for one millisecond to live a day in my life since
        September 11th.  Maybe they can try and think of their own precious
        mothers, who nurtured and cared and raised them, being maliciously cut into
        pieces and left amid the wreckage that once was the WTC.  Then have that
        compounded by knowing that what is left of her may very well wind up in a
        DUMP.  Think of THAT before you pass judgment on me and what I have to go
        through on a daily basis!  NO ONE BUT THE FAMILIES OF THE VICTIMS IN THIS
        TRAGEDY CAN PROVIDE YOU WITH ADEQUATE UNDERSTANDING OF WHAT IS
        APPROPRIATE!!  In addition I had heard that distribution of the monies from
        this fund may be linked to future earnings on the individual if they would
        have reached retirement age.  Well my mom was 62 years old and only had
        three more years to work.  Does that mean that her life is worth less than
        some 30 year old?  My mother was the heart and sole of my family and our
        hearts have been ripped to shreds.  Do not judge the distribution on age.
        We all loved these people the same.  We all feel the same pain.

        May God Bless all the victims

        Individual Comment
