        Thursday, November 08, 2001 9:15 AM
        Request for compensation

        As I'm sure you are overwhelmed with requests, I will
        keep my letter succinct.

        I didn't loss a job or a loved one.  I lived in
        Battery Park City.  I lost walking my dog and meeting
        with my friends every morning.  I lost feeling safe at
        any hour.  I lost sitting on the Esplanade.  I lost
        being able to walk across the street and jump on the
        subway to any other part of Manhattan. I lost walking
        to work.  I lost my life.

        A job can be replaced, you can't replace a
        neighborhood.  I am a victim of the World Trade Center
        Disaster and as deserving of relief as any other
        victim.  The American people amazed the world with
        their outpouring of generosity.  My loss is as
        legitimate as the people who are now widows and
        orphans and unemployed due to September 11.

        Thank you for your time and consideration.

        Individual Comment
