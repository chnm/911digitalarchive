        Gay and Lesbian Families must be compensated!

        To whom it may concern,

            This is a moment when those proclaiming to believe in "compassionate
        conservatism" can show what they mean by that.

            Just as many heterosexual families were destroyed by the events on
        9/11, so were many gay and lesbian families.

            Let's show ourselves to be human and recognize that all families
        that were affected should be treated equally:  gay and lesbian families
        (recognized by the fact that they are same-sex couples who live at the
        same address, share financial responsibilities, and name each other as
        beneficiaries in wills and other legal documents) deserve the same
        compensation as others who were legally married and lost their spouse.
        The gay and lesbian families would have established the bond of marriage
        if it had been afforded them.

            Certain religious judgments aside, isn't it just humane to care
        equally about those who lost the person they cared for most in the
        world?

        Sincerely,

        --

        Individual Comment
        San Mateo, CA
