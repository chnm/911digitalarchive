        To Whom It May Concern,

        I have two comments to point out:

        (1) My father and mother raised my brother (who was a victim of 911) for most
        of his life.  My brother had a wife and 2 kids.  However, it would be unjust,
        in my opinion, if my parents and immediate family members are not awarded a
        non-economic benefit.  Please make sure that they are provided something.  My
        parents, me and my sister will never be able to replace my brother.  My
        parents are most devastated and are barely able to work. My brother would
        have wanted something for his parents.

        (2) Deduction of life insurance should not be part of this formula.  My
        brother scraped his nickels and dimes to pay for insurance, and that should
        be his.  Deducting this makes the point of life insurance senseless.

        Thank you for taking this opportunity to review my comments, and please reply
        to my email address with your feedback.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
