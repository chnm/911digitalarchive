        Wednesday, March 13, 2002 9:41 AM
        Compensation for Gay/Lesbian Survivors


        I cannot believe this fund will purposefully overlook gay/lesbian survivors
        because the state in which they reside may/may not recognize their
        relationships.  I for one am very angry over this issue.  I live in a state
        (Georgia) that doesn't recognize my relationship with my partner of 20+ years.
        If something happened to me in that manner, I would want him to receive the
        same compensation as all the other families.

        This situation is the very reason I did not contribute to the fund.  As much as
        I grieved for everyone, I knew gays/lesbians would be discriminated against.
        Therefore, I gave to an organization that would help people like myself.

        I'm ashamed of Mr. Feinberg's comments on Meet the Press.  Someone should have
        run a plane into him.

        Individual Comment
        Decatur, Ga
