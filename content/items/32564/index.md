        Kenneth Zwick, Dir
        Office of Mgt Progs

        Dec 5, 2001

        Dear Director Zwick:

        We were alerted tonite through our denomination, United Fellowship of
        Metropolitan Community Churches, that compensation is available to families
        who lost loved ones in the tragedy of Sept 11.

        I am writing to appeal to you to please consider carefully the needs of
        those in non-traditional relationships such as same sex unions either as
        holy unions, legal civil unions in some states and life partnered people of
        the same gender.

        Many of these relationships can be confirmed using resources of the
        denomination or of the local MCC churches and I am asking you make this
        consideration because I know many such relationships that are no less loving
        and beneficial to our communities and our nation as the more traditional
        ones you might think of.

        I appreciate you time and sensitive attention to this matter and do call or
        write if I can be of any help to you or direct you to those who will help.
        We are behind your efforts to compensate in some small way the undefinable
        and unknowable loss of all people since the tragedy.

        Kind regards,

        Individual Comment
        Greenville SC
