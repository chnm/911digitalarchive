        Compensation to the loved one of Gay Victims

        As a Lesbian who has been in a committed, monogamous relationship for ten
        years I am outraged that the partners of the gay victims of the 9/11 tragedy
        will not be offered the same compensation as the families of heterosexual
        victims. Kenneth Feinberg's reasoning is ludicrous. If gays and lesbians are
        left out of his program to the extent that their own states don't include
        them, how does he justify offering benefits to illegal aliens who are being
        coaxed into coming forward to make claims by an assurance from the Attorney
        General of the US that they will not be penalized and neither will their
        employers. Am I missing something or is Feinberg being selective about the
        laws that will be ignored and those that will be enforced? Personally I have
        no problem with illegal aliens being compensated. They, like all others
        including gay and lesbians, who have had to deal with the gut wrenching agony
        of losing their loved ones, deserve it. My problem is with the homophobia and
        hypocrisy of our government officials.




        Individual Comment
        Royal Oak, Michigan
