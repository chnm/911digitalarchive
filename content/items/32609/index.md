        January 3, 2002


        Re: World Trade Center Disaster

        Dear Honorable Representatives:

             This office represents one of the victims of the United Flight 93.

             I note that regulations were published by Special Master Kenneth Feinberg and the
        Department of
        Justice with regard to compensating victims of the World Trade Center disaster. I believe that the
        regulations
        are totally inadequate in that they place arbitrary limits on both economic and non-economic loss.
        Additionally,
        they establish predetermined, presumptive guidelines that will be utilized to make awards. In
        essence this means
        that the award has been predetermined without listening to any evidence whatsoever. I ask that
        you contact
        Kenneth L. Zwick, Director of the Office of Management Programs, Civil Division, at the United
        States
        Department of Justice and express your dissatisfaction with these regulations.

             Thank you for your courtesy and cooperation.

                                           Very truly yours,

                                           Individual Comment
        Walden, NY
