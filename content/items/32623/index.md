        Monday, March 11, 2002 12:43 PM
        How dare you!


        Mr. Feinberg:

        After reading the Meet the Press March 10 transcript of the comments you
        made, I can only state;  how dare you use the 9/11 tragedy to discriminate
        against gay and lesbian Americans!

        It simply amazes me that you are willing to turn the other cheek, despite the
        direct violation of  Federal and State laws against illegal immigration, to
        allow the distribution of funds to illegal aliens whose loved ones perished
        on 9/11-- but,  you'll blatantly discriminate against lawful citizens of the
        United States because that are nothing more than the same-sex partners of
        those lost!  How outrageous!

        "Otherwise, Tim, I would find myself getting sued in every state by people
        claiming that I'm not following how the state distributes money.  I can't get
        into that local battle. I've got to rely on state law."

        Do you honestly think that some State that condones
        discrimination against gays will sue you if you disburse funds to the
        surviving same-sex partners of those killed on 9/11?  Have you completely
        LOST YOUR MIND?  What state is going to initiate a lawsuit post
         and post 9/11 to stop the humanitarian relief to surviving same-sex
        partners?

        Obviously, given your stupidity, prejudice, intolerance and hate your
        immediate resignation is warranted!  How dare you!

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
