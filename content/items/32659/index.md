        Re: Victims' Compensation Fund Final Rule

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        The federal government's decision to give funds to illegal aliens and not to
        all American citizens can only be explained by its leadership's
        fundamentalism and hate.  The decision certainly isn't very American.  In
        many ways I have been impressed with President Bush's and the country's
        response to the terrorist attack.  However, this one act of fundamentalism
        and hate by our own government in blatant disregard for the lives of
        upstanding and even heroric American citizens overshadows anything good that
        the country as done since September 11.

        I am sad that our government has not risen to the occassion.  While I have
        faith in most Americans, my faith in her leadership has been put to rest by
        this one act of callousness by the fundamentalist regime that was elected to
        uphold her ideals.  There is no doubt that this rule determining who is
        eligible for compensation is motivated by hate.  If the Executive branch
        truley were worried about upholding the law, it would not ignore all
        immigration laws or state laws that might apply.

        My only hope is that the country will recognize the value of all her
        citizens.  If she does, then the current fundamentalist regime will be
        limited.  We will be lucky if the division and damage done by the current
        leadership can be overcome by a more compassionate and just  President in
        the future.

        Respectfully submitted,

        Individual Comment
        Seattle, WA
