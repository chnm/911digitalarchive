        Monday, December 10, 2001 10:09 PM
        September 11 Victim Compensation Fund

        Thank you for your work in setting up the September 11 Victim
        Compensation Fund.  It would be a mistake to focus all of our energy on
        bringing those responsible to justice and not also do everything
        possible to ease the suffering of the victims so I really appreciate
        your work.

        I understand that part of your job is to establish regulations governing
        the "September 11 Victim Compensation Fund."  I know that this will be a
        complex task but I hope you will include an important consideration as
        you do your work.  Please make sure that compensation is available to
        all surviving families of those killed, including the committed partners
        and non-biological children of gay and lesbian victims.

        Both Islamic and Christian fundamentalists will try to tell you that
        God's love is conditional and that they (the fundamentalists) are the
        only ones who really understand those conditions.  Islamic
        fundamentalists created tremendous suffering on September 11.  Please
        don't let fundamentalists of any persuasion convince you to treat the
        suffering of one group differently than the suffering of another.
        Please make sure your regulations do not compound the suffering of gay
        and lesbian families.  Thank you from a straight, Christian voter from a
        republican district in Kansas.

        Individual Comment
        Wichita, KS
