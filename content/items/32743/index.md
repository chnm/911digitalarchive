        ----- Original Message -----


        I've written so much about this that I don't even know where to go or where to begin.  However reduntant and obviously clear it is to me and the other families involved it is still being misreported and misrepresented.  It is perhaps true that you are only a pawn in the larger shceme of things, that your hands are tied by the federal gov't offices including the president's, budget and finance, and the atty general.  who should we really be writting to or calling?

        Here it goes, once again I will state what I know to be as the facts.
        1. My husband was murdered!
        2. Numerous reports and articles have been put out (but no one in the general public
            seems to be reading this) stating that the gov't ignored and ignored and ignored
            warning after warning regarding the threat of terrorist attacks in our country.
            Ignored the information we had about Al Queda and the Taliban, certainly never
            shared vital information with the American public.  Clinton was too busy with his
            sexual exploits, impeachment, and keeping the economy going as it was for his
            approval rating.  The incoming Pres. Bush did not seem to prioritize this issue either
            but he was to my understanding working on some of the issues. It was just too
            little too late - but mostly because of what was not done during eight years of the
            Clinton administration.
            -since the '93 bombing, holes that were supposed to have been closed up in
            the immigration system never were.
            -Airline security was suppposed to have been
            improved according to a proposal by VP Gore but the changes were never put into
            effect (gee what a surprise especially given that they still cannnot manage proper
            security even now after Sept.11)
            -Two of the terrorist were on a known terrorist list yet they came
            in through INS (how?) and if the information had been properly shared with the
            airlines at least these men would have been stopped - the whole plan may have been
            stopped.
           -Visas had expired on 3 of the terrorists but of course the INS never checks
            that.
           -Was it two or three hijackers who came in on student visas and never attended
            any education institution, but again of course why would the INS be respnsible for
            checking that?
           -Moussoui the 20th hijacker was caught befor 9/11 and  even with the
            reason there was to further investigate him it was not done (an issue of political
            profiling feared? Too much beauracracy to get through? total incompetnece? - Even
            the man who ran the fight school alerted the FBI that a plane loaded with fuel could
            be used as a bomb!).
           -The US had warnings in the spring about a terrorist attack being
            planned.  Was there anything done about that?  I don't think so.
           - My understanding is that NORAD was alerted to hijacked planes initially at 8:10AM,
             and then again at 8:40 before the first plane hit the WTC but the F15 bomber did
             not take off until after the first building was hit.
           -Other screw ups include the miscommunication at the site itself.
            My husband was in building 2.  It should have been evacuated.  a man I know told me
            that as he ran to the towers to find his wife, he was told be a NYPD officer on the
            street to get away from the area, "go up Braodway, and get out of here as fast as
            you can, another plane is on the way" he was told.  This while my husband and
            countless others were told the building was secure and to stay put, or for some, to
            go back up to their offices after making it down.
            ARE YOU SICK YET? I know I am!
            IS THERE ANYONE WHO GETS THIS?
            HOW MUCH MORE DOES THERE HAVE TO BE BEFORE WE ARE HEARD AND TREATED
            THE WAY WE SHOULD BE!  AS THE SURVIVORS OF MURDERED INNOCENT VICTIMS
            WHOM THE GOVERNMENT FAILED TO PROTECT!
            Maybe you need more.  Maybe you need to be reminded that the victims families of
            Lockerbie received a minimum of $2 million.  oh yes, they were able to sue because
            the gov't did not screw them by so severely restricting the families ability to sue the
            airlines.  You know that is what you did to us.  You know the compensation fund was
            approved as the alternative, that when approved there was upwards of 6,000 victims
            families factored into the plan and there were enough billions to cover all those
            victims of which we now know is half that amount.
        3. My husband was 35 years old, had a job as a currency trader that paid $60,000
            per year.  I worked part time.  We were a happy family. we loved caring for our
           twin boys 2 years old, and decided to have a third.  This baby is expected in
           March '02.  My husband cared for our family responsibly and took out life insurance.
           Because of his years of work, he paid into the social security system that now pays
           my children "survivors benefits" - who the hell do you think wants to be a recepient
           of that.  My husband also put the maximum amount into his 401k so we could have
           a good retirement package and enjoy ourselves in the years to come.  So all of
           that is to be deducted from the initial figure of around $1.4 - 1.5 million.  Shall I
           mention the other deductions from workman's Compensation.  Just between life
           insurance and the years my children are entitled to the "survivors benefits" that we
           paid into wipes out more than half of that so called "award".  BLOOD MONEY is what I
           prefer to call it.
        4. My life has been ruined.  My babies are all going to grow up without their father.  MY
           life who I shared with my best friend, companion, my husband, has been ripped from
           me.  This is what I live with the rest of my life, and the knowledge that this could
           have been avoided, that my husband and countless others didn't have to die.
           should I go back to work now.  Would my salary of $28,000 pay the mortgage, bills,
           the live in that I will now have to get with or without going back to work.  Should I
           simply or magicallly find myself a new job that pays what our combined salaries were?
           Oh wait though, my cost of living has gone up.  I suppose I should find a job that
           pays even more then what we were taking in.  See now, I need to have work done in
           the basement so the live in will have a place to live, then of course I have to pay a
           live in, I also have to pay for all the services my husband performed - the lawn,
           house repairs, etc.  Maybe I shouldn't get a live in you say.  Well then you
           come to my house at night when &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp gets sick in my bed and as I am taking
           care of him, &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp falls in the hallway.  So there I am with two - 2 year olds crying,
           one getting sick and crying, I need to wash him down, change the sheets, hold
           &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp - now add an infant into the picture and you assess whether you can
           handle that on your own.  Should I also mention that my non magical job did not give
           me health insurance for part time employment.  Cobra payments will cost me near
           $900 per month.  Of course many of the families are hoping for some health coverage
           to come through for us but these are the figures many are now looking at.  Even
           if I went back to work full time approx. $250 per pay check would be taken out for
           the cost of familly health insurance.  A paycheck that still would not be enough to
           pay for all the existing bill and all the extras I now need to cover!  Maybe I should
           just live off my life insurance until it's all gone.  Oh but that's not really a good plan
           when you have the responsibility of children or retirement in 25 years and there is
           no longer contributions to a 401k.  Oh yes I can speak of retirement because one
           day when my children are older I will I am sure go back to work.  But I still don't
           think there is a magical job out there for me do you?  maybe I should just bite the
           bullet suffer work a few jobs, leave my kids often.  It's what I would have to do
           if &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp died of a heart attack isn't it?  Oh but wait - &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp didn't have to die if
           everyone out there had done their jobs.  Even if half the people had done their
           jobs!

        Did I make myself heard to anyone out there?  If you are the wrong person to make these points to please foward this onto the the proper contacts.
        I don't want to make points anymore.  I just want everyone to get the information out there, for the gov't and the airlines to take responsibility for what was allowed to happen and what is continuing to happen.  Stop victimizing us.  IF YOU COULD GIVE ME MY LIFE BACK YOU COULD KEEP THE DAMN MONEY!  Is there anyone who can do that? It would save me and my children all the pain in the world.
