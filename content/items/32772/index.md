        Friday, November 30, 2001 11:03 PM


        Dear Attorney General Ashcroft,

        The deaths on September 11th were the result of an attack on America.
        Please do not discriminate against the unmarried partners of September 11th
        victims.  All unmarried partners, gay or straight, young and old and their
        dependents should be eligible for compensation.

        The vast majority of western civilization including religious institutions
        used to believe that women and African Americans should not be afforded
        equal rights.  It's hard to believe that it was ever this way, yet the same
        type of rigid thinking discriminates against unmarried and homosexual
        people today.

        I know that this does not make your job any easier, nor does it make it
        easy to administer funds to survivors of victims, and while fairness often
        comes with a price, fairness also pays greater social dividends over time.
        It would be hard to imagine women and African-Americans being treated today
        as they had been at the turn of the century.  Society at large takes a long
        time to change course and overcome prejudice.  Yet wise people with
        perspective have to help chart that course, making even small changes that
        become significant change over time.

        I have faith in your compassion for all people who justly deserve
        assistance, and in your dedication to not discriminate on the basis of
        religiously-oriented opinion or upon sexual orientation.

        Individual Comment
