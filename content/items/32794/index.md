        Thursday, December 20, 2001 11:07 AM


        Dear Director Zwick:

        I am writing to support the position of the National Gay and Lesbian Task
        Force in their letter to you dated November 21, 2001 regarding disbursement
        of monies from the September 11 Fund. I agree with the need for the
        Department of Justice to Department to "include among relatives eligible for
        compensation those who lost their life partners or de facto parents or
        children, regardless of sexual orientation and marital status." The horrific
        loss of loved ones to a random terrorist act should not be compounded by our
        own government denying survivors whatever benefits may be available simply
        because we fail to have laws to protect all of our citizens. Thank you for
        your attention to this matter.

        Very truly yours,
        Individual Comment
        Cumming, GA
