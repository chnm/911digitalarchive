        Tuesday, January 22, 2002 1:01 PM
        Comments on Sept. 11 Victims Fund

        I am afraid I am a contrarian voice regarding compensation for victims
        of Sept. 11th.

        My compassion goes out to the victims, but I do not think it is the role
        of the U.S. government to assume liability. Is the death of someone in
        the Twin Towers any different from say the child killed in Oakland,
        California, last week when an illegal alien ploughed into a group of
        children in a crosswalk?

        To me both are acts of terrorism resulting from the U.S. government
        failing to enforce our borders.

        With regards to compensation for illegal immigrant victims of Sept. 11th
        I find it offensive to even consider it. Why should I as an American be
        rewarding - even in their death - lawbreakers? Indeed the term victim is
        misapplied here as Americans are the victims of the illegal acts of the
        aliens.

        Generosity is a noble impulse. But it must be tempered keeping in mind
        the consequences.

        I think that zero interest loans based on need are a proper response to
        legal victims.

        Individual Comment
        El Cerrito, CA
