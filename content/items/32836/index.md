        Un-American Treatment of Same Sex Partners

        Dear Sir or Madame:
           If September 11 has taught us anything, it's that our
        patriotism and love of country transcend our differences and
        unite us all.  We were told that September 11 was the day
        that "hyphenated-Americans" were no more, and rather than
        being Italian-Americans, African-Americans or gay-Americans,
        we were now simply Americans, all of us together as one
        American family.  Apparently, that family just got a divorce.

        The hyphens seem not to matter when our government wants to
        use our heroism to inspire others, but when it comes time to
        actually aid the families of those heroes, the hyphens come
        back with a vengeance, and it seems that some American heroes
        are now more equal than others.

        EVEN FETUSES AND ILLEGAL ALIENS ARE COVERED!!!

        Even fetuses and illegal aliens will be receiving benefits
        under this problem - heck, you've even promised to overlook
        federal law and NOT arrest and kick out the families of
        illegal aliens, nor will they punish American companies that
        hired illegals in violation of federal labor laws.

        But when  it comes to gay and lesbian heroes of the day - &nbsp who helped bring down the flight in Pennsylvania, NY Fire
        &nbsp who died in the line of
        duty when the first tower fell, and &nbsp who was
        &nbsp of the plane that crashed into the &nbsp (there
        are many more) - we are told the law is the law and no
        exceptions can be made.

           You should be ashamed. These brave "hyphenated-Americans" played
        a large part in the Sept. 11 tragedy and I'm sure there were many more among
        the
        3,000 victims. When are you going to realize Gays and Lesbians make up
        an important part of all segments of American life,...Military, Executive,
        Medical and so on
        and so on. They deserve the same treatment as any other segment of
        American Society.
           I am deep saddened by this news and as a Gay Republican I am ashamed of
        "my country's"
        leadership.


              Regretfully,
