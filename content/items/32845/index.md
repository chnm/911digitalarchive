        Sept 11 fund distribution

            I've been quite shocked as to how much money has been allocated to the
        Sept 11th victims.  I personally feel that the average of $1.6 M is way out
        of line and that taxpayers dollars shouldn't be used for this purpose.   I
        don't see how you can pay the Sept 11th people a sum of money like this and
        then only pay US servicemen who have been killed the $250,000 value of their
        SGLI.  Also, considering that some of the victims of Sept 11th were military
        families, how can you justify paying them $1.6M and then pay other servicemen
        killed in Afghanistan OR other wars only the value of their SGLI?  To sum
        things up, I think the level of compensation bothers the average American who
        is struggling to save enough money to retire someday.  Its even more
        bothersome knowing that the taxpayers are footing this bill and making the
        Sept 11th families wealthy.  In conclusion, I'm not sure that the taxpayers
        owe the Sept 11th victims any compensation since past victims of accidents,
        etc... haven't been compensated.  If we have to compensate them, we certainly
        shouldn't be giving them an affluent lifestyle and making it so they never
        have to work again.  That is not right.
