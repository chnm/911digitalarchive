        Friday, March 29, 2002 6:59 AM
        Have we learned nothing?

        I am truly embarassed to consider myself an American when I was informed
        of the DOJ's ruling on the 9/11 Compensation Fund to exclude
        gays/lesbians/transgendered individuals.  Even worse, the fund SUPPORTS
        ILLEGAL aliens.  It is hypocrisy and a direct assalt on the very core of
        the Declaration of Independence and our American committment to true
        JUSTICE that families of loving, legally employed individuals would be
        excluded and that illegal residents be included.  Were the Afghanistan
        government to do the same to some of the many minority tribes there
        would the US Government not be outraged?  Are we not protecting the
        Kurds in Iraq from a similar fate with a no-fly zone?  Have we not
        worked diligently to try to bring together Catholic and Protestant
        factions in Northern Ireland  to avoid factionalization and violence?
        Aren't we attempting to reduce the conflict between Indian Hindus and
        Pakistani Muslims?  Wasn't any lesson learned from the genocide in the
        former Yugoslavia republic?  Doesn't this action just support the same
        strife in our own country? Truly, if this decision was based on fear of
        law suits, then the DOJ is becoming an instrument of evil by dismissing
        the protection of ALL American citizens to meet the desires of the
        majority in power. I guess the next logical step would be requiring
        individuals to register as a minority so you can REALLY know who you are
        dealing with.  Well, here's an idea for you....Why don't you just decide
        to make all gays/lesbians/transgendered people wear pink triangles on
        their clothing?  While you're are at it, let's make Jews wear yellow
        Stars of David... Sound familiar.  I am ashamed that it does, only to
        clearly, to me.

        Individual Comment
