        Pursuant to last night's "Meet the Press" discussion with Kenneth Feinberg,

        NOT a case of "just following existing state laws," as is disingenuously
        claimed, since clearly existing laws regarding immigrant documentation are
        be worked around.  I am disgusted by the discrimination being practiced by
        the small-minded the Public Morals Nazis in the face of this terrible human
        tragedy.
