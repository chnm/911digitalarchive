        December  26, 2001

        Dear Mr. Kenneth Zwick,

        As you consider rules for administering the September 11 Victims Relief
        Fund, I ask that you include among relatives eligible for compensation only
        those
        who were indeed related to the victims by birth or marriage.  Through the
        centuries, 'family' has been understood to be defined by birth and
        marriage, and I am sure that those people within the definition will easily
        use up the money in the relief fund.  Please do not allow other definitions
        of 'family' to take needed money away from spouses and children of the
        victims.

        Thank you for considering my concerns. I look forward to hearing from you
        about your efforts to ensure that victims of the September 11 attacks have
        receive necessary help from the relief fund.


        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Eau Claire, WI
