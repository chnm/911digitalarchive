        Friday, March 29, 2002 2:06 PM
        Gay and Lesbian Benefits and Sept. 11 Fund

        Ladies and Gentlemen,
        I am appalled at the insensitive comments made recently on a national
        news program, stating that gay and lesbian partners of victims of the
        terrorist attacks on our country will not be compensated in a manner
        that befits all those affected by this national tragedy.  Surely none of
        you believes that any one person is less deserving of such recognition
        and assistance than another, based solely on the gender of the partners.

        I myself am a gay man, sharing my life with the same partner for nearly
        22 years.  Together we have raised a beautiful daughter and experienced
        most of the joyful, and painful, things that any committed couple enjoys
        or suffers.  I could not imagine, in the event of a horrific attack like
        that on September 11, my own government, sworn to protect and serve its
        populace, telling me that my relationship - my life! - is less
        important, less meaningful, less valuable, than that of the heterosexual
        couple who met last month, and married on a whim last week!

        I urge you to reconsider your stance on this issue.  Many loyal, loving,
        and committed Americans died on that horrible day - Americans who
        deserve to be honored, respected, and recognized by their nation, for
        their contributions to the fabric of American life.


        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
