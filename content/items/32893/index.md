        Subject:   9/11 compensation disbursements


        Dear Director Zwick,


         I am writing to support the suggestion made by the National Gay
        and Lesbian Task Force (NGLTF) that the Department of Justice
        should "include  among relatives eligible for compensation those
        who lost their life partners or de facto parents of children,
        regardless of sexual orientation and marital status."

         Unless the DOJ adopts this policy, the loss of loved ones to a
        terrorist act will be compounded by the loss of any compensation,
        compensation the government is happy to give to those who are
        allowed to marry. Please do whatever you can to avoid adding
        further to the pain felt by these survivors.

         Thank you,

        Individual Comment
        Louisville, Co.
