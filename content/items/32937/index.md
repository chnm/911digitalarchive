            New York City has always stood in total recognition and support of

            Money itself will obviously not compensate for the loss of a loved one.
        However it will go a long way towards rebuilding their lives, be it through
        sustaining their living expenses or by getting the greif counseling that they
        are going to need! The lives of the many gay and lesbian couples are just as
        in need of the attention and financial contributions that they have
        selflessly contributed to in the past. I urge you to take this into serious
        consideration when making decisions regarding the September 11 Vicitm
        Compensation Fund. Thank you.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
