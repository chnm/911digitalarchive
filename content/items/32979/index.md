        Sunday, February 17, 2002 3:33 PM
        victim's fund

        I would just like to express my opinion regarding the money which will
        be given to the victims of 9/11.  First I would like to say that I cried
        every day for weeks after listening to the stories even tho I didn't
        know any victims personally.  Now when I hear the complaints of these
        people concerning the amount of money they will be given in compensation
        I am totally amazed when they say it is not enough.  Some will be
        receiving more money than most will make in a life time but aren't
        satisfied.  I'm not sure why they feel they should be compensated at all
        from this country.  It's nothing that we did.  It was a horrific event
        and I sympathize deeply, but I think they should be happy with whatever
        they get and move on with their lives.  No money in the world will bring
        back their loved ones, and they seem to forget the money comes from tax
        payers not from a never ending tree out back.
        Thank you.

        Individual Comment
