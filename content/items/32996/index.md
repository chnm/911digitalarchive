        9/11 FUND UNFAIR TO GAYS


        Dear 9-11 Victim Compensation Fund:

        I am writing to express first my complete dismay, and disbelief that after
        9-11...we are still divide by a hyphen! Because...Gays aren't now
        considered good enough to receive the amount that every other American is
        or will be receiving.

        This is discrimination pure and simple! And definably not the American way
        to go! For isn't it...united we stand, divided we fall.

        I just feel very sad that though, Gays are good enough to serve and die for
        one's country...it is fine. But(!) when it comes to real equality...we are
        again left out, and marginalzed again.

        So I protest that partners of gay and lesbian heroes of 9-11 will most
        likely not receive the same benefits as the spouses of heterosexual victims
        from this horrible terror attacks.

        Please reconsider this blight on the memories of all the heros who died on
        9-11.!!!

        FYI: When me and my partner contributed to the American Red Cross...after
        9-11...we were doing for all who died...and for their families...whatever
        their orientation!

        Thank-you,

        Individual Comment
