        Sunday, January 27, 2002 11:22 PM
        Comment on 9/11 victim compensation


        I have been thoroughly disgusted by the behavior of many of the victims'
        families.  Congress has set a terrible precedent by providing these
        funds; many others deserve help too, but the government shouldn't be
        doing this for just one group of people, nomatter how horrible their
        losses (does Congress or the vocal victims remember there are such people
        as the 40 million with no medical insurance?).  Instead of being grateful
        for the private generosity of the American people and being grateful they
        will be the beneficiaries of large life insurance policies or other
        assets that are to be deducted from the $1.6 million average government
        payments (YES, we DO understand that not everyone is getting that much
        and many of us think your charity payments should also be deducted from
        the government payments) so many of these family members, such as Mr.
           , have behaved like avaricious game-show contestants.  When did they
        even grieve???  And, for siblings and parents of victims, who presumably
        were not dependents of those killed, to feel such entitlement is
        especially disgusting.
        Individual Comment
