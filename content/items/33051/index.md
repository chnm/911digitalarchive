        Thursday, December 06, 2001 9:21 AM
        Sept 11th compensation fund

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        Please know that I support and urge you to support compensation for all
        victims and their relatives of the September 11th terrorist attack.  I am
        54 years old and have never felt so bonded to my country.   I would be
        deeply disappointed to hear that you did not consider domestic partners in
        your compensation package.  The profound pain and financial loss is as
        great for any gay and lesbian couple as heterosexual couple.  I want my
        country to continue to grow in its openness to diversity so that I can feel
        that pride.  Again, I urge the Justice Department to
        treat gay and lesbian couples as you would all others.

        Thank you,



        Individual Comment
        Lewisburg, PA
