        Compensation for all victims


        I am very angry that the families of gay and lesbian victims of the 9/11
        tragedy are not being compensated!  If illegal aliens can recieve benefits,
        then so can the legal residents of this country, including gay or lesbian
        citizens.

        Don't forget that one of the heros of that downed plane in PA was &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp , a gay man.  If it weren't for him, that plane may have smashed into
        the US Congress.  There was also the &nbsp&nbsp&nbsp&nbsp who died while giving last
        rites at the World Trade Center site.  He was known and loved by many.  And
        these are only two of the many courageous gay folks that I know about -- I'm
        sure there are many more.

        Please remind me that we live in the land of the free and extend benefits to
        everyone who rightly deserves it.  Give the gay and lesbian families of the
        victims the same help as everyone else.

        A Proud American,
