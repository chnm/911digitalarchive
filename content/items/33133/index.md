        Tuesday, November 06, 2001 11:24 PM
        My comments

        I work with crime victims in three cities near Dallas and Fort Worth.  I
        highly recommend that you look at the program of Crime Victims Compensation
        in Texas and California.  Both of these states have very good programs and
        you might learn from them.  I think that if you are using tax payer money,
        you need to be even more careful how you disburse it.

        The other aspect is to be sure that you have adequate manpower and the
        latest fax/scanner/computer equipment to do the job.  The State of Texas now
        has machines that you fax to and the document is scanned  directly into the
        computer of the worker and decisions can be made quickly.  Outsourcing
        documents is a disaster.  Hiring some consultants from other states might
        save you a lot of time and money.  I definitely think you should make the
        fund a "payer of last resort".

         As much as I am for helping the victims of New York, nothing will erase the
        pain and sorrow.  Money will ease the hardship to some extent but
        overreaction can serve to victimize the tax payers if you don't use
        discretion.

        I wish I were there to help.

        Individual Comment
        Euless, TX
