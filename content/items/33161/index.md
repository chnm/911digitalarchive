        Dear Mr. Zwick,

        I was outraged to learn that the partners of gay and lesbian victims of
        Sept. 11 will not automatically receive the same benefits as heterosexual
        victims' spouses.  The September 11 Victim Compensation Fund will apparently
        even provide for fetuses and illegal aliens -- but not homosexual partners?

        This blatant discrimination is so totally un-American that it makes me sick
        to my stomach.  What makes some American heroes more equal than others,
        simply because they are heterosexual?  The terror of 9/11 should have united
        our country -- but here again is division.

        How sad that some have learned so very little from such a great loss.  Each
        life that was lost was a tragedy -- not just heterosexual lives.  The
        partners of the homosexual heroes of 9/11 deserve the same compensation that
        any victim's family receives.

        Shame on the September 11 Victim Compensation Fund for such horrific
        discrimination in the wake of this tragedy.

        An extemely disappointed American,



        Individual Comment
        Alexandria, VA
