        Benefits for Gays and Lesbians


        To whom it may concern:

              Please reconsider your position in denying benefits of the September
        11th fund to the partners of gays and lesbians.  This is a discriminatory
        position, and certainly not one in keeping with the spirit of naitonal unity
        people embraced after September 11th.  The partners of gays and lesbians
        lost loved ones just like everyone else, and now is the time to say to those
        who lost loved ones that we as a society value them and that their loved
        ones did not die only so that gays and lesbians could be discriminated
        against further.  Being generous with this fund is the right thing to do.  I
        hope the government will do the right and decent thing.
