        Rules For Compensation

        To Whom It May Concern:

        Hang tough.  As I indicated in my initial comments, no one who looses a
        loved one can really be compensated by money.  Those who have received money
        from insurance and other means must accept the hard facts of life - one of
        which is death.  Others who loose their loved ones in tragedy don't have the
        Federal Government to bail them out.  There is a difference between needy
        and greedy.  Being in the wrong place at the wrong time doesn't "entitle"
        those making a lot of money to be made even richer.  Help is help - not a
        guarantee of life without worry.

        Thank you.
        Individual Comment
