        Tuesday, December 04, 2001 9:05 PM

        Dear Mr. Zwick,

            I am distressed to learn that the Department of Justice is receiving
        recommendations to withhold funds from families of gay and lesbian victims of
        the September 11 disasters.  Just as the terrorists who caused the deaths of
        thousands of Americans of all faiths and ethnic backgrounds made no
        distinctions among their victims, so should relief funds be distributed to
        all familes without discrimination.

            The loss of a loved one is no less painful to a lesbian woman or gay man
        than to anyone else suffering such a loss; the support of our government and
        its citizens should be no less generous to the survivors of any of the
        victims of these tragic attacks.

            I urge you to treat all families alike, to treat families of gay and
        lesbian victims as any others.

        Sincerely,
        Individual Comment
        Vernon, CT
