        To Whom it May Concern:

        I strongly believe that any Federal aid should be reduced by the amount
        of charitable aid received.  To both protect the airlines from
        litigation and quicken payments to affected families, the government has
        set up this generous fund at great potential taxpayer's expense.  It's
        purpose I believe, is to supplement money received by the families from
        insurance, charity and other sources, in other words to "make people
        whole."  The government didn't have to create this fund and it is
        nobody's right to receive it.  Therefore, it should be viewed as a
        supplement to charity with the charity amounts deducted from final
        payments.

        Sincerely,

        Individual Comment
