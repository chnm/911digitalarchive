        Saturday, March 16, 2002 1:43 PM
        Compensation discrimination

        Dear Dept. of Justice,

        I am firmly against the DOJs decision to not reimburse the partners of gay
        and lesbian victims of September 11th - some of whom were heroes on that
        day.  Why should their loved ones not receive the same consideration and
        compensation as heterosexual couples?  After all, you are making an
        exception when it comes to undocumented workers who were in this country
        illegally and should not be entitled to government money since they are not
        citizens.

        I was once in a same-sex relationship for over 14 years and even had a Holy
        Union in a church.  If we had the option of becoming legally married, we
        would have taken it.  But that option doesn't exist.  As a result, the
        partners of those who died should not be punished for it - especially in
        light of all the other exceptions being made. The loved ones of those who
        died on that horrific day have already suffered enough; please do not add to
        the misery.

        Individual Comment
        Yukon, Oklahoma
