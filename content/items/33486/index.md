        Additional Comments


        At today's Family Meeting held in Ridgewood, NJ with Special Master Kenneth
        Feinberg, it was stated that additional comments would be allowed up until
        around Feb 1.

        In the FINAL RULES, please be specific as to the definition of the
        collateral offset of Life Insurance.  There were many different policies
        paid as a reult of the WTC disaster - Accidental Death and Dismemberment;
        Business Travel, etc. Do these fall under the umbrella of "Life" insurance,
        or does the Special Master have the power to interpret what specific
        policies apply to the statute?  Since the statute is not specific, this may
        be an area where the Special Master can lessen the collateral offsets
        required by the statute.
        Thank you,
        Individual Comment
