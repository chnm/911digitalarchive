The second photo is called "The Morning After" and this photo was shot at about 8:30-9:00am on 9/12/01. Near the Trade Center site. At the WallStreet  train station. The 3rd photo is call " Men of Stone" It is of a group of New York City Fire fighters standing near Liberty Street after the building came down. Photo taken about 1:00pm on 9/11/01. The 4th photo is called "Golgotha" this photo of New York City FireFighter inside ground zero on 9/11/01. After 3PM. The 5th photo is called "Redeemers". The photo is of the first group of National Guardmen. That came marching down the Westside Highway near Liberty street towards the WorldTrade Center site. and the 5th photo is called " A Walk In hell" photo taken on 9/111/01 insde ground zero with three New York fighters wlaking among the site.
