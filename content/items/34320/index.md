Photo was taken from the VIP reviewing-stand on December 27th, 2001. Not having been in New York on the tragic day of  9-11-01, I was unsure if I ever 
wanted to go to Ground Zero. It was only the day before my scheduled return to 
Florida, after spending the Christmas holidays with my children and friends that I 
felt a deep need to go there. I stayed just briefly enough to say a prayer for ALL 
who were lost and to take this picture. The American flag flowing from the brisk 
December, breeze is a reminder that we must NEVER forget what has been done 
to our City, Country, Uniformed Services and Civilians. The background demonstrates 
that we cannot become complacent again.
