I used to work across the street from WTC between 1984-1985. I was working a temp job processing paper the same way every day. Even though my job was menial, my office was one that a high level executive would have had. I took hundreds of serious photographs of WTC. It wasn't the towers themselves that interested me most it was their entire environment that I liked. 
Walking around WTC I would notice that most of the people around me were not looking up at the towers or around at all. I never felt that the mass of people walking fast did not care about the buildings, they were just part of them because they worked around / in them and needed WTC to move through as did I. I created a commercial website around some of my stronger images. I would like to donate portfolios to appropriate groups or institutions. If you have any suggestions please let me know. http://worldtradecenterproject.com
