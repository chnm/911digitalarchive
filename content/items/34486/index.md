This picture was taken on Oct. 1, 2001.  I don't remember what street I was looking down but it was about Rector St.  My sister worked in the first building on the left.  I had gone to NYC because of the 9-11 attack to visit with my sister and her family.  On Sept. 11, 2001, I was at home in CO.  That morning I woke to the news on the radio.  Thinking it was some kind of joke I turned on the TV to the image of the second plane slamming into the tower.  Knowing my sister worked just a few short blocks from the trade center and did banking and other business in the buildings, I immediatly started to make phone calls to NYC.  Finally, my brother in law got a call to me.  She was ok and had not gone to work on Sept. 11, 2001.
