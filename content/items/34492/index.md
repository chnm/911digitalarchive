My Father made this float for Labour Day to honor all the workers that have died on the job or from occupational diseases.  I felt it fitting to play around with the scroll the angel is holding and honour the many that lost their lives on September 11, 2001.  Of course I didn't have much time to play around with it on my lunch break so the print's a little sloppy but I still wanted to add it.  I added another picture of the guys carrying the float because it also seemed fitting.  Although I'm Canadian, I was still affected and my heart goes out to all who lost loved ones.
