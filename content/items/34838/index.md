I was so shocked and saddened by the events unfolding on TV that morning that I had to get out of the house and drive, so I took my digital camera with me and happened to stop and take this picture.

It is taken by me standing outside the JCPenny store at the Westfield Shopping Town here in Vancouver, Washington, USA on the morning of September 11th, 2001 at around 10:30AM PDT (1:30PM Eastern time). The notice on the doors is a somber reminder of the affects that those events were having on everyone everywhere that day. 

The notice reads: "Westfield Shopping Town has made the decision to close the mall this business day Tuesday September 11th. We will re-open for business at 10:00 AM on Wednesday September 12th. Thank you for your understanding JCPenny Management"
