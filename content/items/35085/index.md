We visited the WTC site the day after Thanksgiving, 2001.  We were walking our way around the perimeter of the site, passing the police memorial and the fragile but still standing Winter Garden.  The street washers were still fighting a losing battle to clean the ash and soot that was still coating the streets.
This is one of the last remaining "shells" of one of the Trade Center buildings.  Fires were still burning and were not officially put out until about a week after our visit.
