In July, 2001 I flew out of LaGuardia Airport.  I had just packed up my folks' apartment to have their belongings shipped to my home in Miami--we moved them here after illnesses and age made it necessary to have them nearby.  These photos were taken as my last views of "home", which I expect I'll not see much of in my lifetime since my home base is now with me here.  It was an emotional leavetaking, I felt for the first time in the 25 years I'd been away from New York that I was truly leaving home.  The Big Apple was and always remained home to me. Now even these remarkable symbols of home are gone.   

Photo taken through the window of a 737 during takeoff from LaGuardia, July, 2001.
Sony Digital 8 450 X Handycam, stored on Memory Stick; widefield view.
