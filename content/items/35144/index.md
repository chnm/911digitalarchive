This is a picture I took while my wife and I were visiting New York on August 18th, just three weeks prior to the attacks. We had intended to go to the top to see the view, but because of the long wait, we decided we'd do it the next time we were in NYC. Following September 11th, I remembered having taken the picture, and seeing it again was especially poignant as we had been there so recently. The towers were remarkable structures; I hope this picture can serve as a reminder of their immense scale.
