My wife and I were on Holidays in Hawaii when the 911 attacks occured. We were lucky the 911 attacks only delayed our trip home to Sydney Australia. During our extended stay in Honolulu we wondered the streets. The flags came out from every where. The picture I submitted shows a man who we past on the street many times on this one day. I knew then that this incident had touched every one no matter where they were.. Lest We Forget.
