This was taken during my first ever trip abroad (date can be seen from the picture). It was the best experience of my life. I was so lucky to see the World Trade Center before 9/11. Together with my sisters, we posed for a souvenir picture before heading towards the Statue of Liberty. I was so disappointed at myself because we could've chosen to go to the WTC instead. Even though, this photo was so memorable to me because the Woolworth Bldg. was positioned exactly in between the Twin Towers, and not all photographers can get an exact snapshot of those buildings in-sync. I guess my mother did a wonderful photo-magic.
