An aerial image to behold.....

Here is a view that many of you surely have seen if 
you've flown into New York's La Guardia airport, as 
I was on this incredibly crystal clear day, about 
2 months before Sept 11's horrific and senseless 
terrorist act against New Yorkers and this icon of 
New York's skyline. Frequently, flights would pass 
north along either the Hudson or East River, and 
depending upon the time of day and your seat, 
you'd most likely be treated to a breathtaking view. 
Surely, this will be no more. Taken from a Delta Airlines
MD88 N961DL.
