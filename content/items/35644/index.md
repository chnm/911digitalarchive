Sunday, September 09, 2001, 4:18:10 PM

I was in NJ for a business trip, and had Sunday free.  A coworker and I spent the day in Manhattan.  At the end of the day, we went to the top of the WTC and I took a lot of 1600x1200 shots from the observation deck.  Then we discovered that we could go all the way outside by going up an escalator.  My coworker took this picture of me with the other tower and the city to the North.

We went out Monday night and got some grainy pictures of the towers at night.  Tuesday morning, we watched in horror from Edison as the second plane struck.
