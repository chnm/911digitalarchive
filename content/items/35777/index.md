An artist's tribute to the fallen was exhibited through September at The Port Authority in Times Square. Using photos of those lost supplied by loved ones Stephen Beveridge has resurrected one of the World Trade Center towers in a piece called "One Power" 
The artist's belief in the unifying power of God inspired him on September 11th. to turn from the news of the tragedy and commune with spirit through art in the co-creation of this symbol of hope in the face of contrary appearances. 
The collage/painting was added at a reception Friday, September 14th, to an exhibition entitled "The Big One". The exhibition was created Friday, September 7th, at an action/performance of the three internationally acclaimed artists-- Stephen Beveridge, Stefan Eins &amp; David Ferrando.
