"determined odysseus" by anthony h. risser, ph.d.
(march 2002) 

"ulysses" by italian sculptor ugo attardi.
located in battery park city,
beyond the end of liberty street,
once roughly 350 yards from the south tower
of the world trade center;
world financial center's
2 wfc, 3 wfc (american express),
and scaffolded winter garden in background.

another version of this 1997 work by attardi
can be found in the palazzo valentini
in rome. attardi recently illustrated an
italian translation of the odyssey.
