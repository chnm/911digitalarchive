I am a military police officer with the US Army Reserves. I returned home on Nov. 27, 2003 after serving in military operations in Iraq as part of Operation Iraqi Freedom. Every vehicle in our unit has this decal, representing the Twin Tovwers, The Pentagon and Somerset, PA, site of the Crash of Flight 94. There are 160 MPs from the 88th MP Co. out of Ft Eustis, Va; most of us are cops, firefighters and EMTs in our regular jobs and will never forget... or forgive.
