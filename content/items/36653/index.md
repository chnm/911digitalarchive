I turned to her and said " We can come back another time. It will ALWAYS be here. Well I was wrong about that. I make a calendar every year for my family and friends. The 2002 Calendar was all trade center pictures I had taken. 2003 included the Image of the flag raising that I had taken at Madame Tseus Wax Musem in NYC. This year I created this one special. I hope you like it. Paul
