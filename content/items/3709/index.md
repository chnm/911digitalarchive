        I will never forget the feeling on that day as I sat at
        my desk doing my morning routine.  A co-worker asked me to
        come over by his desk to see this picture of the World Trade
        Centers.  As we watched the news coverage of the first tower
        up in smoke a second plane came in.  I couldn't believe my
        eyes.  Who on earth could be so heartless??  And why??  After
        awhile I went back to my work checking the news periodically
        only to find out that a plane had also hit the Pentagon and
        another had crashed somewhere in PA.  I went home that day for
        lunch just to verify what I had saw on the websites.  It just
        didn't sink in.  Not until my 3 year old asked my that evening
        why someone would do that.  I cried trying to explain to him
        what had happened and that there are some very bad people out
        there that had killed a lot of innocent people.  Every now and
        then he builds two towers with his Legos and again asks why
        someone would fly planes into those two buildings.  It scares
        me that he remembers and I only hope that I explained things
        to him without scaring him.  I again cried today remembering
        all the people that lost their lives a year ago and all the
        family that is left to grieve their loss.  There has not been
        a day that has gone by that I have not thought of every single
        person.  Rest in peace & God Bless America!!
