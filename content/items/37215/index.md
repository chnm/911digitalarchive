      text: |-
        ... but where I worked, 7 World Trade Center,
        collapsed at about 5 yesterday afternoon.  I still
        can't believe what happened, but am very thankful
        that I was able to make it home to my family safely.
        My prayers go out to those who didn't.

        For those on this list who know people from the
        Park Slope UMC, it appears so far that everyone
        is ok-- and so far, also the families of Learning
        Community Charter School students.

        We evacuated our building after we heard the
        second explosion, not knowing at the time it
        was the second plane.  We walked down from
        the 22nd floor.  I had called Michael after the
        first crash when we weren't sure what
        had happened.  We were well away by
        the time the first building collapsed.  I was driven
        by an inner voice to get home, to keep walking.
        Fortunately, I made it to the Christopher Street
        PATH station before it stopped running (even
        getting a seat in an air-conditioned car) & took
        it to Hoboken where the train station was shut
        down.  I started walking home (about a mile, but
        a steep climb up the Palisades) because the buses
        weren't running -- they were just waiting for
        further instructions.  After a few blocks, I was
        able to catch a bus.

        I gave 2 people I'd never met before a ride to
        Newark after running up to tell Michael I was
        OK.  My return trip to Jersey City was
        complicated because the Skyway which I'd
        taken & the Turnpike were closed -- but I
        was able to get over to Rt. 280 & get back
        to JC and pick up Philip (although the traffic
        was horrendous with lots of streets closed off
        around the hospitals & lots of emergency
        vehicles going by).

        By the time I got to his school, most of the
        children had been evacuated (I think it started
        informally with parents just coming to get their
        kids & evolved into a formal evacuation).  I
        took home 2 kids who live around the corner,
        because no one could get their dad's cell number
        or their emergency contacts.  He eventually took
        a ferry to Weehawken.

        The computer people from my company were in
        our back office located in Jersey City (which also
        was evacuated yesterday), posted a notice on our
        website (fhlbny.com) and are implementing our
        disaster recovery plan to get the bank back to
        business.  Since I give away the money, I most
        likely will not be going back as quickly.  Obviously
        our files are history (as are my drawer full of shoes),
        but we have a lot of electronic information backed-up
        & all that stuff.

        Hugs to all of you,

        Beth
