        This is a poem my 14 year old daughter wrote about September 11th:

        THE BRAVE

        BY: LINDSAY MEDNICK

        LOOK AT THE WORLD AROUND US
        LOOK AT ITS CRUMBLING WALL
        BUILDINGS GOING DOWN IN FLAMES
        WITH IT WILL OUR UNITY FALL

        WILL WARS GO ON FOR DECADES
        OR WILL WE FINALLY REGAIN PEACE
        DO YOU WANT TO LIVE IN A HOSPITABLE WORLD
        OR ONE THAT WILL EVENTUALLY BECOME DECEASED

        EVERYDAY ANOTHER DEATH
        ONE TO ADD TO THE GROWING LOT
        SADNESS AND MISERY ARE NORMAL EMOTIONS
        HAPPINESS AND LAUGHTER HAVE JUST BEEN FORGOT

        BUT WHEN A TRAGEDY HAS ENDED
        AND IT SUDDENLY BEGINS TO FADE FROM SOMEONES MIND
        WHEN THE AMERICAN FLAGS START COMING DOWN
        THEN WHAT WILL WE FIND

        WILL WE FIND PEOPLE THANKING FIREFIGHTERS
        OR PEOPLE PUTTING FLOWERS ON A STRANGERS GRAVE,
        WE WILL PROBABLY FIND ADULTS AND CHILDREN LAUGHING
        NOT EVEN THINKING ABOUT THE DEATHS AND INJURIES OF THE BRAVE


        THE PEOPLE WHO RISKED THEIR LIVES
        WHO TOOK THEIR OWN LIFE SO A CHILD COULD LIVE
        THE ONES WHO WALKED THROUGH THE SMOKE AND THE FIRE
        AND GAVE ALL THAT THEY HAD TO GIVE

        THOSE ARE THE PEOPLE WHO CHANGED OUR LIVES
        BECAUSE THEY GAVE UP EVERYTHING THEY KNEW
        THEY DIED AS AN ACT OF KINDNESS,
        SOMETHING NOT MANY PEOPLE ARE WILLING TO DO

        THEY WALKED INTO FIRE
        DRAWN BY THE CRIES
        AND FOR THEIR FUTURE,
        A TOTAL SURPRISE

        DEAD OR ALIVE
        WE OWE THEM SO MUCH
        WE SHOULD NEVER FORGET THEM
        OUR HEARTS THEY WILL ALWAYS TOUCH

        THEY GAVE UP FAMILY, EXPERIENCE, AND LOVE
        TO DIE FOR THOSE IN NEED
        THEY IMPACTED OUR SOULS
        AND DID AN INDESCRIBABLE DEED

        THEY TOOK THEIR LIFE
        AND WALKED INTO DEATH
        WITH NO GOOD-BYES
        AND ONLY ONE LAST BREATH

        © Lindsay Mednick 1/02
