        On September 11th 2001 I witnessed history in action; the effects of this have come no where near an end.  For the first time in this day and age a large number of innocent civilians were killed in the United States.  It is hard to say how this will fall into the rest of history because the event is such a watershed.  Never again will our nation be the same in its security and living of day to day life.
        	From that day I have two main memories both of which will never leave my memory.  The first is when I heard the news, the class I was in had a broken loud speaker so I didnt hear the announcement at school right away.  Then after class I questioned another teacher about the announcement and she told me about the first tower.  I thought she was joking and just went on with my day to my locker.  Here I heard others discussing the event and I could not comprehend what had happened.  It seemed like something that could never happen to the United States.  The second memory that I have is driving home around three in the afternoon.  I took 93 North threw the city of Boston which was deserted.  I could see no other cars behind or in front of me, nor a single person in any building.  It felt so unreal that I will never be able to overcome the feeling.
        	This has had an impact on my day to day life.  I live knowing that my time here is sort, and existence is fragile.  I now more than ever try to get the most out of life while having fun with the time I have.  I dont want to have any regrets and since 9-11 I have lived my life to the full that way.
