      text: I was working at my lawfirm at the time and was connected to MSNBC on-line.  Someone yelled that a plane hit one of the Trade Towers.  At first, we thought it was a small private plane, but then someone yelled that the second Tower had been hit.  We were then informed that the Pentagon had been hit by a third plane.  Meanwhile we were panicked as reports were flying that a plane was headed for the immediate DC area.  Then all heck broke loose and in a matter of minutes some 2,300 employees out of our total of 2,325 employees began leaving the building to walk wherever they could get to.  Streets were total gridlock.  I lived in Virginia at the time and I had no place to go if I left the building.  I had an open telephone line, an on-line connection, a restroom, a cafeteria and plenty of water.  I tried to get a ride to Virginia with a co-worker but we could not get his car out of the parking garage.  Too much gridlock.  I decided to remain in our building until the subways were up and running again.  At that time I rode the subway to Virginia where my husband picked me up.
