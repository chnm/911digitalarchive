        That Night, September 11, 2001
               I kiss my daughter good-night
        	Shes tangible, touchable
        	I want the twisted steel screams
        	To subside
        	I can still hear the cell phone I love yous,
        	they linger in the thick smoky air
        	Echoing that frantic love,
        	I absently stroke my daughters hair
        	Searing white hot disbelief blinds me -
        	I feel as stone cold as the rescuers
        	Ash covered agony repeats and repeats in the days images
        	Flash frozen forever in time and in my mind
        	There is no blanket of forgetfulness -
        	Except this moment
        	With my daughter
        	I let my tears fall into
        	Her sweet hair -
        	A final kiss good bye.

               -- Karyl Goldsmith
