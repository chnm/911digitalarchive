My first thoughts were that they were broadcasting a modern version of the "War between the Worlds" at a strange hour.  Several minutes later there was another report about the WTC.  Then, my thoughts were that there must be something to this story, it is time to get out of bed and go to the family room to check any TV reports.

Then the reports continued about the plane hitting the building, and then the video showing the hit!

What madness is causing this destruction?  Then the first tower collapes!  Then the second!  Then the Pentagon!How many thousands have died - ten, twenty - how many?
