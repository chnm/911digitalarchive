  'NMAH Story: Story':
    - html: false
      text: On September 11, 2001, everyone witnessed history.  During the attacks, I was sitting in my seventh grade classroom, unaware of what had happened.  The school did not inform the students because they didnt want to scare us, but all the kids could tell that something was wrong from the way that the teachers were acting.  They kept telling us to go straight home from school and not to worry our parents that day.  After school, I had soccer practice, and my coach informed us of what had happened.  She told us that a plane had flown into the World Trade Center.  I did not realize how serious it was, until I got to my friends house and watched the news.  On TV, we saw footage of what had happened earlier that day.  It was scary and upsetting to see the Twin Towers being hit, and then collapsing.  It also made me mad to think how someone could do that to so many innocent people.  I was extremely confused as to why it had happened.  I never really thought this could happen to our country.  September 11th made me realize how much Americans take their security in this country for granted.  These people just got on a plane, or went to work, not expecting anything out of the ordinary to happen.  But unfortunately, something out of the ordinary did happen.  Hopefully, out country wont ever have to witness history like that again.
