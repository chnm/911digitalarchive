It was so surreal for two days not only seeing the lack of planes in the air but not hearing that noise that has become such a normal part of life that I never usually even notice.  Well, that is till that day.  I will never forget that when the planes began to take to the sky again.. everyone just stoped what they were doing.  Stood still, staring at the sky.  A little fearful yes, but more than anything proud that Americia was getting back on her feet. May God forever bless America and may we forever be proud of the country we are so fortunate to live in, and never fail to protect our freedom!
