  'NMAH Story: Story':

      text: I was in class, English, and the class phone rang and my teacher answered it.  She told us that a plane had just crashed into the first of the WTC towers.  The class response was disbelief but it soon passed and class continued.  When the teacher found out about the second tower and the pentagon, the whole class was confused and in need of answers to questions that were on all of our minds.  For the rest of the day, every classroom had a tv tuned into a news station and we all stared at it waiting for anything new that would help us understand what happened and why and who and all the questions that raced through all of our heads. When I got home, I immediately turned on the tv and watched the news all night, in disbelief of the events of the day.  I know I will never forget it.
