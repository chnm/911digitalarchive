        Linuss Blanket

        Ripped from my arms,
        that wrap of secure safety
        I grew up with in my grasp.
        That tattered but sturdy clothe that
        I swaddled my shivering self in.
        I search all day for those frayed remnants
        of magically impenetrable protection.
        I cannot find that sense of sanctuary anywhere,
        not in the eyes of the man on the T.V.,
        nor in the skies above.
        The sky, where I only look for signs and more signs.
        Where is Linuss blanket?
        I would share half with you,
        if I could only discover where it got to today.

        September 11th, 2001
        Tammy Nuzzo-Morgan
