I was doing my usual classroom work when the phone rang. My substitute teacher picked it up as we all listened to her curiously. She sounded worried , but no one knew why. On the bottom of the screen scrolled a message stating that the World Trade Center had been hit by an airplane.  I stared up at the TV in disbelief.  I couldn't handle what I was seeing.  The tower was flaming and the downtown area of New York City was in total chaos.  People were jumping out of the windows, running for their lives and barely escaping the falling debris tumbling down from above.  Before I could take in the shock of the hit, I saw another plane hit the second tower. Now, both towers had huge flames emerging from them.  I sat there in complete denial wondering how this could possibly happen.  It couldn't, i thought, it was too unreal, too much like a dream or a movie.  The entire day was spent watching the TV and hearing of the attack made on the Pentagon and a plane crashing in PA. Even when I believed nothing worse could happen , the two towers collapsed , one right after the first, leaving thousands dead. I was shocked, scared, and filled with fear.  I felt fear for my myslf, my family and friends , and most of all, my country. It puzzled me to think why someone would want to do such a horrible thing to America and to our people.
