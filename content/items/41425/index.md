      text: At first, I couldn't believe what was truly happening.  I was in my 3rd period biology class carrying a slide back to my desk when the substitute teacher told us that she received a phone call that her son (who was in NY) was ok, I didn't understand. A few minutes later, we saw, live, the second world trade center get hit by a plane and begin to burn along with the first one.
