        Attack on the Twin Towers
              I  started  September  11th,  2001  the  same  as  any  other  day.  I  had  to  drag  my self  out  of  bed  and  barely  got  to  school  on  time.  I  went  to  my first block, which was Spanish with Mrs. Kingsley.  We were checking some homework when   some senior came into the room telling Mrs. Kingsley that something happened to the world trade centers. At first I didnt think much of it, until I went to 2nd   block    math with Ms. Duncan, and she came into the classroom and turned on the television. Then I saw the first tower get hit by a big airliner. I was shocked, confused, and flabbergasted all at the same time. I just didnt know what to think. Then the second plane hit the other tower, it looked even worse. We all were confused at what we had just seen happen in front of our eyes. Some asked, Did that really just happen? There was an eerie silence   that went all over the room at once. Then a little after that happened it collapsed, and the 1st tower followed. I thought if they got hit like that, they would have fallen over but they collapsed inward. I wondered if any one could have survived such horrible catastrophe. And if they did what condition they would be in. I didnt learn until about five minutes later that it was not an accident and that terrorist had hijacked a plane, then   on CNN about the pentagon.  For about a week it didnt seem it was real, I would hear more about it television but it seemed so far away. Like it didnt happen at all. There were lots of people that had love ones that died. In a weird way that made me appreciate my parents and my sister, and made them not seem as bad. There were lots of people on television talking about their family members who were businessmen and firemen who died saving other peoples lives. This made me wonder if I could have done that. When I think back to when it all happen it brings a lot of pain and sorrow. To think that all those innocent people died because of some differences between two countries. And that the reason the terrorist did this all because of religion.
