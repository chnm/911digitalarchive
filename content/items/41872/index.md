  'NMAH Story: Story':

      text: |-
        I was at work, I am employed as a Case Manager with the Virginia Department of Corrections, at Nottoway Correctional Center.  It was almost 0900 hours, when my wife called me to say she was watching the today show, and they had sent a live crew to the WTC, as they had received reports of a plane crashing into one of the ETC towers.  I ran and told several others that had tvs in their office about my phone conversation with my wife.  This took me less than 5 minutes, by the time I had returned to my desk.  My wife called me a second time to tell me that a second plane had crshed into the other WTC tower.  One of my co-workers and I got the cable & electrical power hooked up to the tv in her office.  We began to watch the horror of this tragic day unfold a piece at a time.  This event caused the time calander with in me to stand still and then when my internal clock began to work again a minute felt like an hour, an hour like a day.  This seemed to linger with me for the rest of the week.  I will not forget the fear that it produced in my then 4 year old daughter Frances.  I do not believe that she is over this experience a year after the events of tragedy on 11 Sep. 2002.  I do not believe that I nor most Americans are over what happened to this great nation of ours one year ago today.  I thank this agency for giving me this oportunity to take part in recording this chapter of U. S. History.

                                 Respectfully Submitted,

                                    Raymond L. Parmenter, Jr.
