I love my husband more than life itself & the day that he told me he wanted me to leave & he didn't love me anymore, just ripped my heart out. This was on Sept. 9, 2001. The morning of Sept. 11, 2001, I was getting ready for work. As I was talking to my sister-in-law, we happened to turn our heads to the t.v. & watched as the first plane hit. What the hell is going on, was our words to one another. I thought, this can't be happening, haven't I gone through enough already. I sat unbeleiving & than the second plane hit. I didn't think my heart could sink deeper than it already had. We called my brother on his cell phone. He & my younger brother work for a produce farm & where on their way to New Jersey to make their usual deliveries. He said he could see the smoke from the Thruway. Needless to say they are both volunteer fireman at the Accord Fire Department. They were both o.k. THANK GOD! Our day continued as normal as we could make it. Just a note: my husband & I got back together since then."
