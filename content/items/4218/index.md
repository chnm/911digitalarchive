        My son was sick that day so I stayed home from work.  We were watching TV when all the sudden the first plane hit.  I thought it was pilot error until I saw with my own eyes the second plane hit and then I knew we were under attack.  I watched this whole event live on natinal TV.

        I did not know one soul in that mass destruction but yet I have cried, prayed and followed this event closely over the past year.  My heart aches terribly for all of the people involved.  I wish I could have been at ground zero to give my love and support and to possibly help in some way.  Unfortunately, I was never given that chance.  I wish I could tell those people who lost love ones that this one person in Ft. Mitchell, Kentucky loves them all very much and cries for them and prays for them to find some kind of peace and closure. That they have touched my heart tremendously and if there were anything I could do for any last one of them I would and be honored to do so!  I'm so sorry for all your heartache and pain.

        You will forever be in my heart, thoughts, prayers and dreams.

        GOD BLESS YOU AND GOD BLESS AMERICA

        Sincerely
        And with All My Love,
