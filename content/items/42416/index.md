      text: On September 11th 2001, the day that the world stopped turning, I witnessed the terrorist attacks while I was a freshman at Uniontown High School.  It was a Tuesday morning and I was sitting in homeroom talking to friends when an announcement was made stating something about the World Trade Centers.  I wasn't really paying attention to the announcement when it was made.  The televisions came on and showed a picture of one of the World Trade Centers burning.  I didn't think anything of it.  I thought it was just a really bad fire, since I couldn't hear the television.  While walking to second period, my friends told me what was really happening.  They told me that terrorists took over a plane and flew it into the Trade Center.  My heart skipped a beat.  When I entered my second period class, the television was on showing us live pictures of the attacks.  At that point I knew I was living through history.  After the day ended, I made my way into my house and watched the broadcasts with my sister.  We flipped through the channels and on every station there was something on about the attacks.  I spent the day that changed America in Uniontown High School.  Even if I was somewhere else on September 11th, I would have the same thoughts and emotions about America, the citizens of American, and what the people of this country were going through.
