By September 12 almost every house on my block had a flag in the window or hanging up outside.  It was really neat to see our country's patriotism grow so rapidly in such a short amount of time.   'NMAH Story: Story':

      text: On September 11, 2001 I was in my second hour Language Arts class getting ready to take pictures for the yearbook.  But right before we could go get our pictures taken Mrs. Fienning, my teacher announced that a plane had crashed into the World Trade Center.  I didn't believe her at first.  Then I thought it wasn't as big of a deal as she was making it.  Little did I know that a second plane was on its way into the other tower, the Pentagon, and in Shanksville, Pennsylvania.  That day when I suddenly remembered that my Uncle Mike works in New York City about a mile and a half from the Twin Towers.  When I expressed my concern to my mom she told me that she had already talked to my uncle earlier in the day.  He was told that he couldn't leave work that evening because it was too dangerous, so he spent the night in the city.  Finally, what I thought was impossible became reality; the September 11 tragedy was personally affecting my family and I.
