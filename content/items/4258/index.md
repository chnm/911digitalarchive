        This is a poem I wrote about September 11th & Ground Zero. It is entitled "The World." Please read!!

        THE WORLD
        I am the world,
        my burial ground is known as Ground Zero.
        I watch as they piece me back together,
        hands helping hands.

        I am the world,
        I was lost that fateful day.
        God put His hands upon me,
        & told me the time was near.

        I am the world,
        torn in two by one altering second.
        Eyes watched me as I crumbled,
        & all I could do was give in.

        I am the world,
        whom has withstood a multitude of years.
        Though I have been momentarily disabled,
        I will OVERCOME IT -

        For I am the world,
        & I am everone...

        Maggie Anne Drabing
