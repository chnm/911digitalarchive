      text: Last year at September 11, I was in Korea. I was just watching TV when our Korean news suddenly switched to CNN. As it showed the first building get hit, I thought it was a movie or a play going on, because it was unbelieveable. Then when I realized what it is, I was shocked with horror. Everybody just froze, nothing able to say. As the second building got hit again, I got angry. How could something happen to people? It was so sympathetic seeing people throwing themselves of the building. I had been to the World Trade Center a lot of times, even on the roof. So it made me able to feel how it may be up there. As Pentagon was attacked again, I was praying to stop these disasters, let it be dream. But it was too true. After it happened, I listened to the radio day by day, hearing these very sad stories of the people who died, and their families and friends. As I recall my memory, I still remember it was too shocking. This terrorism was shown live in all countries, including Korea. This was a very sad mark in human history, and I hope it never happens again. I am really sorry for the people who died at September 11th, 2001.
