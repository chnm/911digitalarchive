        At first I thought they were showing video footage of the Oklahoma bombing, then suddenly I realize that it was live footage of what was happening right now! I was crushed and later I wrote this poem as a memorial...

        Let Today...Be The Day

        With a trembling heart I had to look
        around our office the building shook
        the blackest smoke had surround me
        and out a window I just glanced to see

        Within every wall I heard muffled screams
        awake I thought from this strange dream
        with a heavy roar and grumbling sounds
        it's a horrible thought of how to get down
        I am many floors up in this huge sky-rise
        "God Help Us Please" and hear our cries
        we think at first it's a great earth quake
        and from this dream we all try to wake

        The walls are rolling like an ocean's wave
        and so we pray this will not be our graves
        my mind races back to my family at home
        as I fumble around searching for my phone
        the air has turned thick it's harder to breathe
        tears are flowing while my eyes cannot see
        I found a phone but then I dialed too fast
        as this building I hoped forever would last
        oh and it doesn't matter now about my call
        for the cord has come loose from the wall

        I grasp my hands and now I pray very deep
        "Dear Lord, Father of My Soul,
        please keep...
        my family and my friends from mourning pleas
        let them think of the good while remembering me
        because I can see now that these walls will cave in
        and that this day forward my life comes to an end"
        "Amen"

        Crumbling down is the building and we all tend to slide
        while I can feel something pressing up against my side
        and I must have blacked out for I know nothing more
        except a glowing light that shines through some door
        and right now there's a hand reaching closer to me
        while hearing a soft melody I look around to see
        this is strange I thought on how it didn't take long
        then I smile as I realize it's a rescue Angel's Song


        So loved-ones of mine do not weep at my grave
        God will bring us together in Heaven someday
        and let today be the day to do a good deed
        by just helping someone who's really in need
        give all of your love you have plenty to spare
        and remember us love in all of your prayers.

        for the WTC victim's loved-ones
        September 11, 2001
