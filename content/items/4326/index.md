THIS IS MY STORY OF THAT DAY SEPT.11,2001.I STILL FEEL CHANGED SINCE THIS HAPPEN TO US.MY HEART GOES OUT TO EVERYONE THAT WAS TOUCHED PERSONALLY IN SUCH A SAD TIME. LOVE AND MAY THE TIME OF HEALING GET EASIER FOR EVERYONE.LIFE MUST GO ON. SINCERELY SANDY TALLENT.
