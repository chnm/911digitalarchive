  'NMAH Story: Story':
    - html: false
      text: I was at work in Amsterdam Airport, The Netherlands, when the radio broadcast told us of a terrorist attack in NYC. As we have several offices in NYC we offcourse turned on the television set hoping for information about our colleagues. What we saw on the news blew the group of people present away. Some stood around, others had to sit down, as we watched the events unfold. When another plane hit the second tower we all cried out in horror and some of us burst into tears when it dawned on us how many people were killed by this insane act. Although our logistics company is one of the busiest places at the airport, all activity came to a stand still, and most of us went home keeping our ears to the media hoping to learn of survivors being found in the ruins.
