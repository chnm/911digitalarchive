  'NMAH Story: Story':
    - html: false
      text: I was actually sleeping and a friend called us and told us a plane hit the WTC.  We turned on the TV right away and saw what truly looked like a scene from a movie. I just sat there glued to the TV with my mouth wide open at first and then after the news sunk in, I just couldn't help but cry. My fiancee is a pilot for an airline and I am a airline employee as well, As we found out that the airplanes were commercial airlines my thoughts went immediately for those on board and the crew members, and what they must have been going though. I was so devistated. I am also very grateful because as horrible as it was that day, my dad was not in the WTC.  He went there for business once a month for the past 5 years.  He was supposed to meet a friend on Sept 10 for lunch but he did not go, she died the next day in the World Trade Center.  My dad had alot of friends that worked in the Twin Towers, some made it out ok but others unfortunetly did not.
