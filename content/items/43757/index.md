      text: Just starting a vacation in Albuquerque, New Mexico, my husband and I had just got up to get ready for our first full day there.  As I went into the kitchen to get some tea I stopped and turned on the television for some local news and weather.  I began to pour the tea when I turned around to look at the television to see what exactly they were reporting.  I called my husband and just pointed and said, "Look at this"! Being we had not taken a vacation in ten years we decided to take it day by day and to remain there. The homes and cars in Albuquerque began to display American Flags in every size.  Groups started collecting money for the victims.  We were able to keep abreast of all the news on television and local radio.  As much as we looked forward to this trip I can say we were very happy to come home.
