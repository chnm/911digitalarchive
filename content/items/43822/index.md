  'NMAH Story: Story':
    - html: false
      text: |-
        I was sitting in my Spanish III class awaiting the dismissal bell to go to next period.
        It was about 10 a.m.  My friend and I were laughing about something, as usual, when
        the principal's voice came on the speaker.  As soon as he started to speak, I could sense
        something was wrong.  He told us the twin towers in New York had been hit just minutes
        ago by what was believed to be terrorists.  I was shocked, but not really able to understand
        the full impact this would have on our nation.  It seemed far away.  After all, I was in
        Manassas, Va, a whole 6-7 hours away from the Big Apple.  It didn't seem to affect me.   But
        then, I heard the Pentagon had been hit.  Many parents in my area work there, and
        that really hit close to home.  Many were worried, few were laughing, and all were
        awaiting more information.  The event was on the news almost 24-7 for the next couple
        weeks, and is discussed often even today, 2 years later.  I will never forget September
        11, 2001.
