          Here is a copy of what you gave us:

          How did you witness history on September 11th? Tell
          us your story:
           After a long time of silence and many many months
          of
          professional help I am now able to talk about some
          of the events of this Horrific day that not only
          change the World but changed my life forever. I had
          been in Los Angeles and just returned on the 9th to
          hand over my position to my partner who had also just
          arrived from Canada to take my place.
          I had been very ill during the previous year with
          Leukemia and had taken some time off to have a bone
          marrow transplant which was scheduled for the 26th
          September.
          I was at a meeting with my business partners,
          eighteen staff and four clients high in the North
          Tower at the 101 level.  We were enjoying the
          clearness of the early morning views after having had
          a breakfast at Window on the World.

          I, along with my assistant Anna left the meeting to
          attend another meeting just prior to
          AAFlight 11 slammed into our building. I had seen the
          plane and commented that it looked like it was heading
          our way but was reassured that it was not and would
          divert over the river.
          There were eight people who were trapped in the
          Elevator nearest to impact including myself. We knew
          that it was a bomb or
          something very serious had just happened. We could
          hear the extremely
          loud noises, smell smoke and fuel as all our
          lights went out and our elevators stopped.
          Some of the men climbed up on to the roof through
          the manhole to see what had happened and how we
          could escape. Five people
          around us died as huge explosions blow metal like
          paper and  fire raged. Those people who climbed up
          were blown to pieces or fell down the shaft. Three of
          us managed to escape death. We climbed out onto a beam
          through a huge hole that was blown in the elevator
          wall tp our left and then went down a service ladder
          on inside of the elevator shaft.
          We climbed down with our injuries and through a
          small opening into a landing that eventually took us
          to the stairwell. Smoke and water covered us but the
          lights were on. I remember looking back toward the
          outside and saw a huge hole, smoke and fire
          everywhere. Eventually we reached
          the main stairwell. People were injured and very
          afraid.
          There were people pushing and trying to get out. No
          order was present as they panicked and tried to save
          themselves. I saw things on this day that shall stay
          in my mind and heart forever.
          By the Mercy and help of God we escaped only seconds
          before the building fell.  I had not felt the piece
          of steel sticking out of my head at the lower part
          behind the ear. I had not felt the cut across my
          throat and cuts and abasions. Nor the burns down my
          back. I just knew that Anna was badly burned and the
          young man who was with us had lost his hand at the
          wrist. I had managed to stop the flow of blood with
          his neck tie and belt. I had placed his severed hand
          into my shoulder bag. How any one of the three of us
          were able to climb down is A MIRACLE. God held us in
          his hands.
          I had been a successful business woman and after 9/11
          my life was dramatically changed. I lost everything on
          that day. I returned after my release to California to
          recover and could not speak of what happened. I know
          God was there on that day and after.
          Huge Miracles did happen . God was there for so many
          along with the brave firemen, medics, civilians and
          did save my life and many others.
          We were graced by a living God who heard our cries
          and saved us. Psalms 18 1-7.


          Has your life changed since September 11, 2001? If
          so, tell us how?
           In every way my life has changed. I value life and
          the source of where it came from so much more. I
          value
          what is really valuable  "All the things that money
          can not buy" All the things man can not make start
          or stop.

          What do you think should be remembered about
          September 11th?
           Never forget what happened this day.  Never go back
          to your comfortable arena's. America your freedom
          shall never be as it was again. You have to stand
          behind our President and fight terrorists. Do not take
          things so for granted.
          Please do not place a building on ground zero. For
          me it represents the place where my partners and
          beloved staff are and no one should place a
          building over them.
           Put a memorial wall with
          beautiful gardens with water running were people can
          come and feel what  they need too. Were there can
          come and say goodbye or just remember.
          To have some peace and closure.
          (let me design a monument even though it is not my
          field) It would be from my heart, and that
          represents the hearts of the world.
          America we must UNITE in every way "one nation under
          God".
          Hold on to what made this country great. Hold one
          another tight in love and be faithful and true.

          Did you fly an American flag after the events of
          September 11th? Have your feelings about the
          American flag changed as a result of September 11th?

           I am a stronger American than ever. Loyal and
          faithful.
