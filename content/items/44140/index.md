        One of the residents of the Health Care Center where I work had the tv on in the lobby. I was watching the reports of the first plane collision when I saw the second collision and realized this was no accident.
        For the remainder of my work day I kept the radio on by my desk, wondering how many targets would be hit.
        That evening I watched tv at home and saw which area churches were having prayer meetings. I picked one and went to pray with a standing-room-only crowd.
        I had a deep desire to memorialize this loss of lives. Over the next few weeks this poem developed in my being:

        Holy Smoke
        Twin towers--
        funeral pyers.
        Common cremation
        of ninety nations.
        Communion of ash:
        Sacred mass of the
        massacred.
        Bread unleavened
        of nine-eleven
        to the world served.

        Kathryn McKee Hendrix

        funeral pyers=unwilling incineration of the living.
        ninety nations represented among those lost.
        communion of ash-we all breathe the air they became.
        bread unleavened=the rapid departure of a large number
              of people as in Exodus 12:34 &39.
