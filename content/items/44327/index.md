      text: |-
        I was at home in Clearwater, Florida. I didn't have
        the tv or radio on at the time so I didn't know that two Commercial Jet planes had crashed into the World Trade
        Center until my daughter stopped by and yelled, "A plane
        crashed into the World Trade Center"!!  I turned on the
        tv immediately and there, live, was the World Trade Center
        with thick black smoke and flames billowing from the
        windows. By the time I turned the tv on, both towers had
        been hit, but someone caught the second plane as it hit
        tower two, on film, and that particular segment was shown
        over again, several times. I remember thinking, "Those
        poor people"!!  I have flown a few times and I don't know
        why, but I've always flown United, and when I learned that
        one of the planes that flew into one of the towers was
        a United Airlines Jet, I remember thinking that I could
        have been on that plane. That was a scarey thought. And
        the same words kept running through my head, "Those poor
        people, those poor people".
             I was glued to the tv, horrified at what I was seeing,
        but still having to watch. I shed a lot of tears during that time, thinking of all those innocent people who died
        a horrible death all because some crazed maniac thought he
        was doing God's will.  Still today, as we approach the
        one year anniversary of that horrible tragedy, the tears
        fall as I think of all the survivors and the loved ones
        they will never see on earth again. Never can they hold
        them, kiss them, or talk to them. Never again can they say,
        "I love you". GOD BLESS THEM AND GOD BLESS AMERICA!
