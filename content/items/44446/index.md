      text: I remeber September 11th very well. I lived in Michigan.I was a sophmore at Plymouth-Canton High School. I started the day as usually. I went to my first to classes, which by my second class, the terror had all ready begun. I arrived to my 3rd pr. class to discover the t.v. in the room was on and tuned to CNN. I found that to be wierd. Then as i took my seat, I began to understand what had happened. CNN kept playing the scene of the first plane crashing in to the first tower. I remeber the news achors not know what to report as for why this was happing, just that the first tower had been hit. My teacher wanted to start class so the t.v. was turned off, but thanks to the other teachers who were still watching the event unfold, one of them came in and informed us that again the second tower had been hit. Now my class was confused and wanted to know more, but class still continued. Then agian the same teacher came in a informed us that the Pentagon had been hit. By this time 3rd pr. was over and I was at lunch. I gather with some of my friends and all agreed that we wanted to figuire out what is going on. In all most every class we pasted the t.v. was on and tuned to the news, and you could hear even in the news achors voice, that what was happening has horrific and unbelievable. I finally stopped in a history class that had to big screen t.v.'s and stood and watch the pictures that came on the screen. I saw people run, hurt, bloody, the planes crashing, firemen and police men. It was hard to imagen that what I was seeing was a city. More than that a city that I knew and had visited a few summers ago. It was hard to imagen such a painful, scary event was happening here in the U.S. and that it wasn't an accident. Everyone at my school was talking about the planes crashing in to the Trade Towers. Many sharing their memories of past visits there and how they had a family memeber there or near there. That day we all that of it as 4 comerical planes accidentally crashing. But soon the next day in turned into terrorist attacks agaisnt the United States of America. That right there, terrorist attacks, scaried me the most and opened another world to us in in the U.S. That event changed me. I realize now where I live and how I need to live my life.
