      text: On September 11th, 2001, my day started like any other day. Graduating from High school in June, i was still adjusting to my new work schedule and espcially getting up at 6 a.m. to be on time. I was employed at The Community Medical Center,a Level 2 Trauma Center in Northeast Pennsyalvania about two hours away from New York City. When i arrived at work that morning it was depressing to have to work inside and not be outiside enjoying the  last bit of summer. I was the only Orderly on shift that morning and surpisingly i was having a wonderful day with only two or three tasks for me to completer until about 8:30 when the first transport for the Catherization lab came in. I Made my way to the Cath Lab on the Seconjd floor of the facility and was greated by my co-workers preparing for the first procedures of the day. As i was preparing a cot in the corner of the room, my day dreaming was interupted by the gasp of one of the Nurses stating she couldn't believe what was happeneing. Being a inquisitve 18 yr old i had to see for myself. I walked over to the small Tv that people had been watching and was automaticlly speechless. The view of thick black smoke billowing from the Trade Towers was UN-EXPLAINABLE!
