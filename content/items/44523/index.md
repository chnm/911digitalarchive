        September 11, 2001.                                                             Robert Litvinchuk



        On September 11, 2001, at 8:46 a.m. (New York time), I still lied on my bed sleeping while the first air plane crash into the world trade center. About an hour later 9:03 a.m., I was awake watching the live coverage of the world trade center attacks when the second plane crashed into the second tower. This destructive image didnt affect me at all. As soon as I turned on the TV, and saw the replayed video of the first plane crash, I knew that this was a terrorist attack, and the start of a whole new fear; Terrorism. I got to school at 7:30 a.m. (California time). Everyone in class was talking about the start of a war against the enemy they didnt know. I corrected them by telling asking them how are you going to have a war if you dont know the enemy? They looked at me funny and rolled their eyes into another conversation. At my age at the time, I was pretty mature about world conflicts and struggles because I was into revolutionary music.
