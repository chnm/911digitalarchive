         I was in Mrs. Gaydon's social studies class in 2nd period

        know what was going on.  I was having mixed emotions.  I had

        to the United States of America?  What did we do wrong?

        It was so shocking what had happened.I didn't know if I
        wanted to go home or not, but I was happy to be with my
        classmates and best friends.  I was happy to be alive,but
        at the same time I felt bad that it had happened to all
        of those people and I was safe and sound at school.  It
        took a few days for it to actually sink in that this had
        happened to "the land of the free".I was upset, but happy
        at the same time.It changed my view on the U.S.A.  I
        appreciate life more now.
