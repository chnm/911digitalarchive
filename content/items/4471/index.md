      text: On September 11, 2001 I received a phone call from my brother, which was unusual because he never calls me that early in the morning.  I heard him screeming "Oh my  God, the world is on fire" I couldnt understand him at first because I was still half asleep. I asked him to calm down and repeat what was just said. He told me to turn on the TV and I asked what channel? I didnt need to know, It was on every channel.  Images of the World Trade Center on fire. All kinds of thoughts went in my head.  What happened? I told my family that I loved them over and over. I will never forget that day. Today is September 11, 2002. Although its been 1 year.
