I remember just sitting there and feeling this overwhelming feeling come over me, that it wasn't possible. Then I went on to the other classes of the day and there were many rumors about hijacked planes flying around all the major cities and some of the being gunned down by the air force. My mother worked at logan airport and I was a bit worried about her. However, close to the end of the day when I was in the science lab, one of the members of the faculty, came up to me, and said that my mother had just called from the house and said that everything was alright. That lifted a great bruden off my shoulders. It wasn't until I got home and watched the news that I got the whole story, that both world trade center towers had fallen down after being struck by hijacked planes, and that a hijacked plane crashed somewhere in pennsylvannia, and another plane was flown into part of the pentagon. When I found out about all these tragedies I was struck with disbelief and amazment, I've talked to alot of people about what happened, but I think its just one of those things in life that are just too hard, or even impossible to put into words.
