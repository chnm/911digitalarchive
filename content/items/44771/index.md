      text: I had just moved to Seattle and woke up that morning to the phone ringing. It was my mom and she told me that something terrible had happened - to turn on the television. By that time it was already 11am on the east coast and the news stations kept playing footage of the towers coming down, over and over. It took me a while to comprehend what had happened, it was as if my brain couldn't process something so horrible. I just sat on my bed and cried as I watched the exodus out of Manhattan, the people covered in dust and thought about my sister who lives there. I remember not being able to turn off the tv, I just wanted to hear one piece of good news- that things were going to be okay. I kept thinking about the people who got up that morning to go to work, kissed their kids goodbye, and never came back.
