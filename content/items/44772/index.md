      text: |-
        I work at a 4th/5th grade school.  I heard about the attacks and immediately turned on the tv to see the first building come down.  My heart was in my throat.  I was really terrified, but had to retain composure for all the children.  We were told to tell the children that something had happened to our country and they were instructed to go home and ask their parents about it.  We felt this was something that the parents might want to handle personally as everyone handles grief in their own way.

        For weeks on in - I felt a lonliness - I couldn't watch the tv without crying and feeling so empty.  For the first time in my life I was as scared as a child even though I was 40.  I have never felt like our security was an issue in the United States because we have always had such freedom.  My own children asked question after question.  I still have my daughters journal from last year.  She was in first grade.  Her entry for September 11, 2001 was ....
        "The bildings fell down".  She drew the Trade Towers and a sad face.  It broke my heart.

        Then after the shock of it all it renewed my faith in America to see how many people responded in compassionate and patriotic ways.  Through something so tragic, we have gained....we are ONE.
