        The Lessons of September 11th

        On that fateful September morning,
        Many lives were changed forever
        With the crash on the Pentagon,
        In Pennsylvania, and at the World Trade Center

        We cried out in anger
        And wept in our grief,
        We watched scenes of horror
        That cut to the deep

        Though many cried,
        Where was God in all this!
        But He saw all that happened,
        God was there in our midst

        He was saddened by the
        Death and destruction caused
        And He knows of the
        Loved ones weve lost

        But theres other reasons why all this has happened
        There are many things He wants us to learn
        See, love and compassion and unity
        Are some things we have seen in return.

        Respect for our God-given freedom
        Is another thing we also must value,
        We often overlook the price that was paid
        By many men and women, just so you could live today.

        We must remember that freedom was something
        That Washington and his men wouldnt sacrifice.
        In signing that Declaration of Independence
        It was for freedom that they would give their very lives

        From the trenches of Europe to the jungles of the Pacific,
        Through the death and destruction and tears
        People have fought for their freedom and country
        They gave their lives for what they held very dear

        Ever since September 11th,
        Our lives have never been the same,
        People have lost loved ones buried under the rubble
        And others may lie unnamed

        From all that has happened
        Through the tragedy of all this,
        We know love, compassion, unity
        And our God-given freedom are priceless.
