I was at work and we were in the middle of a staff meeting when the business owners' Mother called and said.. turn the T.V. on.. we are under attack!!! We all stood in disbelief as to what we were seeing on the television.  I attempted to call my daughter, Shawna Humphrey Spigone, but of course was unable to get through the D.C. area.  Shortly after seeing the chaos, my phone rang, thank God, it was my daughter.  She said "Mom, I am standing outside of the Pentagon, I borrowed someones Cel Phone, we are staged and getting ready to go inside, I just wanted to call and tell you that I love you"  All I could say through the held back tears was "Be Careful and I love you too" .. Needless to say, I was not much use to anyone for the rest of the day, until many hours later, she called and said I am back at the station and we are all okay.  I thank God for that every day!
