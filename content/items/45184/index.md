      text: I was in my car on the way to the first MOPS(Mothers of Preschoolers)meeting of the year.  It was a beautiful fall day and I was listening to the radio when the first report came in about the WTC being hit.  My first thought was, "This is a bad attempt at a joke, even for a radio morning show."  Over the next few minutes, though, I realized that this was REAL, not a gimmick.  I was numb at first and then saddened and enraged at the nerve of these people, whoever they were.  All I could think of was all the MOPS in New York who had just lost their husbands, brothers, fathers, as well as all the children who lost their mothers.  I did not watch any TV coverage for two days because I just could not bear to see the images played over and over.  My two children, ages 5 and 10 at the time, had many questions such as "Why did somebody do something so mean?" and "Is Dad going to have to fight a war?"  Those were questions that not even I knew the answers to.  My personal relationship with God is what gave me peace and sanity among the chaos and destruction of that day.
