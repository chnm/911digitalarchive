I go to school less that 5 away and that is where President Bush decided he should address the country from. It is on of the safest military bases in the nation. I went to the bathroom and called my mom. I told her to call the school and to check me out so that I could go home because I wanted to see what was really going. My mom did as I asked and I went home. Within minutes I was in my back yard watching Air Force one Flying over my home. I got a calls from alot of younger people at school whos mom told them they could leave school, but they needed a ride. I drive a very small car. We waited there for what seemed like ever. There were people stacked into every room of my 5 bedroom home. I asked my mom to get food for people on the way home. She didn't relize how many. I have never seen such an outpouring of love. I am one of those people who doesn't really fall into the typical High school catagory because I don't just have on group of friends. It was amazing to sit in own home and see the Head-cheerleader comfort the goth, or the biggest nerd in school comfort the school jock. My school is just a small example of what our country did.
