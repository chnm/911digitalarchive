  'NMAH Story: Story':

      text: |-
        I was just getting out of bed (California time) to put my older daughter in
        carpool.  My husband came in to say a plane had hit the World Trade Center in
        NY.  I immediately assumed it was an accident, and said how awful.  I decided
        not to turn on the TV as I knew it would be on the news and didn't want my 3
        year old to see it.  When I took my daughter to pre-school, there was a sign up
        that said: Today we are cancelling school.  You are invited to just stay with your
        child. It's a time for us to be together.  This was my first inkling that this was
        more than a freak accident.  We stayed at the preschool till almost lunch time.
        The parents at the preschool talked in hushed whispers and shared what
        information was known.  We all had the urge to protect our children from this
        horrific event for as long as possible.  I called in to work and heard that the
        University would be closing soon, so not to bother coming in at all.



        On the way home, my daughter said bad guys bumped a building with an
        airplane and now there was a fire.  Could we go see it.  I said the firefighters
        were helping to put it out and we would need to stay out of the way, and let
        them do their job to help people.
