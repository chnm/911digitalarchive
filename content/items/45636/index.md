      text: I am employeed for a bank in Jacksonville, FL.  My teamates and I were in a team meeting the morning of the 11th.  When we came out of the meeting a woman in another department who has a small tv on her desk had it on and there was a crowd gathered around her.  We asked what was going on, and someone said that a plane had crashed into the World Trade Center building, it wasn't until about 30 minutes later that we learned another plane had hit the 2nd building and then we also found out that the plane had been hijacked from Boston.  Our manager brought out a larger tv and everyone from our floor gathered around watching the news coverage, I just could not believe what I was watching.  The destruction and chaos was unbelievable. Then the report came in about the Pentagon being hit by yet a third plane that was hijacked. We then learned that terrorist had hijacked the planes and because we work in a tall building we began to wonder about our safety.  Management was assembled and agreed to let everyone in the department go home to be with our families.  I remember that all I could think of was getting to my 2 year old daughter's daycare and making sure that she was okay.  We then went to my 15 year old son's school to pick him up.  There were many students that were scared and wanted to go home and were trying to call their parents.  At some point between picking up my daughter and arriving at my son's school, I remember praying "Lord, please touch the lives of those that have committed these acts and guide any more that might be waiting to committ more and show them the realization of what has been done to innocent lives"
