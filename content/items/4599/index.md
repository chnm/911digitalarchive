        For Chris Hanley, Friend, Advisor, Missing:

        I keep wondering, what am I supposed to do?
        I existed, until two days ago, with purpose

        Shake like a giant King Kong.

        Able to swat planes out of the sky,
        Then perhaps I could dream about
        Bananas again, and days in the sun.
