        I live in England and on September 11th 2001 I had just got home from shopping, it was about 2.30, the children were due home at 3. I turned on the tv and saw the aeroplane crashing into the twin towers, then the second plane. It was on every channel, I stared in shock and horror and my first thought was, "Oh my God, my sisters there."
        You see my sister Becky Wood was supposed to be in New York that day to return home from a long trek across America.I remember just sitting and crying and then phoning my mum who told me that the last she heard from Becky was when she was at the pentagon the previous day on a sight seeing trip.
        My tv was on all day and it wasn't till early evening that I had the phone call to say that she was alive but stranded in New York.
        I remember thinking that so many people had been affected by that day and I felt for them all.
        It was an agonising few days before my sister was back in England.Their tour guide had stuck with the group despite having lost contact with his own family.
