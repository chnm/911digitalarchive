        It was just a typical Tuesday at Penn Tank Lines, Inc.  We were just doing our billing when our mechanic came in and said that he heard that the World Trade Center had been bombed.  We had a television for conference videos.  We set it up and to our surprise it was true.  Or at least we thought until the second plane hit.  Standing there in disbelief, I looked outside to see the bright sunshine and wonder how did the plane not see the buildings.  Then it hit like a brick in a pond; the Pentagon has now been hit.  I just couldn't believe what I was witnessing.  I told my husband that I wanted to get our four children from school and I wanted them NOW.  Shortly after him calming me down, I recieved a phone call from a daughter of one of our co-workers.  It seems that she couldn't locate her father who was out on a business trip for our company.  I immediately called corporate to see if anyone knew what flight he was taking, if any one had heard from him.  It was then I started to realize what kind of trouble we were in.  I mean here is a family man who, thank God didn't, could of been on the wrong plane and been lost just like all of the other souls.  I just can't imagine what the family members of the victims are and have been going through.

        I did go to our corporate office that day which is only 3 miles away from where I worked.  I remember I kept looking in the sky for a plane to fall from the sky.  I remember feeling helpless.
