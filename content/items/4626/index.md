      text: I was at home with my two children.  I was at home that morning doing my usual morning routine.  I turned on the television so my daughter could watch cartoons before we left for the day.  Instead what I saw will stay etched in my mind for the rest of my life, like so many other people.  The first plane had already crashed into Tower and a just sat there stunned. I didn't even leave my home that day.  I didn't know any one person directly that was at any of the sites.  But I still felt a loss as most Americans did.  Those we lost may, we will be greatly missed.  Those Brave Fireman, EMS workers, Police, you go out there every day for us, may god be with you.  To those you lost, you are in my prayers. We have great leaders in this country who stood strong, and gave us leadership, and courage when it would have been easy to run and hide, but America has never done that and it showed the world that.  Still to this day the National Anthem will bring tears to my eyes for the pride I have for the WONDERFUL COUNTRY that I live in.  And today 1 year later my FLAG is still flying.
