I called my husband to make sure he got up for work and he told me a plan crashed into the WTC, looked like an accident, and said it was on the news.  I plugged my TV into the cable and watched.  I ran down the hall to another S.S. teacher and asked if she heard and to turn on the TV.  I stopped at the English teachers room and mentioned something. I proceeded to map out for my students where all of this was taking place, being it was geography class afterall.  Chaos in D.C., questions from my students, I responded as best as I could at that point.  All I could say is "history in the making" and we were in the middle of the nation (Fargo, ND), to calm their fears.  All I could think of was how fortunate my husband's aunt and uncle, who live in NYC, were visiting Fargo the night before.  Then I thought of an ex-boyfriend whom I dated off an on, who lived in NYC, and knew he worked in the financial sector...it took days to get a hold of him, phone lines were jammed.
