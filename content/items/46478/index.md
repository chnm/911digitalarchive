      text: The morning of Speptember 11, 2001 was like any other day.  I woke up and got ready for school as usual. Met my friends at school and talked about sports or what we watched on tv last night.  Not too much was really happening, but all of that changed during second hour.  What I remember most is that I was in my civics class and someone came and pulled our teacher out side the classroom to talk.  During that time the other students and I started to get suspious.  We could tell right away that something was wrong.  My teacher came back into the classroom turned on the tv and that's when we say the second plane crash into the Twin Towers. Nobody really said anything.  I think the whole class had mixed feelings.  I felt confused, because I really didn't know what was going on, but yet sad because of what my eyes were seeing.  Once I found out more about what had happened I felt angry, because I couldn't understand why someone would be so selfish.  I call the high-jackers selfish, because they not only killed many people that day, they also took them away from there loved ones.  The people they killed never called them a dirty name or pushed them in the mud.  I mean they had families and lives, but that all ended that day.  When a selfish act ruined many, many lives.  The rest of the day is pretty blurred. I do remember that every class I went to we watched the news.  I almost think that we were looking for some kind of hope.  We were just waiting for someone to tell us we were dreaming or that it was just a joke, but unfortunatly it is all very real.
