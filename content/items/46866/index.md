        On September 11th I woke up as if it was any normal day, I took a shower, brushed my hair and prepared for school. When I walked into my living room my step-father had cnn news on. I saw the first tower that had been hit. Smoke was coming out the side and fire was blazing. The first thing I thought was of all the people on the airplane and all their loved ones, so I prayed for them. Then at fatal thing happened, the first tower fell; right then and there I knew thousands of people had lost their lives so again I prayed. Soon it was time for me to go to school but before I left I heared faintly on the television that the second tower had been hit. I glanced at the TV and it showed the impact between the tower and the airplane.
             I got in my brothers car and he turned on the radio; on every channel there was news about the towers. I sat in the car and for 45 minutes, listened to the updates on the towers, and at one point I remember the person on the radio announcing that the second tower had fell. When I arrived at school that was all everyone was talking about. In the Purple Penguin, a little school store, I watched the TV that was placed in there and watched them show the plane hitting the second tower and both of the towers falling.
