      text: I remember lying awake that morning in bed as the phone call came shortly before 7 AM mountain time.  My best friend Mark explained to me that a plane had hit the World Trade Center.  I spent the next few minutes talking to him as I watched the news.  Moments later I watched live the second plane hit the south tower.  I could not believe what I had just seen.  At that moment, I remember a horrible and tragic feeling hitting my heart.  Although at that moment I knew no one in New York City, it still was heart wrenching to see that.  I called my father as I started to head to school.  One scene that I will always remember is walking into the student union at New Mexico State University, and seeing a huge mass of students transfixed on the big screen television.  The rest of that day played out as many who I saw were very low key and mournful of the events that took place that morning. That will be the day I remember, as Tuesday, September 11, 2001.
