        Firemen of 9/11/01
        Tommy Haskell
        Timmy Haskell

        Who are these men,
        these brave hearts,
        these men of valor?

        They are your sons,
        your husbands,
        your fathers,
        and your brothers.

        These mere mortal men
        grew inside you dear mothers,
        held you loving brides,
        greeted you at days end fearless children,
        and loved you wombmates.

        Some will not return home
        to smells of holiday dinner,
        as the snow blankets the wounded earth.
        Their seats will be empty
        and their plates untouched.
        But set a place for them,
        somewhere they do partake in the feast.

        9/14/01
        Tammy Nuzzo-Morgan


        For  Vincent Carbone's
        Aunt Dawn (Haskell) Carbone's brothers
        Tammy
