      text: I work the overnight shift for a large airline in the mid-west. I had been home a few minutes and decided to run some errands. When I returned, I turned on the television to one of the cable news stations and thought I was watching something from a movie promo. I turned it up and was horrified. The things I remember most about those days were going to work at night with no inbound flights and our ramp literally full of airplanes from every carrier you could think of. I also remember the silence. There were no aircraft flying! I was 37 years old then and I have never lived more than 20 miles from an airport. All of my life I heard jet engines in the background.
