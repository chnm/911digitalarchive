  'NMAH Story: Story':

      text: |-
        I was working at Allegheny Airlines in Flight Operations. I remember the shift was very quiet.Excellent weather, no Air Traffic control problems etc. I was sipping coffee, and chatting with the dispatcher next to me, when a Crew Scheduler turned around in her chair, and said a plane just hit the WTC. We thought it was probably a sightseeing plane, or traffic helicopter or some such thing.
        We eventually did turn the TV on in the office, and saw the fire. I phoned to Cape Cod to my Mom and told her to put the TV on, and while talking to her, the second plane hit. The fact it was a second airplane really didn't register as we thought it was a tape of the first airplane.
        Around this time, we got word of the Air Traffic System shutting down till noon, then 2pm and then indefinately. The timing of the crashes(Pentagon,WTC) were such that our planes were mostly in their hubs. After confirming all our aircraft were on the ground, I went out for air and a co worker came out for air shortly after and mentioned the crash of flt. 93. She also mentioned a prison was nearby. Through all this, we realized how close we worked to Three Mile Island (our office is 2 or 3 miles from TMI) we wondered if it were a target. Soon after, I gave up my computer to crew scheduling so they could move around displaced crews and I went home.
