        On September 11, 2001, I was at my boyfriend's house when the phone rang.  It was his mother calling to tell us to turn on the TV.  I couldn't believe what I was looking at.  It was like watching a movie.  My friend, Natasha, worked on the 97th floor of tower 1.  I tried getting a hold of her, but no answer.  I didn't stop trying until I saw the towers fall.  My heart sank into my stomach and I knew she was gone.  The only thing I could feel at the time was anger and hate.  Natasha was a beautiful woman who had everything going for her.  Moving to New York was a huge deal for her.  She had always wanted to live and work in the big apple.  I miss her terribly, and cannot believe that 3 years have gone by since the attack.  I have not been to New York since 1999, and I'm fearing going back for emotional reasons.  My heart goes out to all the families who lost loved ones 3 years ago in the attack.  We must move on, though.  God bless you all!

        Sincerely,
        Sandra Owen
