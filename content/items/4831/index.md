        I was in a temporary duty status at Wright-Patterson AFB, in Ohio.

        I along with several collegues were attending a software class on that fateful day.  We had not taken a break all morning and finally broke for lunch about 1:00PM.  One of the first persons out the door went downstairs in the building we were in to the restuarant we had seen on our way to class.
