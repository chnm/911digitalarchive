        I, Julie Hoot was driving to work and over the radio I heard the news.  It was like a crude joke.  Actually it would have been great if it was such.  Unfortunately I arrived at work and it was confirmed even more, by the news on T.V.  I called my boyfriend John Asta and left a message since he was still asleep.  He is a Nasdaq Stock Trader. MSNBC was a morning thing for us and he was completely shocked at what was shone on the screen.  "It was like watching the movie DieHard." he said.  I think it was the longest day that I have experienced in a long time.  It is hard to believe that a year has passed since that day.  The patriotism that has come from that tragic day is still strong and will always stay that way.  All that lost their lives on that day will never be forgotten.  God Bless America.

        Julie Hoot and John Joseph Asta, Jr.
