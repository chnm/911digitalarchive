        When I first heard that a plane hit the World Trade Center, I thought it was the one in Dallas but Dallas is just 2 story high and then I realized that it was in New York.

        I started to cry for the lost ones whom did not even get a chance to get out.

        As the day went by, we watch the TV and listen to the radio and just could not beleive that could happen to us. When I heard the the Muslims were dancing in the street, I was angry but what they do not realize it that it could happen to them and it did because their family lives within the United States.

        As September 11, 2002 approaches, we all fear that it would happen again but I do not think that they would do it on the same date but it could happen anytime and we need to be ready to strike back.

        I have a nephew-in-law whom is in the Army and he has a wife and a 10 month old boy whom he is not with because he is out trying to protect our Nation and I hope that all service men and women will be able to come home to their ones soon.

        My heart goes out the families that lost either their spouse, son, daughter, grandson, granddaughter, nephew or niece. You are really the heroes because you had to go on living and taking over your family responibilities and I know that has to be really hard. I do not know what I would do if I lost my husband or stepdaughter but I know that I would look back at this day and see all the families that lost someone and that would give me the courage to go on because you did.

        God Bless each everyone of you and may he watch over you every day and night.

        God Bless.
