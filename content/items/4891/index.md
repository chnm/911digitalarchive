        I remeber being at college and i recieved a text message telling me about the attack on the world trade center. At first, i didnt really know much about the WTC. I got home later that day to see it all over the news and in our local papers. I was horrified. It was like something out of Die hard Movie. Although i never knew any victims of 9/11, Even now, it plays on my mind and sometimes I even dream about it, being there and watching it for myself. I dont know why. I have my own little Minute silences each year on september 11th wherever i am to remeber those innocent heros and victims who were taken away by such evil people. I cant write what i really think of these animals, but I'm sure everyone knows what I mean.
        If i could afford to, I would love to come over to visit Ground Zero and lay some flowers.

        Although I live miles away, I still never forget.

        Cheryl Spray, Nottingham, Uk.
