        Its funny how on days like September 11th we remember
        the silly things. I can remember what I did that morning
        before school, and what I ate for breakfast. I can remember
        what I was wearing and that I was in a fight with one of my
        friends. I remember going to first period just like any
        other day. Lucy and I spelled like grease because it we were
        cooking in foods class. We moved to second period just like
        any other day and I sat down in my seat. I looked outside
        the room and noticed that the teachers were running around
        like crazy in the hallways. I was confused but I just
        figured they were nuts. Then Mrs. Harlin ran into the room
        and told my teacher to turn on the tv. We watched as the
        first tower burned, we saw the flames and the huge amount
        of smoke coming from the building. My heart skipped a beat
        and I felt like someone had just punched me in the stomach.
        All I could think of to do was pray. When I opened my eyes
        I watched the second plane fly into the second tower. Anger
        and confusion came over me, I had no idea why anyone could
        think of doing something so terrible. I don't think I have
        ever watched the news so much in my life. All night and all
        the next day I watched, eagerly waiting for news.
             One year later I sat in school watching the towers fall
        and tears fell down my face. It is one year later and I am
        still as confused as I was when it happened. How is it
        possible to have so much hate in your heart to do something
        of this magnitude. In the end, after all of the lives that
        were lost and all of the pain and suffering that we endured
        I can't help but think about all of the lives that were
        saved and all of the people who are still living in this
        world.
             The months after September 11th were filled with many
        different emotions. Many people looked to the Lord for help,
        so I can't help but think that God had this planned so he
        could bring more people closer to him. Everywhere I looked
        I saw the American flag, it had new meaning now. God belss
        America was on every poster and every sign around town,
        the whole nation was united as one. I just wish that it
        wouldn't have had to take a tragic event like this to bring
        all of us together.
              I look back on the year and the only thing I am sure
        of is that I am going to live my life to the fullest and not
        ever take anything for granted, because you never know when
        it can all be taken away from you. My thoughts and prayers
        go out to the families and friends of all of the victims.
        May God bless them.
