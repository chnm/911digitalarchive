      text: Well, I was taking my daughter to school at 8:40 oklahoma time,  I was listening to the radio and all they kept saying was they thought a bomb had went off at the WTC.  I was like oh no not again.  Well I dropped her off and in the 3 minutes it takes to get home from her school they had said it was a terrorist attack and that smoke was billowing out from the building.  I rushed inside to turn on the television and I still get choked up at the absolute horror I saw! I couldnt believe it.  I remember feeling my 6 month pregnant belly as to comfort my baby.  I just sat there in tears and awe.  I began to pray for everyone involved. Then the second plane hit.  I cried more.  I went to wake my husband up.  We just sat there for a long time watching this.  I remember going to pick my daughter up from school early that day.  I was pregnant with her when the Federal Building was blown up here in Oklahoma City.  I remember having to explain why those plains hit the buildings to my daughter.  She and I prayed together.  It is something I will not forget.  I have deep sorrow for everyone who lost a loved one or their own life.
