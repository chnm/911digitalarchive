      text: I had gone to San Diego to a conference for work.  This was my first plane trip.  I was scared as it was being in an unfamiliar city by myself.  I was getting ready when I turned on the television.  I came back into the room when the first plane flew into the first towers.  I thought "Oh my god, those poor people". At this time airlines were shut down and they were not letting anyone out of the city.  We had gone to a meeting to decide if we were going to cancel the conference or keep going.  I was sitting there with my Emporia State jacket on when a lady came up to me and asked if I was from Emporia Kansas.  I told her I was.  She asked how I was getting home.   I told her I had no idea but if she was going to rent a car I would go in on it.  That is what we did.  It took awhile before someone would rent us one.   I know it was an act of god that we had met each other.  I will never forget 9-1-1.
