        It was an normal work day, untill the bad news came.
        I was working @ H&M - Utrecht in the Netherlands.
        Helping customers, when my floormanager came to tell the terrible news.

        Just could not believe what I was hearing.
        What was she talking about?!! Untill I came home and saw the images of the plane crashing into the towers.
        I just still can not understand, why?

        I can not imagine what it must be like.
        But I can tell you, my love goes out to them who must missed that one special person or people..and the departments who helped and reach out there hands to save them. And knowing they could die, and the ones who died for them.

        This week I saw the reconstruction documentary about 9 | 11
        And yesterday, 9|11 after 5 yrs. It really hit me in the face... Cause it broke my heart to imagine what it must have been to be there and feeling so helpless.

        I really hope that it never never happen again..
        But for who where there and did happend to.
        I'll pray 4 you.


        We Shall Never Forget!!!

        Love, Peace & Unity,

        The Lily Angel.
