      text: I really don't have an exciting story to tell. I did not know anyone who was in any of the buildings, or planes, I do not know anyone who parished in the tragedy.  I was at home with my best friend, Tammy , when we decided to see what was on our talk show before putting on cartoons for my 4 year old. When i turned on the t.v. we froze. It was so devestating to watch, knowing so many must have lost their lives. Now the anniversary of this tragedgy has passed and even though I didn't know anyone who died, I couldn't watch to much t.v. that day, it was just to heartbreaking. I wish I had something inspirational to say to those who lost their loved ones, or to anyone who doesn't know how to feel or who to trust anymore, but all I can say is one day we will all be reunited with our lost loved ones, (I really believe that)and until then the good Lord will take care of us all, and if someone should try to harm us again, He will be there to take us home.
