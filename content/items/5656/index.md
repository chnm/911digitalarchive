        1.]How did you witness history on September 11th? Share your experience.

        I had just watched the Lynnhaven Middle School bus leave at approximately 8:25 AM, and I sat down to watch "Good Morning America". At approximately ten minutes to nine o'clock they announced that a small plane had hit The World Trade Center. They planned the cameras to view The World Trade Center buildings smoke was billowing out of one of them. I just sat there and stared at the T.V. in disbelief. Shortly after there i watched a second plane hit the second tower. I continued to watch T.V. all day long as planes hit the Pentagon and in Pennsyvania.

        2.]Has your life changed because of September 11th 2001? If so tell us how.

        I think everybodys life changed in some way that day. I feel prowd to be an American, and I have more Pride in the Human spirit.

        3.]What do you think should be remembered about September 11th?

        That we as a nation will do anything to protect our rights to freedom.

        4.]Did you fly an American flag after the events of September 11th? Have your feelings about the American flag changed as a result of September 11th?

         Yes I didn't fly one on a pole, but I displayed in my home and on my car.  The meaning of the flag is greater. I have a new found sense of pride in the U.S.
