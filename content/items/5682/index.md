        I remember that day like it was yesterday.  But, beore I get into that I first of all want to express my deepest sympathy to those families and friends who lost loved ones in any of the three terror attacks.  I can only imagine what is must be like to have that happen.
        I was working like normal when my friend came in and said a plane had hit the WTC, I thought to myself surely that must be a mistake,then I went to where we had the TV on CNN and I saw the second plane hit.  I could hardly believe my eyes.  I was shocked.  President Bush was only a few miles away in Sarasota getting ready to ready to the kids.  That day has changed my life forever.  I have a friend who works for the Disaster Unit for THE SALVATION ARMY in Tampa, FL.and he was called to go to New York City to help out.  I talked to him when he came back and the stories were unreal.  I remember going to church the next Sunday after the attach and so many people there, it has been felt by everyone.  I have cried many times when I see pictures, read stories hear songs like " Proud to be an American " .  I am so far from the WTC but, yet it has impacted me for life and I hope I will always feel the way I felt after that, the patrotism and so happy to be free.  I pray that someday we will have World Peace and we in America will never have to feel afraid again.  I pray for each family and friend who lost a loved one, I pray each day will get a litte easier for you but, we should never forget how that one day has impacted each and everyone of us.  May God Bless You all and GOD BLESS AMERICA!!!!
