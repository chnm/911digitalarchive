The planes that hit the towers were going to California so they knew that they were full of fuel.  When I first heard about all of this I thought that it was not real but it just never went away and I just figured out that it would not go away. It was real as real as real can be.  An hour after the planes had crashed the buildings fell to the ground because of weak structure. The heat from the fire weakened the structure.
