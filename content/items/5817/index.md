        Today, September 11, 2006, has been a reflection on the past five years.  My youngest son was only in first grade and now he is in sixth grade. The 9/11 attacks only seemed like it happened months ago not years ago.
        My heart was heavy as I watched CNN this morning replaying the events of that horrible day. For a moment I had to tell myself that the WTC and the pentagon attacks happened five years ago.
        I cried on my way to work listening to the names of the deseased. I also was angry that most of the radio stations and TV programs went on about their normal buisness. Like America forgot about 9/11.
        I was in Madison five years ago. 70 miles from my family. I live in West Milwaukee. The horror and the fear that I felt that day will always be a part of me.
        My heart aches for everyone who died that day. I only hope that God has mercy on the souls who participated in the loss of innocent lives.
        God Bless America!!!
        Never forget!!!
        Love your neighbor!!
        Ann M. Wiese
        West Milwaukee, WI
