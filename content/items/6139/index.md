        After 26 1/2 hours of labor my son, Tracy, was born.  He was born on September 10, 2001. My mom knew that the two of them were flying on a plane similiar to the ones that flew into the twin towers, but she didn't know what route they were taking to get there.  I believe it was September 12 that we found out that there plane was supposed to be going to Washington D.C., but they got re-routed so they were safe in Poland.
