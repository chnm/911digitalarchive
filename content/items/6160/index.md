        I was sleeping in my bedroom when my friend's sister rushed in and woke me up.  She wouldn't stop screaming.  I walked groggily out into the living room.  I saw the plane crash into the second tower. Cool Movie I thought...then I saw the the words CNN Live go across the screen.  My jaw dropped and I fell to the floor.  My Uncle was in New York on business that week, and I had no idea if he was ok.  I sat and prayed for almost an hour, then picked up the phone and called my Aunt.  She said he was fine.  He had taken a train out of Manhattan earlier that morning.  My family was one of the lucky few to have him returned home safely, and we know that.  But I still grieve for those who were lost.  I did not know any of them personally, but they were my brothers and sisters.  They were all Americans.

        Now it is one year later, and I remember it like it was yesterday.  Those towers crashing...They might've won that battle, but we will win the war.

        I saw something that touched my heart today...a comic strip in today's paper.  It was a comic strip written in a childs shaky hand saying "God, please don't let any child grow up without a mommy or daddy again."  I think that says it all.

         September 11, 2002
