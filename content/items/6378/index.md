      text: My name is Mary Ellen Casey.  I was born in 1953 and live in Bristol, Conn.  After the death of John F. Kennedy and the Vietnam war that there would be some kind of Peace in the world. I had the car radio tuned to a college campus station and as always change it after to a National Public radio station.  I did so after mass was over and i was on my way home.  I thought I had heard the annoucer say somthing about a plane hitting one of the Trade Towers.  When I got home my mother had the t.v. on and it was true.
