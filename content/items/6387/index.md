        It is hard to begin of the things that I felt and saw on T.V. When the first plane hit my heart sanked, I have seen plane crashes prior, but when the second one hit I knew the United States was in trouble. I turned on my computer to see if my son had emailed me. My son is in the Navy and his job along with others is to take care of situations when needed, "he is encharge of missals". No message from my son, I then emailed him to wish him the best along with his crew on the ship. I couldn't go to work that day, I needed to be at home watching T.V., and if needed to be by my phone and computer in case my son needed to reach me. My heart is still saden of what had happened on September 11th,2001. I will never forget that day, nor will I forget the pain that all of us Americans felt.Our trust was robed from our country. No one will ever be able to give that back to us.  I wish those to be blessed with what they have, since their our others that have lost alot.
