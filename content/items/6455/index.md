        September 11

        I first heard about the Sept.11 attack when I came to school because when I got off the bus there were kids shouting about it. That was when I heard about it. After that I went to my friends and we talked about it. I felt really bad because I knew that a lot of people died either in the building or trying to save people. I remember in homeroom when our principal came in and talked to us about it. He said that he was watching the morning news when it came on. Later, in P.E. we all got together and had a moment of silence. When I got home my mom and I watched the news. This is my story of the Sept.11.
