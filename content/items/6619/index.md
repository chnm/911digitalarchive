        Sept 11th

        	When sept 11th  happened I was in school ,I didn't know what was happening, because the teacher's didn't let us watch the TV in class rooms. When I came home I turned on the TV, and saw what had happened I was confused and shocked. Even if I am not from this country, but I still felt sad about the people in the World Trade Center that couldn't escape out.
        	When I saw the Twin Towers collapsing I was even more shocked because just think how many people where under the Twin Towers helping others. How many firefighters, police, and doctors where there helping people to get out. What I wished the most is to at least give blood for people that needed it ,and help them in anyway I could ,but I couldn't do that, because I was far away from NEW YORK. All I could do is watch how people were falling off the Towers and Watch how the Towers were collapsing. At the same time I was angry at the people that took over the plain , and crashing them in the World Trade Center.
        	Sept 11th changed a lot of people in United States, and even me about helping others, and carrying. I fell sad for the people that lost families in the Towers, plains, and in the
        Pentagon. Not only do I fell sad for the Twin Towers , but for the pentagon also. I am thankful every day to god that I am alive, and my family too. We will be always thankful to the firefighters, police, and doctors for helping the people at the Twin Towers.
