        In Memory of:
        Local 94's HEROS

        Charlie McGee
        John Griffin Jr
        Dave Williams
        Veto DeLeo

        I worked with Charlie McGee for seven years, he was a great
        co worker and a great friend.I didn't know the other guys too
        well, but the stories I heard about what these four
        men went through on that day was simply "HEROIC".

        OUR STORY

        My self and part of the crew were trapped in the lobby of
        7 wtc when the 1st tower collapsed. We had no idea that a
        plane let alone two planes had crashed into the towers.
        We knew there were explosions but we had made it through
        the 93 blast so we thought it was the same thing, just
        bigger. We had already assisted in evacuating the building,
        Thank god every body started evacuating on there own, and
        then we made the final evacuation announcement on the PA
        system and like I said all got out safe, the crew and myself
        stayed to man the building because of all the important
        equipment running, not knowing we were In any danger (again
        because of our experience with the 93 blast) and then it
        got dark and this tremendous screeching, thundering, and
        Rumbling started!!!And all I can see was the face of one
        of the crew who happened to being looking out the window of
        the lobby when this all started. For about 2 minutes I
        thought we were all going to DIE! We ran behind a wall laid
        on the floor and covered our heads and Prayed. I tell you it
        sounded like a missile and an earthquake was coming right at
        us at the same time. Well thank god it was neither.
        Well this eventually passed and we did not die. Now we all
        get up, look at each other, which was kind of difficult
        because of all the smoke, darkness and dust. We all panicked
        but we kept our heads, we were scrambling to get out of the
        building. Half of them went out thru the rumbled lobby, which
        they have a story of there own. Three others and myself went
        up one floor because we could not breathe and we knew
        exactly where the fire exits were on that floor which happen
        ed to be the Cafeteria. We went to one exit and there was no
        handle and the door was locked. We ran across the floor to
        the other side where the kitchen was, opened that door and
        smoke just poured out of the staircase so we closed that door.
        Now were running back to the other side of the floor to look
        out the window too see what was going on and that was useless,
        all you can see is all this smoke and dust. Now we are starting
        to get panicky and all we keep saying is we don't want to die!!
        We just made the decision to run out the smoke filled staircase,
        we wrapped our faces with wet towels and made a mad dash to the
        Street. We flew down the stairs literally. When we got out we
        went about a block behind the building, we all met up and
        started hugging and I mean hugging and happy to see each
        other. A few minutes go by and all of a sudden the floor
        starts shaking and you hear this rumbling and thundering
        again. We just took off running as fast as we can holding
        our heads again, but I had to look over my shoulder too see
        what was happening, I tell you it looked like the devil was
        coming, a big ball of smoke with huge hands and debris
        flying everywhere.

        Well we made it and I just had to share this story.
