        It was just another typical morning.  Or so I thought.  While heading up Route 495 North in Tewksbury, MA I turned on my radio.  I heard a woman reporter saying the World Trade Center had been hit.  At that time, it appeared to be a blast of smoke and fire.  No one knew exactly what had happened.  When I got to work every one was in shock!  How could this happen in the United States?  And why were we chosen?  As the day unfolded I continued to log on to CNN news from my computer.  Every time I heard more devastating news, I'd run into my boss' office and fill him in.  I work in Sales, and we didn't sell a thing that day.  We just couldn't pick up the phone and chat with our customers regarding our high tech solutions when people were dying.  I kept thinking about my deceased father who had served five years in the Army; he had come through the Battle of the Bulge with Patton.  I think had he been alive, he would have been deeply disturbed because he had seen the horrible effects of what bombs can do.  I was lucky...I didn't loose any one to that day.

        Marie A. Holzwasser
        255 North Road
        Unit 179
        Chelmsford, MA 01824
        September 11, 2002
