        September 11, 2001:  My office is in Staines, a town near Heathrow Airport, in England. It was my lunch break and I went out for a walk in the town with no particular aim other than to have a break.  On the spur of the moment I went into Dorothy Perkins, a ladies clothes shop, and a T shirt caught my eye - it was grey with the stars and stripes flag on the front - the stars were silver metal and the effect was eye-catching.  I have visited USA many times and love the flag, so I bought the T shirt.

        On returning to my office I was greeted with the news that the first tower had been struck.  My colleagues and I watched the TV screen in our Board Room with absolute disbelief and horror as the nightmare unfolded.  I had visited New York in 2000 and had been to the top of the WTC, so the experience seemed quite personal to me and I could not help imagining the scene as if I was there, too.

        I then remembered the T shirt and, on looking at the till receipt, saw that I had purchased it at UK time 13:43, USA time 08:43 - a matter of minutes before the first plane hit. I find this coincidence hard to understand.

        Naturally, I wore the T shirt that day and frequently thereafter and was glad to have this symbol to express my sorrow publicly.
