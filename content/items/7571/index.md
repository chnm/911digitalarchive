      text: I am a New Yorker.  Even though I was born in Miami, i moved up to NY with my family when I was 4 years old and was raised in Westchester County.  When I heard about the planes hitting the WTC, I was just about to start my reading lesson.  The Assistant Principal came into my room, walkie talkie in hand, and asked me if I was from New York.  Then she proceeded to tell me about the planes and by that time, one of the towers had already collapsed.  I asked if she could please take over my class, and I walked briskly, heart pounding because I knew of one person who worked in the WTC...my friend's husband.  When I saw what was being broadcasted on TV in the main office, I could not believe my eyes.  It was like a dream...a bad one. All I could think of "Jack is in there.  He's dead."  Well, luckily, I found out later, he had made it out.  But not without scars.  He will always have them emotionally.  It was a horrific day and I can only imagine what it must have been like for the survivors and those who never made it out.  It is a painful reminder of man's inhumanity to man.  I pray something of this magnitude will never happen again.
