        I  was  near  the  end  of  my  daily  28 mile  commute  to  work  when  the the  first  announcement  came  over  the  car  radio  that  an  airliner  had  struck  one  of  the  Twin Towers  in  NYC.  At  first  it  seemed  like  a  terrible  accident.

        Got  to  work  and  turned  on  the  radio  for  more  news of  the  plane  crash  only  to  hear  that  another  plane  had  crashed  into  the other  Twin Tower  and  then  the  awful  feeling  that  these  were  no  accidents.

        One  of  the  guys  in  the  office  went  out  to  buy  a  portable  TV  for  the  office  so  that  for  most  of  the  rest of  the  day,  we  stood  around  in  a  state  of  shock  while  trying  to  stay  functional  for  the  work  that  we  were  supposed  to  be  doing.

        I  will  never  forget  the shock  and  sorrow  for  those  lives  so  wantonly  taken  away  from  us  and  the  tragedy  that  those  who  perpretated  these  horrible  events  could  be  led  into  such  acts  by  such  a  mis-guided  philosophy.  While  we  attempt  to  deal  with  these  people  in  our  own  pathetic  ways,  my  real  belief  is  that  they  will  meet  their  appropriate  judgements  in  whatever comes  beyond  this  life  as  we  know  it.
