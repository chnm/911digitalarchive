        I had a 9 O'Clock class that day in Intro
         American Government. I had not seen the news
         before entering class and heard about the airline crash
        as I came out of class. I immediately tried to find out more info on
         online, but the lines were jammed. Finally, I went out and
        bought a radio so everyone in the dept. could hear what was
        happening.

        Like everyone else, I was stunned and the rest of the day
         was spent watching the events on tv ond talking through
        the events with friends. The deliberate, single-mindedness
        of the act struck us all as hard as the number of deaths.
        There was no goal to the destruction-no hostages to release,
        no policy to change, no specific act to protest. People
        died because they were in a building which was a symbol of
        America to fanatics.

        The university held multi-faith candle-light vigils and a
         series of symposiums on Islam, Terrorism, and American
         policies to help people deal with the event. The aftershocks
        are still occuring, but the initial question remains
        unanswered: What kind of mind plans and condones such an
        unprovoked attack on innocent people because they are
        different.
