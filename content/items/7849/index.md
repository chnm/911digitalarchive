        On September 11,2001 at 2:10 when my teacher Mrs.  Wood came into our classroom and told me and the class room that the WTC collapsed. When I got home to my house. My brother turned on the news and then it showed a big cloud of smoke.

            That was when I found out how serious the crash was.  I did not really know too much on the World Trade Centers but after seeing the crashes, and seeing the buildings fall down, I was beginning to get scared because our school is a big place and they could do some thing to it.

            This changed my life in many ways. One is because my cousin's friend worked at the WTC. Luckily she wasn't in the building. Her subway system was broken by a crashed train.  Another reason it changed my life was that my brother's gym teacher's husband was in one of the planes.  His name was Brian Sweeney.  He died and alot of other people did to.

            I think that everyone should be remembered that was involved in  September 11.The people who should be remembered are the people in the planes, the people in the buildings. The firefighters should also be remembered .
