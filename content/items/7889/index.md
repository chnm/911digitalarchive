        I was sitting at the UFO meeting (like the PTA) at my son's school, the University School, in Davie, FL.  It was the first meeting of the year.  I had just dropped off my daughter at her first day of Preschool at the Family Center.  I was with my friend, Colleen, when her Mother called on her cell phone, telling her that a plane had crashed into the WTC.  We weren't sure what to think.  Unfortunate accident?  Then my husband called with the same story & the 2nd plane.  Do we stay at the meeting?  I went upstairs where I Knew there was a tv.  The kid working at the snack bar wasn't even aware what was happening, as he had the sound off.  It was true, & the pictures were horrific!  I first called my friend, Lori, in NYC to find out if she was okay.  She was fine, but her husband was supposed to pitch for the Mets that night & had been evacuated from the Laguardia airport.  Now she had no idea where he was & couldn't reach him by cell.  I just begged her not to let him get on a plane.  She had to go get her daughter, as it was her 1st day of school.  My next call was to my brother-in-law in San Fran.  He was with the FBI. He should know what was going on.  I woke him up.  He had no idea what had happened.  He hadn't been called in yet.  Ironically, he had been asking for a transfer to FL & had just been denied the previous day.  I went back dowstairs to tell Colleen about the pictures I had seen.  Cell phones were ringing all around the room.  We were asked to turn them off.  The Powers That Be didn't know what was going on.  Then the Pentagon - I felt it was time to leave the meeting.

        I got in my car & immediately tuned to Howard Stern.  He was informing us exactly what was happening as he was watching it from his windows.  It seemed more real than watching it on tv.  I can vividly remember when he interrupted a caller to shout out that the Tower had just fallen, & Robin let out a shriek.  I called my husband, & he thought Howard was joking, trying to make light of things.  But that was the best part about his show.  He was honest with us & stayed on the air for hours & hours just keeping us informed.  THANKS HOWARD!
