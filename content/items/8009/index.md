      text: |-
        I was at work watching the T.V. in the break room when I heard and saw what happened.  I couldn't believe it.  Thousands of innocent people dead and the NY Skyline changed forever!  I was stunned all day. I got into my car at the end of the day and sobbed for a good half hour.

        When I was driving home to my other job (dance teacher for my mom's studio; Ceil Sharon School of Dance)I tried to compose myself so I would be strong for my students.  I walked into the studio, and said "ok, we ready to dance?".  One of the girls approached me and said "isn't it sad what happened today?".  I lost it again.  I couldn't believe that this innocent 13 year old was so touched.  She wasn't acting selfishly like most teens I know, but rather she was concerned and not interested in dancing at the time.  Soon after the other children piped up with their feelings.  The class turned into a discussion of 9/11...I will never forget that day.
