        Loss of a special Friend

        On September 11, 2001 my husband (Mike) lost his brother ,Jeff , on American Airlines Flight 11. I lost a special friend. Jeff was a kind , brillant man. He had several college degrees but the one that meant the most was from Wheaton College (Ministry). He was president of one company (E-Logic) and Vice-President of another (Cahners). His main goal in life was to be worthy of the Grace of God. Jeff taught Bible study and counciled young people. He took away my fears when I had cancer surgery. This man(Age 43) was a wonderful husband, father, son , brother, and friend. It was a privilage to be in the same room as him. What a loss!

        God Bless!
