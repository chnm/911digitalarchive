      text: Well I didn't have classes on tuesday, so I was sleeping in and I woke up for a little while to watch ER.  I fell back asleep while watching it, and when I woke back up, I saw the second plane hit the tower, and I wondered what "movie" I was watching.  THen I found out it wasn't a movie, and I just sat up in bed for the next 2 hours with my mouth hanging open.  It was unreal!  I then got a call from my mom.  About 5 minutes after the second plane hit, my Grandmother who had cancer, passed away peacefully in her sleep.  I was jealous of her for awhile, because she never knew of the horror, and I wish I never knew of it either.  Yet the amount of pride I have now for my country is totally unbelievable.  Proud to be an american has a whole new meaning now.  Now, a year later, I have started clinicals for nursing school.  We have to be trained for a terriost attack as well as a bioterism attack, which is really sad, that the world has come to this.  But in another way I'm so glad to have my life and my family.  When I babysat my neighbors two boys, I asked them what they wanted to be when they grew up.  both of them answered a fireman so they could be brave like those who were in New York.  God bless America, and let us thrive and continue to grow stronger each day.
