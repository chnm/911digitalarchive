        I was just getting ready for work, a "normal" day.  I remembering my mother telling me about it and watching it briefly on the news before I had to leave to work. The mall was slow and because of the events and out of respects we closed down the whole mall. I walked around the Idaho Falls greenbelt that day and newscasts were getting peoples thoughts and opinions.... what do u say to that? it was a terrible empty feeling in all of us. My love and prayers went out to those NY people and their families...I cried for weeks for the children that had lost their parents and people that had lost their spouces. There wasn't much I could do 1/2 way around the country but my prayers will always be with NY....
