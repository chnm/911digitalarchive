        The day began as any other normal day. I was ready to begin my job as a teacher at Yazoo City High School. I assumed the day would be like any other day. Both myself and my students would receive a lesson that will never be forgotten.
            First period came and went. Second period Advanced Placement Biology students and I went to the science lab to complete an experiment. The teacher next door to us said you have to turn on the television and see what has just happened in New York City. In a small town in Mississippi, I watched, along with my five students, as the World Trade Center buildings fell to the ground. I immediately thought of the people who did not make it out of those buildings and the individuals aboard the planes who became innocent pawns in a so called "spiritual"war.

I felt the need to call my mother and make sure everything was okay. I called students parents to let them know they were okay. Then we simply watched the news unfold throughout the day.
