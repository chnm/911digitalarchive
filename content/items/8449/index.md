        Following is the email I sent to my family and friends around the world on September 30th, which expalins how I faced the 9/11.

        To all my loving family & friends,

        I know you have already heard and seen what has happened on September 11th in
        NY. I thank you each and every one of you for your concern and loving
        thoughts of us since that unforgettable, unthinkable tragedy. I was very
        unfortunate to witness it, but lucky to be still alive and able to write this
        letter to you. After all I thought I owe you let you know my story with
        details. Thank you very much again to all of you. I'll send all of you this
        email at once and try to send each one a separate email  as and when time
        allows.

        Usually I take 8 AM ferry from Staten Island to Manhattan which reach there
        by 8.30AM. Most of the time I walk to office from ferry terminal during
        summer. If I walk from the ferry terminal in Manhattan to my office (48th
        floor 1 WTC, north tower) it takes about 10-15 minutes. If I take subway it
        takes only 5-7 minutes. So I always reach office between 8.45 to 9.00 am. It
        was September 11th=911 (we dial 911 when there is an emergency)  Tuesday. As
        usual I reached Manhattan by 8.30. First I thought to take subway to reach
        office earlier that day since I didn't go to work on previous day. I had a
        second thought and walked to office since it was a sunny day. I reached the
        ground floor lobby by 8.46AM. I was waiting for an elevator to go to 48th
        floor. All of sudden I heard a big sound which came from opposite corner of
        where I was waiting. My first thought it was an elevator crash. Within
        seconds came a huge force of smoke & dust with a severe heat. It came with a
        such a force. I came to know later that one of my coworkers  who also was
        waiting for elevator thrown few yards away by that force. I was running
        towards a Citibank (in the lobby of WTC) passing some counters in the lobby.
        I remember kneeling by the counters while running. That must have helped me
        from throwing away by that force.
        I waited in the citibank for few minutes with few others. I was trying to
        call from Dhammika from there which was no success since telephone lines were
        jammed. Still I had no clue exactly what had happened. I thought it was a
        bomb. I was covered with dust and ashes. Some body's coffee was all over my
        cloths. Later I realized that my hair and ear got burned with that heat came
        down. That was 1st attack on 1 WTC. Then I came out of  1 WTC. I saw glasses
        were broken on ground floor. Those were few inches thick. I can't believe how
        it happened since the attack was on above 80th floor. I saw a guy lying on
        the ground who had burned all over. He was waiting for an ambulance.  He must
        have been on the opposite side of where I was standing. Because there was no
        time for anyone to come down from upper floors. I was lucky I was waiting for
        the elevator on the other end. I was lucky I walked to office that day. Other
        wise I would have come earlier. I was luckiy I didn't get an elevator sooner.
         Who knows? I could have stucked in an elevelor If I was on  one.

        I walked about few yards (on west side highway) towards the ferry. Mean time
        I saw 1 WTC top floor are on fire. People were just watching. Then I heard a
        huge noise of an airplane. It was like right above on my head. I didn't have
        time to lift my head to see it. That was the 2nd plane attack on 2 WTC.
        People realized then that this is not an accident and it is an attack. Most
        of them started to run. Still I remember some were looking at the burning
        buildings. However I didn't wait. I was trying to call Dhammika from every
        pay phone near by. I tried with several cell phones form strangers. But I
        couldn't. Finally I came to ferry terminal and was able to call dhammika. It
        was around 9.15AM. Dhammika has gone to drop Sachi to school. I left a
        message at our answering machine. WTC can be seen various parts of NY.
        Dhammika has seen WTC burning from Sachi's school. Some people have seen the
        2nd attack from there too. She knows that I am at office by then. However she
        had managed to come home. She has forgotten to take baby from car. She had
        tried to call my office. Obviously she couldn't. Then she tried to call a
        friend (Tanya) who works in down town and had a cell phone. But she couldn't
        get her either. Still she hasn't checked the messages. Finally she noticed
        and played the messages. 1st 2 were from friends calling and asking Dhammika
        to contact me. They were afraid to give all the details. The 3rd was my
        message. So she was relived. Later I called again and was able to talk to
        her. I was going to take 10 AM ferry to Staten Island and Dhammika was going
        to pick me up from ferry.

        While I was waiting to ferry 2 WTC collapsed. Everyone was in a shock. Some
        were crying. I don't think anyone ever thought that WTC would collapse like
        that. It was a chaos in the ferry. All were taking out life jackets and ready
        for some disaster. I was like numbed. I saw some were crying and shaking with
        fear. Once ferry moved few yards on the river I saw the 1 WTC collapsing. It
        was such a sad scene. I don't have proper words to explain my feelings. I was
        thinking about the people who I am working with. I have been working in that
        building for last 5 years. Then whole down town area started to cover with
        smoke and dust. I saw people vanished in the thick smoke who were on the
        land. At the same time ferry also covered with smoke and dust. It started to
        clear once ferry rode away from  Manhattan. Finally I came to Staten Island.
        There were no buses running from ferry terminal in SI. No cars were allowed
        come to terminal either. However Dhammika had come to the terminal been
        waiting for me. I had taken a wrong exit and didn't see her. I think I was in
        a shock and didn't know that I took a wrong exit. I thought Dhammika couldn't
        come and walked all the way (about 1-2 miles) to home. Dhammika was really
        worried thinking that I may have gone back to see the burning building since
        she didn't see me came by that ferry. Finally when she came home I was
        waiting outside. Then only she calmed down.

        Since then we were getting phone calls from so many people. It rang nonstop
        for few days. Most of them had to try for a long time to get the connection.
        I was really touched by everyone's concern. It's a nice feeling to know that
        other people really care about you. I thank again each and every one of you
        for your concern.

        Now I have recovered from the shock. But the sad feeling won't go away
        easily. Our office started in a temporary location in New Jersey. I never
        knew that it would be such a happy moment see my coworkers on the first day
        when we met each other at office. All were hugging each other. Luckily no one
        from my bank were missing or dead. Some were injured. But nothing major.
        Sadly a lot of people were missing at the other bank, (Fuji) with our bank
        merged.

        Still I take ferry everyday to go to work. Everyday I see down town Manhattan
        from Ferry. It used to be a beautiful scene from ferry. Specially in the
        night. Now without the two giants buildings it seems so empty. I still can't
        beleive how can a human being be so crual and hate another human being and
        create such a tradegy. I hope and pray who ever leads or participates in such
        acts to realize that no one would ever win any thing by hate or war.

        It became a long letter. Let's all of us pray for peace and never ever to
        come accross such a disater. There are about 7000 lives lost without any
        reason. I could have easily been among them.

        Take care.

        With love.

        Vipula/aiya/malli/putha
