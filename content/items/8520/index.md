        It was a normal day like any other day.  I was getting the kids ready for school/daycare.  I work for the Military full-time and decided to take that week off.  That morning I decided to put my military PTS on because they are so confortable and through my hair in a pony tail and the darnest thing I asked my son "is today a holiday"?  He looked at me and said "No".   To this day I still don't know why I said this.  After dropping the kids off, I decided to head for the college so I could buy my book for class.  I would say it was probably near 8:50 at this time.  I started going thru the radio channels and a man came on and said a plane went into the world trade center.  I couldn't believe it.  I ran in the book store and bought my book.  As I was heading back toward Auburn, MA (I felt like shopping that day)  I guess all the planes in the air were ordered to land.  I was near Worcester air port and the planes were coming in.  I have never seen so many big airplanes coming in all at once.  It was a very scary site.  The people on the radio were at this time reporting another plane going into the World trade center.  I knew right then we were under attach.  The first time could have been an accident but not the second.  I headed on 290 West and got off on exit 9 which was near rt 12/290 and I stopped at the set of lights and listened to the radio.  As I was sitting their a man with dark eyes and arabic features was starring at me.  I looked at him but quickly looked away because of the look on his face.  It was 0935.  As the man approached me because he was taken a right turn toward rt 290/mass pike.  I looked at him again and this time he took his left hand and slid it accross his throat like to slit it.  It was a matter of seconds I just sat their saying to myself, "He didn't do what I think he did"??I proceeded to the mall and it really didn't hit me until I saw the planes hitting the WWTc on TV.  I was scarred and I told everyone about it.  What really frightens me is after this man did this to me two minutes later a plane went into the pentagon.  Two months later I was sitting at the ford dealership getting my truck worked on and an older gentleman was sitting their too.  I told him this story and he told me he was on the masspike and spotted an arabic looking (suspicious) man driving in a white truck and he called the police.  All I can wonder if it was the same man who I saw and threated me.  It made me feel better but I still to this day live in fear.

        Rodna M. Happy
