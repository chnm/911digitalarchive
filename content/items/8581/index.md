      text: September 11, 2001 started out just like any other day.  I had been to work that morning and then to class later that afternoon.  I had stopped off at my apartment after work to prepare for class.  I was in such a rush that I did not have time to turn the television on or talk to anyone.  So it was not until I got to school that I overheard my classmates discussing what had happened.  I asked one of my classmates what was going on.  She said, "Where have you been, Mars?" It was really scary for me living in Texas City because there are a lot of power plants located in that area.  I also heard that Houston was a possible target.  I never really imagined something like that could have hit so close to home and me not even know what was going on.
