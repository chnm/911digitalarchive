        When my husband first showed me the link for this book, I had no interest whatsoever.  Fortunately, we knew no one personally who was lost in this tragic event. However, as a New Yorker and a human being, it was very difficult for me to deal with what had happened.  Initially I couldn't sleep and I would wake up crying about all of those innocent people who had lost their lives that day for no reason - whether in the towers, the planes or the pentagon.  When my husband received this book he encouraged me to look through it and I did.  Your book helped me realize that I should have been praying for all of the souls lost that day instead of crying.  I should have been praying for all of the families that had to suffer such a great lost.  So, from this point on, when I see an image of 9/11, instead of crying, I will pray that the survivors become stronger with each passing day and hopefully that there will come a time where they can truly be at peace.  Thank's for sharing your view with the world. Let us ever forget, and let us pray that nothing like this ever happens again.  DSC
