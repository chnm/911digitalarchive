        And God Said...

        Three weeks before the events of 9/11/01 I had a vivid dream.  I don't normally remember my dreams, but this was one of those dreams that wakes you up and makes you remember it.
        I was part of some bomb-squad team and we were all trying to get through a huge crowd of people and into a very tall glass building to stop a bomb.  Everyone in the crowd was frozen still and they each had a Bible in their hands--which was very strange for me to see because I never have religious symbols of any kind in my dreams.  Anyway, to make a long story shorter, I got into the building and went about 3/4 of the way up and had found the area of the bomb.  But it went off before I could stop it.  I was flipped over by the explosion and felt the building collapse under my back.  Then an even stranger symbol appeared in the dream.  I was "picked up" out of my body by what could only be called god--again, not religious and have never experienced religious symbols in any dreams.  A voice said to me, "Just watch.  Just observe my work."  And a huge gust of wind blew over all of those people in the crowd who had been clutching their bibles; and I saw thousands dead in my dream.  Normally this would have been a horrifying scene, but I felt a sense of peace.  I knew that even in this tragedy, a god was there; and there was a purpose for it even if I couldn't see it or change the circumstances.
        This photograph captures for me that same sense of peace and calm and moved me to tears the first time I layed eyes on it.  In my frame of reference, it is truly art because it captures the highest ideal of its particular subject matter.
        Thank you to the artist for seeing this image; and more importantly thank you for recording it for the world to share.
