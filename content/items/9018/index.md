        It was such a clear, beautiful day and, as always I could see the
        twin towers from my office building at JFK Airport.  Standing out-
        side, we noticed smoke coming from one of the towers and
        thought it might be in front of the tower, when we were told
        that a plane "hit" the WTC.  We entered the bldg and our 2nd floor
        hall is glass enclosed and faces the WTC and we all watched
        in horror as the 2nd plane hit and we saw the fireball.  Cries
        of agony came from all of us and we were not sure if another
        target would be the airport.  I am the mother of a NYFD
        Capt and did not know if my son was at work that day; his
        firehouse is located near the World Trade Center.  It wasn't
        until hours later that I found out that he did not have to
        report to work that morning and to this day I thank God for
        sparing him.  He lost 4 of his men and is still attending
        memorial services for all of his "brothers".  My heart will
        always ache for all who perished that day; I appreciate my Country
        and all it stands for and will never forget those evil acts.
        My comunity, Rockaway, lost so many of our bravest and finest.
        but we continue to stand together and never forget those who
        perished that day.  God bless us all.
