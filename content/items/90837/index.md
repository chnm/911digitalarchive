        All of you taking photos

        I saw the "Here is New York" exhibition just around the corner from Guggenheim Soho in November 2001 and this image just hit me so hard.  It's a wonderful meta-narrative presented here: a photograph of a text telling people not to take photos.  I didn't go that close to Ground Zero, though I have been asked many times since my visit whether I did, as if somehow visiting New York so soon after the events of Sept 11 made it an imperative tourist activity.  I had no intention of going to the site, and this image helped me to explain why when I got back to England.  At that moment especially, when so much work was still being done to clear the area, it was not even a burial site: it was an open grave, the site of a still burning funeral pyre ... and I am of the belief that watching and photographing such things is at the very least ghoulish. thank you to the photographer who captured this open letter, and to the firegirl of New York City who so eloquently criticised the peering crowds.
