        I remember at 8:47, a co-worker next door came in and told
        us that a plane had just hit the World Trade Center.  My
        first reaction was - How can someone miss the World Trade
        Center???  As events unfolded, it was becoming clearer that
        someone hadn't "missed" the WTC, but instead INTENDED to hit
        it. Within a few minutes - a 2nd plane hit the South Tower
        and a bit after that was the plane crash in PA.  I remember
        thinking, How many planes are going to crash!?  At the
        same time, reports of terrorism were being circulated
        and it then became complete chaos.  I didn't personally lose
        anyone in this disaster, however, it's something I will
        never forget and my thoughts often go back to the victims
        and their families whose lives have been forever changed.
        Maybe some day we will understand the reason for this seemingly
        unreasonable and dispicable act, but until then, my faith
        in God - although somewhat shaken - needs to take over.  I
        hope the children of our future never have to go through
        anything like this.         Maria
