        I Dropped to My Knees

        On the 11th of September, I was teaching at a New Jersey high school.  I knew nothing until 10:20 am when the principal made the announcement.  When I finally made it to a TV during my lunch period, the first thing I saw was a tape of one of the towers collapsing.  I don't even know which one it was, probably the second one.  As I watched the close up of the floors collapsing on top of one another, I collapsed to my knees.  I don't know when I was able to stand again.  I don't think I got up until the very loud bell rang to signal the end of the period.  It scared me so that I jumped up and started running.  When I realized that it was just the same school bell that I hear over 25 times each day, I realized how scared I was.  This photo reminded me of that.
