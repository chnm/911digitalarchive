      text: My daughter was getting ready to film a commercial for a local consignment shop of childrens clothing.  The first thing we heard was that two planes had crashed in the air.  A little later we were informed that that information was incorrect and that the WTC had been hit by a plane. We were at a television station when the commerical was filming so there were TV sets to watch.  We kept filming the commerical, then everyone was asked to leave.  I received a call from my husband on my cell phone.  He wanted me to hurry home and let him know when I got there. When I got home I did indeed call my husband to let him know our daughter(age 3 at the time) and I were indeed home safe and sound.  I immediately turned on the TV and started to watch the events.  I think I was in total shock.  I made my daughter go to her room to play.  Later in the day I had to go to work.  It was very difficult to work that day.  Everyone had their radio's on.  I had a radio that would receive TV channels.  I listened as I worked.  Now, I do hug my daughter a little tighter.  My husband & I had always given each other a hug and a kiss before we left to start our day before Sept. 11.  Now I feel it is even more important.  My birthday is Sept. 12.  Since 2001, my birthday will not be as happy as before 2001.  I will always remember those that lost their lives.  God Bless the heros of September 11, 2001 and God Bless the United States of America.
