        On the morning of September 11, 2001, I was sitting down to eat breakfast at our kitchen table.  I was a senior in college at Montana Tech; I had an 8:00 class that day.  We had a small TV in our kitchen that my mom always has tuned into the "Today" show.  It was my morning routine to sit down and eat my Cheerios while watching.  I remember coming into the kitchen and seeing the first tower smoking.  I asked my mom what was going on, and she said a plane had crashed into the tower.  I thought it was an accident, until I watched in horror as the second plane hit.  My dad walked in just as it happened, and he said "Is that a movie or something?"  I told him no, that it wasn't.  I knew then that I was witness to history in the making.  I stayed home from school that day and watched television.  I watched as both towers collapsed and listened as a news correspondent felt the shake in the Pentagon as the 3rd plane hit.  I remember feeling so helpless to be so far away.  I was so shocked and disgusted that anyone could do something like this in the name of their God.  I will never ever forget how it felt, and that's the way it should be.

        God Bless America.  I love you all, and I'm so proud of you, my brothers and sisters.
