Leif Boman presented his piece "ge.nos0911a" at the Stockholm Culture house for the one year anniversary of 9/11. Working with the Linköpings University, Leif found a special spectrographic technique to get the unique sound waves from the ash and the concrete dust from Ground zero and translate them into music. Leif  traveled the world from 1993 through 2000 collecting soil samples from different couintries and transmuting them into memorials.
