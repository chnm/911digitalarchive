Jaime Davidovich was an artist in residence at the World Views Program at the WTC in 2000.  He describes his work – video pieces and stills – which incorporates everyday objects and views from his WTC studio with images of TVs. He talks about how his work's meaning has changed after 9/11. Jaime commissions soundtracks from the group Reynols – the members are Miguel Tomasin, Roberto Conlazo, Alan Courtis and Pacu Conlazo.
