Pia Lindman was an artist in the "New Views" program at the World Financial Center October 30, 2002 throiugh January 17, 2003. Her sound installation "Bridge" was located on the pedestrian bridge between 1 and 2 WFC, a space from which one could see both the World Trade Center site and the harbor. The audio integrates elements of the neighborhood: the soft lapping of the waves and the loud booming  of demolition and reconstruction.
