  How has your life changed because of what happened on September 11, 2001?:
    - html: false
      text: 9/11 is forever now a part of our everyday life & soul.  When I hear those numbers,they mean the day our country's heart was broken.  I've not changed as the terrorists intended,becoming a hateful,fearful person. I've grown to love my country even more & all that she stands for.  I'm not as naive as I once was, and no longer accept that those who would do such serious harm can be reasoned with as civilised people.  I don't trust as much as I did but I also love more those who truly stand for good positive values in life.
  How will you remember the 10th anniversary of the September 11 attacks?:
    - html: false
      text: As I write this,the 11th anniversary will be coming up in a couple of months.  I spent that day alone,watching some memorial on TV.  I wasn't able to do that until about 4 yrs. ago as it was too painful. I cried & sat in inspired awe as I listened to reports of stories I'd not heard,about the bravery & commitment of so many people to help each other.  God bless the USA & those who live as she does.  Imperfect though we may be in many ways,we've been a great nation & I thank God to be here!
