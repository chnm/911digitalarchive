  How has your life changed because of what happened on September 11, 2001?:
    - html: false
      text: Interviewing Blaine McCartney he believes security has had a great deal of change brought to it. Airport’s having far more ways to deal with an attack now than they ever did before. People worry another attack might take place or that our economy will never recover. America will always remember September 11, 2001 it was a turning point for the government, gaining support for the war in Afghanistan and spreading fear to the people.
  How will you remember the 10th anniversary of the September 11 attacks?:
    - html: false
      text: Mr. McCartney had this to say, I remember September 11 every year by going to a Patriotic parade, doing three Hail Mary’s for every person killed, and lighting off fireworks to end the night. I feel the anniversary of 9/11 is an important day for all Americans to remember those lost in the Twin Towers.
