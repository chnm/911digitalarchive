  How will you remember the 10th anniversary of the September 11 attacks?:
    - html: false
      text: While interviewing Patty Flanigan I asked her questions on what she remembered from September 11, 2001.  She was working at a medical facility, giving chemo therapy. The room became chaotic, but then there was a moment of silence in disbelief. She was shocked, her, the doctors, and the patients couldn’t believe it happened. She thought it would lead to a war, and that a lot of people had lost their lives. Even though she was at work she wanted to keep listening to find out more information.
