      text: How has my life changed since September 11, 2001? I feel that I was really scared and the feeling of not knowing what is going to happen and seeing the people on TV panicking is how every person in the United States felt that day. I would say now days we are a lot more cautious with airport security and how we make everyday decisions we make. 9/11 was a day we people who lived through it won’t forget but for a new generation it would be like Pearl Harbor. How it is that one man can bring total chaos to a nation but we have seen this before in Germany. Is peace impossible anymore? Fighting over what a skin tone, a religion? People need to open their eyes and see that we all are the same in many more ways than different. Why hurt innocent people for what you stand for. 9/11 should be a day in the future where humanity looks back not as a country but as a whole and sees the mistakes and makes them correct.
