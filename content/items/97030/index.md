  How has your life changed because of what happened on September 11, 2001?:
    - html: false
      text: |-
        I was asleep on a bed at an army hospital on September 11.  We were awaken by the cry of other patients who were watching the news that early morning.  A nurse walked into my room and told me that the USA is under attack and to turn on the news.  A few seconds into the broadcast, I felt a weird feeling deep in my guts knowing that many lives will be lost.  I picked up the phone calling relatives and friends to turn on their TV.  I could heard in the tone of their voices the fear and the sadness of the tragedy that was right in front of us.  You can heard people shouting and burst out crying from the end of ward as the newscaster reported the number of deaths and the details of such devastating moment.

        God bless America.  From around the world, people expressed their deepest sympathy.  And for a moment, there is nothing in your heart except the great AMERICA.
