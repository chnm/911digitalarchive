  How has your life changed because of what happened on September 11, 2001?:
    - html: false
      text: Since I was fairly young when 9/11 happened, it is difficult to say whether the way I live my life is directly related to what happened on that day, or if I would be the same person whether that had happened or not. I am certainly more aware of the world and how, especially with the internet, everyone is becoming more connected. It is easier to know more about countries and cultures than ever before, but it is also easier to make judgments. In general, I have become more aware of other people and how they view the world, and that is something I am grateful for.
