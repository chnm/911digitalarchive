<p><em>The September 11 Digital Archive</em> would like to thank its partners for their generosity in helping us to collect, preserve and present the history of September 11.</p>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.loc.gov/"><img alt="Library of Congress" border="0" class="floatleft" height="108" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/LCicon.gif" width="125"/></a>
<p><em>The September 11 Digital Archive</em> is pleased to announce that it has partnered with the <a href="http://www.loc.gov/" target="external">Library of Congress</a> to ensure the long-term preservation of its collections. To mark the occasion a day-long symposium entitled September 11 as History: <em>Collecting Today for Tomorrow</em> was hosted at the Library on September 10, 2003. <a href="http://www.loc.gov/rr/program/911symposium/" target="external">Click here</a> for more information about the symposium. <a href="http://www.loc.gov/today/pr/2003/03-142.html" target="external">Click here</a> for more information about the Archive's historic partnership with the Library of Congress.</p>
</div>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.americanhistory.si.edu/september11"><img alt="Smithsonian Institution" border="0" class="floatleft" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/sipartner.gif"/></a>
<p><em>The September 11 Digital Archive</em> is working with the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History, Behring Center to collect both the text and digital audio stories of visitors to the exhibition and website "September 11: Bearing Witness to History," open at the museum from September 11, 2002 until July 6, 2003.</p>
</div>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.sonicmemorial.org"><img alt="Sonic Memorial" border="0" class="floatleft" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/sonicpartner.gif"/></a>
<p>Shortly after September 11, the creators of NPR's "Lost &amp; Found Sound", brought together radio producers, artists, historians, archivists, and the public broadcasting community came together to collect and preserve audio traces of the World Trade Center, its neighborhood and the events of 9/11. "Lost &amp; Found Sound" and NPR set up the Sonic Memorial phone line to record your stories and collect audio contributions.</p>
</div>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.redcross.org/museum/" target="external"><img alt="American red Cross" border="0" class="floatleft" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/arch2lr.gif"/></a>
<p><em>The September 11 Digital Archive</em> is pleased to announce its collaboration with the <a href="http://www.redcross.org/museum/" target="external">American Red Cross Museum</a> to document the personal histories of Red Cross employees, volunteers, and those involved in the humanitarian efforts in New York, Western Pennsylvania, and Northern Virginia.</p>
</div>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.mixnet.com/"><img alt="MIXNET" border="0" class="floatleft" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/mixnet.gif"/></a>
<p>MIXNET develops and hosts customized, off-the-shelf web based applications for businesses, non-profit organizations and government. Mixnet is working with the September 11 Digital Archive to digitize and archive the September 11 recollections of visitors to the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History.</p>
</div>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.uReachCorp.com/"><img alt="uReach" border="0" class="floatleft" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/ureach.jpg"/></a>
<p>A leading provider of communications solutions that range from voice mail to unified communications, uReach is helping the September 11 Digital Archive record the digital audio recollections of our visitors.</p>
</div>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.seagate.com/"><img alt="Seagate" border="0" class="floatleft" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/seagate.gif"/></a>
<p>The world's largest manufacturer of disc drives, magnetic discs and read-write heads, Seagate Technology is helping the September 11 Digital Archive meet its long-term archival data storage needs.</p>
</div>
<div class="gallery"><a href="http://www.streamingculture.org/"><img alt="Streaming Culture" border="0" class="floatleft" src="http://911digitalarchive.org/imgs/sclogosmall.gif"/></a>
<p>A non-profit organization, StreamingCulture makes streaming audio and video affordable to arts and cultural organizations. StreamingCulture is helping the September 11 Digital Archive to meets its high-volume streaming requirements.</p>
</div>
